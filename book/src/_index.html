About this book关于本书

Independent component analysis recovers hidden signals from their linear mixtures using two assumptions: the signals are statistically independent, and at most one of them is Gaussian. These two assumptions lead to a remarkably complete theory, with a sharp identifiability theorem, a family of contrast functions linked by information theory, algorithms with provable convergence, exact asymptotic error formulas and a Cramér–Rao bound, and clear limits once the model is stretched to more sources than sensors or to nonlinear mixing. It is also one of the workhorses of brain imaging.

独立成分分析只依靠两个假设，即各信号相互统计独立、且其中至多一个服从高斯分布，就能从线性混合中恢复隐藏的信号。这两个假设引出了一套相当完整的理论：一个精确的可辨识性定理，一族由信息论联系起来的对比函数，可证明收敛的算法，精确的渐近误差公式与 Cramér–Rao 界，以及当模型推广到源多于传感器或非线性混合时清晰的边界。它也是脑影像分析的主力工具之一。

This book develops the subject from first principles to research level. Every chapter states its results precisely, proves the central ones or points to where the proofs can be found, and checks the theory against small numerical experiments that readers can run themselves. Each chapter closes with exercises, from routine checks to derivations of published results, with worked solutions.

本书从基本原理一直讲到研究前沿。每一章都精确陈述结论，对核心结果给出证明或指出证明的出处，并用读者可以自己运行的小型数值实验来检验理论。每章末尾配有习题，从常规练习到已发表结果的推导，都附有完整解答。

How to read it如何阅读

The prerequisites are linear algebra (eigendecompositions, orthogonal matrices) and probability at the level of an undergraduate course. The chapters on statistical theory also use basic asymptotic statistics; Appendix A collects what is needed. Two paths through the book work well:

预备知识是线性代数（特征分解、正交矩阵）和本科程度的概率论。统计理论各章还会用到基本的渐近统计，所需内容汇集在附录 A。推荐两条阅读路线：

  • Practice first. Chapters 1, 3, 5 and 8, then Part VI on fMRI.
  • Theory first. Parts I and II in order, then Chapters 7, 10, 12 and 13.
  • 应用优先。第 1、3、5、8 章，然后读第六部分（fMRI）。
  • 理论优先。按顺序读第一、二部分，再读第 7、10、12、13 章。

Use the switch at the top of every page to read in English, in Chinese, or in both languages paragraph by paragraph; the choice is remembered. Links jump between chapters, theorems, equations and exercises, and the contents panel on the left (behind the Contents button on a phone) follows you through the book.

每页顶部的切换按钮可以选择英文、中文，或者中英逐段对照阅读，选择会被记住。链接可以在章节、定理、公式和习题之间跳转；左侧的目录栏（手机上点目录按钮打开）在全书中随时可用。

Further reading延伸阅读

Four books cover the field from complementary angles:

以下四本书从不同角度覆盖了这一领域：

  1. Hyvärinen, A., Karhunen, J., & Oja, E. (2001). Independent Component Analysis. Wiley.
  2. Comon, P., & Jutten, C. (Eds.) (2010). Handbook of Blind Source Separation: Independent Component Analysis and Applications. Academic Press.
  3. Cichocki, A., & Amari, S. (2002). Adaptive Blind Signal and Image Processing: Learning Algorithms and Applications. Wiley.
  4. Stone, J. V. (2004). Independent Component Analysis: A Tutorial Introduction. MIT Press.
