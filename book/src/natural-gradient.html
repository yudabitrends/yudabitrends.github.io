From a contrast to an update rule从对比函数到更新规则

reduced ICA to an optimization problem: find the unmixing matrix $\bW$ that maximizes the log-likelihood , or to minimize the mutual information of the outputs. These objectives share separating optima under a correct source model, but a fixed misspecified likelihood also contains marginal mismatch and is not generally equivalent to mutual-information minimization. This chapter turns that problem into algorithms that adjust $\bW$ step by step. Three questions run through it. In which direction should $\bW$ move? How does the behaviour of the algorithm depend on the unknown mixing matrix $\bA$? And which solutions are attractors, so that the algorithm actually settles on them?

把 ICA 归结为一个优化问题：寻找使对数似然 最大的解混矩阵 $\bW$，或者使输出的互信息最小。源模型正确时，它们共享分离的最优解；但固定的失配似然还包含边缘失配项，通常不等价于互信息最小化。本章把这个问题变成逐步调整 $\bW$ 的算法。贯穿全章的有三个问题：$\bW$ 应当沿什么方向移动？算法的行为如何依赖于未知的混合矩阵 $\bA$？哪些解是吸引子，也就是说，算法实际会停在哪些解上？

The answers are, in order: along the relative or natural gradient, which needs no matrix inversion; not at all, a property called equivariance; and the separating solutions attract the algorithm when a simple condition linking the nonlinearity to the source distributions holds, and not when it fails. The same condition explains why the original Infomax algorithm separates typical super-Gaussian sources such as speech but fails on some sub-Gaussian ones, and how the extended version repairs this.

三个答案依次是：沿着相对梯度（即自然梯度）的方向移动，这一方向不需要矩阵求逆；完全不依赖，这一性质称为等变性；当非线性函数与源分布之间满足一个简单的条件时，分离解吸引算法，条件不满足时则不然。同一个条件解释了为什么原始的 Infomax 算法能分离语音这类常见的超高斯源，却在某些亚高斯源上失效，以及扩展 Infomax 如何弥补这一缺陷。

The ordinary gradient普通梯度

For one observation, the log-likelihood of $\bW$ is $\sum_i \log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$, where $\bw_i^\top$ is the $i$-th row of $\bW$. Its expectation

对单个观测，$\bW$ 的对数似然为 $\sum_i \log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$，其中 $\bw_i^\top$ 是 $\bW$ 的第 $i$ 行。它的期望

$$\ell(\bW) = \E\Bigl[\sum_{i=1}^n \log p_i(y_i)\Bigr] + \log|\det\bW|, \qquad \by = \bW\bx,$$

is the objective of this chapter. Batch algorithms replace $\E$ by the sample average $\hat\E$ over $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$, which turns $\ell$ into $\frac1T\log L(\bW)$. Since $\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$ () and $\partial\log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx)/\partial\bw_i = -\varphi_i(y_i)\,\bx$, the gradient is

就是本章的目标函数。批量算法把 $\E$ 换成对 $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$ 的样本平均 $\hat\E$，此时 $\ell$ 就是 $\frac1T\log L(\bW)$。由 $\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$（）以及 $\partial\log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx)/\partial\bw_i = -\varphi_i(y_i)\,\bx$，梯度为

$$\nabla\ell(\bW) = \bW^{-\top} - \E\bigl[\bphi(\by)\,\bx^\top\bigr].$$

Setting the gradient to zero and multiplying on the right by $\bW^\top$ gives the estimating equations $\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top] = \bI$, that is, $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\,y_j] = \delta_{ij}$ for all $i, j$. Every separating matrix satisfies the off-diagonal equations, whatever the nonlinearities: if $y_i$ and $y_j$ are multiples of two different sources, then $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_j] = \E[\varphi_i(y_i)]\,\E[y_j] = 0$ by independence and zero means. The diagonal equations only fix the scale of each output. This is the robustness to a wrong source model noted in : the model does not move the separating points. What it decides is whether an algorithm converges to them, the subject of .

令梯度为零并右乘 $\bW^\top$，得到估计方程 $\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top] = \bI$，即对所有 $i, j$ 有 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\,y_j] = \delta_{ij}$。无论非线性函数取什么，任何分离矩阵都满足其中的非对角方程：若 $y_i$ 与 $y_j$ 分别是两个不同源的倍数，则由独立性与零均值，$\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_j] = \E[\varphi_i(y_i)]\,\E[y_j] = 0$。对角方程只确定各输出的尺度。这正是中提到的对源模型设错的稳健性：模型不会移动分离点，它决定的是算法能否收敛到分离点，这是的主题。

Plain gradient ascent, $\bW \leftarrow \bW + \mu\nabla\ell(\bW)$ with a step size $\mu > 0$, has two flaws. It needs the inverse $\bW^{-\top}$ at every step. More seriously, its behaviour depends on $\bA$. Write the update in terms of the global matrix $\bP = \bW\bA$, using $\bW^{-\top} = \bP^{-\top}\bA^\top$, $\bx^\top = \bs^\top\bA^\top$ and $\by = \bP\bs$:

普通的梯度上升 $\bW \leftarrow \bW + \mu\nabla\ell(\bW)$（步长 $\mu > 0$）有两个缺点。第一，每一步都要计算逆矩阵 $\bW^{-\top}$。第二，也是更严重的，它的行为依赖于 $\bA$。利用 $\bW^{-\top} = \bP^{-\top}\bA^\top$、$\bx^\top = \bs^\top\bA^\top$ 和 $\by = \bP\bs$，把更新改写成全局矩阵 $\bP = \bW\bA$ 的形式：

$$\bP \leftarrow \bP + \mu\bigl(\bP^{-\top} - \E[\bphi(\by)\,\bs^\top]\bigr)\,\bA^\top\bA.$$

The fixed matrix $\bA^\top\bA$ acts as a preconditioner chosen by nature. When $\bA$ is ill-conditioned, the steps are long in some directions and very short in others, and no single step size suits all of them. 固定矩阵 $\bA^\top\bA$ 相当于一个由“自然”指定的预条件矩阵。$\bA$ 病态时，更新在某些方向上步子很大，在另一些方向上步子极小，没有一个统一的步长能同时适合所有方向。的（a）图显示了这一效应：条件数为 10 时，普通梯度上升在 150 次迭代中几乎原地不动。

Relative and natural gradients相对梯度与自然梯度

Before the geometry. Read $\Delta\bW=\mu\bH\bW$ as “a small correction in the present output coordinates.” The rightmost $\bW$ converts that correction back to a sensor-to-output map. You can use and check this matrix multiplication before studying the right-invariant metric below.

先理解操作，再看几何。把 $\Delta\bW=\mu\bH\bW$ 读成“在当前输出坐标里作一个小修正”。最右边的 $\bW$ 把修正接回从传感器到输出的映射。可以先会算、会核对这个矩阵乘法，再研究下面的右不变度量。

$$\bW \leftarrow \bW + \mu\bigl(\bI - \E[\bphi(\by)\,\by^\top]\bigr)\bW.$$

Amari, Cichocki and Yang (1996) reached the same rule as a natural gradient, a steepest-ascent direction for a geometry adapted to the problem; Amari (1998) develops the idea in general. On the group of invertible matrices, measure a small change $d\bW$ by the size of the relative change, $\|d\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F$. This metric is invariant under right multiplication, $\bW \to \bW\bB$, because $(d\bW\,\bB)(\bW\bB)^{-1} = d\bW\,\bW^{-1}$; right multiplication is the natural symmetry of the problem, since $\by = \bW\bx = (\bW\bB)(\bB^{-1}\bx)$. To find the steepest direction, maximize the first-order change $\langle\nabla\ell, d\bW\rangle$ subject to $\|d\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F = \delta$. Equivariance等变性

Multiplying on the right by $\bA$ removes the mixing matrix from the dynamics altogether.

把 右乘 $\bA$，混合矩阵就从动力学中完全消失了。

Two caveats. Equivariance is a property of the noiseless model: with $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$ the outputs are $\bP\bs + \bW\bn$, and the noise term depends on $\bW$ and hence on $\bA$ (). And it says nothing about the starting point: with $\bW_0 = \bI$ the algorithm starts at $\bP_0 = \bA$, which is far from separation when $\bA$ is ill-conditioned. Whitening first starts it at the orthogonal matrix $\bP_0 = \tilde\bA$ instead, which is one good reason to whiten the data before iterating. Because performance does not depend on $\bA$, the statistical analysis of can assume $\bA = \bI$ without loss of generality.

有两点需要注意。其一，等变性只对无噪声模型成立：若 $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$，输出为 $\bP\bs + \bW\bn$，噪声项依赖于 $\bW$，从而依赖于 $\bA$（）。其二，等变性不涉及起点：取 $\bW_0 = \bI$ 时，算法从 $\bP_0 = \bA$ 出发，$\bA$ 病态时这个起点离分离很远。先做白化，起点就变成正交矩阵 $\bP_0 = \tilde\bA$，这是迭代之前先白化数据的一个充分理由。由于性能与 $\bA$ 无关，的统计分析可以不失一般性地假设 $\bA = \bI$。

Local stability of the separating points分离点的局部稳定性

What the proof answers. Imagine nudging an already separating matrix slightly. Stability asks whether nearby updates shrink or enlarge that error. It does not promise that an arbitrary start reaches this neighborhood, and a finite step can fail even when the infinitesimal direction is favorable.

这些条件只涉及单个源和源的两两组合，从不涉及三个源。在归一化条件下记 $\gamma_i = k_i\sigma_i^2$。条件（ii）与（iii）合起来就是：$\gamma_i > 0$，且对所有源对有 $\gamma_i\gamma_j > 1$。一个简单的充分条件是每个源都有 $\gamma_i > 1$；若有两个源的 $\gamma < 1$，则这一对不稳定。对高斯源，无论非线性函数取什么，都有 $\gamma_i = 1$（），所以高斯源总是处在边界上；下面的结果表明，用真实得分函数时，每个非高斯源都严格位于稳定区域之内。

import numpy as np

def natural_gradient_ica(X, phi, mu=0.1, iters=500):
    """Batch natural-gradient ICA: W <- W + mu (I - E[phi(y) y^T]) W."""
    n, T = X.shape
    W = np.eye(n)
    for _ in range(iters):
        Y = W @ X
        W += mu * (np.eye(n) - phi(Y) @ Y.T / T) @ W
    return W

def extended_infomax(X, mu=0.1, iters=500):
    """phi_i(y) = y + k_i tanh(y), k_i = +1 or -1 set by the switching rule."""
    n, T = X.shape
    W = np.eye(n)
    k = np.ones(n)  # retain the previous sign when the statistic is near zero
    for _ in range(iters):
        Y = W @ X
        stat = (np.mean(1 - np.tanh(Y) ** 2, axis=1) * np.mean(Y ** 2, axis=1)
                - np.mean(np.tanh(Y) * Y, axis=1))
        k = np.where(stat > 1e-6, 1, np.where(stat < -1e-6, -1, k))
        W += mu * (np.eye(n) - (Y + k[:, None] * np.tanh(Y)) @ Y.T / T) @ W
    return W

def interference(P):
    """Max over rows of P of second-largest / largest |entry|; 0 = separated."""
    R = np.sort(np.abs(P), axis=1)
    return np.max(R[:, -2] / R[:, -1])

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
T = 20000
A = np.array([[1.0, 0.6],
              [0.4, 1.0]])

           "Laplace": rng.laplace(0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), (2, T))}        # kappa = +3
methods = {"tanh": lambda X: natural_gradient_ica(X, np.tanh),
           "cube": lambda X: natural_gradient_ica(X, lambda y: y ** 3),
           "extended": extended_infomax}
for sname, S in sources.items():
    for mname, method in methods.items():
        W = method(A @ S)
        print(f"{sname:8s} {mname:9s} interference = {interference(W @ A):.3f}")

Bell and Sejnowski (1995) arrived at the same family of algorithms from a different principle. Pass the outputs $y_i = \bw_i^\top\bx$ through the logistic function $g(y) = 1/(1 + e^{-y})$ and choose $\bW$ to maximize the joint entropy of $\bu = (g(y_1), \dots, g(y_n))^\top$, an output-entropy criterion motivated by information transmission with small output noise. For a deterministic continuous invertible map the input–output mutual information is generally infinite; the finite objective here is the output entropy, as explained in . Bell 与 Sejnowski（1995）从另一个原理出发得到了同一族算法。把输出 $y_i = \bw_i^\top\bx$ 通过 logistic 函数 $g(y) = 1/(1 + e^{-y})$，再选取 $\bW$ 使 $\bu = (g(y_1), \dots, g(y_n))^\top$ 的联合熵最大，这是由小输出噪声下的信息传输所启发的输出熵准则。对确定性连续可逆映射，输入与输出的互信息通常为无穷大；这里的有限目标是输出熵，详见。由于 $g$ 单调递增且 $\by = \bW\bx$，由变量替换公式和得

$$H(\bu) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW| + \sum_{i=1}^n \E\bigl[\log g'(y_i)\bigr].$$

Up to the constant $H(\bx)$ this is the objective $\ell(\bW)$ of with model densities $p_i = g'$, the logistic density: Infomax is maximum likelihood with a logistic source model (Cardoso, 1997; ). Since $g' = g(1 - g)$, the score of this model is $\varphi(y) = 2g(y) - 1 = \tanh(y/2)$ (). Bell and Sejnowski's gradient is $\nabla H(\bu) = \bW^{-\top} + \E[(\mathbf{1} - 2\mathbf{g}(\by))\bx^\top]$, where $\mathbf{1}$ is the vector of ones and $\mathbf{g}$ acts componentwise; this is with $\bphi = 2\mathbf{g} - \mathbf{1}$. They also adapt a bias $\bw_0$ in $\by = \bW\bx + \bw_0$, which we omit for centered data. In practice the algorithm is usually run in its natural-gradient form.

除去常数 $H(\bx)$，这正是 中的目标函数 $\ell(\bW)$，其模型密度为 $p_i = g'$，即 logistic 密度：Infomax 就是以 logistic 分布为源模型的最大似然（Cardoso, 1997；）。由于 $g' = g(1 - g)$，这一模型的得分函数为 $\varphi(y) = 2g(y) - 1 = \tanh(y/2)$（）。Bell 与 Sejnowski 给出的梯度是 $\nabla H(\bu) = \bW^{-\top} + \E[(\mathbf{1} - 2\mathbf{g}(\by))\bx^\top]$，其中 $\mathbf{1}$ 是全 1 向量，$\mathbf{g}$ 逐分量作用；这就是取 $\bphi = 2\mathbf{g} - \mathbf{1}$ 时的 。他们还在 $\by = \bW\bx + \bw_0$ 中自适应调整偏置 $\bw_0$；对已中心化的数据，这里略去偏置。实践中通常使用它的自然梯度形式。

  1. Center the data; optionally whiten them, $\bz = \bV\bx$, so that the iteration starts from an orthogonal global matrix.
  2. Set $\bW = \bI$ and choose a step size $\mu$ and a block size $B$ ($B = T$ gives the batch algorithm).
  3. For each block of $B$ samples, compute $\by = \bW\bx$ for the block and update $\bW \leftarrow \bW + \mu\bigl(\bI - \hat\E_B[\tanh(\by/2)\,\by^\top]\bigr)\bW$, where $\hat\E_B$ averages over the block.
  4. After each pass through the data, reduce $\mu$ if the change of $\bW$ has grown; stop when the change falls below a tolerance.
  5. Return $\bW$ (or $\bW\bV$ if the data were whitened).
  1. 中心化数据；可以选择先白化 $\bz = \bV\bx$，使迭代从正交的全局矩阵出发。
  2. 令 $\bW = \bI$，选取步长 $\mu$ 与块大小 $B$（$B = T$ 即批量算法）。
  3. 对每个含 $B$ 个样本的数据块，计算该块的 $\by = \bW\bx$，并更新 $\bW \leftarrow \bW + \mu\bigl(\bI - \hat\E_B[\tanh(\by/2)\,\by^\top]\bigr)\bW$，其中 $\hat\E_B$ 表示块内平均。
  4. 每遍历一次数据后，若 $\bW$ 的变化量增大，就减小 $\mu$；当变化量低于容差时停止。
  5. 返回 $\bW$（若数据已白化，返回 $\bW\bV$）。

Batch iteration. With a constant step size, is a fixed-point iteration whose linearization at a separating point is $\bepsilon \leftarrow \bepsilon - \mu\,\mathcal{L}(\bepsilon)$, where $\mathcal{L}$ is the linear map of the proof of . Online learning. The online rule updates $\bW$ with one sample, or a small block, at a time. With a constant step size $\bW$ does not converge; it keeps fluctuating around the separating point, with a spread that shrinks as $\mu$ decreases. The mean flow describes the average motion, so the stability conditions of decide which points the online algorithm can hover around. Step sizes that decrease with $\sum_t\mu_t = \infty$ and $\sum_t\mu_t^2 < \infty$, as in stochastic approximation (Robbins & Monro, 1951), control accumulated noise under additional regularity and stability conditions. They do not alone guarantee convergence: bounded iterates, suitable noise moments and a basin of attraction are also needed, and a stable unwanted root can still be reached. Online learning suits data that arrive as a stream or do not fit in memory, and it can track a mixing matrix that drifts slowly; for a fixed data set, batch or block updates are simpler to tune.

在线学习。在线规则每次只用一个样本（或一个小数据块）更新 $\bW$。步长固定时 $\bW$ 不会收敛，而是在分离点附近持续波动，波动幅度随 $\mu$ 减小而减小。平均流 描述的是平均运动，所以在线算法能在哪些点附近徘徊，同样由的稳定性条件决定。像随机逼近（Robbins & Monro, 1951）那样取满足 $\sum_t\mu_t = \infty$ 且 $\sum_t\mu_t^2 < \infty$ 的递减步长，在附加的正则性与稳定性条件下可控制累积噪声；仅靠这两个级数条件并不能保证收敛，还需要迭代有界、适当的噪声矩条件与吸引域，并且算法仍可能到达稳定但不需要的根。在线学习适合以数据流形式到达或内存放不下的数据，也能跟踪缓慢漂移的混合矩阵；对于固定的数据集，批量或分块更新更容易调参。

An ascent direction does not guarantee an ascent for every finite step. Set $\bH=\bI-\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top]$, so $\bW_{\rm new}=(\bI+\mu\bH)\bW$. The condition $\mu\|\bH\|_2<1$ is sufficient for invertibility, but not objective improvement. In batch learning with a fixed differentiable source model, backtracking can reduce $\mu$ until the candidate is nonsingular, all values are finite and the sample likelihood increases. Compute $\log|\det\bW|$ with slogdet and $\log\cosh u$ as $|u|+\log(1+e^{-2|u|})-\log2$. Reject a singular or nonfinite candidate.

上升方向不保证任意有限步长都使目标上升。记 $\bH=\bI-\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top]$，则 $\bW_{\rm new}=(\bI+\mu\bH)\bW$。$\mu\|\bH\|_2<1$ 是可逆性的充分条件，却不保证目标改善。对固定可微源模型的批量学习，可回溯减小 $\mu$，直到候选矩阵非奇异、所有数值有限且样本似然上升。用 slogdet 计算 $\log|\det\bW|$，用 $|u|+\log(1+e^{-2|u|})-\log2$ 计算 $\log\cosh u$。应拒绝奇异或非有限的候选值。

A small increment may indicate stationarity or merely a tiny step size. Record the relative increment $\|\Delta\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F=\mu\|\bH\|_F$, the residual $\|\bH\|_F$, objective changes and the iteration limit separately. An estimate returned at the limit must be labelled unconverged. In minibatch learning assess a residual on a larger fixed block because individual updates are noisy. Save the training mean: new data must be transformed as $\bW(\bx_{\rm new}-\bar\bx_{\rm train})$.

增量小可能表示已接近驻点，也可能只是步长过小。应分别记录相对增量 $\|\Delta\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F=\mu\|\bH\|_F$、残差 $\|\bH\|_F$、目标变化与迭代次数上限。达到上限后的结果应标明未收敛。小批量学习的单次更新含噪，应在较大的固定数据块上评价残差。还应保存训练均值：新数据的变换为 $\bW(\bx_{\rm new}-\bar\bx_{\rm train})$。

Extended Infomax changes its nonlinearity when a sign switches, so the fixed-objective monotonicity argument cannot be applied automatically. Estimate switching statistics on adequate blocks, use a tolerance near zero and record switches. A Gaussian or nearly Gaussian output gives a zero or uncertain statistic; the theorem asserts local stability for a self-consistent separating point with nonzero switching statistics, not global convergence of a switching algorithm. The sub-Gaussian mixture above is also not genuinely bimodal: its score $y-\tanh y$ is positive for $y>0$, so the density has a single, flat-topped mode at zero.

Key points

本章要点

  • The relative or natural gradient $(\bI - \E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top])\bW$ is the ordinary gradient multiplied on the right by $\bW^\top\bW$. It is the steepest-ascent direction for the metric $\|d\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F$ and needs no matrix inversion.
  • Natural-gradient algorithms are equivariant: in terms of the global matrix $\bP = \bW\bA$ they do not depend on $\bA$, so their exact relative iterations agree under corresponding transformed initializations; finite-precision conditioning and the choice of initialization still matter.
  • When the scalar scale equations are solvable and the expectations exist, separating points solve the estimating equations; whether they attract the algorithm depends on $k_i$, $m_i$ and $\sigma_i^2$ through conditions on single sources and pairs. With the true scores they always hold when at most one source is Gaussian.
  • Infomax is maximum likelihood with a logistic source model, $\varphi(y) = \tanh(y/2)$; its separating points are stable for typical super-Gaussian sources (Laplace, logistic) but not, for example, for a pair of uniform sources. Extended Infomax switches each output between a super- and a sub-Gaussian model by a rule that is the stability condition itself.
  • Batch iterations converge locally for $0 < \mu < 2/\vartheta_{\max}$, where $\vartheta_{\max}$ is the largest eigenvalue of the linearization; online updates need decreasing step sizes to settle. EASI whitens and separates in one equivariant serial update and is stable when $\lambda_i + \lambda_j < 0$ for all pairs.
  • 相对梯度（自然梯度）$(\bI - \E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top])\bW$ 就是普通梯度右乘 $\bW^\top\bW$。它是度量 $\|d\bW\,\bW^{-1}\|_F$ 下的最速上升方向，不需要矩阵求逆。
  • 自然梯度算法是等变的：用全局矩阵 $\bP = \bW\bA$ 表示时它们与 $\bA$ 无关，在相应变换的初值下，其精确相对迭代一致；有限精度下的条件数与初值选择仍然重要。
  • 当标量尺度方程可解且期望存在时，分离点满足估计方程；它们能否吸引算法，取决于 $k_i$、$m_i$ 和 $\sigma_i^2$，条件只涉及单个源和源对。使用真实得分函数时，只要至多一个源是高斯的，这些条件总成立。
  • Infomax 是以 logistic 分布为源模型的最大似然，$\varphi(y) = \tanh(y/2)$；它的分离点对常见的超高斯源（拉普拉斯、logistic）是稳定的，但对一对均匀源等情形则不稳定。扩展 Infomax 按一条规则让每个输出在超高斯与亚高斯模型之间切换，而这条规则正是稳定性条件本身。
  • 批量迭代在 $0 < \mu < 2/\vartheta_{\max}$ 时局部收敛（$\vartheta_{\max}$ 为线性化的最大特征值）；在线更新需要递减步长才能稳定下来。EASI 用一个等变的串行更新同时完成白化与分离，当所有源对满足 $\lambda_i + \lambda_j < 0$ 时稳定。
