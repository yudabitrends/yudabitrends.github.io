From one subject to many从单个被试到多个被试

Most fMRI studies compare networks between people: patients and controls, young and old, before and after treatment. ICA of each subject separately returns components in arbitrary order, sign and scale (), and sometimes with different splits of the same network, so the components must be matched across subjects before anything can be compared. Matching by spatial correlation or by clustering is possible but error-prone, especially at higher model orders. Group methods build the correspondence into the model instead. They differ in what they assume to be shared: the maps (temporal concatenation, ); the time courses (spatial concatenation, Svensén, Kruggel & Benali, 2002, which suits experiments in which all subjects follow the same stimulus timing, but not rest); both maps and time courses up to subject-specific loadings (tensor PICA; Beckmann & Smith, 2005); or neither, with corresponding components linked only by statistical dependence (independent vector analysis, ). Calhoun, Liu and Adalı (2009) review these approaches. This chapter develops the ones most used in practice, then turns to what is computed from the components: connectivity between networks and links between imaging modalities.

大多数 fMRI 研究要在人与人之间比较网络：患者与对照、年轻人与老年人、治疗前与治疗后。对每个被试单独做 ICA，得到的成分顺序、符号与尺度都是任意的（），同一个网络有时还会以不同的方式被拆分，因此在做任何比较之前，必须先把成分在被试之间对应起来。按空间相关或聚类来匹配是可行的，但容易出错，模型阶数较高时尤其如此。组方法则把对应关系直接写进模型。它们的区别在于假设哪些东西是共享的：共享空间图（时间拼接，）；共享时间过程（空间拼接，Svensén, Kruggel & Benali, 2002，适合所有被试遵循相同刺激时序的实验，不适合静息态）；空间图和时间过程都共享、只差被试特有的载荷（张量 PICA；Beckmann & Smith, 2005）；或者都不共享，相互对应的成分只靠统计依赖联系起来（独立向量分析，）。Calhoun、Liu 与 Adalı（2009）综述了这些方法。本章先讨论实践中最常用的几种，再转向由成分计算的量：网络之间的连接，以及不同成像模态之间的联系。

Temporal concatenation时间拼接

Let $\bX_i \in \R^{T\times V}$, $i = 1, \dots, M$, be the preprocessed data of $M$ subjects, registered to a common template and centered over voxels. Group ICA by temporal concatenation (Calhoun, Adalı, Pearlson & Pekar, 2001) assumes that the subjects share the same maps but have their own time courses, $\bX_i = \bA_i\bS$. Stacking the subjects in time gives an ordinary spatial ICA model () with $MT$ "sensors":

设 $\bX_i \in \R^{T\times V}$（$i = 1, \dots, M$）是 $M$ 个被试经过预处理、配准到同一模板并在体素方向中心化后的数据。时间拼接的组 ICA（Calhoun, Adalı, Pearlson & Pekar, 2001）假设各被试共享相同的空间图，但各有自己的时间过程，即 $\bX_i = \bA_i\bS$。把各被试在时间方向上堆叠起来，就得到一个普通的空间 ICA 模型（），其“传感器”个数为 $MT$：

$$\begin{pmatrix}\bX_1\\ \vdots\\ \bX_M\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}\bA_1\\ \vdots\\ \bA_M\end{pmatrix}\bS, \qquad \begin{pmatrix}\bX_1\\ \vdots\\ \bX_M\end{pmatrix} \in \R^{MT\times V}, \quad \bS \in \R^{n\times V}.$$

The stacked matrix is large, and its PCA reduction is computed in two stages. Each subject's data are first reduced to $q$ dimensions, $\bY_i = \bF_i\bX_i \in \R^{q\times V}$, where the rows of $\bF_i \in \R^{q\times T}$ are the $q$ leading eigenvectors of $\frac1V\bX_i\bX_i^\top$. The reduced data are stacked into $\bY \in \R^{Mq\times V}$ and reduced again to $n$ dimensions, where the rows of $\bG \in \R^{n\times Mq}$ are the $n$ leading eigenvectors of $\frac1V\bY\bY^\top$. Partition $\bG = [\bG_1, \dots, \bG_M]$ into blocks $\bG_i \in \R^{n\times q}$, so that $\bG\bY = \sum_i \bG_i\bY_i$. Spatial ICA of the $n\times V$ matrix $\bG\bY$ () gives the group maps $\hat\bS$ and an invertible $n\times n$ mixing matrix $\bB$ with

堆叠后的矩阵很大，它的 PCA 降维分两步进行。先把每个被试的数据降到 $q$ 维：$\bY_i = \bF_i\bX_i \in \R^{q\times V}$，其中 $\bF_i \in \R^{q\times T}$ 的各行是 $\frac1V\bX_i\bX_i^\top$ 的前 $q$ 个特征向量。再把降维后的数据堆叠成 $\bY \in \R^{Mq\times V}$，并降到 $n$ 维，其中 $\bG \in \R^{n\times Mq}$ 的各行是 $\frac1V\bY\bY^\top$ 的前 $n$ 个特征向量。把 $\bG$ 分块为 $\bG = [\bG_1, \dots, \bG_M]$，$\bG_i \in \R^{n\times q}$，于是 $\bG\bY = \sum_i \bG_i\bY_i$。对 $n\times V$ 矩阵 $\bG\bY$ 做空间 ICA（），得到组空间图 $\hat\bS$ 与可逆的 $n\times n$ 混合矩阵 $\bB$，满足

$$\bG\bY = \bB\hat\bS.$$

The subject-level step removes noise specific to each subject and keeps memory requirements manageable; the group-level step keeps the dimensions that the subjects share. Here the reducing matrices have orthonormal rows, $\bF_i\bF_i^\top = \bI$ and $\bG\bG^\top = \bI$, as in the derivation of Erhardt et al. (2011). Implementations such as GIFT also whiten at each stage, dividing the rows of $\bF_i$ and of $\bG$ by the square roots of the corresponding eigenvalues. At the group level this only moves a diagonal factor into $\bB$; at the subject level it changes how the back-reconstructed maps relate to the time courses (). With $T = 100$, $q = 5$, $M = 6$ and $n = 3$, as in the example of , $\bY$ is $30\times V$ and $\bG\bY$ is $3\times V$.

被试水平的降维去除每个被试特有的噪声，并使内存需求保持在可控范围内；组水平的降维保留各被试共有的维度。这里降维矩阵的行是单位正交的：$\bF_i\bF_i^\top = \bI$，$\bG\bG^\top = \bI$，与 Erhardt 等（2011）的推导一致。GIFT 等软件还在每一步做白化，即把 $\bF_i$ 与 $\bG$ 的各行除以相应特征值的平方根。组水平的白化只是把一个对角因子并入 $\bB$；被试水平的白化则会改变反重建得到的空间图与时间过程之间的关系（）。取 $T = 100$，$q = 5$，$M = 6$，$n = 3$（与的例子相同），则 $\bY$ 为 $30\times V$，$\bG\bY$ 为 $3\times V$。

Back-reconstruction反重建

Group maps are only half the answer: comparisons between subjects need each subject's own time courses and maps. Back-reconstruction obtains them by running the reductions backwards. If the group PCA loses little, $\bY \approx \bG^\top\bG\bY = \bG^\top\bB\hat\bS$, and the rows that belong to subject $i$ give $\bY_i \approx \bG_i^\top\bB\hat\bS$; if the subject PCA loses little, $\bX_i \approx \bF_i^\top\bY_i$. This suggests the subject time courses ($T\times n$) and maps ($n\times V$)

组空间图只是答案的一半：被试之间的比较需要每个被试自己的时间过程与空间图。反重建通过把降维步骤倒推回去来得到它们。若组水平 PCA 损失很少，则 $\bY \approx \bG^\top\bG\bY = \bG^\top\bB\hat\bS$，取出属于被试 $i$ 的那些行，得到 $\bY_i \approx \bG_i^\top\bB\hat\bS$；若被试水平 PCA 损失很少，则 $\bX_i \approx \bF_i^\top\bY_i$。由此得到被试的时间过程（$T\times n$）与空间图（$n\times V$）：

$$\check\bA_i = \bF_i^\top\bG_i^\top\bB, \qquad \check\bS_i = \bigl(\bG_i^\top\bB\bigr)^+\bY_i.$$

This is the back-reconstruction of Calhoun et al. (2001), written in the present notation; Erhardt et al. (2011) call it GICA1. Because $\bF_i^\top$ has orthonormal columns, $\check\bA_i^+ = (\bG_i^\top\bB)^+\bF_i$, so $\check\bS_i = \check\bA_i^+\bX_i$: the subject maps are the least-squares regression of the subject's data on the subject's time courses. This identity relies on the orthonormal rows of $\bF_i$; describes what changes when the subject-level reduction is whitened. Erhardt et al. (2011) compared several back-reconstruction schemes on simulated and task data. One of them, GICA3, which they introduced, produces subject maps that add up to the group maps, with subject time courses and maps that reproduce each subject's data about as accurately as the PCA reduction allows; they recommended GICA3 together with subject-specific PCA, and GICA3 is the back-reconstruction recommended in the GIFT toolbox. They also observed that GICA1 approximates dual regression, the method of the next section. makes this exact.

这就是 Calhoun 等（2001）的反重建方法，这里用本章的记号写出；Erhardt 等（2011）称之为 GICA1。由于 $\bF_i^\top$ 的列单位正交，$\check\bA_i^+ = (\bG_i^\top\bB)^+\bF_i$，所以 $\check\bS_i = \check\bA_i^+\bX_i$：被试的空间图就是该被试数据对其时间过程的最小二乘回归。这一恒等式依赖于 $\bF_i$ 的行单位正交；被试水平的降维做白化时有何变化，见。Erhardt 等（2011）在仿真数据和任务数据上比较了几种反重建方案。其中由他们提出的 GICA3 给出的被试空间图之和等于组空间图，而且被试的时间过程与空间图重现被试数据的精度，大致达到 PCA 降维所允许的程度；他们推荐将 GICA3 与被试特异的 PCA 配合使用，GIFT 工具箱也把 GICA3 作为推荐的反重建方法。他们还指出 GICA1 近似于下一节的双回归。把这一近似关系精确化。

Dual regression双回归

Keep the direction of prediction straight. First, known group maps predict a subject’s images and yield time courses. Second, those estimated time courses predict the same data voxel by voxel and yield subject maps. The second step allows a new map; it does not test that the map is independent or biologically valid.

分清预测方向。先用已知组空间图预测某位被试的图像，得到时间过程；再用这些估计的时间过程逐体素预测同一份数据，得到被试空间图。第二步允许形成新空间图，但并不检验它是否独立或具有生物学有效性。

Dual regression (Beckmann et al., 2009; Filippini et al., 2009), called spatio-temporal regression (STR) by Erhardt et al. (2011), skips the PCA bookkeeping and uses only a set of group maps $\hat\bS$, from group ICA or from elsewhere, together with the subject's own data. Stage 1 is a spatial regression: each image of subject $i$ is regressed on the group maps, which gives the subject's time courses. Stage 2 is a temporal regression: each voxel's time series is regressed on these time courses, which gives the subject's maps. Stage 1 solves $\min_{\bA}\|\bX_i - \bA\hat\bS\|_F$ and stage 2 solves $\min_{\bS}\|\bX_i - \hat\bA_i\bS\|_F$; in closed form,

双回归（Beckmann et al., 2009; Filippini et al., 2009），Erhardt 等（2011）称之为时空回归（spatio-temporal regression, STR），它不需要 PCA 的记账，只用一组组空间图 $\hat\bS$（来自组 ICA 或其他途径）和被试自己的数据。第一步是空间回归：把被试 $i$ 的每一幅图像对组空间图回归，得到该被试的时间过程。第二步是时间回归：把每个体素的时间序列对这些时间过程回归，得到该被试的空间图。第一步求解 $\min_{\bA}\|\bX_i - \bA\hat\bS\|_F$，第二步求解 $\min_{\bS}\|\bX_i - \hat\bA_i\bS\|_F$，其闭式解为

$$\hat\bA_i = \bX_i\hat\bS^\top\bigl(\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top\bigr)^{-1} = \bX_i\hat\bS^+, \qquad \hat\bS_i = \bigl(\hat\bA_i^\top\hat\bA_i\bigr)^{-1}\hat\bA_i^\top\bX_i = \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i.$$

The displayed inverses require $\hat\bS$ to have full row rank and $\hat\bA_i$ full column rank. Pseudoinverses still define minimum-norm fits if a rank is lost, but component-specific coefficients then cease to be unique, and identities using $\hat\bA_i^+\hat\bA_i=\bI$ no longer apply. In code use SVD/QR-based least squares, inspect the singular values, and do not form the large $V\times V$ projector explicitly. Subject-level truncation should retain at least the intended group rank if all components are to be estimated for each subject. Different subjects can have different scan lengths $T_i$; temporal concatenation does not require synchronizing their resting-state time points, but it does require a common spatial grid and mask.

上式中的普通逆矩阵要求 $\hat\bS$ 行满秩且 $\hat\bA_i$ 列满秩。秩不足时，伪逆仍可定义最小范数拟合，但各成分的系数不再唯一，使用 $\hat\bA_i^+\hat\bA_i=\bI$ 的恒等式也不再成立。程序应采用基于 SVD／QR 的最小二乘并检查奇异值，不要显式构造巨大的 $V\times V$ 投影矩阵。若要为每个被试估计全部组成分，被试水平截断至少应保留所需的组秩。不同被试可以有不同扫描长度 $T_i$；时间拼接不要求静息态时间点在被试间同步，但要求共同的空间网格与掩模。

In the FSL implementation the time courses can be variance-normalized before stage 2. Replacing $\hat\bA_i$ by $\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1}$, with $\bD_i$ the diagonal matrix of their standard deviations, multiplies the rows of $\hat\bS_i$ by these standard deviations (), so the maps then reflect both the shape and the amplitude of each network (Nickerson et al., 2017). The subject maps are finally compared across subjects voxel by voxel, for instance with permutation tests. The closed forms show what the subject maps contain.

在 FSL 的实现中，可以在第二步之前把时间过程方差归一化。把 $\hat\bA_i$ 换成 $\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1}$（$\bD_i$ 是由各时间过程标准差构成的对角矩阵），相当于把 $\hat\bS_i$ 的各行乘以这些标准差（），于是空间图同时反映每个网络的形状和幅度（Nickerson et al., 2017）。最后在体素水平上比较各被试的空间图，例如用置换检验。闭式解揭示了被试空间图里到底包含什么。

Let $\hat\bS$ have full row rank and $\hat\bA_i$ full column rank, and let $\bP_{\hat\bS} = \hat\bS^+\hat\bS$ be the $V\times V$ orthogonal projector onto the row space of $\hat\bS$. Then the stage-2 maps of are

设 $\hat\bS$ 行满秩，$\hat\bA_i$ 列满秩，$\bP_{\hat\bS} = \hat\bS^+\hat\bS$ 是到 $\hat\bS$ 行空间上的 $V\times V$ 正交投影矩阵。则 第二步得到的空间图为

$$\hat\bS_i = \hat\bS + \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i\,(\bI - \bP_{\hat\bS}).$$

By stage 1, $\bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} = \bX_i\hat\bS^+\hat\bS = \hat\bA_i\hat\bS$, so $\hat\bA_i^+\bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} = \hat\bS$, because $\hat\bA_i^+\hat\bA_i = \bI$. Split $\bX_i = \bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} + \bX_i(\bI - \bP_{\hat\bS})$ in $\hat\bS_i = \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i$.

由第一步，$\bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} = \bX_i\hat\bS^+\hat\bS = \hat\bA_i\hat\bS$；又 $\hat\bA_i^+\hat\bA_i = \bI$，所以 $\hat\bA_i^+\bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} = \hat\bS$。在 $\hat\bS_i = \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i$ 中把 $\bX_i$ 拆成 $\bX_i\bP_{\hat\bS} + \bX_i(\bI - \bP_{\hat\bS})$ 即得。

Without variance normalization, then, every subject map equals the group map plus a correction built only from the part of the subject's data that lies outside the span of the group maps, regressed on time courses that were themselves estimated with the group maps. The two stages rest on different assumptions, common maps in stage 1 and subject-specific maps in stage 2, a point stressed by Erhardt et al. (2011). For group maps from spatial ICA, $\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = V\bI$ (), so stage 1 reduces to $\hat\bA_i = \frac1V\bX_i\hat\bS^\top$ and each time course is computed independently of the other maps; stage 2 is different, since the time courses are correlated and every map depends on all of them. The relation to back-reconstruction is the following.

可见，不做方差归一化时，每个被试的空间图都等于组空间图加上一个修正项；修正项只由被试数据中落在组空间图张成空间之外的部分构成，并且是对用组空间图估计出的时间过程回归得到的。两步回归依据的假设不同：第一步假设空间图是共同的，第二步假设空间图因被试而异，Erhardt 等（2011）强调过这一点。若组空间图来自空间 ICA，则 $\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = V\bI$（），第一步化为 $\hat\bA_i = \frac1V\bX_i\hat\bS^\top$，每条时间过程的计算与其他空间图无关；第二步则不同，因为各时间过程相关，每张空间图都依赖于全部时间过程。与反重建的关系如下。

In the setting of , with the orthonormal PCA reducing matrices $\bF_i$ and $\bG$ of that section and group maps $\hat\bS$ of full row rank, the dual-regression time courses and the GICA1 time courses satisfy

在的设定下，降维矩阵 $\bF_i$ 与 $\bG$ 是该节中行单位正交的 PCA 矩阵，组空间图 $\hat\bS$ 行满秩，则双回归的时间过程 与 GICA1 的时间过程 满足

$$\hat\bA_i = \check\bA_i + (\bI - \bF_i^\top\bF_i)\,\bX_i\hat\bS^+.$$

In particular, if the subject-level reduction keeps all of subject $i$'s data, $\bF_i^\top\bF_i\bX_i = \bX_i$, the two methods give the same time courses and the same maps.

特别地，若被试水平的降维保留了被试 $i$ 的全部数据，即 $\bF_i^\top\bF_i\bX_i = \bX_i$，则两种方法给出相同的时间过程和相同的空间图。

Write $\bY = \bG^\top\bG\bY + \boldsymbol\Delta$ with $\boldsymbol\Delta = (\bI - \bG^\top\bG)\bY$. The rows of $\bG$ are eigenvectors of $\bY\bY^\top$, say $\bY\bY^\top\bG^\top = \bG^\top\bLambda$, so $\boldsymbol\Delta\bY^\top\bG^\top = (\bI - \bG^\top\bG)\bG^\top\bLambda = \bzero$. Since $\hat\bS = \bB^{-1}\bG\bY$ by , this gives $\boldsymbol\Delta\hat\bS^\top = \bzero$ and hence $\boldsymbol\Delta\hat\bS^+ = \bzero$. Taking the rows of subject $i$, $\bY_i = \bG_i^\top\bB\hat\bS + \boldsymbol\Delta_i$, so $\bY_i\hat\bS^+ = \bG_i^\top\bB$. Finally $\bX_i = \bF_i^\top\bY_i + (\bI - \bF_i^\top\bF_i)\bX_i$; multiplying on the right by $\hat\bS^+$ and using $\bF_i^\top\bG_i^\top\bB = \check\bA_i$ gives the stated relation. When the last term vanishes, the time courses agree, and so do the maps, since both methods obtain them by regressing $\bX_i$ on the time courses.

The difference is the part of the subject's data that its own PCA discarded, regressed on the group maps: dual regression uses all of the subject's data, GICA1 only what survived the subject-level reduction. The group-level reduction causes no difference, because what it discards is orthogonal, across voxels, to the group maps. The following script runs the whole pipeline on six synthetic subjects whose three maps are the same blobs shifted at random by up to four voxels, with subject-specific random-walk time courses.

两者之差是被试自身 PCA 舍弃的那部分数据对组空间图的回归：双回归使用了被试的全部数据，GICA1 只用了经被试水平降维后保留下来的部分。组水平的降维不造成差别，因为它舍弃的部分在体素方向上与组空间图正交。下面的脚本在六个合成被试上运行整个流程：每个被试的三张空间图是同样的斑块，只是随机平移了至多四个体素，时间过程是各被试特有的随机游走。

import numpy as np
from itertools import permutations

def fastica(Z, rng, iters=1000):                       # symmetric FastICA, g = tanh
    U = np.linalg.qr(rng.standard_normal((len(Z), len(Z))))[0]
    for _ in range(iters):
        U_old = U.copy()
        Y = U @ Z
        U = np.tanh(Y) @ Z.T / Z.shape[1] - np.diag((1 - np.tanh(Y)**2).mean(1)) @ U
        u, _, vt = np.linalg.svd(U); U = u @ vt
        if np.max(np.abs(np.abs(np.diag(U @ U_old.T)) - 1)) < 1e-9:
            return U
    raise RuntimeError("FastICA did not converge; inspect or restart.")

def pca_rows(X, k):                                   # k leading eigenvectors, as rows
    d, E = np.linalg.eigh(X @ X.T / X.shape[1])
    return E[:, ::-1][:, :k].T, d[::-1][:k]

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
side, T, M, q, n = 30, 100, 6, 5, 3                   # grid, scans, subjects, PCA dims
yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:side, 0:side]
blob = lambda cy, cx, r: np.exp(-((yy - cy)**2 + (xx - cx)**2) / (2 * r**2)).ravel()
Xs, Ss = [], []
for i in range(M):                                    # blobs shifted at random per subject
    S = np.vstack([blob(cy + rng.integers(-4, 5), cx + rng.integers(-4, 5), r)
                   for cy, cx, r in [(8, 8, 3), (20, 22, 4), (8, 22, 3)]])
    A = np.cumsum(rng.standard_normal((T, n)), 0)    # subject-specific time courses
    X = (A - A.mean(0)) / A.std(0) @ S + 0.3 * rng.standard_normal((T, side**2))
    Xs.append(X - X.mean(1, keepdims=True)); Ss.append(S)

Fs = [pca_rows(X, q)[0] for X in Xs]                  # subject PCA: q x T each
Y = np.vstack([F @ X for F, X in zip(Fs, Xs)])        # temporal concatenation: Mq x V
G, lam = pca_rows(Y, n)                               # group PCA: n x Mq
Z = np.diag(lam ** -0.5) @ G @ Y
U = fastica(Z, rng)
S_g, B = U @ Z, np.diag(lam ** 0.5) @ U.T             # group maps; G Y = B S_g

def match(S_est, S_true):                             # one-to-one assignment; n = 3 here
    C = np.abs(np.corrcoef(S_est, S_true)[:n, n:])
    return max(C[range(n), p].mean() for p in permutations(range(n)))
r0, r1, r2 = [], [], []
for X, F, Gi, S in zip(Xs, Fs, np.split(G, M, axis=1), Ss):
    S_1 = np.linalg.pinv(Gi.T @ B) @ F @ X            # GICA1 back-reconstruction
    A_dr = X @ np.linalg.pinv(S_g)                    # dual regression, stage 1
    S_dr = np.linalg.pinv(A_dr) @ X                   # dual regression, stage 2
    r0.append(match(S_g, S)); r1.append(match(S_1, S)); r2.append(match(S_dr, S))
print(Y.shape, S_g.shape)
print(np.round([np.mean(r0), np.mean(r1), np.mean(r2)], 3))

Implementations such as GIFT whiten the subject-level reduction, so that the group stage works on $\bY_i = \bLambda_i^{-1/2}\bF_i\bX_i$, where $\bLambda_i$ is the diagonal matrix of the eigenvalues that belong to the rows of $\bF_i$; back-reconstruction undoes the whitening with $\bF_i^\top\bLambda_i^{1/2}$. Spatially constrained ICA and NeuroMark空间约束 ICA 与 NeuroMark

Back-reconstruction and dual regression obtain subject components by regression and never ask them to be independent. Spatially constrained ICA instead runs ICA on each subject's data and uses reference maps to guide it. In the semi-blind spatial ICA of Lin et al. (2010), each component maximizes a non-Gaussianity contrast subject to a constraint that keeps it close to a spatial reference, such as a mask defined by an atlas. In group-information-guided ICA (GIG-ICA; Du & Fan, 2013), the references are the group maps, and each subject component is found by a multi-objective optimization that balances its independence against its similarity to the corresponding group component. Schematically, with $\bz$ the whitened data of subject $i$ at a random voxel and $\bar s_l$ the value of reference map $l$ there, the unit vector $\bw_l$ that extracts component $l$ solves

反重建与双回归都通过回归得到被试的成分，从不要求它们相互独立。空间约束 ICA 则对每个被试的数据做 ICA，并用参考空间图来引导它。在 Lin 等（2010）的半盲空间 ICA 中，每个成分在保持与某个空间参考（例如由图谱定义的掩模）足够接近的约束下，最大化一个非高斯性对比函数。在组信息引导 ICA（GIG-ICA；Du & Fan, 2013）中，参考就是组空间图，每个被试成分通过一个多目标优化求得，在独立性与它和对应组成分的相似性之间取得平衡。示意地说，记 $\bz$ 为被试 $i$ 在随机体素处的白化数据，$\bar s_l$ 为参考图 $l$ 在该处的取值，则提取成分 $l$ 的单位向量 $\bw_l$ 求解

$$\max_{\|\bw_l\| = 1}\ \Bigl(\, J\bigl(\bw_l^\top\bz\bigr),\ \ \E\bigl[\bar s_l\,\bw_l^\top\bz\bigr] \Bigr),$$

two objectives combined, for example, by a weighted sum, where $J$ is a negentropy approximation () and the reference is standardized. Because every subject is decomposed on its own, the references provide a component correspondence, though they do not guarantee biological validity, and new subjects can be processed one at a time without rerunning a group analysis.

两个目标可以通过加权和等方式合并，其中 $J$ 是负熵的近似（），参考图已标准化。由于每个被试单独分解，参考图提供成分对应关系，但并不保证其生物学有效性，新被试可以逐个处理，无需重新做组分析。

NeuroMark (Du et al., 2020) turns this into a pipeline for comparisons across studies. Group ICA of two large healthy-control data sets, from the Human Connectome Project and the Genomics Superstruct Project with 1,828 subjects in total, yielded 53 intrinsic networks that replicated across the two data sets; they are grouped into seven functional domains: subcortical, auditory, sensorimotor, visual, cognitive control, default mode and cerebellar. The networks of each new subject are estimated by spatially constrained ICA with these templates as references, which keeps components and connectivity measures comparable across data sets and disorders. Du et al. applied the pipeline to several brain disorders, including schizophrenia, autism, mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer's disease, bipolar disorder and major depression.

NeuroMark（Du et al., 2020）把这一思路做成了可用于跨研究比较的流程。对两个大型健康对照数据集（来自人类连接组计划与基因组超结构计划，共 1,828 名被试）做组 ICA，得到 53 个在两个数据集之间可复现的内在网络，它们分属七个功能域：皮层下、听觉、感觉运动、视觉、认知控制、默认模式与小脑。每个新被试的网络都以这些模板为参考，用空间约束 ICA 估计，从而使成分与连接指标在不同数据集和不同疾病之间保持可比。Du 等把这一流程用于多种脑疾病，包括精神分裂症、自闭症、轻度认知障碍与阿尔茨海默病、双相障碍和重度抑郁。

Independent vector analysis独立向量分析

Temporal concatenation assumes common maps; dual regression and constrained ICA estimate subject maps around group references. Independent vector analysis (IVA; Kim, Eltoft & Lee, 2006) treats the subjects symmetrically and links them only through statistical dependence. There are $K$ data sets, here the subjects after their own PCA reduction, each with its own mixing matrix:

时间拼接假设空间图是共同的；双回归与约束 ICA 围绕组参考来估计被试的空间图。独立向量分析（independent vector analysis, IVA；Kim, Eltoft & Lee, 2006）则对称地对待各被试，只通过统计依赖把它们联系起来。设有 $K$ 个数据集（这里是经过各自 PCA 降维的被试），每个数据集有自己的混合矩阵：

$$\bx^{[k]} = \bA^{[k]}\bs^{[k]}, \quad k = 1, \dots, K, \qquad \bs_i = \bigl(s_i^{[1]}, \dots, s_i^{[K]}\bigr)^\top, \quad i = 1, \dots, n.$$

The vector $\bs_i$ collects the $i$-th source of every data set and is called the $i$-th source component vector (SCV). The model assumes that the SCVs $\bs_1, \dots, \bs_n$ are mutually independent, while the components within an SCV may be, and are expected to be, dependent: the default-mode map of one subject resembles that of another. For spatial ICA of fMRI the samples are again voxels, so $s_i^{[k]}$ is the value of map $i$ of subject $k$ at a random voxel.

向量 $\bs_i$ 汇集了各数据集的第 $i$ 个源，称为第 $i$ 个源成分向量（source component vector, SCV）。模型假设各 SCV $\bs_1, \dots, \bs_n$ 相互独立，而同一 SCV 内部的分量可以相互依赖，而且通常预期是相互依赖的：一个被试的默认模式图与另一个被试的相似。对 fMRI 的空间 ICA 而言，样本仍然是体素，所以 $s_i^{[k]}$ 是被试 $k$ 的第 $i$ 张空间图在随机体素处的取值。

IVA estimates unmixing matrices $\bW^{[1]}, \dots, \bW^{[K]}$, with outputs $\by^{[k]} = \bW^{[k]}\bx^{[k]}$ and estimated SCVs $\by_i = (y_i^{[1]}, \dots, y_i^{[K]})^\top$, by minimizing the mutual information among the estimated SCVs,

IVA 估计解混矩阵 $\bW^{[1]}, \dots, \bW^{[K]}$，输出为 $\by^{[k]} = \bW^{[k]}\bx^{[k]}$，估计的 SCV 为 $\by_i = (y_i^{[1]}, \dots, y_i^{[K]})^\top$，所用的准则是最小化各估计 SCV 之间的互信息

$$I(\by_1; \dots; \by_n) = \sum_{i=1}^n H(\by_i) - H\bigl(\by^{[1]}, \dots, \by^{[K]}\bigr).$$

The transformation is block diagonal, which gives the first identity below; the second splits the entropy of an estimated SCV into its marginal entropies minus the mutual information $I(\by_i)$ among its components:

变换是分块对角的，由此得到下面第一个等式；第二个等式把一个估计 SCV 的熵分解为各边缘熵之和减去其分量之间的互信息 $I(\by_i)$：

$$\begin{aligned} H\bigl(\by^{[1]}, \dots, \by^{[K]}\bigr) &= H\bigl(\bx^{[1]}, \dots, \bx^{[K]}\bigr) + \sum_{k=1}^K \log\bigl|\det\bW^{[k]}\bigr|, \\ H(\by_i) &= \sum_{k=1}^K H\bigl(y_i^{[k]}\bigr) - I(\by_i). \end{aligned}$$

Hence the cost is

因此代价函数为

$$\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{IVA}} = \sum_{i=1}^n \Bigl[\sum_{k=1}^K H\bigl(y_i^{[k]}\bigr) - I(\by_i)\Bigr] - \sum_{k=1}^K \log\bigl|\det\bW^{[k]}\bigr| - H\bigl(\bx^{[1]}, \dots, \bx^{[K]}\bigr)$$

(Anderson, Adalı & Li, 2012; Adalı, Anderson & Fu, 2014). Without the terms $I(\by_i)$, the cost would be, up to a constant, the sum of $K$ separate ICA costs . These terms reward dependence within each estimated SCV, and they are what aligns the components across data sets. With two data sets separated correctly but in different orders, an estimated SCV would pair sources from two different SCVs, which are independent, so its $I(\by_i)$ would vanish; with a common order $I(\by_i) = I(\bs_i) > 0$, which lowers the cost (). IVA thus solves the correspondence problem as part of the estimation.

（Anderson, Adalı & Li, 2012; Adalı, Anderson & Fu, 2014）。若去掉 $I(\by_i)$ 项，代价函数就是（相差一个常数）$K$ 个各自独立的 ICA 代价 之和。这些项奖励每个估计 SCV 内部的依赖，正是它们把各数据集的成分对齐。若两个数据集都已正确分离但顺序不同，则估计的 SCV 会把两个不同 SCV 中的源配在一起，而它们是独立的，所以其 $I(\by_i)$ 为零；顺序一致时 $I(\by_i) = I(\bs_i) > 0$，代价更低（）。这样，IVA 在估计过程中顺带解决了对应问题。

Algorithms differ in the density they assume for the SCVs. The first IVA algorithms (Kim, Eltoft & Lee, 2006) used a super-Gaussian multivariate density of Laplacian type. IVA-G (Anderson, Adalı & Li, 2012) models each SCV as multivariate Gaussian and so uses only second-order statistics, the correlations of corresponding components across data sets. Even Gaussian sources can then be separated, provided their correlation structures across data sets differ. Two Gaussian SCVs whose covariance matrices satisfy $\bSigma_m = \bD\bSigma_l\bD$ for a diagonal $\bD$ cannot be separated (), and Anderson, Fu, Phlypo and Adalı (2014) showed that obstructions of this kind are essentially the only ones. The situation parallels SOBI (), with data sets in place of time lags.

各种算法的区别在于对 SCV 假设的密度。最早的 IVA 算法（Kim, Eltoft & Lee, 2006）采用拉普拉斯型的超高斯多元密度。IVA-G（Anderson, Adalı & Li, 2012）把每个 SCV 建模为多元高斯，因而只利用二阶统计量，即对应成分在各数据集之间的相关。这样一来，即使是高斯源也能分离，只要它们在各数据集之间的相关结构不同。若两个高斯 SCV 的协方差矩阵满足 $\bSigma_m = \bD\bSigma_l\bD$（$\bD$ 为对角矩阵），它们就无法分离（）；Anderson、Fu、Phlypo 与 Adalı（2014）证明，这类障碍本质上是唯一的障碍。这与 SOBI 的情形（）相似，只是用数据集代替了时间延迟。

Lee et al. (2008) applied IVA to group fMRI. Michael et al. (2014) compared group ICA with back-reconstruction and IVA on simulated data with spatially varying networks. As the variability of the maps across subjects grew, IVA stayed close to the ground truth, whereas group ICA produced subject maps that were partly composites of other maps. They also noted that group ICA remains preferable when group-level maps are the goal, and that IVA is more demanding in memory and computation.

Lee 等（2008）把 IVA 用于组 fMRI 分析。Michael 等（2014）在空间网络存在变异的仿真数据上比较了带反重建的组 ICA 与 IVA：随着空间图在被试之间的变异增大，IVA 的估计仍接近真实值，而组 ICA 得到的被试空间图部分地混入了其他空间图。他们也指出，若目标是组水平的空间图，组 ICA 仍更合适，而且 IVA 对内存与计算的要求更高。

Functional network connectivity功能网络连接

Spatial ICA leaves the time courses unconstrained, so their correlations carry information about how networks interact. Jafri et al. (2008) introduced functional network connectivity (FNC), the correlation among the time courses of spatially independent components, and applied it to schizophrenia. For subject $i$, let $\tilde\bA_i$ be the time courses of , or of any back-reconstruction, after centering in time and usually detrending and low-pass filtering. The static FNC is the $n\times n$ correlation matrix

空间 ICA 对时间过程不加约束，所以时间过程之间的相关携带了网络之间相互作用的信息。Jafri 等（2008）提出了功能网络连接（functional network connectivity, FNC），即空间独立成分的时间过程之间的相关，并把它用于精神分裂症研究。对被试 $i$，记 $\tilde\bA_i$ 为 （或任何一种反重建）得到的时间过程，经过时间上的中心化，通常还做了去趋势与低通滤波。静态 FNC 是 $n\times n$ 相关矩阵

$$\bR_i = \diag(\bC_i)^{-1/2}\,\bC_i\,\diag(\bC_i)^{-1/2}, \qquad \bC_i = \tilde\bA_i^\top\tilde\bA_i,$$

Here $\diag(\bC_i)$ is the diagonal matrix with the same diagonal as $\bC_i$; the common covariance normalization cancels in the correlation. For each off-diagonal edge with $|r|<1$, one often uses the Fisher transform $z=\tanh^{-1}(r)$ before across-subject modelling. For independent bivariate-Gaussian observations its variance is approximately $1/(T-3)$; this formula does not hold unchanged for filtered, autocorrelated BOLD data. The diagonal has $r=1$ and is excluded. Dynamic FNC estimates these correlations in sliding, often tapered windows. Allen et al. (2014) used windows of about 44 seconds and clustered windowed matrices into recurring states. Short and overlapping windows give noisy, dependent estimates; fluctuation and clustering alone do not demonstrate changes in the underlying coupling ().

其中 $\diag(\bC_i)$ 与 $\bC_i$ 有相同的对角元；协方差的公共归一化因子在相关系数中抵消。对每条满足 $|r|<1$ 的非对角边，常在跨被试建模前作 Fisher 变换 $z=\tanh^{-1}(r)$。对独立的二元高斯观测，其方差近似为 $1/(T-3)$；这一公式不能原样用于滤波后、有自相关的 BOLD 数据。对角线 $r=1$，不参与变换。动态 FNC 在滑动且常带锥形权重的窗口内估计相关。Allen 等（2014）使用约 44 秒的窗口，并把窗口矩阵聚类成重复出现的状态。短窗口与重叠窗口给出的估计有较大噪声且相互依赖；仅有波动和聚类，并不能证明底层耦合发生变化（）。

Sign conventions and valid group inference符号约定与有效的组推断

FNC is invariant to positive rescaling of a time course, but not to its arbitrary ICA sign. If $\bA_i$ is replaced by $\bA_i\bD$ and its maps by $\bD^{-1}\bS_i$, the reconstruction is unchanged while the correlation matrix becomes $\bH\bR_i\bH$, with $\bH=\diag(\sign D_{jj})$. Align component order and map signs to common references before comparing signed edges. Taking absolute correlations discards anticorrelation information and changes the scientific question. FNC measures statistical association; shared motion, physiology or a common task can create it without direct communication between networks.

FNC 对时间过程的正尺度缩放不变，却会随 ICA 任意符号改变。若把 $\bA_i$ 换成 $\bA_i\bD$，空间图换成 $\bD^{-1}\bS_i$，重建不变，但相关矩阵变成 $\bH\bR_i\bH$，其中 $\bH=\diag(\sign D_{jj})$。比较带符号的边之前，须按共同参考统一成分顺序与空间图符号。取相关绝对值会丢弃负相关信息，改变科学问题。FNC 衡量统计关联；共同运动、生理过程或任务可以造成这种关联，而无需网络间直接通信。

For two independent stationary processes under the null, a large-sample approximation is $T_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx T/[1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\rho_1(\ell)\rho_2(\ell)]$, assuming summable autocorrelations. For two AR(1) series with coefficient $0.8$, it gives $T_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx T(1-0.8^2)/(1+0.8^2)$: 300 scans contain about 66 effective observations for this correlation statistic. This is a diagnostic approximation, not a universal degrees-of-freedom correction; use stationary null simulations or resampling that reproduces filtering and autocorrelation when studying dynamic FNC.

在两个独立平稳过程的零假设下，若自相关可求和，大样本近似为 $T_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx T/[1+2\sum_{\ell\ge1}\rho_1(\ell)\rho_2(\ell)]$。对两个系数为 $0.8$ 的 AR(1) 序列，得到 $T_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx T(1-0.8^2)/(1+0.8^2)$：300 个时间点对该相关统计量大约只提供 66 个有效观测。这是诊断性近似，不是通用自由度校正；研究动态 FNC 时，应使用保留滤波与自相关特征的平稳零假设模拟或重采样。

Across-subject inference has subjects as the independent units, not voxels, scans or overlapping windows. Model prespecified covariates such as age, site and motion; repeated sessions and relatives require a design and permutation exchangeability scheme that respects their dependence. There are $n(n-1)/2$ unique undirected edges, or 1,378 for 53 networks. Define the tested family and control multiplicity over those edges, and over maps/components as applicable; correcting voxels separately within each component does not automatically control the error rate across all components. FSL's dual-regression workflow passes the subject-level maps to a group design and permutation procedure, with variance normalization chosen according to whether amplitude belongs in the estimand (FSL dual-regression and randomise documentation; Nickerson et al., 2017).

跨被试推断的独立单位是被试，而不是体素、时间点或重叠窗口。应预先指定年龄、站点、运动等协变量；重复扫描与亲属数据需要尊重其依赖性的设计及置换可交换性方案。无向网络有 $n(n-1)/2$ 条不同的边，53 个网络对应 1,378 条。须定义检验族并控制这些边及适用的空间图／成分上的多重比较；逐个成分内部校正体素，并不自动控制跨全部成分的错误率。FSL 双回归流程把被试水平空间图送入组设计与置换程序，并根据目标量是否包含幅度选择方差归一化（FSL 双回归与 randomise 文档；Nickerson et al., 2017）。

For prediction, learn group templates, model order, confound adjustments and any supervised feature selection inside the training folds, then apply the frozen pipeline to held-out subjects. A group ICA using all subjects may be acceptable for a declared transductive analysis, but is not a test of performance on an unseen cohort. In descriptive group testing, using group labels to choose a component or order and then testing that same choice on the same subjects is circular. Separate exploration from confirmatory evaluation and report sensitivity to plausible preprocessing and order choices.

预测任务中，组模板、阶数、混杂调整及监督特征选择都应在训练折内拟合，再将固定流程应用于留出的被试。使用全部被试做组 ICA 可以属于明确声明的传导式分析，但不是对未见队列泛化能力的检验。在描述性组比较中，先用组标签选择成分或阶数，再在同一批被试上检验同一个选择，会造成循环分析。应区分探索与确认性评估，并报告对合理预处理和阶数选择的敏感性。

Multimodal fusion多模态融合

ICA can also link different kinds of data from the same subjects. Suppose each of $M$ subjects contributes one feature map from each of two modalities, for instance a gray-matter map from structural MRI and a contrast map from a task fMRI experiment, and stack them into $\bX_1 \in \R^{M\times V_1}$ and $\bX_2 \in \R^{M\times V_2}$, one row per subject. Joint ICA (Calhoun et al., 2006) assumes that the modalities share the mixing matrix:

ICA 还能把同一批被试的不同类型数据联系起来。设 $M$ 个被试中每人在两种模态下各提供一张特征图，例如结构 MRI 的灰质图与任务 fMRI 实验的对比图，把它们按每个被试一行堆叠成 $\bX_1 \in \R^{M\times V_1}$ 与 $\bX_2 \in \R^{M\times V_2}$。联合 ICA（joint ICA；Calhoun et al., 2006）假设两种模态共享混合矩阵：

$$\bigl[\bX_1,\ \bX_2\bigr] = \bA\,\bigl[\bS_1,\ \bS_2\bigr], \qquad \bA \in \R^{M\times n}.$$

Each joint source, a row of $[\bS_1, \bS_2]$, is a pair of linked maps, one per modality, and the corresponding column of $\bA$ gives its expression in each subject, the subject loadings. The samples are the $V_1 + V_2$ features, and independence is imposed on the joint sources. Differences between groups in the loadings, such as between patients and controls, point to linked changes in both modalities. Joint ICA forces the modalities to share their loadings exactly. Parallel ICA (Liu et al., 2009) relaxes this. It runs a separate ICA for each modality, $\bX_1 = \bA_1\bS_1$ and $\bX_2 = \bA_2\bS_2$, and adds to the combined Infomax objective a term that rewards correlation between the loadings of a component of one modality and those of a component of the other, so that linked components emerge where the data support them; Liu et al. used it to relate fMRI components to genetic (SNP) components. In both models the link between modalities runs through the subject dimension, the one thing the data sets have in common.

每个联合源（$[\bS_1, \bS_2]$ 的一行）是一对相互关联的图，每种模态一张；$\bA$ 中对应的列给出它在每个被试中的表达强度，称为被试载荷。样本是 $V_1 + V_2$ 个特征，独立性施加在联合源上。载荷在组间（例如患者与对照之间）的差异，意味着两种模态中存在相互关联的变化。联合 ICA 强制两种模态的载荷完全相同。并行 ICA（parallel ICA；Liu et al., 2009）放宽了这一点：它对每种模态分别做 ICA，$\bX_1 = \bA_1\bS_1$，$\bX_2 = \bA_2\bS_2$，并在合并的 Infomax 目标函数上加一项，奖励一种模态某个成分的载荷与另一种模态某个成分的载荷之间的相关，使相互关联的成分只在数据支持时才出现；Liu 等用它把 fMRI 成分与遗传（SNP）成分联系起来。在两种模型中，模态之间的联系都通过被试这一维度建立，这是各数据集唯一的共同之处。

Key points

本章要点

  • Group ICA by temporal concatenation assumes common maps and subject-specific time courses; a two-stage PCA, first per subject and then of the stacked reduced data, makes the $MT\times V$ problem tractable.
  • Back-reconstruction (GICA1) inverts the reductions; dual regression regresses each subject's data on the group maps and then on the resulting time courses. Both have closed forms. Their time courses differ only by the regression, on the group maps, of the part of the subject's data that the subject-level PCA discards, whether or not the reductions are whitened; if that PCA discards nothing, the time courses coincide, and so do the maps unless the subject-level reduction is whitened.
  • Without variance normalization, dual-regression maps equal the group maps plus a correction built from the part of the data outside the span of the group maps; with it, they also reflect network amplitude.
  • Spatially constrained ICA (GIG-ICA, NeuroMark) estimates each subject's components by ICA guided by reference maps, which provides a reference-based correspondence across subjects and studies.
  • IVA links data sets through dependence within source component vectors; the within-SCV mutual information aligns the components and, with second-order statistics, can separate Gaussian sources whose cross-dataset correlations differ.
  • FNC and dynamic FNC analyse correlations among component time courses; joint and parallel ICA link imaging modalities through subject loadings.
  • 时间拼接的组 ICA 假设空间图共同、时间过程因被试而异；两步 PCA（先对每个被试，再对堆叠后的降维数据）使 $MT\times V$ 规模的问题变得可行。
  • 反重建（GICA1）把降维步骤倒推回去；双回归先把每个被试的数据对组空间图回归，再对所得时间过程回归。两者都有闭式解。无论降维是否白化，它们的时间过程之差都只是被试水平 PCA 所舍弃的那部分数据对组空间图的回归；若该 PCA 不舍弃任何数据，则时间过程一致，而且只要被试水平的降维不做白化，空间图也一致。
  • 不做方差归一化时，双回归的空间图等于组空间图加上一个修正项，修正项来自数据中落在组空间图张成空间之外的部分；做方差归一化后，空间图还反映网络的幅度。
  • 空间约束 ICA（GIG-ICA、NeuroMark）在参考图引导下对每个被试做 ICA，为被试之间、研究之间提供基于参考图的成分对应关系。
  • IVA 通过源成分向量内部的依赖把各数据集联系起来；SCV 内部的互信息把成分对齐，而且只用二阶统计量就能分离跨数据集相关结构不同的高斯源。
  • FNC 与动态 FNC 分析成分时间过程之间的相关；联合 ICA 与并行 ICA 通过被试载荷把不同成像模态联系起来。

A study has $M = 50$ subjects with $T = 200$ volumes of $V = 50{,}000$ voxels. Each subject is reduced to $q = 30$ dimensions and the group to $n = 20$. Give the sizes of the stacked data in , of $\bF_i$, $\bY$, $\bG$, $\bG_i$ and $\bB$, and of the back-reconstructed $\check\bA_i$ and $\check\bS_i$. Compare the covariance matrices whose eigenvectors are needed with and without the two-stage reduction.

某研究有 $M = 50$ 名被试，每人 $T = 200$ 个体积，每个体积 $V = 50{,}000$ 个体素。每个被试降到 $q = 30$ 维，组水平降到 $n = 20$ 维。写出 中堆叠数据的尺寸，$\bF_i$、$\bY$、$\bG$、$\bG_i$、$\bB$ 的尺寸，以及反重建得到的 $\check\bA_i$ 与 $\check\bS_i$ 的尺寸。比较采用与不采用两步降维时，需要求特征向量的协方差矩阵的大小。

The stacked data are $MT \times V = 10{,}000 \times 50{,}000$. Then $\bF_i$ is $30 \times 200$, $\bY$ is $Mq \times V = 1{,}500 \times 50{,}000$, $\bG$ is $20 \times 1{,}500$, each block $\bG_i$ is $20 \times 30$, $\bB$ is $20 \times 20$, $\check\bA_i$ is $200 \times 20$ and $\check\bS_i$ is $20 \times 50{,}000$. The two-stage reduction needs the eigenvectors of 50 matrices of size $200 \times 200$ and of one of size $1{,}500 \times 1{,}500$, and never holds more than one subject's raw data together with $\bY$. A single PCA of the stacked data needs the eigenvectors of a $10{,}000 \times 10{,}000$ matrix computed from all $5 \times 10^8$ entries of the stacked data.

堆叠数据为 $MT \times V = 10{,}000 \times 50{,}000$。$\bF_i$ 为 $30 \times 200$，$\bY$ 为 $Mq \times V = 1{,}500 \times 50{,}000$，$\bG$ 为 $20 \times 1{,}500$，每个分块 $\bG_i$ 为 $20 \times 30$，$\bB$ 为 $20 \times 20$，$\check\bA_i$ 为 $200 \times 20$，$\check\bS_i$ 为 $20 \times 50{,}000$。两步降维只需求 50 个 $200 \times 200$ 矩阵和一个 $1{,}500 \times 1{,}500$ 矩阵的特征向量，内存中至多同时保存一个被试的原始数据和 $\bY$。若对堆叠数据直接做一次 PCA，则要从堆叠数据的全部 $5 \times 10^8$ 个元素算出一个 $10{,}000 \times 10{,}000$ 的矩阵并求其特征向量。

Let $\hat\bA_i$ have full column rank and let $\bD_i$ be the diagonal matrix of the standard deviations of its columns. Show that regressing $\bX_i$ on $\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1}$ instead of $\hat\bA_i$ multiplies the rows of the stage-2 maps by the diagonal entries of $\bD_i$. Why does this make the maps sensitive to the amplitude of each network?

设 $\hat\bA_i$ 列满秩，$\bD_i$ 是由其各列标准差构成的对角矩阵。证明：把 $\bX_i$ 对 $\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1}$（而不是 $\hat\bA_i$）回归，相当于把第二步空间图的各行乘以 $\bD_i$ 的对角元。为什么这会使空间图对各网络的幅度敏感？

Since $\bD_i$ is diagonal and invertible,

由于 $\bD_i$ 是可逆的对角矩阵，

$$\begin{aligned} (\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1})^+ &= \bigl(\bD_i^{-1}\hat\bA_i^\top\hat\bA_i\bD_i^{-1}\bigr)^{-1}\bD_i^{-1}\hat\bA_i^\top \\ &= \bD_i(\hat\bA_i^\top\hat\bA_i)^{-1}\hat\bA_i^\top = \bD_i\hat\bA_i^+, \end{aligned}$$

so the new maps are $\bD_i\hat\bA_i^+\bX_i = \bD_i\hat\bS_i$. By , the unnormalized maps $\hat\bS_i = \hat\bS + \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i(\bI - \bP_{\hat\bS})$ are anchored to the group maps whatever the size of the subject's fluctuations: the amplitude of network $l$ is carried by its time course, whose standard deviation is the $l$-th diagonal entry of $\bD_i$. After normalization the time courses have unit variance, and the amplitude moves into the map: a subject whose network fluctuates twice as strongly gets a map twice as large. Group comparisons of normalized maps therefore test shape and amplitude together, comparisons of unnormalized maps mainly shape.

所以新的空间图为 $\bD_i\hat\bA_i^+\bX_i = \bD_i\hat\bS_i$。由，未归一化的空间图 $\hat\bS_i = \hat\bS + \hat\bA_i^+\bX_i(\bI - \bP_{\hat\bS})$ 无论被试的波动有多大都锚定在组空间图上：网络 $l$ 的幅度由其时间过程携带，其标准差就是 $\bD_i$ 的第 $l$ 个对角元。归一化之后时间过程方差为一，幅度转移到空间图中：某网络波动强一倍的被试，其空间图也大一倍。因此，比较归一化后的空间图同时检验形状与幅度，比较未归一化的空间图则主要检验形状。

Let $K = 2$ and suppose that both data sets are separated exactly, but possibly in different orders: $\by^{[1]} = \bs^{[1]}$ and $y_i^{[2]} = s_{\pi(i)}^{[2]}$ for a permutation $\pi$. Show that the IVA cost exceeds its value for $\pi = \mathrm{id}$ by $\sum_{i:\,\pi(i)\neq i} I(\bs_i)$, where $I(\bs_i)$ is the mutual information between $s_i^{[1]}$ and $s_i^{[2]}$.

设 $K = 2$，两个数据集都被准确分离，但顺序可能不同：$\by^{[1]} = \bs^{[1]}$，$y_i^{[2]} = s_{\pi(i)}^{[2]}$，其中 $\pi$ 是一个置换。证明 IVA 代价函数 比 $\pi = \mathrm{id}$ 时的值大 $\sum_{i:\,\pi(i)\neq i} I(\bs_i)$，其中 $I(\bs_i)$ 是 $s_i^{[1]}$ 与 $s_i^{[2]}$ 之间的互信息。

Permuting the outputs of the second data set replaces $\bW^{[2]}$ by $\bPi\bW^{[2]}$ for a permutation matrix $\bPi$, which leaves $|\det\bW^{[2]}|$ unchanged, and the last term of the cost does not depend on the unmixing matrices. The marginal entropies $H(y_i^{[k]})$ are the same numbers in a different order, so their sum is unchanged. Only the terms $-I(\by_i)$ can differ. If $\pi(i) = i$, then $\by_i = \bs_i$ and $I(\by_i) = I(\bs_i)$, as for the identity. If $\pi(i) \neq i$, then $\by_i = (s_i^{[1]}, s_{\pi(i)}^{[2]})$ pairs components of two different SCVs, which are independent, so $I(\by_i) = 0$. The cost therefore increases by $\sum_{i:\,\pi(i)\neq i} I(\bs_i) \ge 0$, strictly whenever one of these SCVs has dependent components. The ICA terms decide how each data set is unmixed; the within-SCV terms decide how the data sets are aligned.

把第二个数据集的输出重新排序，相当于把 $\bW^{[2]}$ 换成 $\bPi\bW^{[2]}$（$\bPi$ 为置换矩阵），这不改变 $|\det\bW^{[2]}|$；代价函数的最后一项与解混矩阵无关。各边缘熵 $H(y_i^{[k]})$ 只是换了顺序，其和不变。只有 $-I(\by_i)$ 项可能不同。若 $\pi(i) = i$，则 $\by_i = \bs_i$，$I(\by_i) = I(\bs_i)$，与恒等置换时相同。若 $\pi(i) \neq i$，则 $\by_i = (s_i^{[1]}, s_{\pi(i)}^{[2]})$ 把两个不同 SCV 的分量配在一起，它们相互独立，所以 $I(\by_i) = 0$。因此代价增加了 $\sum_{i:\,\pi(i)\neq i} I(\bs_i) \ge 0$；只要这些 SCV 中有一个的分量相互依赖，增加量就严格为正。ICA 项决定每个数据集如何解混，SCV 内部的互信息项决定各数据集如何对齐。

Two network time courses are independent sequences of independent Gaussian values. Their sample correlation $r$ over a window of $L$ time points has mean zero and variance $1/(L-1)$, and $r\sqrt{L-2}/\sqrt{1-r^2}$ has a Student $t$ distribution with $L - 2$ degrees of freedom. With $\mathrm{TR} = 2$ s and windows of 44 s, compute $L$, the standard deviation of $r$ and $P(|r| > 0.4)$. Real BOLD time courses are positively autocorrelated; does this make matters better or worse?

两条网络时间过程是相互独立的序列，各自由独立的高斯值构成。它们在 $L$ 个时间点的窗口内的样本相关系数 $r$ 均值为零、方差为 $1/(L-1)$，且 $r\sqrt{L-2}/\sqrt{1-r^2}$ 服从自由度为 $L - 2$ 的 Student $t$ 分布。取 $\mathrm{TR} = 2$ 秒、窗长 44 秒，计算 $L$、$r$ 的标准差以及 $P(|r| > 0.4)$。真实的 BOLD 时间过程存在正的自相关，这会让情况变好还是变坏？

$L = 44/2 = 22$, so the standard deviation of $r$ is $1/\sqrt{21} \approx 0.22$. For $r = 0.4$, $t = 0.4\sqrt{20}/\sqrt{0.84} \approx 1.95$, and $P(|t_{20}| > 1.95) \approx 0.065$: in about one window in fifteen, a pair of completely unrelated networks shows $|r| > 0.4$ by chance. (A simulation with $2\times 10^5$ windows gives $0.065$ as well.) Positive autocorrelation makes neighboring samples partly redundant, so the effective number of independent samples is smaller than $L$ and the sampling variance of $r$ is larger: matters get worse, and fluctuations of windowed correlations must be compared with what stationary data with the same autocorrelation would produce.

$L = 44/2 = 22$，所以 $r$ 的标准差为 $1/\sqrt{21} \approx 0.22$。当 $r = 0.4$ 时，$t = 0.4\sqrt{20}/\sqrt{0.84} \approx 1.95$，而 $P(|t_{20}| > 1.95) \approx 0.065$：大约每十五个窗口中就有一个，在两个毫不相关的网络之间仅凭偶然就出现 $|r| > 0.4$。（用 $2\times 10^5$ 个窗口做仿真，结果同样是 $0.065$。）正的自相关使相邻样本部分冗余，有效的独立样本数小于 $L$，$r$ 的抽样方差因而更大：情况更糟。因此，窗口相关的起伏必须与具有相同自相关的平稳数据所能产生的起伏相比较。

In the IVA model , let two SCVs $\bs_l, \bs_m \in \R^K$ be zero-mean Gaussian, independent of each other and of the other SCVs, with covariance matrices $\bSigma_l$ and $\bSigma_m = \bD\bSigma_l\bD$, where $\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_K)$ is invertible. For an angle $\theta$, define in each data set $k$ the new sources

在 IVA 模型 中，设两个 SCV $\bs_l, \bs_m \in \R^K$ 是零均值高斯向量，彼此独立，也与其他 SCV 独立，协方差矩阵分别为 $\bSigma_l$ 与 $\bSigma_m = \bD\bSigma_l\bD$，其中 $\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_K)$ 可逆。对角度 $\theta$，在每个数据集 $k$ 中定义新的源

$$\begin{aligned} \tilde s_l^{[k]} &= \cos\theta\,s_l^{[k]} + \sin\theta\,s_m^{[k]}/d_k, \\ \tilde s_m^{[k]} &= -d_k\sin\theta\,s_l^{[k]} + \cos\theta\,s_m^{[k]}. \end{aligned}$$

Show that the new SCVs $\tilde\bs_l$, $\tilde\bs_m$ are independent of each other and of the other SCVs, have covariance matrices $\bSigma_l$ and $\bSigma_m$, and are obtained from the old sources by an invertible linear transformation within each data set. Conclude that IVA cannot separate these two sources.

证明新的 SCV $\tilde\bs_l$、$\tilde\bs_m$ 彼此独立、也与其他 SCV 独立，协方差矩阵分别为 $\bSigma_l$ 与 $\bSigma_m$，并且是在每个数据集内对旧源作可逆线性变换得到的。由此说明 IVA 无法分离这两个源。

Let $\bu = \bs_l$ and $\bv = \bD^{-1}\bs_m$. Then $\bu$ and $\bv$ are independent zero-mean Gaussian vectors with the same covariance, $\Cov(\bv) = \bD^{-1}\bSigma_m\bD^{-1} = \bSigma_l$. The new SCVs are $\tilde\bs_l = \cos\theta\,\bu + \sin\theta\,\bv$ and $\tilde\bs_m = \bD(-\sin\theta\,\bu + \cos\theta\,\bv)$. They are jointly Gaussian, with

令 $\bu = \bs_l$，$\bv = \bD^{-1}\bs_m$。则 $\bu$ 与 $\bv$ 是相互独立的零均值高斯向量，协方差相同：$\Cov(\bv) = \bD^{-1}\bSigma_m\bD^{-1} = \bSigma_l$。新的 SCV 为 $\tilde\bs_l = \cos\theta\,\bu + \sin\theta\,\bv$，$\tilde\bs_m = \bD(-\sin\theta\,\bu + \cos\theta\,\bv)$。它们联合高斯，且

$$\begin{aligned} \Cov(\tilde\bs_l) &= (\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta)\,\bSigma_l = \bSigma_l, \\ \Cov(\tilde\bs_m) &= \bD\bSigma_l\bD = \bSigma_m, \\ \E\bigl[\tilde\bs_l\tilde\bs_m^\top\bigr] &= (-\cos\theta\sin\theta + \sin\theta\cos\theta)\,\bSigma_l\bD = \bzero, \end{aligned}$$

and uncorrelated jointly Gaussian vectors are independent. They are functions of $(\bs_l, \bs_m)$ only, hence independent of the other SCVs. In data set $k$, $(s_l^{[k]}, s_m^{[k]})$ is mapped to $(\tilde s_l^{[k]}, \tilde s_m^{[k]})$ by the matrix

不相关的联合高斯向量相互独立。它们只是 $(\bs_l, \bs_m)$ 的函数，因而与其他 SCV 独立。在数据集 $k$ 中，$(s_l^{[k]}, s_m^{[k]})$ 经下面的矩阵变为 $(\tilde s_l^{[k]}, \tilde s_m^{[k]})$：

$$\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta/d_k \\ -d_k\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix},$$

whose determinant is $1$. Replacing columns $l$ and $m$ of each $\bA^{[k]}$ accordingly, using the inverse of this matrix, therefore gives a second IVA model that produces exactly the same observations, with SCVs that satisfy every assumption and have the same distributions as before. No criterion computed from the observations can prefer $\theta = 0$, and for $\theta$ not a multiple of $\pi/2$ the new sources are genuine mixtures of the old ones. The case $\bD = \bI$ shows that Gaussian SCVs with identical covariance across data sets are never separable, just as two Gaussian sources are not in ICA.

其行列式为 $1$。用这个矩阵的逆相应地替换每个 $\bA^{[k]}$ 的第 $l$、$m$ 列，就得到另一个 IVA 模型，它产生完全相同的观测，其 SCV 满足所有假设且分布与原来相同。任何由观测计算的准则都无法偏好 $\theta = 0$；而当 $\theta$ 不是 $\pi/2$ 的整数倍时，新的源是旧源的真正混合。取 $\bD = \bI$ 可见，跨数据集协方差相同的高斯 SCV 永远无法分离，正如 ICA 中两个高斯源无法分离一样。
