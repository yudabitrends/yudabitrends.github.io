ICA usually involves a handful of sources, so the limit theorem itself is not what matters. What matters is a statement for finitely many terms: a combination of independent sources sits, in a precise sense, between the sources and the Gaussian. For kurtosis the statement is simple. If $s_1, \dots, s_n$ are independent with unit variance and the same kurtosis $\kappa$, then their normalized sum $n^{-1/2}\sum_i s_i$ has kurtosis $\kappa/n$, and no unit-variance combination has kurtosis larger in absolute value than the most kurtotic source (). ICA 通常只涉及少数几个源，所以要紧的并不是极限定理本身，而是一个关于有限项的论断：独立源的组合在确切的意义上介于源与高斯之间。对峭度而言，这个论断很简单。若 $s_1, \dots, s_n$ 独立、方差为一且峭度同为 $\kappa$，则其归一化和 $n^{-1/2}\sum_i s_i$ 的峭度为 $\kappa/n$；并且任何单位方差的组合，其峭度的绝对值都不会超过峭度最大的那个源（）。熵的相应论断留到讨论。

Throughout the chapter the data are whitened, $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$, with $\tilde\bA$ orthogonal and independent zero-mean sources of unit variance and finite fourth moments (). A one-unit method looks for a unit vector $\bw$ such that $y = \bw^\top\bz$ is a source. In source coordinates, $\bq = \tilde\bA^\top\bw$, the projection is $y = \bq^\top\bs$, and $\|\bq\| = \|\bw\| = 1$ because $\tilde\bA$ is orthogonal; in particular $\E[y^2] = \|\bq\|^2 = 1$. Searching over unit vectors $\bw$ is therefore the same as searching over unit vectors $\bq$, and $y$ is a source exactly when $\bq = \pm\be_i$, a standard basis vector.

本章始终假定数据已经白化：$\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$，其中 $\tilde\bA$ 是正交矩阵，各源独立、均值为零、方差为一且四阶矩有限（）。单单元方法寻找一个单位向量 $\bw$，使 $y = \bw^\top\bz$ 是某个源。在源坐标 $\bq = \tilde\bA^\top\bw$ 下，投影为 $y = \bq^\top\bs$；由于 $\tilde\bA$ 正交，$\|\bq\| = \|\bw\| = 1$，特别地 $\E[y^2] = \|\bq\|^2 = 1$。因此在单位向量 $\bw$ 上搜索，等价于在单位向量 $\bq$ 上搜索；而 $y$ 恰为某个源，当且仅当 $\bq = \pm\be_i$ 是标准基向量。

The same idea appeared earlier in exploratory statistics as projection pursuit. Friedman & Tukey (1974) proposed to explore multivariate data by numerically optimizing a "projection index" over one- or two-dimensional projections. Huber (1985) argued that the Gaussian is the least interesting distribution, since most projections of high-dimensional data look nearly Gaussian, so that a projection index should measure departure from Gaussianity. One-unit ICA contrasts are projection indices of exactly this kind, applied to whitened data; under the ICA model the interesting directions are the sources (Hyvärinen, Karhunen & Oja, 2001).

同样的思想更早出现在探索性统计中，名为投影寻踪。Friedman & Tukey（1974）提出，在一维或二维投影上数值优化一个“投影指标”，以此探索多元数据。Huber（1985）认为高斯分布是最没有意思的分布，因为高维数据的大多数投影看上去都近似高斯，所以投影指标应当度量偏离高斯的程度。单单元 ICA 对比函数正是这类投影指标，只不过作用在白化数据上；在 ICA 模型下，有意思的方向就是源（Hyvärinen, Karhunen & Oja, 2001）。

Kurtosis of a linear combination线性组合的峭度

For a zero-mean variable, $\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$ is the fourth cumulant (). It vanishes for the Gaussian; variables with $\kurt(y) < 0$ are called sub-Gaussian (uniform: $-1.2$; a binary $\pm 1$ variable: $-2$, the smallest possible value at unit variance) and variables with $\kurt(y) > 0$ super-Gaussian (Laplace: $3$; centered exponential: $6$). As a cumulant it has two properties that drive everything in this chapter: for independent $u$ and $v$ and a real number $\alpha$,

对零均值变量，$\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$ 就是四阶累积量（）。高斯变量的峭度为零；$\kurt(y) < 0$ 的变量称为亚高斯的（均匀分布为 $-1.2$；取 $\pm 1$ 的二值变量为 $-2$，这是单位方差下可能取到的最小值），$\kurt(y) > 0$ 的变量称为超高斯的（拉普拉斯分布为 $3$；中心化的指数分布为 $6$）。作为累积量，它有两条性质，本章的一切都由它们推出：对独立的 $u$、$v$ 与实数 $\alpha$，

$$\kurt(u + v) = \kurt(u) + \kurt(v), \qquad \kurt(\alpha u) = \alpha^4 \kurt(u).$$

设 $s_1, \dots, s_n$ 独立，均值为零、方差为一，峭度为 $\kappa_i = \kurt(s_i)$。则对任意 $\bq \in \R^n$，

$$\kurt\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^n q_i s_i\Bigr) = \sum_{i=1}^n q_i^4\, \kappa_i .$$

If $\|\bq\| = 1$, then $|\kurt(\bq^\top\bs)| \le \max_i |\kappa_i|$. When $\max_i |\kappa_i| > 0$, equality holds only for $\bq = \pm\be_j$ with $|\kappa_j| = \max_i |\kappa_i|$. If all $\kappa_i$ equal $\kappa$, then $\kurt(\bq^\top\bs) = \kappa\sum_i q_i^4$ lies between $\kappa/n$ and $\kappa$, and the value $\kappa/n$ is attained when all $q_i^2 = 1/n$.

若 $\|\bq\| = 1$，则 $|\kurt(\bq^\top\bs)| \le \max_i |\kappa_i|$。当 $\max_i |\kappa_i| > 0$ 时，等号只在 $\bq = \pm\be_j$ 且 $|\kappa_j| = \max_i |\kappa_i|$ 时成立。若所有 $\kappa_i$ 都等于 $\kappa$，则 $\kurt(\bq^\top\bs) = \kappa\sum_i q_i^4$ 介于 $\kappa/n$ 与 $\kappa$ 之间，且当所有 $q_i^2 = 1/n$ 时取到 $\kappa/n$。

The identity follows from additivity over the independent terms $q_i s_i$ and from $\kurt(q_i s_i) = q_i^4 \kappa_i$. For a unit vector, $\sum_i q_i^4 \le (\sum_i q_i^2)^2 = 1$, with equality only when a single $q_j$ is nonzero; hence $|\sum_i q_i^4\kappa_i| \le \max_i|\kappa_i| \sum_i q_i^4 \le \max_i|\kappa_i|$, and equality in the last step forces $\bq = \pm\be_j$, after which the first step forces $|\kappa_j| = \max_i|\kappa_i|$. Finally, by the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, $1 = (\sum_i q_i^2)^2 \le n\sum_i q_i^4$, with equality exactly when all $q_i^2$ are equal.

恒等式由峭度对独立项 $q_i s_i$ 的可加性以及 $\kurt(q_i s_i) = q_i^4 \kappa_i$ 得到。对单位向量，$\sum_i q_i^4 \le (\sum_i q_i^2)^2 = 1$，等号只在只有一个 $q_j$ 非零时成立；因此 $|\sum_i q_i^4\kappa_i| \le \max_i|\kappa_i| \sum_i q_i^4 \le \max_i|\kappa_i|$，后一个不等式取等号迫使 $\bq = \pm\be_j$，此时前一个不等式取等号又迫使 $|\kappa_j| = \max_i|\kappa_i|$。最后，由 Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式，$1 = (\sum_i q_i^2)^2 \le n\sum_i q_i^4$，当且仅当所有 $q_i^2$ 相等时取等号。

Three consequences shape the rest of the chapter. First, mixing can never produce more absolute kurtosis than the most kurtotic source contains, which is the precise form of the central-limit heuristic. Second, a mixture can still be more kurtotic than some of the sources: mixing a nearly Gaussian source with a Laplacian one gives a combination that is less Gaussian than the first source. Third, kurtoses of opposite signs can cancel: for a uniform and a Laplacian source, $\kurt(\cos\theta\, s_1 + \sin\theta\, s_2) = -1.2\cos^4\theta + 3\sin^4\theta$ vanishes at $\tan^4\theta = 0.4$, that is $\theta \approx 38.5^\circ$, although this combination is certainly not Gaussian. Kurtosis is a signed measure, and the quantities to maximize are $|\kurt|$ or $\kurt^2$.

由此得到的三点决定了本章其余部分。其一，混合产生的峭度绝对值永远不会超过峭度最大的源，这正是中心极限启发式论证的精确形式。其二，混合仍可能比某些源的峭度更大：把一个接近高斯的源与一个拉普拉斯源混合，得到的组合比前者更不像高斯。其三，符号相反的峭度会相互抵消：对一个均匀源和一个拉普拉斯源，$\kurt(\cos\theta\, s_1 + \sin\theta\, s_2) = -1.2\cos^4\theta + 3\sin^4\theta$ 在 $\tan^4\theta = 0.4$，即 $\theta \approx 38.5^\circ$ 处为零，而这个组合显然不是高斯的。峭度是带符号的量，应当最大化的是 $|\kurt|$ 或 $\kurt^2$。

The map $\bw \mapsto \tilde\bA^\top\bw$ is an isometry of the sphere, so local maxima in $\bw$ and in $\bq$ correspond. The global maximum of $|F|$ sits at a source with the largest $|\kappa_i|$. The question that matters for algorithms, which climb from a random starting point, is whether $|F|$ has other local maxima. Either $\mu = 0$, in which case $\kappa_i = 0$ on $S$ and $F(\bq) = 0$; or $\mu \neq 0$, in which case all $\kappa_i$ with $i \in S$ are nonzero and have the sign of $\mu$, and

单位向量 $\bq$ 是 $F$ 在球面上的临界点，当且仅当存在数 $\mu$，使对支撑集 $S = \{i : q_i \neq 0\}$ 中的每个 $i$ 都有 $\kappa_i q_i^2 = \mu$。此时要么 $\mu = 0$，于是在 $S$ 上 $\kappa_i = 0$，且 $F(\bq) = 0$；要么 $\mu \neq 0$，于是所有 $i \in S$ 的 $\kappa_i$ 都非零且与 $\mu$ 同号，并且

$$q_i^2 = \frac{\mu}{\kappa_i} \ \ (i \in S), \qquad \mu = \Bigl(\sum_{i \in S} \frac{1}{\kappa_i}\Bigr)^{-1} = F(\bq).$$

On the sphere, $\bq$ is critical exactly when $\nabla F(\bq) = 4(\kappa_1 q_1^3, \dots, \kappa_n q_n^3)^\top$ is parallel to $\bq$, that is $\kappa_i q_i^3 = \mu q_i$ for all $i$ and some $\mu$. For $q_i \neq 0$ this reads $\kappa_i q_i^2 = \mu$. If $\mu \neq 0$, then $q_i^2 = \mu/\kappa_i > 0$ forces the sign condition, and $\sum_{i \in S} q_i^2 = 1$ gives the value of $\mu$. Finally $F(\bq) = \sum_{i \in S} (\kappa_i q_i^2) q_i^2 = \mu \sum_{i \in S} q_i^2 = \mu$.

在球面上，$\bq$ 是临界点当且仅当 $\nabla F(\bq) = 4(\kappa_1 q_1^3, \dots, \kappa_n q_n^3)^\top$ 与 $\bq$ 平行，即存在 $\mu$ 使对一切 $i$ 有 $\kappa_i q_i^3 = \mu q_i$。对 $q_i \neq 0$，这就是 $\kappa_i q_i^2 = \mu$。若 $\mu \neq 0$，则 $q_i^2 = \mu/\kappa_i > 0$ 给出符号条件，而 $\sum_{i \in S} q_i^2 = 1$ 给出 $\mu$ 的值。最后 $F(\bq) = \sum_{i \in S} (\kappa_i q_i^2) q_i^2 = \mu \sum_{i \in S} q_i^2 = \mu$。

Every single-source point $\pm\be_i$ is critical, with $\mu = \kappa_i$. The critical points with $|S| \ge 2$ mix sources whose kurtoses share a sign, with weights $q_i^2 \propto 1/\kappa_i$; the theorem below shows that none of them is a local maximum of $|F|$.

每个单源点 $\pm\be_i$ 都是临界点，对应 $\mu = \kappa_i$。$|S| \ge 2$ 的临界点则把峭度同号的若干个源混合在一起，权重为 $q_i^2 \propto 1/\kappa_i$；下面的定理说明，它们没有一个是 $|F|$ 的局部极大点。

Let $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$ with $\tilde\bA$ orthogonal and independent sources of zero mean, unit variance and kurtoses $\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_n$, not all zero. The local maxima of $\mathcal{K}(\bw) = |\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ on the unit sphere are exactly the points $\bw = \pm\tilde\ba_i$ with $\kappa_i \neq 0$, where $\tilde\ba_i$ is the $i$-th column of $\tilde\bA$; there $\bw^\top\bz = \pm s_i$. Each of them is a strict local maximum, and the global maximum value of $\mathcal{K}$ is $\max_i |\kappa_i|$.

设 $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$，$\tilde\bA$ 正交，各源独立、均值为零、方差为一，峭度为 $\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_n$ 且不全为零。则 $\mathcal{K}(\bw) = |\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ 在单位球面上的局部极大点恰好是满足 $\kappa_i \neq 0$ 的点 $\bw = \pm\tilde\ba_i$，其中 $\tilde\ba_i$ 是 $\tilde\bA$ 的第 $i$ 列；在这些点上 $\bw^\top\bz = \pm s_i$。它们都是严格局部极大点，$\mathcal{K}$ 的全局最大值为 $\max_i |\kappa_i|$。

Work with $|F|$ in source coordinates. Sources are strict local maxima. Let $\kappa_i \neq 0$ and $\kappa_{\max} = \max_j |\kappa_j|$. For a unit vector $\bq$ near $\pm\be_i$, put $\varepsilon = 1 - q_i^2 = \sum_{j \neq i} q_j^2$, which is small and positive unless $\bq = \pm\be_i$. Two practical remarks follow. First, the absolute value matters. Maximizing $\kurt$ itself finds super-Gaussian sources, but if all sources are sub-Gaussian then $F < 0$ everywhere and its maximum is the critical point with the largest $\mu$, which is the one with full support: the direction that mixes all sources (). An algorithm must either maximize $|\kurt|$ or $\kurt^2$, or know the sign of the kurtosis it is looking for. Second, a source with $\kappa_i = 0$ produces no local maximum at all: kurtosis cannot see it, even if it is far from Gaussian. We return to this in .

由此得到两点实际的提醒。第一，绝对值不能省。直接最大化 $\kurt$ 能找到超高斯源；但若所有源都是亚高斯的，则 $F$ 处处为负，其最大值落在 $\mu$ 最大的临界点，也就是支撑集为全集的那个点：把所有源混在一起的方向（）。算法要么最大化 $|\kurt|$ 或 $\kurt^2$，要么事先知道所找峭度的符号。第二，$\kappa_i = 0$ 的源根本不产生局部极大点：即使它远离高斯，峭度也看不见它。我们将在回到这一点。

All components at once: Comon's contrast同时估计全部成分：Comon 对比函数

One-unit methods find the sources one at a time. To estimate all of them together, search over orthogonal matrices $\bU$, with outputs $\by = \bU\bz$, and add up a one-unit contrast over the outputs. Comon (1994) proposed the sum of squared kurtoses,

单单元方法一次只找一个源。若要同时估计全部源，可以在正交矩阵 $\bU$ 上搜索，输出为 $\by = \bU\bz$，并把单单元对比函数对各个输出求和。Comon（1994）提出了峭度平方和

$$\Psi(\bU) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \kurt(y_i)^2, \qquad \by = \bU\bz .$$

For outputs that are symmetric and close to Gaussian, maximizing $\Psi$ approximately minimizes their mutual information (). With the orthogonal matrix $\bQ = \bU\tilde\bA$ we have $\by = \bQ\bs$, and by , $\kurt(y_i) = \sum_j Q_{ij}^4 \kappa_j$.

对于对称且接近高斯的输出，最大化 $\Psi$ 近似于最小化输出的互信息（）。记正交矩阵 $\bQ = \bU\tilde\bA$，则 $\by = \bQ\bs$，并由得 $\kurt(y_i) = \sum_j Q_{ij}^4 \kappa_j$。

For every orthogonal $\bU$, $\Psi(\bU) \le \sum_j \kappa_j^2$. If at most one source has zero kurtosis, equality holds if and only if $\bQ = \bU\tilde\bA$ is a signed permutation matrix, that is, if and only if $\bU$ separates the sources.

对任意正交矩阵 $\bU$，$\Psi(\bU) \le \sum_j \kappa_j^2$。若至多一个源的峭度为零，则等号成立当且仅当 $\bQ = \bU\tilde\bA$ 是带符号的置换矩阵，即当且仅当 $\bU$ 把源分离开。

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality with weights $Q_{ij}^2$, $\bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^2 \cdot Q_{ij}^2\kappa_j\bigr)^2 \le \bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^4\bigr)\bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^4\kappa_j^2\bigr) \le \sum_j Q_{ij}^4\kappa_j^2$, because each row of $\bQ$ has unit norm and so $\sum_j Q_{ij}^4 \le 1$. Summing over $i$ and using that each column also has unit norm,

由带权 $Q_{ij}^2$ 的 Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式，$\bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^2 \cdot Q_{ij}^2\kappa_j\bigr)^2 \le \bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^4\bigr)\bigl(\sum_j Q_{ij}^4\kappa_j^2\bigr) \le \sum_j Q_{ij}^4\kappa_j^2$，因为 $\bQ$ 的每一行都是单位向量，故 $\sum_j Q_{ij}^4 \le 1$。对 $i$ 求和，并利用每一列也是单位向量，得

$$\Psi(\bU) \le \sum_j \kappa_j^2 \sum_i Q_{ij}^4 \le \sum_j \kappa_j^2 \Bigl(\sum_i Q_{ij}^2\Bigr)^2 = \sum_j \kappa_j^2 .$$

Equality in the last step requires $\sum_i Q_{ij}^4 = 1$, i.e. column $j$ equal to $\pm$ a standard basis vector, for every $j$ with $\kappa_j \neq 0$. These columns are orthonormal, so they occupy distinct rows; if at most one $\kappa_j$ vanishes, the remaining column is a unit vector orthogonal to all the others and is therefore a signed basis vector too. Conversely, for a signed permutation each $\kurt(y_i)$ equals one of the $\kappa_j$, each used once, and $\Psi = \sum_j \kappa_j^2$.

最后一步取等号要求：对每个 $\kappa_j \neq 0$ 的 $j$，都有 $\sum_i Q_{ij}^4 = 1$，即第 $j$ 列等于 $\pm$ 某个标准基向量。这些列相互正交，因而位于不同的行；若至多一个 $\kappa_j$ 为零，剩下的那一列是与其余各列都正交的单位向量，于是也是带符号的基向量。反之，若 $\bQ$ 是带符号的置换矩阵，则每个 $\kurt(y_i)$ 恰为某个 $\kappa_j$，且每个 $\kappa_j$ 恰好出现一次，故 $\Psi = \sum_j \kappa_j^2$。

Unlike the one-unit contrast, $\Psi$ tolerates one source with zero kurtosis. It also has a useful interpretation. The sum of the squares of all fourth-order cumulants of $\by$, $\sum_{ijkl}\cum(y_i, y_j, y_k, y_l)^2$, does not depend on the rotation and equals $\sum_j \kappa_j^2$ (). Maximizing the diagonal part $\Psi$ is therefore the same as minimizing the sum of the squared cross-cumulants, which is the idea behind JADE ().

与单单元对比函数不同，$\Psi$ 能容忍一个峭度为零的源。它还有一个有用的解释：$\by$ 的全部四阶累积量的平方和 $\sum_{ijkl}\cum(y_i, y_j, y_k, y_l)^2$ 与旋转无关，恒等于 $\sum_j \kappa_j^2$（）。因此最大化对角部分 $\Psi$，等价于最小化交叉累积量的平方和，这正是 JADE 的思路（）。

Gradient ascent, a fixed point, and deflation梯度上升、不动点与逐个提取

Gradient ascent. For whitened data $\E[(\bw^\top\bz)^2] = \|\bw\|^2$, so $\kurt(\bw^\top\bz) = \E[(\bw^\top\bz)^4] - 3\|\bw\|^4$ and

梯度上升。对白化数据有 $\E[(\bw^\top\bz)^2] = \|\bw\|^2$，所以 $\kurt(\bw^\top\bz) = \E[(\bw^\top\bz)^4] - 3\|\bw\|^4$，且

$$\nabla_{\bw} \kurt(\bw^\top\bz) = 4\,\E\bigl[\bz\,(\bw^\top\bz)^3\bigr] - 12\,\|\bw\|^2\,\bw .$$

To increase $|\kurt|$, take a step along the gradient multiplied by the sign of the current kurtosis, then renormalize. The second term of the gradient points along $\bw$; after normalization it only rescales the step, so it can be dropped:

为了增大 $|\kurt|$，沿梯度乘以当前峭度的符号走一步，再归一化。梯度的第二项沿 $\bw$ 方向，归一化之后只改变步长，因此可以略去：

$$\bw \leftarrow \bw + \mu\, \sign\bigl(\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)\bigr)\, \E\bigl[\bz\,(\bw^\top\bz)^3\bigr], \qquad \bw \leftarrow \bw / \|\bw\| .$$

With the expectation replaced by a sample average this is a batch algorithm; with one sample per step it is a stochastic (online) algorithm. Because $|\kurt|$ has no local maxima other than the sources (), gradient ascent with a small step size cannot be trapped at a spurious maximum. Its costs are a step size $\mu$ that must be tuned and only linear convergence.

把期望换成样本平均，就得到批处理算法；每一步只用一个样本，就得到随机（在线）算法。由于 $|\kurt|$ 除源方向外没有其他局部极大点（），步长足够小的梯度上升不会被困在伪极大点。它的代价是需要调节步长 $\mu$，而且只有线性收敛速度。

A fixed-point iteration. At a critical point on the sphere the gradient is parallel to $\bw$, so $\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$ is a multiple of $\bw$. Hyvärinen & Oja (1997) turned this condition into an iteration without a step size:

不动点迭代。在球面上的临界点处，梯度 与 $\bw$ 平行，所以 $\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$ 是 $\bw$ 的倍数。Hyvärinen & Oja（1997）把这个条件变成了一个无需步长的迭代：

$$\bw \leftarrow \E\bigl[\bz\,(\bw^\top\bz)^3\bigr] - 3\bw, \qquad \bw \leftarrow \bw / \|\bw\| .$$

In source coordinates the iteration is strikingly simple. For a unit vector $\bq$, expand $\E[s_i(\bq^\top\bs)^3] = \sum_{j,k,l} q_j q_k q_l \E[s_i s_j s_k s_l]$. By independence and zero means only the terms in which the indices pair up survive: all four equal to $i$, or one of $j, k, l$ equal to $i$ and the other two equal to some $m \neq i$. This gives $q_i^3\E[s_i^4] + 3q_i\sum_{m \neq i} q_m^2 = q_i^3(\kappa_i + 3) + 3q_i(1 - q_i^2) = \kappa_i q_i^3 + 3q_i$. Since $\tilde\bA^\top\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] = \E[\bs(\bq^\top\bs)^3]$, the update before normalization is

在源坐标下，这个迭代简单得出奇。对单位向量 $\bq$，展开 $\E[s_i(\bq^\top\bs)^3] = \sum_{j,k,l} q_j q_k q_l \E[s_i s_j s_k s_l]$。由于独立且均值为零，只有下标两两配对的项保留下来：四个下标都等于 $i$，或者 $j, k, l$ 中有一个等于 $i$、另两个等于某个 $m \neq i$。于是得到 $q_i^3\E[s_i^4] + 3q_i\sum_{m \neq i} q_m^2 = q_i^3(\kappa_i + 3) + 3q_i(1 - q_i^2) = \kappa_i q_i^3 + 3q_i$。由于 $\tilde\bA^\top\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] = \E[\bs(\bq^\top\bs)^3]$，归一化之前的更新为

$$q_i^{+} = \kappa_i\, q_i^{3}, \qquad i = 1, \dots, n .$$

Let $r_j = |\kappa_j|^{1/2}|q_j|$. One step maps $r_j$ to $r_j^3$ up to a common normalizing factor, so all ratios are cubed: $r_j^{+}/r_i^{+} = (r_j/r_i)^3$. If index $i$ has the strictly largest $r_i$ at the start, every other ratio decays like $(r_j/r_i)^{3^k}$ after $k$ steps: the iteration converges cubically to $\bq = \pm\be_i$, that is, $\bw \to \pm\tilde\ba_i$. Sources with zero kurtosis drop out after one step. This is FastICA with the nonlinearity $g(u) = u^3$; derives FastICA as an approximate Newton method and analyses its convergence for general nonlinearities ().

令 $r_j = |\kappa_j|^{1/2}|q_j|$。一步迭代在相差一个公共归一化因子的意义下把 $r_j$ 变为 $r_j^3$，所以所有比值都被立方：$r_j^{+}/r_i^{+} = (r_j/r_i)^3$。若下标 $i$ 在起点处的 $r_i$ 严格最大，则 $k$ 步之后其余比值都按 $(r_j/r_i)^{3^k}$ 衰减：迭代三次收敛到 $\bq = \pm\be_i$，即 $\bw \to \pm\tilde\ba_i$。峭度为零的源在一步之后就消失了。这正是非线性函数取 $g(u) = u^3$ 的 FastICA；把 FastICA 推导为近似牛顿法，并对一般的非线性函数分析其收敛性（）。

Deflation. To find all sources, run the one-unit iteration repeatedly, each time in the orthogonal complement of the directions already found. Once $\bw_1 = \pm\tilde\ba_{i_1}$ is known, the constraint $\bw \perp \bw_1$ reads $q_{i_1} = 0$ in source coordinates: the problem is the same with source $i_1$ removed, and applies to the remaining sources. This is the deflation approach of Delfosse & Loubaton (1995).

逐个提取（紧缩）。要找到全部源，就反复运行单单元迭代，每次都限制在已找到方向的正交补中。一旦求得 $\bw_1 = \pm\tilde\ba_{i_1}$，约束 $\bw \perp \bw_1$ 在源坐标下就是 $q_{i_1} = 0$：问题与去掉源 $i_1$ 之后的问题完全相同，对剩下的源依然适用。这就是 Delfosse & Loubaton（1995）的逐个提取方法。

  1. Center and whiten the data: $\bz = \bV\bx$.
  2. For $p = 1, \dots, n$: draw a random unit vector $\bw$ and repeat until $|\bw^\top\bw_{\text{old}}|$ is within a tolerance of $1$: set $\bw \leftarrow \hat\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$, then $\bw \leftarrow \bw - \sum_{k < p} (\bw_k^\top\bw)\,\bw_k$, then $\bw \leftarrow \bw/\|\bw\|$. Store $\bw_p = \bw$.
  3. Return $\bU = [\bw_1, \dots, \bw_n]^\top$, the unmixing matrix $\bW = \bU\bV$ and the outputs $\by = \bU\bz$.
  1. 对数据中心化并白化：$\bz = \bV\bx$。
  2. 对 $p = 1, \dots, n$：随机取单位向量 $\bw$，重复以下步骤直到 $|\bw^\top\bw_{\text{old}}|$ 与 $1$ 的差小于容差：令 $\bw \leftarrow \hat\E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$，再令 $\bw \leftarrow \bw - \sum_{k < p} (\bw_k^\top\bw)\,\bw_k$，然后 $\bw \leftarrow \bw/\|\bw\|$。记 $\bw_p = \bw$。
  3. 返回 $\bU = [\bw_1, \dots, \bw_n]^\top$、解混矩阵 $\bW = \bU\bV$ 以及输出 $\by = \bU\bz$。

The script below implements for three sources: a uniform, a Laplacian and a binary one.

下面的脚本对三个源（一个均匀源、一个拉普拉斯源和一个二值源）实现。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
T, n = 10000, 3
S = np.vstack([rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), T),  # kurtosis -1.2
               rng.laplace(0.0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), T),       # kurtosis +3
               rng.choice([-1.0, 1.0], T)])               # kurtosis -2
A = rng.standard_normal((n, n))
X = A @ S

X = X - X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)                     # whiten: z = V x
d, E = np.linalg.eigh(np.cov(X))
V = E @ np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T
Z = V @ X

U = np.zeros((n, n))                          # rows: directions found so far
for p in range(n):
    w = rng.standard_normal(n)
    w /= np.linalg.norm(w)
    for it in range(1, 101):
        y = w @ Z
        w_new = (Z * y**3).mean(axis=1) - 3 * w           # fixed point
        w_new -= U[:p].T @ (U[:p] @ w_new)                # Gram-Schmidt
        w_new /= np.linalg.norm(w_new)
        done = abs(w_new @ w) > 1 - 1e-12                 # converged
        w = w_new
        if done:
            break
    U[p] = w
    y = w @ Z
    kurt = np.mean(y**4) - 3 * np.mean(y**2)**2
    print(f"unit {p + 1}: {it} iterations, kurtosis {kurt:+.2f}")
print(np.round(U @ V @ A, 2) + 0.0)           # close to a signed permutation

On this run the three units converge in 6, 5 and 2 iterations and recover the uniform source (sample kurtosis $-1.21$), the binary source ($-2.00$) and the Laplacian source ($+2.78$), in that order. The last unit has no freedom left after deflation, so it converges at once. The global matrix is close to a permutation:

这次运行中，三个单元分别在 6、5、2 次迭代后收敛，依次恢复了均匀源（样本峭度 $-1.21$）、二值源（$-2.00$）和拉普拉斯源（$+2.78$）。逐个提取之后，最后一个单元已没有自由度，所以立即收敛。全局矩阵接近一个置换矩阵：

$$\bU\bV\bA \approx \begin{pmatrix} 1.00 & 0.00 & -0.01 \\ 0.00 & 0.00 & 1.00 \\ -0.01 & 1.01 & -0.01 \end{pmatrix}.$$

With exact expectations every unit would land exactly on a source. With sample averages the error in $\bw_1$ is passed on to all later units through the orthogonality constraint, so errors accumulate along the deflation sequence. Symmetric orthogonalization, which updates all units in parallel, avoids this ordering ().

若期望是精确的，每个单元都会恰好落在某个源上。用样本平均时，$\bw_1$ 的误差会通过正交约束传给之后的所有单元，误差沿提取顺序累积。对称正交化同时更新所有单元，避免了这种顺序依赖（）。

Statistical weaknesses of kurtosis峭度的统计弱点

The theory above uses exact expectations. In practice the kurtosis of each candidate projection is estimated from $T$ samples, and the quality of the estimate decides whether the landscape of can be seen at all. Kurtosis is a fourth-order statistic, so its estimate is governed by moments up to order eight.

上面的理论用的是精确的期望。实践中，每个候选投影的峭度都要从 $T$ 个样本估计，估计的好坏决定了中的地形能否被看清。峭度是四阶统计量，所以它的估计受到直到八阶的矩的支配。

Let $y_1, \dots, y_T$ be independent copies of a variable $y$ with known zero mean, unit variance and moments $m_k = \E[y^k]$ with $m_8 < \infty$. Let $\hat m_k = \frac1T\sum_t y_t^k$. For the raw estimator $\hat\kappa = \hat m_4 - 3\hat m_2^2$, $\sqrt{T}(\hat\kappa - \kappa)$ converges in distribution to $\Ncal(0, V)$, and for the standardized estimator $\hat\kappa_{\mathrm{s}} = \hat m_4/\hat m_2^2 - 3$, $\sqrt{T}(\hat\kappa_{\mathrm{s}} - \kappa)$ converges to $\Ncal(0, V_{\mathrm{s}})$, where

设 $y_1, \dots, y_T$ 是变量 $y$ 的独立副本，$y$ 的均值已知为零、方差为一，矩 $m_k = \E[y^k]$ 满足 $m_8 < \infty$。令 $\hat m_k = \frac1T\sum_t y_t^k$。对未标准化的估计量 $\hat\kappa = \hat m_4 - 3\hat m_2^2$，$\sqrt{T}(\hat\kappa - \kappa)$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(0, V)$；对标准化的估计量 $\hat\kappa_{\mathrm{s}} = \hat m_4/\hat m_2^2 - 3$，$\sqrt{T}(\hat\kappa_{\mathrm{s}} - \kappa)$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(0, V_{\mathrm{s}})$，其中

$$V = m_8 - m_4^2 - 12\, m_6 + 48\, m_4 - 36, \qquad V_{\mathrm{s}} = m_8 - m_4^2 + 4\, m_4^3 - 4\, m_4 m_6 .$$

By the central limit theorem, $\sqrt{T}(\hat m_2 - 1, \hat m_4 - m_4)$ is asymptotically Gaussian with covariance entries $\Var(y^2) = m_4 - 1$, $\Var(y^4) = m_8 - m_4^2$ and $\Cov(y^2, y^4) = m_6 - m_4$. The map $(a, b) \mapsto b - 3a^2$ has gradient $(-6, 1)$ at $(1, m_4)$, so by the delta method () $V = 36(m_4 - 1) - 12(m_6 - m_4) + (m_8 - m_4^2)$, which is the stated expression. The map $(a, b) \mapsto b/a^2 - 3$ has gradient $(-2m_4, 1)$ there, which gives $V_{\mathrm{s}} = 4m_4^2(m_4 - 1) - 4m_4(m_6 - m_4) + (m_8 - m_4^2)$.

由中心极限定理，$\sqrt{T}(\hat m_2 - 1, \hat m_4 - m_4)$ 渐近服从高斯分布，其协方差的各元素为 $\Var(y^2) = m_4 - 1$、$\Var(y^4) = m_8 - m_4^2$ 与 $\Cov(y^2, y^4) = m_6 - m_4$。映射 $(a, b) \mapsto b - 3a^2$ 在 $(1, m_4)$ 处的梯度为 $(-6, 1)$，由 delta 方法（）得 $V = 36(m_4 - 1) - 12(m_6 - m_4) + (m_8 - m_4^2)$，整理即得所述表达式。映射 $(a, b) \mapsto b/a^2 - 3$ 在该点的梯度为 $(-2m_4, 1)$，由此得 $V_{\mathrm{s}} = 4m_4^2(m_4 - 1) - 4m_4(m_6 - m_4) + (m_8 - m_4^2)$。

For the Gaussian, $V = V_{\mathrm{s}} = 24$, the familiar $24/T$. For the uniform law $V = 1728/175 \approx 9.87$ but $V_{\mathrm{s}} = 1152/875 \approx 1.32$, and for the Laplace law $V = 1656$ and $V_{\mathrm{s}} = 1188$ (). In practice the kurtosis is usually computed on standardized data, as in the code and figure of this chapter, because whitening standardizes every projection. Standardization helps a great deal for light tails and little for heavy ones: with $T = 1000$ samples the standard error of the standardized sample kurtosis is about $0.04$ for a uniform source, whose kurtosis is $-1.2$, but about $1.1$ for a Laplacian source, whose kurtosis is $3$. Heavy tails make the estimate of a large kurtosis very noisy. Centering by the sample mean leaves both variances unchanged when $m_3 = 0$, as for symmetric laws; otherwise it adds a term ().

对高斯分布，$V = V_{\mathrm{s}} = 24$，即熟知的 $24/T$。对均匀分布，$V = 1728/175 \approx 9.87$，而 $V_{\mathrm{s}} = 1152/875 \approx 1.32$；对拉普拉斯分布，$V = 1656$，$V_{\mathrm{s}} = 1188$（）。实践中通常在标准化后的数据上计算峭度，本章的代码与插图也是如此，因为白化会把每个投影都标准化。标准化对轻尾分布帮助很大，对重尾分布帮助有限：当 $T = 1000$ 时，均匀源（峭度 $-1.2$）的标准化样本峭度的标准误约为 $0.04$，而拉普拉斯源（峭度 $3$）的标准误约为 $1.1$。重尾使得对大峭度的估计非常嘈杂。用样本均值中心化时，若 $m_3 = 0$（例如对称分布），两个方差都不变；否则会多出一项（）。

Heavier tails. If $m_8 = \infty$, for example for Student's $t$ distribution with $\nu \le 8$ degrees of freedom, the sample fourth moment has infinite variance and the error of the sample kurtosis decays more slowly than $1/\sqrt{T}$. If $m_4 = \infty$ ($\nu \le 4$), the kurtosis is not even defined, although the source is perfectly separable in principle.

更重的尾部。若 $m_8 = \infty$（例如自由度 $\nu \le 8$ 的 Student $t$ 分布），样本四阶矩的方差为无穷大，样本峭度的误差衰减得比 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 更慢。若 $m_4 = \infty$（$\nu \le 4$），峭度根本没有定义，尽管这样的源在原则上完全可以分离。

Outliers. A single observation $x_0$ changes $\hat m_4$ by about $x_0^4/T$, so its influence is unbounded. A point ten standard deviations out, in a sample of $T = 1000$, adds about $10$ to the estimated fourth moment, far more than the kurtosis of any sub-Gaussian source. In one simulated sample of $1000$ unit-variance uniform values, the sample kurtosis after standardizing was $-1.18$; appending a single value at $5$ changed it to $-0.64$, and a single value at $10$ to $+7.13$. One point out of $1001$ turned a clearly sub-Gaussian sample into a strongly super-Gaussian one.

离群值。单个观测 $x_0$ 会使 $\hat m_4$ 改变约 $x_0^4/T$，因此其影响是无界的。在 $T = 1000$ 的样本中，一个偏离十个标准差的点会使四阶矩的估计增加约 $10$，远大于任何亚高斯源的峭度。在一次模拟中，$1000$ 个单位方差均匀样本标准化之后的样本峭度为 $-1.18$；只添加一个取值为 $5$ 的点，它就变为 $-0.64$；添加一个取值为 $10$ 的点，它变为 $+7.13$。$1001$ 个点中的一个，就把一个明显亚高斯的样本变成了强烈超高斯的样本。

Sources with small kurtosis. Let $s$ be uniform with probability $5/7$ and Laplacian with probability $2/7$, both components having unit variance. Then $\E[s^4] = \frac57\cdot\frac95 + \frac27\cdot 6 = 3$, so $\kurt(s) = 0$, yet $s$ is clearly not Gaussian: its density jumps at $\pm\sqrt3$. By such a source creates no local maximum of $|\kurt|$, and two of them make $\Psi$ blind to any rotation within their plane, since every projection onto that plane has zero kurtosis. More generally, a source whose kurtosis is small compared with the standard error $\sqrt{V/T}$ is invisible to kurtosis in practice, however non-Gaussian it may be in other respects.

峭度很小的源。令 $s$ 以概率 $5/7$ 服从均匀分布、以概率 $2/7$ 服从拉普拉斯分布，两个分量的方差都为一。则 $\E[s^4] = \frac57\cdot\frac95 + \frac27\cdot 6 = 3$，所以 $\kurt(s) = 0$；但 $s$ 显然不是高斯的：它的密度在 $\pm\sqrt3$ 处有跳跃。由，这样的源不会产生 $|\kurt|$ 的局部极大点；有两个这样的源时，$\Psi$ 对它们所在平面内的任何旋转都无动于衷，因为该平面上的每个投影的峭度都为零。更一般地，只要一个源的峭度与标准误 $\sqrt{V/T}$ 相比很小，峭度在实践中就看不见它，无论它在其他方面多么非高斯。

All three weaknesses have the same cause: the function $u^4$ grows fast, so the estimate depends on rare large values and needs high moments to be stable. derives contrasts from likelihood and information theory and approximates them with slowly growing functions such as $\log\cosh u$. Their sample averages have small variance under weak moment conditions, a single outlier has bounded or at most linear influence, and they respond to non-Gaussianity that kurtosis misses, including the zero-kurtosis source above ().

这三个弱点出自同一个原因：函数 $u^4$ 增长太快，估计依赖于少见的大值，需要高阶矩才能稳定。从似然与信息论出发构造对比函数，并用 $\log\cosh u$ 这类增长缓慢的函数来近似它们。在较弱的矩条件下，这些函数的样本平均方差很小，单个离群值的影响有界或至多是线性的，而且它们能察觉峭度察觉不到的非高斯性，包括上面那个峭度为零的源（）。

Key points

本章要点

  • For whitened data a projection is $y = \bq^\top\bs$ with $\|\bq\| = 1$, and $\kurt(y) = \sum_i q_i^4\kappa_i$. Its absolute value never exceeds $\max_i|\kappa_i|$: mixing cannot create kurtosis.
  • The local maxima of $|\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ on the unit sphere are exactly $\pm$ the source directions with nonzero kurtosis (Delfosse & Loubaton, 1995). The other critical points, with $q_i^2 \propto 1/\kappa_i$ on a support of size at least two, are not maxima, so one-unit searches have no spurious solutions.
  • The absolute value matters: when all sources are sub-Gaussian, maximizing $\kurt$ itself finds the most mixed direction. Comon's contrast $\sum_i\kurt(y_i)^2$ over rotations is maximized exactly at separating solutions when at most one source has zero kurtosis.
  • The fixed-point iteration $\bw \leftarrow \E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$ acts as $q_i \mapsto \kappa_i q_i^3$ in source coordinates and converges cubically; deflation finds the sources one after another.
  • The sample kurtosis has variance about $V/T$, where $V$ depends on moments up to order eight (24 for the Gaussian, more than 1000 for the Laplace law), unbounded sensitivity to outliers, and no response to non-Gaussian sources with zero kurtosis. These weaknesses motivate the contrasts of .
  • 对白化数据，投影为 $y = \bq^\top\bs$，$\|\bq\| = 1$，且 $\kurt(y) = \sum_i q_i^4\kappa_i$。其绝对值永远不超过 $\max_i|\kappa_i|$：混合不会制造峭度。
  • $|\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ 在单位球面上的局部极大点恰好是峭度非零的源方向及其反方向（Delfosse & Loubaton, 1995）。其余临界点在大小至少为二的支撑集上满足 $q_i^2 \propto 1/\kappa_i$，都不是极大点，因此单单元搜索没有伪解。
  • 绝对值不能省：所有源都是亚高斯时，直接最大化 $\kurt$ 会找到混合最充分的方向。若至多一个源的峭度为零，Comon 对比函数 $\sum_i\kurt(y_i)^2$ 在旋转上的最大值恰好在分离解处取得。
  • 不动点迭代 $\bw \leftarrow \E[\bz(\bw^\top\bz)^3] - 3\bw$ 在源坐标下就是 $q_i \mapsto \kappa_i q_i^3$，三次收敛；逐个提取依次找出各个源。
  • 样本峭度的方差约为 $V/T$，其中 $V$ 依赖于直到八阶的矩（高斯分布为 24，拉普拉斯分布超过 1000）；它对离群值的敏感性无界，对峭度为零的非高斯源毫无反应。这些弱点促成了中的对比函数。

Maximizing one is minimizing the other; at a separating solution all cross-cumulants vanish.
