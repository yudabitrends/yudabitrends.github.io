Independence独立性

Two random quantities are independent when knowing the value of one tells us nothing about the other. The definition applies to scalars and vectors alike; independence between groups of variables appears later, for the Gaussian subspace in and for independent vector analysis in .

两个随机量独立，是指知道其中一个的取值，对另一个不提供任何信息。这一定义对标量和向量同样适用；变量组之间的独立性在后文还会出现，例如中的高斯子空间，以及中的独立向量分析。

Random vectors $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$, with $\by_i \in \R^{d_i}$, are (mutually) independent if $P(\by_1 \in B_1, \dots, \by_k \in B_k) = \prod_{i=1}^k P(\by_i \in B_i)$ for all Borel sets $B_i \subseteq \R^{d_i}$. The components of $\by = (y_1, \dots, y_n)^\top$ are independent when the scalars $y_1, \dots, y_n$ are; equivalently, the joint distribution function factorizes, $F_{\by}(\bu) = \prod_{i=1}^n F_{y_i}(u_i)$ for all $\bu \in \R^n$.

设随机向量 $\by_i \in \R^{d_i}$，$i = 1, \dots, k$。若对所有 Borel 集 $B_i \subseteq \R^{d_i}$ 都有 $P(\by_1 \in B_1, \dots, \by_k \in B_k) = \prod_{i=1}^k P(\by_i \in B_i)$，则称 $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$（相互）独立。向量 $\by = (y_1, \dots, y_n)^\top$ 的分量独立，是指标量 $y_1, \dots, y_n$ 相互独立；等价地，联合分布函数可以分解为 $F_{\by}(\bu) = \prod_{i=1}^n F_{y_i}(u_i)$，对所有 $\bu \in \R^n$ 成立。

The following equivalent forms are used throughout the book.

下面几种等价形式在全书中反复使用。

For random vectors $\by_1$ and $\by_2$ the following statements are equivalent:

对随机向量 $\by_1$ 与 $\by_2$，以下陈述等价：

  1. $\by_1$ and $\by_2$ are independent;
  2. $\E[g(\by_1)\,h(\by_2)] = \E[g(\by_1)]\,\E[h(\by_2)]$ for all bounded measurable functions $g$ and $h$;
  3. (when $(\by_1, \by_2)$ has a density $p$ with marginal densities $p_1$ and $p_2$): $p(\bu_1, \bu_2) = p_1(\bu_1)\,p_2(\bu_2)$ for almost every $(\bu_1, \bu_2)$.
  1. $\by_1$ 与 $\by_2$ 独立；
  2. 对一切有界可测函数 $g$ 与 $h$，$\E[g(\by_1)\,h(\by_2)] = \E[g(\by_1)]\,\E[h(\by_2)]$；
  3. （当 $(\by_1, \by_2)$ 有密度 $p$、边缘密度为 $p_1$ 与 $p_2$ 时）：对几乎所有 $(\bu_1, \bu_2)$，$p(\bu_1, \bu_2) = p_1(\bu_1)\,p_2(\bu_2)$。

Statement 2 extends to all $g$ and $h$ for which $\E|g(\by_1)|$ and $\E|h(\by_2)|$ are finite.

陈述 2 可以推广到一切使 $\E|g(\by_1)|$ 与 $\E|h(\by_2)|$ 有限的 $g$ 与 $h$。

1 ⇒ 2. For indicator functions $g = 1_{B_1}$ and $h = 1_{B_2}$, statement 2 is the definition. By linearity it holds for simple functions (finite linear combinations of indicators); every bounded measurable function is a uniform limit of simple functions, so it holds for all bounded $g$ and $h$. Monotone convergence, applied to the positive and negative parts, extends it to integrable functions. 2 ⇒ 1: take indicators. 3 ⇒ 1: integrate the factorized density over $B_1 \times B_2$. 1 ⇒ 3: by statement 1, the function $p_1(\bu_1)p_2(\bu_2)$ integrates over every product set $B_1 \times B_2$ to $P(\by_1 \in B_1)\,P(\by_2 \in B_2) = P(\by_1 \in B_1, \by_2 \in B_2)$. A probability measure on $\R^{d_1 + d_2}$ is determined by its values on product sets, so $p_1p_2$ is a density of the joint distribution, and two densities of the same distribution agree almost everywhere.

1 ⇒ 2：当 $g = 1_{B_1}$、$h = 1_{B_2}$ 为示性函数时，陈述 2 就是定义本身。由线性性，它对简单函数（示性函数的有限线性组合）成立；任何有界可测函数都是简单函数的一致极限，所以它对一切有界的 $g$、$h$ 成立。再对正部与负部分别使用单调收敛定理，即可推广到可积函数。2 ⇒ 1：取示性函数即可。3 ⇒ 1：把分解后的密度在 $B_1 \times B_2$ 上积分即得定义。1 ⇒ 3：由陈述 1，函数 $p_1(\bu_1)p_2(\bu_2)$ 在任一乘积集 $B_1 \times B_2$ 上的积分等于 $P(\by_1 \in B_1)\,P(\by_2 \in B_2) = P(\by_1 \in B_1, \by_2 \in B_2)$。$\R^{d_1 + d_2}$ 上的概率测度由它在乘积集上的取值唯一确定，所以 $p_1p_2$ 是联合分布的一个密度；而同一分布的两个密度几乎处处相等。

Statement 2 is the form that ICA algorithms use. Independence makes every function of $\by_1$ uncorrelated with every function of $\by_2$, $\Cov(g(\by_1), h(\by_2)) = 0$, and estimating equations such as $\E[g(y_i)\,y_j] = 0$ in test special cases of this. Directly from the definition, functions of independent vectors are again independent: if $\by_1$ and $\by_2$ are independent, so are $f_1(\by_1)$ and $f_2(\by_2)$ for any measurable $f_1$ and $f_2$, because $\{f_i(\by_i) \in B_i\} = \{\by_i \in f_i^{-1}(B_i)\}$. The proof of used this fact.

ICA 算法用的正是陈述 2。独立性使 $\by_1$ 的任何函数都与 $\by_2$ 的任何函数不相关，即 $\Cov(g(\by_1), h(\by_2)) = 0$；中的估计方程，例如 $\E[g(y_i)\,y_j] = 0$，检验的就是它的特例。由定义直接可知，独立向量的函数仍然独立：若 $\by_1$ 与 $\by_2$ 独立，则对任意可测函数 $f_1$、$f_2$，$f_1(\by_1)$ 与 $f_2(\by_2)$ 也独立，因为 $\{f_i(\by_i) \in B_i\} = \{\by_i \in f_i^{-1}(B_i)\}$。的证明就用到了这一点。

Pairwise and mutual independence两两独立与相互独立

Mutual independence of $n$ variables is stronger than independence of every pair. Let $y_1$ and $y_2$ be independent with $P(y_i = 1) = P(y_i = -1) = \tfrac12$, and let $y_3 = y_1y_2$. Every pair is independent (for instance $P(y_1 = a, y_3 = b) = P(y_1 = a, y_2 = ab) = \tfrac14$ for all signs $a$ and $b$), but $y_3$ is a function of $(y_1, y_2)$, and $P(y_1 = y_2 = y_3 = 1) = \tfrac14 \ne \tfrac18$. In general, then, pairwise checks are not enough. Linear mixtures of independent sources are an exception: by Comon's theorem (), if $\bs$ has independent, non-degenerate components with finite variances, at most one of them Gaussian, and $\bP$ is invertible, then pairwise independence of the components of $\by = \bP\bs$ already forces $\bP = \bPi\bD$, so the outputs are mutually independent.

$n$ 个变量相互独立，比它们两两独立要强。设 $y_1$ 与 $y_2$ 独立，$P(y_i = 1) = P(y_i = -1) = \tfrac12$，并令 $y_3 = y_1y_2$。任意两个变量都独立（例如对任意符号 $a$、$b$，$P(y_1 = a, y_3 = b) = P(y_1 = a, y_2 = ab) = \tfrac14$），但 $y_3$ 是 $(y_1, y_2)$ 的函数，且 $P(y_1 = y_2 = y_3 = 1) = \tfrac14 \ne \tfrac18$。所以一般来说，只检验两两独立是不够的。独立源的线性混合是一个例外：由 Comon 定理（），若 $\bs$ 的分量独立、非退化、方差有限且至多一个是高斯的，$\bP$ 可逆，那么 $\by = \bP\bs$ 的分量两两独立就已经迫使 $\bP = \bPi\bD$，从而输出相互独立。

Uncorrelated is weaker than independent不相关弱于独立

Random variables $y_1$ and $y_2$ with finite variances are uncorrelated if $\Cov(y_1, y_2) = \E[y_1y_2] - \E[y_1]\,\E[y_2] = 0$. Independence implies uncorrelatedness: take $g$ and $h$ to be the identity in . The converse fails, as the following example and show.

若方差有限的随机变量 $y_1$、$y_2$ 满足 $\Cov(y_1, y_2) = \E[y_1y_2] - \E[y_1]\,\E[y_2] = 0$，则称二者不相关。独立蕴含不相关：在中取 $g$、$h$ 为恒等函数即可。反之则不成立，下面的例子和都说明了这一点。

Let $s_1$ and $s_2$ be independent and uniform on $[-\sqrt3, \sqrt3]$, so that they have zero mean, unit variance and $\E[s_i^4] = 9/5$. Rotate them by 45°: $z_1 = (s_1 - s_2)/\sqrt2$ and $z_2 = (s_1 + s_2)/\sqrt2$. Then $\E[z_1z_2] = (\E[s_1^2] - \E[s_2^2])/2 = 0$ and $\E[z_1^2] = \E[z_2^2] = 1$, so the vector $\bz$ is white: its components are uncorrelated with unit variance. With $g(u) = h(u) = u^2$, however,

设 $s_1$、$s_2$ 独立，都服从 $[-\sqrt3, \sqrt3]$ 上的均匀分布，于是它们均值为零、方差为一，且 $\E[s_i^4] = 9/5$。把它们旋转 45°：$z_1 = (s_1 - s_2)/\sqrt2$，$z_2 = (s_1 + s_2)/\sqrt2$。则 $\E[z_1z_2] = (\E[s_1^2] - \E[s_2^2])/2 = 0$，且 $\E[z_1^2] = \E[z_2^2] = 1$，所以向量 $\bz$ 是白的：各分量不相关且方差为一。然而取 $g(u) = h(u) = u^2$，有

$$\E[z_1^2z_2^2] = \tfrac14\,\E\bigl[(s_1^2 - s_2^2)^2\bigr] = \tfrac14\bigl(2\,\E[s_1^4] - 2\bigr) = \tfrac25 \;\ne\; 1 = \E[z_1^2]\,\E[z_2^2],$$

so $z_1$ and $z_2$ are dependent. Geometrically, $\bz$ is uniform on a square rotated by 45°, and the larger $|z_1|$ is, the narrower the range of $z_2$.

所以 $z_1$ 与 $z_2$ 并不独立。从几何上看，$\bz$ 在一个旋转了 45° 的正方形上均匀分布，$|z_1|$ 越大，$z_2$ 的取值范围就越窄。

For jointly Gaussian vectors the two notions coincide ( below). Gaussian marginals are not enough: constructs two uncorrelated standard Gaussian variables that are dependent.

对联合高斯向量而言，这两个概念是一回事（见下文）。仅有高斯的边缘分布是不够的：构造了两个不相关、却不独立的标准高斯变量。

Characteristic functions特征函数

A density need not exist and moments need not be finite, but every distribution has a characteristic function. It turns independence into factorization and sums of independent variables into products, and its logarithm defines the cumulants of the next section.

密度不一定存在，矩也不一定有限，但每个分布都有特征函数。特征函数把独立性变成乘积分解，把独立变量之和变成乘积；它的对数还定义了下一节的累积量。

The characteristic function of a random vector $\by \in \R^d$ is $\varphi_{\by}(\bomega) = \E[\exp(i\,\bomega^\top\by)]$, $\bomega \in \R^d$.

随机向量 $\by \in \R^d$ 的特征函数定义为 $\varphi_{\by}(\bomega) = \E[\exp(i\,\bomega^\top\by)]$，$\bomega \in \R^d$。

Because $|e^{i\bomega^\top\by}| = 1$, the expectation always exists. Moreover $\varphi_{\by}(\bzero) = 1$, $|\varphi_{\by}| \le 1$, $\varphi_{\by}$ is uniformly continuous, and $\varphi_{\by}(-\bomega) = \overline{\varphi_{\by}(\bomega)}$. Three further facts are used repeatedly; the first is the uniqueness theorem for characteristic functions (Billingsley, 1995; Lukacs, 1970), the others follow from the definition.

由于 $|e^{i\bomega^\top\by}| = 1$，这个期望总是存在。此外，$\varphi_{\by}(\bzero) = 1$，$|\varphi_{\by}| \le 1$，$\varphi_{\by}$ 一致连续，且 $\varphi_{\by}(-\bomega) = \overline{\varphi_{\by}(\bomega)}$。以下三个事实会反复用到：第一个是特征函数的唯一性定理（Billingsley, 1995; Lukacs, 1970），其余两个可由定义直接推出。

  • Uniqueness. Two random vectors have the same distribution if and only if they have the same characteristic function.
  • Affine maps. For $\bM \in \R^{k \times d}$ and $\bb \in \R^k$, $\varphi_{\bM\by + \bb}(\bomega) = e^{i\bomega^\top\bb}\,\varphi_{\by}(\bM^\top\bomega)$. Since $\varphi_{-\by}(\bomega) = \overline{\varphi_{\by}(\bomega)}$, uniqueness shows that $\varphi_{\by}$ is real exactly when $\by$ and $-\by$ have the same distribution.
  • Examples. $\Ncal(\mu, \sigma^2)$: $e^{i\mu\omega - \sigma^2\omega^2/2}$; uniform on $[-a, a]$: $\sin(a\omega)/(a\omega)$; Laplace with density $e^{-|y|/b}/(2b)$: $1/(1 + b^2\omega^2)$; exponential with rate $\lambda$: $\lambda/(\lambda - i\omega)$; symmetric binary $\pm 1$: $\cos\omega$.
  • 唯一性。两个随机向量同分布，当且仅当它们的特征函数相同。
  • 仿射变换。对 $\bM \in \R^{k \times d}$ 与 $\bb \in \R^k$，有 $\varphi_{\bM\by + \bb}(\bomega) = e^{i\bomega^\top\bb}\,\varphi_{\by}(\bM^\top\bomega)$。由于 $\varphi_{-\by}(\bomega) = \overline{\varphi_{\by}(\bomega)}$，由唯一性可知：$\varphi_{\by}$ 取实值，当且仅当 $\by$ 与 $-\by$ 同分布。
  • 例子。$\Ncal(\mu, \sigma^2)$：$e^{i\mu\omega - \sigma^2\omega^2/2}$；$[-a, a]$ 上的均匀分布：$\sin(a\omega)/(a\omega)$；密度为 $e^{-|y|/b}/(2b)$ 的拉普拉斯分布：$1/(1 + b^2\omega^2)$；速率为 $\lambda$ 的指数分布：$\lambda/(\lambda - i\omega)$；对称二值分布 $\pm 1$：$\cos\omega$。

A random vector $\by$ is (jointly) Gaussian if every linear combination $\ba^\top\by$ is Gaussian, possibly degenerate (constant). If $\by$ has mean $\bmu$ and covariance $\bSigma$, possibly singular, then $\bomega^\top\by \sim \Ncal(\bomega^\top\bmu,\ \bomega^\top\bSigma\bomega)$, and evaluating the scalar characteristic function at $1$ gives

若随机向量 $\by$ 的每个线性组合 $\ba^\top\by$ 都服从高斯分布（允许退化为常数），则称 $\by$ 是（联合）高斯的。设 $\by$ 的均值为 $\bmu$、协方差为 $\bSigma$（可以奇异），则 $\bomega^\top\by \sim \Ncal(\bomega^\top\bmu,\ \bomega^\top\bSigma\bomega)$，在 $1$ 处计算这个标量的特征函数，得到

$$\varphi_{\by}(\bomega) = \exp\bigl(i\,\bomega^\top\bmu - \tfrac12\,\bomega^\top\bSigma\,\bomega\bigr).$$

(a) Random vectors $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ are independent if and only if the characteristic function of $(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ factorizes: $\varphi_{(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)}(\bomega_1, \dots, \bomega_k) = \prod_{i=1}^k \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega_i)$ for all $\bomega_1, \dots, \bomega_k$. (b) If $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ are independent and have the same dimension, then $\varphi_{\by_1 + \dots + \by_k}(\bomega) = \prod_{i=1}^k \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega)$.

（a）随机向量 $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ 独立，当且仅当 $(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ 的特征函数可以分解：对所有 $\bomega_1, \dots, \bomega_k$，$\varphi_{(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)}(\bomega_1, \dots, \bomega_k) = \prod_{i=1}^k \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega_i)$。（b）若 $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ 独立且维数相同，则 $\varphi_{\by_1 + \dots + \by_k}(\bomega) = \prod_{i=1}^k \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega)$。

(a) If the vectors are independent, the expectation of $\prod_i e^{i\bomega_i^\top\by_i}$ equals the product of the expectations, by applied to real and imaginary parts and by induction on $k$. Conversely, suppose that the characteristic function factorizes. Let $\tilde\by_1, \dots, \tilde\by_k$ be independent, each $\tilde\by_i$ distributed as $\by_i$. By the first part, $(\tilde\by_1, \dots, \tilde\by_k)$ has characteristic function $\prod_i \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega_i)$, the same as $(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$. By uniqueness the two vectors have the same distribution, so the $\by_i$ are independent. (b) Write $e^{i\bomega^\top(\by_1 + \dots + \by_k)} = \prod_i e^{i\bomega^\top\by_i}$, take expectations and use (a) with $\bomega_1 = \dots = \bomega_k = \bomega$.

（a）若这些向量独立，对实部与虚部分别应用并对 $k$ 归纳，可知 $\prod_i e^{i\bomega_i^\top\by_i}$ 的期望等于各因子期望之积。反之，设特征函数可以分解。取相互独立的 $\tilde\by_1, \dots, \tilde\by_k$，使每个 $\tilde\by_i$ 与 $\by_i$ 同分布。由前半部分，$(\tilde\by_1, \dots, \tilde\by_k)$ 的特征函数为 $\prod_i \varphi_{\by_i}(\bomega_i)$，与 $(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ 的特征函数相同；由唯一性定理，两个向量同分布，所以各 $\by_i$ 相互独立。（b）写出 $e^{i\bomega^\top(\by_1 + \dots + \by_k)} = \prod_i e^{i\bomega^\top\by_i}$，取期望，并在（a）中令 $\bomega_1 = \dots = \bomega_k = \bomega$ 即可。

Let $(\by_1, \by_2)$ be jointly Gaussian. Then $\by_1$ and $\by_2$ are independent if and only if their cross-covariance $\E[(\by_1 - \E\by_1)(\by_2 - \E\by_2)^\top]$ is zero. In particular, the components of a Gaussian vector are independent if and only if they are uncorrelated.

设 $(\by_1, \by_2)$ 是联合高斯向量。则 $\by_1$ 与 $\by_2$ 独立，当且仅当它们的互协方差 $\E[(\by_1 - \E\by_1)(\by_2 - \E\by_2)^\top]$ 为零。特别地，高斯向量的各分量独立，当且仅当它们互不相关。

Independence always gives zero cross-covariance. Conversely, if the covariance is block diagonal, with diagonal blocks $\bSigma_{11} = \Cov(\by_1)$ and $\bSigma_{22} = \Cov(\by_2)$, then by the characteristic function of $(\by_1, \by_2)$ is $\exp(i\bomega_1^\top\bmu_1 - \tfrac12\bomega_1^\top\bSigma_{11}\bomega_1)\,\exp(i\bomega_2^\top\bmu_2 - \tfrac12\bomega_2^\top\bSigma_{22}\bomega_2)$. Each factor is the characteristic function of the corresponding marginal, which is again Gaussian, so (a) gives independence. No invertibility of $\bSigma$ is needed.

独立总蕴含互协方差为零。反之，若协方差是分块对角的，对角块为 $\bSigma_{11} = \Cov(\by_1)$ 与 $\bSigma_{22} = \Cov(\by_2)$，则由 ，$(\by_1, \by_2)$ 的特征函数等于 $\exp(i\bomega_1^\top\bmu_1 - \tfrac12\bomega_1^\top\bSigma_{11}\bomega_1)\,\exp(i\bomega_2^\top\bmu_2 - \tfrac12\bomega_2^\top\bSigma_{22}\bomega_2)$。每个因子都是相应边缘分布（仍为高斯分布）的特征函数，于是由（a）得到独立性。这里不需要 $\bSigma$ 可逆。

For Gaussian data, decorrelation therefore already achieves independence, and since a rotation of independent standard Gaussian components leaves them independent, nothing more can be learned (). For non-Gaussian data, independence is a much stronger requirement than decorrelation, and ICA exploits exactly this gap. Part (b) of also shows why the logarithm of the characteristic function is the natural object for sums of independent variables: it turns their product into a sum.

Moments and cumulants矩与累积量

If $\E|y|^k < \infty$, the characteristic function of a scalar $y$ is $k$ times continuously differentiable, with $\varphi_y^{(j)}(0) = i^j\,\E[y^j]$ for $j \le k$, so that $\varphi_y(\omega) = \sum_{j=0}^{k} \E[y^j]\,(i\omega)^j/j! + o(|\omega|^k)$ as $\omega \to 0$. Moments are the Taylor coefficients of $\varphi_y$. Cumulants are the Taylor coefficients of its logarithm, which is well defined near $\omega = 0$ because $\varphi_y(0) = 1$ and $\varphi_y$ is continuous.

若 $\E|y|^k < \infty$，则标量 $y$ 的特征函数 $k$ 次连续可微，且对 $j \le k$ 有 $\varphi_y^{(j)}(0) = i^j\,\E[y^j]$，于是当 $\omega \to 0$ 时 $\varphi_y(\omega) = \sum_{j=0}^{k} \E[y^j]\,(i\omega)^j/j! + o(|\omega|^k)$。矩是 $\varphi_y$ 的 Taylor 系数，累积量则是其对数的 Taylor 系数。由于 $\varphi_y(0) = 1$ 且 $\varphi_y$ 连续，这个对数在 $\omega = 0$ 附近有定义。

Let $v_1, \dots, v_j$ be random variables, not necessarily distinct, with finite moments of order $j$, and let $\bv = (v_1, \dots, v_j)^\top$. Their (joint) cumulant is

设随机变量 $v_1, \dots, v_j$（不必互不相同）的 $j$ 阶矩有限，记 $\bv = (v_1, \dots, v_j)^\top$。它们的（联合）累积量定义为

$$\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j) = (-i)^j\, \frac{\partial^j \log\varphi_{\bv}(\bomega)}{\partial\omega_1 \cdots \partial\omega_j}\bigg|_{\bomega = \bzero}.$$

The cumulant of order $j$ of a single variable is $\kappa_j(y) = \cum(y, \dots, y)$, with $j$ arguments. Equivalently, if $\E|y|^k < \infty$, then $\log\varphi_y(\omega) = \sum_{j=1}^{k} \kappa_j(y)\,(i\omega)^j/j! + o(|\omega|^k)$ as $\omega \to 0$. The function $\log\varphi$ is called the second characteristic function.

单个变量的 $j$ 阶累积量是 $\kappa_j(y) = \cum(y, \dots, y)$（共 $j$ 个自变量）。等价地，若 $\E|y|^k < \infty$，则当 $\omega \to 0$ 时 $\log\varphi_y(\omega) = \sum_{j=1}^{k} \kappa_j(y)\,(i\omega)^j/j! + o(|\omega|^k)$。函数 $\log\varphi$ 称为第二特征函数。

The first four cumulants of a scalar with mean $\mu$ are

均值为 $\mu$ 的标量的前四阶累积量为

$$\begin{aligned} \kappa_1 &= \E[y], & \kappa_2 &= \E[(y - \mu)^2] = \Var(y), \\ \kappa_3 &= \E[(y - \mu)^3], & \kappa_4 &= \E[(y - \mu)^4] - 3\Var(y)^2. \end{aligned}$$

In terms of the raw moments $m_j = \E[y^j]$, $\kappa_3 = m_3 - 3m_2m_1 + 2m_1^3$ and $\kappa_4 = m_4 - 4m_3m_1 - 3m_2^2 + 12m_2m_1^2 - 6m_1^4$. For zero mean the derivation takes one line. Write $\varphi_y(\omega) = 1 + u$ with $u = -\sigma^2\omega^2/2 - i\,m_3\omega^3/6 + m_4\omega^4/24 + o(\omega^4)$. Then $\log(1 + u) = u - u^2/2 + O(u^3)$, where $u^2 = \sigma^4\omega^4/4 + o(\omega^4)$ and $u^3 = o(\omega^4)$, so $\log\varphi_y(\omega) = -\sigma^2\omega^2/2 - i\,m_3\omega^3/6 + (m_4 - 3\sigma^4)\,\omega^4/24 + o(\omega^4)$. Matching coefficients with $\sum_j \kappa_j (i\omega)^j/j!$ gives $\kappa_2 = \sigma^2$, $\kappa_3 = m_3$ and $\kappa_4 = m_4 - 3\sigma^4$.

用原点矩 $m_j = \E[y^j]$ 表示，$\kappa_3 = m_3 - 3m_2m_1 + 2m_1^3$，$\kappa_4 = m_4 - 4m_3m_1 - 3m_2^2 + 12m_2m_1^2 - 6m_1^4$。均值为零时，推导只需一行。记 $\varphi_y(\omega) = 1 + u$，其中 $u = -\sigma^2\omega^2/2 - i\,m_3\omega^3/6 + m_4\omega^4/24 + o(\omega^4)$。于是 $\log(1 + u) = u - u^2/2 + O(u^3)$，而 $u^2 = \sigma^4\omega^4/4 + o(\omega^4)$、$u^3 = o(\omega^4)$，所以 $\log\varphi_y(\omega) = -\sigma^2\omega^2/2 - i\,m_3\omega^3/6 + (m_4 - 3\sigma^4)\,\omega^4/24 + o(\omega^4)$。与 $\sum_j \kappa_j (i\omega)^j/j!$ 比较系数，得 $\kappa_2 = \sigma^2$，$\kappa_3 = m_3$，$\kappa_4 = m_4 - 3\sigma^4$。

For zero-mean variables, the cumulants of order two and three are moments, $\cum(x_i, x_j) = \E[x_ix_j]$ and $\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k) = \E[x_ix_jx_k]$, while the fourth-order cumulant subtracts from the fourth moment the three ways of pairing the indices ():

对零均值变量，二阶与三阶累积量就是矩：$\cum(x_i, x_j) = \E[x_ix_j]$，$\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k) = \E[x_ix_jx_k]$；而四阶累积量要从四阶矩中减去把四个下标两两配对的三种方式（）：

$$\begin{aligned} \cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l) = \E[x_ix_jx_kx_l] &- \E[x_ix_j]\,\E[x_kx_l] \\ &- \E[x_ix_k]\,\E[x_jx_l] - \E[x_ix_l]\,\E[x_jx_k]. \end{aligned}$$

For $i = j = k = l$ this is $\kappa_4 = \E[x^4] - 3(\E[x^2])^2$ again. The properties that make cumulants useful are collected below.

当 $i = j = k = l$ 时，它又回到 $\kappa_4 = \E[x^4] - 3(\E[x^2])^2$。累积量之所以有用，靠的是下面这些性质。

Assume that all moments that appear are finite. Then:

  1. Symmetry. $\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ is invariant under permutations of its arguments.
  2. Multilinearity. For constants $m_{rp}$, $\cum\bigl(\sum_p m_{1p}x_p, \dots, \sum_p m_{jp}x_p\bigr) = \sum_{p_1, \dots, p_j} m_{1p_1}\cdots m_{jp_j}\,\cum(x_{p_1}, \dots, x_{p_j})$.
  3. Shift invariance. For $j \ge 2$ and a constant $c$, $\cum(v_1 + c, v_2, \dots, v_j) = \cum(v_1, v_2, \dots, v_j)$.
  4. Additivity. If the vectors $(u_1, \dots, u_j)$ and $(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ are independent, then $\cum(u_1 + v_1, \dots, u_j + v_j) = \cum(u_1, \dots, u_j) + \cum(v_1, \dots, v_j)$. In particular $\kappa_j(y_1 + y_2) = \kappa_j(y_1) + \kappa_j(y_2)$ for independent $y_1$ and $y_2$.
  5. Independent groups. If the arguments $v_1, \dots, v_j$ can be split into two nonempty groups that are independent of each other, then $\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j) = 0$.
  6. Gaussian variables. If $(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ is jointly Gaussian and $j \ge 3$, then $\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j) = 0$.
  1. 对称性。$\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ 在自变量的任意置换下不变。
  2. 多重线性。对常数 $m_{rp}$，$\cum\bigl(\sum_p m_{1p}x_p, \dots, \sum_p m_{jp}x_p\bigr) = \sum_{p_1, \dots, p_j} m_{1p_1}\cdots m_{jp_j}\,\cum(x_{p_1}, \dots, x_{p_j})$。
  3. 平移不变性。当 $j \ge 2$ 且 $c$ 为常数时，$\cum(v_1 + c, v_2, \dots, v_j) = \cum(v_1, v_2, \dots, v_j)$。
  4. 可加性。若向量 $(u_1, \dots, u_j)$ 与 $(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ 独立，则 $\cum(u_1 + v_1, \dots, u_j + v_j) = \cum(u_1, \dots, u_j) + \cum(v_1, \dots, v_j)$。特别地，对独立的 $y_1$ 与 $y_2$，$\kappa_j(y_1 + y_2) = \kappa_j(y_1) + \kappa_j(y_2)$。
  5. 独立分组。若自变量 $v_1, \dots, v_j$ 能分成两个非空且相互独立的组，则 $\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j) = 0$。
  6. 高斯变量。若 $(v_1, \dots, v_j)$ 是联合高斯的且 $j \ge 3$，则 $\cum(v_1, \dots, v_j) = 0$。

Property 6 says that Gaussian variables have no cumulants beyond order two. A classical theorem shows that this property nearly characterizes the Gaussian.

性质 6 说明高斯变量没有二阶以上的累积量。一个经典定理表明，这一性质几乎刻画了高斯分布。

If the characteristic function of a random variable has the form $\varphi(\omega) = \exp(P(\omega))$ for all real $\omega$, where $P$ is a polynomial, then $P$ has degree at most 2, and the variable is Gaussian or constant.

若某随机变量的特征函数对所有实数 $\omega$ 都具有形式 $\varphi(\omega) = \exp(P(\omega))$，其中 $P$ 是多项式，则 $P$ 的次数不超过 2，从而该变量是高斯的或为常数。

The theorem is due to Marcinkiewicz (1939); a proof can be found in Lukacs (1970). We use two consequences. First, a random variable with moments of all orders whose cumulants of order three and higher all vanish is Gaussian: its moments then coincide with those of a Gaussian, and the Gaussian distribution is determined by its moments. Second, no non-Gaussian variable has only finitely many nonzero cumulants: if it had, its moment-generating function would be entire and equal to $\exp(P(t))$ for a polynomial $P$, so $\varphi(\omega) = \exp(P(i\omega))$ and the theorem would apply. Cumulants of order three and higher are therefore measures of non-Gaussianity, and the theorem is a step in the proof of the Darmois–Skitovich theorem in . In practice only low orders are used, because the variance of a sample cumulant grows quickly with its order ().

这一定理属于 Marcinkiewicz（1939），证明可见 Lukacs（1970）。我们用到它的两个推论。其一，若一个随机变量的各阶矩都存在，且三阶及以上的累积量全为零，则它是高斯的：此时它的各阶矩与某个高斯分布的矩相同，而高斯分布由其各阶矩唯一确定。其二，任何非高斯变量都不可能只有有限个非零累积量：否则它的矩母函数是整函数，且等于 $\exp(P(t))$（$P$ 为多项式），于是 $\varphi(\omega) = \exp(P(i\omega))$，与定理矛盾。因此，三阶及以上的累积量可以作为非高斯性的度量；在中，这一定理也是证明 Darmois–Skitovich 定理的一步。实践中只用低阶累积量，因为样本累积量的方差随阶数迅速增大（）。

Skewness and kurtosis偏度与峭度

The normalized third and fourth cumulants are the simplest numbers that describe the shape of a distribution beyond its location and scale. The fourth, kurtosis, is the most widely used statistic in ICA.

归一化的三阶与四阶累积量，是在位置与尺度之外描述分布形状的最简单的数。其中四阶的峭度是 ICA 中用得最多的统计量。

Let $y$ have a finite fourth moment, mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2 > 0$. Its kurtosis is the fourth cumulant, $\kurt(y) = \kappa_4(y) = \E[(y - \mu)^4] - 3\sigma^4$; for zero-mean $y$, $\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$. Its normalized kurtosis is $\kurt(y)/\sigma^4$, and its skewness is $\kappa_3(y)/\sigma^3 = \E[(y - \mu)^3]/\sigma^3$. The variable is called sub-Gaussian if $\kurt(y) < 0$ and super-Gaussian if $\kurt(y) > 0$.

设 $y$ 的四阶矩有限，均值为 $\mu$，方差为 $\sigma^2 > 0$。它的峭度是四阶累积量 $\kurt(y) = \kappa_4(y) = \E[(y - \mu)^4] - 3\sigma^4$；当 $y$ 均值为零时，$\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$。$\kurt(y)/\sigma^4$ 称为归一化峭度，$\kappa_3(y)/\sigma^3 = \E[(y - \mu)^3]/\sigma^3$ 称为偏度。若 $\kurt(y) < 0$，称该变量为亚高斯的；若 $\kurt(y) > 0$，称其为超高斯的。

Statisticians call the normalized kurtosis the excess kurtosis; the moment ratio $\E[(y - \mu)^4]/\sigma^4$ (Pearson's $\beta_2$), which many statistics texts call simply the kurtosis, equals the normalized kurtosis plus 3. For unit-variance variables, $\kurt(y)$ and the normalized kurtosis coincide, and for unit-variance sources we write $\kappa_i = \kurt(s_i)$. In probability theory “sub-Gaussian” also describes tails that decay at least as fast as Gaussian tails; in ICA it refers only to the sign of the kurtosis.

统计学中把归一化峭度称为超额峰度；许多统计教材则把矩比值 $\E[(y - \mu)^4]/\sigma^4$（Pearson 的 $\beta_2$）直接称为峰度，它等于归一化峭度加 3。对单位方差的变量，$\kurt(y)$ 与归一化峭度相同；对单位方差的源，记 $\kappa_i = \kurt(s_i)$。在概率论中，“亚高斯”还用来指尾部衰减至少与高斯尾部一样快的分布；在 ICA 中，它只指峭度的符号。

The sign of the kurtosis does not determine the shape of a density, but typical cases look as follows. Super-Gaussian variables usually have heavier tails than a Gaussian of the same variance, and often a sharper peak; kurtosis measures the tails rather than the peak (Westfall, 2014). The standard examples are sparse signals, which are mostly close to zero and occasionally large: speech (Gazor & Zhang, 2003), the outputs of edge filters applied to natural images (Bell & Sejnowski, 1997), and the spatial maps of fMRI networks (). Sub-Gaussian variables are usually flatter, with light tails, and often bounded or multimodal: a uniform variable, a sinusoid with random phase, a binary communication symbol. There are exceptions: a variable equal to $\pm 1$ with probability $0.49$ each and to $\pm 5$ with probability $0.01$ each is bimodal, yet its normalized kurtosis is $+3.15$. compares three typical unit-variance densities.

峭度的符号并不决定密度的形状，但典型的情形如下。超高斯变量通常比同方差的高斯变量尾部更重，峰也常常更尖；峭度刻画的是尾部而不是峰（Westfall, 2014）。标准的例子是稀疏信号，它们大多数时候接近零，偶尔取很大的值：语音（Gazor & Zhang, 2003）、自然图像经边缘滤波器后的输出（Bell & Sejnowski, 1997），以及 fMRI 网络的空间图（）。亚高斯变量通常更平坦、尾部更轻，常常有界或呈多峰形状，例如均匀分布变量、随机相位的正弦波、二值通信符号。例外也是有的：以各 $0.49$ 的概率取 $\pm 1$、以各 $0.01$ 的概率取 $\pm 5$ 的变量是双峰的，其归一化峭度却为 $+3.15$。 比较了三个典型的单位方差密度。

(3) By Jensen's inequality for the convex function $u \mapsto u^2$, $\E[(y - \mu)^4] = \E\bigl[((y - \mu)^2)^2\bigr] \ge \bigl(\E[(y - \mu)^2]\bigr)^2 = \sigma^4$, so $\kurt(y) \ge \sigma^4 - 3\sigma^4$. Since $u \mapsto u^2$ is strictly convex, equality holds if and only if $(y - \mu)^2$ is almost surely constant, that is, $y = \mu \pm \sigma$; the condition $\E[y] = \mu$ then forces the two values to have probability $\tfrac12$.

（1）与（2）由中的多重线性、平移不变性与可加性得到。（3）对凸函数 $u \mapsto u^2$ 用 Jensen 不等式，$\E[(y - \mu)^4] = \E\bigl[((y - \mu)^2)^2\bigr] \ge \bigl(\E[(y - \mu)^2]\bigr)^2 = \sigma^4$，所以 $\kurt(y) \ge \sigma^4 - 3\sigma^4$。由于 $u \mapsto u^2$ 严格凸，等号成立当且仅当 $(y - \mu)^2$ 几乎必然为常数，即 $y = \mu \pm \sigma$；再由 $\E[y] = \mu$ 可知两个值的概率都是 $\tfrac12$。

def kurt(y):                           # fourth cumulant of a sample
    y = y - y.mean()
    return np.mean(y**4) - 3 * np.mean(y**2)**2

def cum22(y1, y2):                     # cum(y1, y1, y2, y2) of a sample
    y1, y2 = y1 - y1.mean(), y2 - y2.mean()
    return (np.mean(y1**2 * y2**2) - np.mean(y1**2) * np.mean(y2**2)
            - 2 * np.mean(y1 * y2)**2)

    "gaussian":    rng.standard_normal(T),
    "binary":      rng.choice([-1.0, 1.0], T),
    "exponential": rng.exponential(1.0, T),
    "student-t10": rng.standard_t(10, T) / np.sqrt(10 / 8),

    print(f"{name:12s} kurt = {kurt(y):+.3f}")

Differential entropy微分熵

Cumulants summarize a distribution by a few numbers; entropy summarizes it through its whole density. For a discrete variable, Shannon's entropy (Shannon, 1948) measures the average uncertainty of an outcome. Its continuous counterpart is differential entropy (Cover & Thomas, 2006).

累积量用几个数概括一个分布，熵则通过整个密度来概括它。对离散变量，Shannon 熵（Shannon, 1948）度量一次结果的平均不确定性；它在连续情形下的对应物是微分熵（Cover & Thomas, 2006）。

Let $\by \in \R^d$ have density $p$. Its differential entropy is $H(\by) = -\int p(\bu)\log p(\bu)\,d\bu = -\E[\log p(\by)]$, whenever the integral exists. For several random vectors, $H(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ is the entropy of the stacked vector.

设 $\by \in \R^d$ 有密度 $p$。它的微分熵定义为 $H(\by) = -\int p(\bu)\log p(\bu)\,d\bu = -\E[\log p(\by)]$（只要该积分存在）。对多个随机向量，$H(\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ 指把它们堆叠成一个向量后的熵。

Three examples follow directly from the definition. A uniform density on an interval of length $L$ has $H = \log L$; for the unit-variance uniform law, $L = 2\sqrt3$ and $H = \log(2\sqrt3) \approx 1.2425$. For $\Ncal(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $-\log p(y) = \tfrac12\log(2\pi\sigma^2) + (y - \mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)$, whose expectation gives $H = \tfrac12\log(2\pi e\sigma^2)$, or $1.4189$ at unit variance; in the same way a Gaussian vector with covariance $\bSigma$ has $H = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$. For the Laplace density $e^{-|y|/b}/(2b)$, $-\log p(y) = \log(2b) + |y|/b$ and $\E|y| = b$, so $H = 1 + \log(2b)$, or $1 + \tfrac12\log 2 \approx 1.3466$ at unit variance.

由定义可以直接算出三个例子。长度为 $L$ 的区间上的均匀密度，$H = \log L$；单位方差的均匀分布 $L = 2\sqrt3$，$H = \log(2\sqrt3) \approx 1.2425$。对 $\Ncal(\mu, \sigma^2)$，$-\log p(y) = \tfrac12\log(2\pi\sigma^2) + (y - \mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)$，取期望得 $H = \tfrac12\log(2\pi e\sigma^2)$，单位方差时为 $1.4189$；同理，协方差为 $\bSigma$ 的高斯向量有 $H = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$。对拉普拉斯密度 $e^{-|y|/b}/(2b)$，$-\log p(y) = \log(2b) + |y|/b$，且 $\E|y| = b$，所以 $H = 1 + \log(2b)$，单位方差时为 $1 + \tfrac12\log 2 \approx 1.3466$。

Differential entropy differs from discrete entropy in two ways. It can be negative (the uniform density on $[0, \tfrac12]$ has entropy $-\log 2$), and it depends on scale: $H(\alpha y) = H(y) + \log|\alpha|$. Both facts are special cases of the next proposition. The quantities that ICA uses, mutual information and negentropy, are combinations of entropies in which these changes cancel.

微分熵与离散熵有两点不同。它可以为负（$[0, \tfrac12]$ 上的均匀密度的熵为 $-\log 2$），而且依赖于尺度：$H(\alpha y) = H(y) + \log|\alpha|$。这两点都是下一命题的特例。ICA 所用的量，即互信息与负熵，都是若干熵的组合，在组合中这些变化恰好相互抵消。

Let $\by \in \R^d$ have a density and finite entropy, let $\bA \in \R^{d \times d}$ be invertible and $\bb \in \R^d$. Then $H(\bA\by + \bb) = H(\by) + \log|\det\bA|$. More generally, if $\mathbf{f}$ is a continuously differentiable bijection of $\R^d$ onto an open set whose Jacobian matrix $\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}$ is nonsingular everywhere, then $H(\mathbf{f}(\by)) = H(\by) + \E\bigl[\log|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\by)|\bigr]$ whenever the right-hand side is finite.

设 $\by \in \R^d$ 有密度且熵有限，$\bA \in \R^{d \times d}$ 可逆，$\bb \in \R^d$。则 $H(\bA\by + \bb) = H(\by) + \log|\det\bA|$。更一般地，若 $\mathbf{f}$ 是从 $\R^d$ 到某个开集的连续可微双射，且其 Jacobi 矩阵 $\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}$ 处处非奇异，则只要右端有限，就有 $H(\mathbf{f}(\by)) = H(\by) + \E\bigl[\log|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\by)|\bigr]$。

By the change-of-variables formula, $\mathbf{f}(\by)$ has the density $p_{\mathbf{f}(\by)}(\mathbf{f}(\bu)) = p_{\by}(\bu)/|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\bu)|$. Hence $H(\mathbf{f}(\by)) = -\E\bigl[\log p_{\mathbf{f}(\by)}(\mathbf{f}(\by))\bigr] = -\E[\log p_{\by}(\by)] + \E\bigl[\log|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\by)|\bigr]$. For $\mathbf{f}(\bu) = \bA\bu + \bb$ the Jacobian is the constant matrix $\bA$.

由变量替换公式，$\mathbf{f}(\by)$ 的密度满足 $p_{\mathbf{f}(\by)}(\mathbf{f}(\bu)) = p_{\by}(\bu)/|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\bu)|$。因此 $H(\mathbf{f}(\by)) = -\E\bigl[\log p_{\mathbf{f}(\by)}(\mathbf{f}(\by))\bigr] = -\E[\log p_{\by}(\by)] + \E\bigl[\log|\det\bJ_{\mathbf{f}}(\by)|\bigr]$。当 $\mathbf{f}(\bu) = \bA\bu + \bb$ 时，Jacobi 矩阵就是常数矩阵 $\bA$。

Two consequences recur in later chapters. Orthogonal transformations preserve entropy, $H(\bQ\by) = H(\by)$, because $|\det\bQ| = 1$: rotating whitened data does not change their joint entropy. And for $\by = \bW\bx$ with $\bW$ invertible, $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$; this is how the term $\log|\det\bW|$ enters the likelihood of ICA in .

有两个推论会在后面各章反复出现。正交变换保持熵不变，$H(\bQ\by) = H(\by)$，因为 $|\det\bQ| = 1$：旋转白化后的数据不改变它们的联合熵。另外，对可逆的 $\bW$ 与 $\by = \bW\bx$，有 $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$；中 ICA 似然里的 $\log|\det\bW|$ 项正是这样来的。

Divergence, mutual information and negentropy散度、互信息与负熵

Entropy by itself measures neither independence nor non-Gaussianity, but two divergences built from it do. Both rest on one inequality.

熵本身既不度量独立性，也不度量非高斯性；但由熵构造的两个散度可以。二者都建立在同一个不等式之上。

For densities $p$ and $q$ on $\R^d$, the Kullback–Leibler divergence is $D(p\,\|\,q) = \int p(\bu)\log\bigl(p(\bu)/q(\bu)\bigr)\,d\bu$, taken to be $+\infty$ if $P(q(\by) = 0) > 0$ for $\by \sim p$.

对 $\R^d$ 上的密度 $p$ 与 $q$，KL 散度（Kullback–Leibler 散度）定义为 $D(p\,\|\,q) = \int p(\bu)\log\bigl(p(\bu)/q(\bu)\bigr)\,d\bu$；若对 $\by \sim p$ 有 $P(q(\by) = 0) > 0$，则规定其为 $+\infty$。

$D(p\,\|\,q) \ge 0$, with equality if and only if $p = q$ almost everywhere.

$D(p\,\|\,q) \ge 0$，等号成立当且仅当 $p = q$ 几乎处处成立。

We may assume $D(p\,\|\,q) < \infty$. Let $\by \sim p$. By Jensen's inequality for the concave logarithm, $-D(p\,\|\,q) = \E\bigl[\log(q(\by)/p(\by))\bigr] \le \log\E\bigl[q(\by)/p(\by)\bigr] = \log\int_{\{p > 0\}} q(\bu)\,d\bu \le \log 1 = 0$. Since the logarithm is strictly concave, equality in the first step requires $q/p$ to be constant on $\{p > 0\}$, and equality in the second requires $\int_{\{p > 0\}} q = 1$; together they give $q = p$ almost everywhere.

不妨设 $D(p\,\|\,q) < \infty$。令 $\by \sim p$。对凹函数 $\log$ 用 Jensen 不等式，$-D(p\,\|\,q) = \E\bigl[\log(q(\by)/p(\by))\bigr] \le \log\E\bigl[q(\by)/p(\by)\bigr] = \log\int_{\{p > 0\}} q(\bu)\,d\bu \le \log 1 = 0$。由于对数严格凹，第一步取等号要求 $q/p$ 在 $\{p > 0\}$ 上为常数，第二步取等号要求 $\int_{\{p > 0\}} q = 1$；二者合起来即得 $q = p$ 几乎处处成立。

The divergence was introduced by Kullback and Leibler (1951). It is not symmetric, and it is not a metric. It is invariant under reparametrization: if both densities are transformed by the same map $\mathbf{f}$ as in , the Jacobian factors cancel in the ratio $p/q$. Its first use here is a classical extremal property of the Gaussian.

这一散度由 Kullback 与 Leibler（1951）提出。它不对称，也不是度量。它在重新参数化下不变：若两个密度经过同一个映射 $\mathbf{f}$（如中）变换，Jacobi 因子在比值 $p/q$ 中相互抵消。它在这里的第一个用途，是证明高斯分布的一个经典极值性质。

Let $\by \in \R^d$ have density $p$, finite entropy, mean $\bmu$ and nonsingular covariance $\bSigma$, and let $p_{\mathrm{G}}$ be the density of $\Ncal(\bmu, \bSigma)$. Then

设 $\by \in \R^d$ 有密度 $p$，熵有限，均值为 $\bmu$，协方差 $\bSigma$ 非奇异，并记 $p_{\mathrm{G}}$ 为 $\Ncal(\bmu, \bSigma)$ 的密度。则

$$D(p\,\|\,p_{\mathrm{G}}) = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) - H(\by) \;\ge\; 0 .$$

Hence $H(\by) \le \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$, the entropy of $\Ncal(\bmu, \bSigma)$, with equality if and only if $\by$ is Gaussian.

因此 $H(\by) \le \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$，即 $\Ncal(\bmu, \bSigma)$ 的熵；等号成立当且仅当 $\by$ 是高斯的。

We have $-\log p_{\mathrm{G}}(\bu) = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) + \tfrac12(\bu - \bmu)^\top\bSigma^{-1}(\bu - \bmu)$. Its expectation under $p$ depends only on the mean and covariance of $\by$: since $\E[(\by - \bmu)^\top\bSigma^{-1}(\by - \bmu)] = \tr(\bSigma^{-1}\bSigma) = d$, it equals $\tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$, the same value as for a Gaussian $\by$. Therefore $D(p\,\|\,p_{\mathrm{G}}) = -H(\by) + \E[-\log p_{\mathrm{G}}(\by)] = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) - H(\by)$, which is nonnegative by and zero only when $p = p_{\mathrm{G}}$ almost everywhere.

我们有 $-\log p_{\mathrm{G}}(\bu) = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) + \tfrac12(\bu - \bmu)^\top\bSigma^{-1}(\bu - \bmu)$。它在 $p$ 下的期望只依赖于 $\by$ 的均值与协方差：由于 $\E[(\by - \bmu)^\top\bSigma^{-1}(\by - \bmu)] = \tr(\bSigma^{-1}\bSigma) = d$，这个期望等于 $\tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr)$，与 $\by$ 为高斯时的值相同。因此 $D(p\,\|\,p_{\mathrm{G}}) = -H(\by) + \E[-\log p_{\mathrm{G}}(\by)] = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) - H(\by)$；由，它非负，且仅当 $p = p_{\mathrm{G}}$ 几乎处处成立时为零。

Among all distributions with a given covariance, the Gaussian is the most disordered. This is the information-theoretic side of the intuition in : mixing independent signals makes them more Gaussian, so a measure of distance from the Gaussian can guide separation.

在协方差给定的所有分布中，高斯分布最“无序”。这是中那条直觉在信息论上的一面：混合独立信号会使它们更接近高斯，所以与高斯分布之间的“距离”可以用来指导分离。

Let $\by = (\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ have joint density $p_{\by}$ and marginal densities $p_{\by_i}$. The mutual information of $\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ is the divergence of the joint density from the product of its marginals,

设 $\by = (\by_1, \dots, \by_k)$ 的联合密度为 $p_{\by}$，边缘密度为 $p_{\by_i}$。$\by_1, \dots, \by_k$ 的互信息定义为联合密度相对于边缘密度之积的散度：

$$I(\by_1, \dots, \by_k) = D\Bigl(p_{\by}\,\Big\|\,\prod_{i=1}^k p_{\by_i}\Bigr) = \sum_{i=1}^k H(\by_i) - H(\by),$$

where the second expression holds when all entropies are finite. For the scalar components of $\by \in \R^n$ we write $I(\by) = I(y_1, \dots, y_n)$.

其中第二个等号在各熵有限时成立。对 $\by \in \R^n$ 的标量分量，记 $I(\by) = I(y_1, \dots, y_n)$。

The second expression follows by taking the expectation of $\log p_{\by}(\by) - \sum_i \log p_{\by_i}(\by_i)$. Mutual information measures exactly what ICA wants to remove.

对 $\log p_{\by}(\by) - \sum_i \log p_{\by_i}(\by_i)$ 取期望即得第二个表达式。互信息度量的恰好是 ICA 想要消除的东西。

(a) $I(\by) \ge 0$, with equality if and only if the components of $\by$ are independent. (b) If $f_1, \dots, f_n$ are continuously differentiable bijections of $\R$ onto open intervals with nonvanishing derivatives, then $I(f_1(y_1), \dots, f_n(y_n)) = I(y_1, \dots, y_n)$. In particular, mutual information does not change when the components are rescaled, change sign or are permuted.

（a）$I(\by) \ge 0$，等号成立当且仅当 $\by$ 的各分量独立。（b）若 $f_1, \dots, f_n$ 都是从 $\R$ 到某个开区间的连续可微双射，且导数处处不为零，则 $I(f_1(y_1), \dots, f_n(y_n)) = I(y_1, \dots, y_n)$。特别地，对各分量作缩放、改变符号或重新排列，互信息都不变。

(a) follows from and statement 3 of . (b) Assume the entropies are finite. The Jacobian of $\bu \mapsto (f_1(u_1), \dots, f_n(u_n))$ is diagonal, so by the joint entropy changes by $\sum_i \E[\log|f_i'(y_i)|]$, while each marginal entropy $H(f_i(y_i))$ changes by $\E[\log|f_i'(y_i)|]$. The changes cancel in $\sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by)$, and a permutation only reorders the sum. (Without finite entropies, use the invariance of the divergence under reparametrization.)

（a）由与的陈述 3 得到。（b）设各熵有限。映射 $\bu \mapsto (f_1(u_1), \dots, f_n(u_n))$ 的 Jacobi 矩阵是对角的，所以由，联合熵改变 $\sum_i \E[\log|f_i'(y_i)|]$，而每个边缘熵 $H(f_i(y_i))$ 改变 $\E[\log|f_i'(y_i)|]$。这些改变在 $\sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by)$ 中相互抵消，而置换只是重新排列求和的次序。（若熵不有限，可改用散度在重新参数化下的不变性。）

对可逆的 $\bW$ 与 $\by = \bW\bx$，由， 变为

$$I(\by) = \sum_{i=1}^n H(y_i) - H(\bx) - \log|\det\bW|,$$

the starting point of (). The entropy $H(\bx)$ does not depend on $\bW$, so minimizing the mutual information of the outputs involves only the marginal entropies and $\log|\det\bW|$.

这是的出发点（）。熵 $H(\bx)$ 与 $\bW$ 无关，所以最小化输出的互信息只涉及各边缘熵与 $\log|\det\bW|$。

Let $\by \in \R^d$ have a density, finite entropy and nonsingular covariance $\bSigma$, and let $\by_{\mathrm{G}}$ be a Gaussian vector with the same mean and covariance. The negentropy of $\by$ is $J(\by) = H(\by_{\mathrm{G}}) - H(\by) = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) - H(\by)$. For a scalar, $J(y) = \tfrac12\log\bigl(2\pi e\Var(y)\bigr) - H(y)$.

设 $\by \in \R^d$ 有密度，熵有限，协方差 $\bSigma$ 非奇异，并设 $\by_{\mathrm{G}}$ 是与 $\by$ 均值、协方差都相同的高斯向量。$\by$ 的负熵定义为 $J(\by) = H(\by_{\mathrm{G}}) - H(\by) = \tfrac12\log\bigl((2\pi e)^d\det\bSigma\bigr) - H(\by)$。对标量，$J(y) = \tfrac12\log\bigl(2\pi e\Var(y)\bigr) - H(y)$。

(a) $J(\by) \ge 0$, with equality if and only if $\by$ is Gaussian. (b) $J(\by) = D(p_{\by}\,\|\,p_{\mathrm{G}})$, where $p_{\mathrm{G}}$ is the Gaussian density with the same mean and covariance. (c) $J(\bA\by + \bb) = J(\by)$ for every invertible $\bA$ and every $\bb$.

（a）$J(\by) \ge 0$，等号成立当且仅当 $\by$ 是高斯的。（b）$J(\by) = D(p_{\by}\,\|\,p_{\mathrm{G}})$，其中 $p_{\mathrm{G}}$ 是均值、协方差与 $\by$ 相同的高斯密度。（c）对任意可逆的 $\bA$ 与任意 $\bb$，$J(\bA\by + \bb) = J(\by)$。

(a) and (b) restate . (c) The vector $\bA\by + \bb$ has covariance $\bA\bSigma\bA^\top$, and the Gaussian vector with its mean and covariance is $\bA\by_{\mathrm{G}} + \bb$. Key points

本章要点

  • Independence means that the joint distribution factorizes; equivalently, $\E[g(\by_1)h(\by_2)] = \E[g(\by_1)]\,\E[h(\by_2)]$ for all bounded $g$ and $h$, or the characteristic function factorizes. Uncorrelatedness is the linear special case and is much weaker, except for jointly Gaussian vectors.
  • Cumulants are the Taylor coefficients of the logarithm of the characteristic function. They are symmetric and multilinear, add up over independent summands, vanish when their arguments split into independent groups, and vanish beyond order two for Gaussian variables.
  • For $\bx = \bA\bs$ with independent sources, $\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l) = \sum_p a_{ip}a_{jp}a_{kp}a_{lp}\,\kappa_p$: fourth-order statistics see the individual columns of $\bA$, which the covariance cannot.
  • Kurtosis, $\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$ for zero-mean $y$, is the fourth cumulant. Its sign separates sub-Gaussian from super-Gaussian variables, and the normalized kurtosis is at least $-2$.
  • Differential entropy changes by $\log|\det\bA|$ under $\by \mapsto \bA\by$, and at fixed covariance it is largest for the Gaussian.
  • Mutual information $I(\by) = \sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by) \ge 0$ vanishes exactly when the components are independent; negentropy $J \ge 0$ vanishes exactly for Gaussian vectors and is invariant under invertible linear maps.
  • 独立意味着联合分布可以分解；等价地，对一切有界的 $g$ 与 $h$ 有 $\E[g(\by_1)h(\by_2)] = \E[g(\by_1)]\,\E[h(\by_2)]$，或者特征函数可以分解。不相关只是其中的线性特例，要弱得多；联合高斯向量是例外。
  • 累积量是特征函数对数的 Taylor 系数。它们对称、多重线性，对独立的加项可加；当自变量能分成相互独立的两组时为零；对高斯变量，二阶以上的累积量全为零。
  • 对源独立的 $\bx = \bA\bs$，$\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l) = \sum_p a_{ip}a_{jp}a_{kp}a_{lp}\,\kappa_p$：四阶统计量能看到 $\bA$ 的每一列，而协方差做不到。
  • 峭度（对零均值的 $y$，$\kurt(y) = \E[y^4] - 3(\E[y^2])^2$）是四阶累积量。它的符号区分亚高斯与超高斯变量，归一化峭度不小于 $-2$。
  • 在 $\by \mapsto \bA\by$ 下，微分熵改变 $\log|\det\bA|$；在协方差固定时，高斯分布的熵最大。
  • 互信息 $I(\by) = \sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by) \ge 0$，当且仅当各分量独立时为零；负熵 $J \ge 0$，当且仅当向量是高斯的时为零，并在可逆线性变换下不变。
