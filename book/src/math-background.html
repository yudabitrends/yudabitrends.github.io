Conventions约定

This appendix collects the matrix and statistical facts used throughout the book. Short and instructive proofs are given in full; for the rest we point to standard references. All matrices are real. For matrices of the same size, $\langle\bA, \bB\rangle = \tr(\bA^\top\bB) = \sum_{ij}a_{ij}b_{ij}$ is the Frobenius inner product and $\|\bA\|_F = \langle\bA, \bA\rangle^{1/2}$ the Frobenius norm; $\|\bx\|$ is the Euclidean norm of a vector. The identity $\tr(\bA\bB) = \tr(\bB\bA)$ is used constantly. For symmetric matrices, $\bA \succeq \bB$ (respectively $\bA \succ \bB$) means that $\bA - \bB$ is positive semidefinite (positive definite).

本附录汇集全书用到的矩阵与统计知识。简短而有启发的证明完整给出，其余给出标准参考文献。所有矩阵都是实矩阵。对同样大小的矩阵，$\langle\bA, \bB\rangle = \tr(\bA^\top\bB) = \sum_{ij}a_{ij}b_{ij}$ 是 Frobenius 内积，$\|\bA\|_F = \langle\bA, \bA\rangle^{1/2}$ 是 Frobenius 范数；$\|\bx\|$ 是向量的欧氏范数。恒等式 $\tr(\bA\bB) = \tr(\bB\bA)$ 会反复用到。对对称矩阵，$\bA \succeq \bB$（或 $\bA \succ \bB$）表示 $\bA - \bB$ 半正定（或正定）。

Every square matrix splits as $\bM = \operatorname{sym}(\bM) + \operatorname{skew}(\bM)$, with symmetric part $\operatorname{sym}(\bM) = (\bM + \bM^\top)/2$ and skew-symmetric part $\operatorname{skew}(\bM) = (\bM - \bM^\top)/2$. The two parts are orthogonal: if $\bS$ is symmetric and $\bK$ is skew-symmetric, then $\tr(\bS\bK) = \tr\bigl((\bS\bK)^\top\bigr) = -\tr(\bK\bS) = -\tr(\bS\bK)$, so $\langle\bS, \bK\rangle = 0$. This simple fact underlies the gradients on the orthogonal group in .

每个方阵都可以分解为 $\bM = \operatorname{sym}(\bM) + \operatorname{skew}(\bM)$，其中对称部分为 $\operatorname{sym}(\bM) = (\bM + \bM^\top)/2$，反对称部分为 $\operatorname{skew}(\bM) = (\bM - \bM^\top)/2$。两部分相互正交：若 $\bS$ 对称、$\bK$ 反对称，则 $\tr(\bS\bK) = \tr\bigl((\bS\bK)^\top\bigr) = -\tr(\bK\bS) = -\tr(\bS\bK)$，所以 $\langle\bS, \bK\rangle = 0$。中正交群上的梯度就建立在这个简单事实之上。

Orthogonal and permutation matrices正交矩阵与置换矩阵

A square matrix $\bQ$ is orthogonal if $\bQ^\top\bQ = \bI$, equivalently $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$ or $\bQ^{-1} = \bQ^\top$. Orthogonal matrices preserve inner products, $(\bQ\bu)^\top(\bQ\bv) = \bu^\top\bv$, and hence lengths, angles and Frobenius norms: $\|\bQ\bM\|_F = \|\bM\bQ\|_F = \|\bM\|_F$. They form a group, the orthogonal group $O(n)$. Since $(\det\bQ)^2 = \det(\bQ^\top\bQ) = 1$, every orthogonal matrix has determinant $\pm 1$. Those with determinant $+1$ form the group $SO(n)$ of rotations; the others include reflections such as $\diag(1, \dots, 1, -1)$.

若方阵 $\bQ$ 满足 $\bQ^\top\bQ = \bI$（等价地，$\bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$，或 $\bQ^{-1} = \bQ^\top$），则称它是正交矩阵。正交矩阵保持内积 $(\bQ\bu)^\top(\bQ\bv) = \bu^\top\bv$，因而保持长度、夹角与 Frobenius 范数：$\|\bQ\bM\|_F = \|\bM\bQ\|_F = \|\bM\|_F$。它们构成一个群，即正交群 $O(n)$。由于 $(\det\bQ)^2 = \det(\bQ^\top\bQ) = 1$，正交矩阵的行列式为 $\pm 1$。行列式为 $+1$ 的正交矩阵构成旋转群 $SO(n)$；其余的包括 $\diag(1, \dots, 1, -1)$ 这样的反射。

$O(n)$ is a smooth manifold of dimension $n(n-1)/2$. Its tangent space at $\bQ$ is $\{\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ : \boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top = -\boldsymbol{\Omega}\}$, and for every skew-symmetric $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ the curve $t \mapsto \exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bQ$ lies in $O(n)$ and passes through $\bQ$ with velocity $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$.

$O(n)$ 是维数为 $n(n-1)/2$ 的光滑流形。它在 $\bQ$ 处的切空间为 $\{\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ : \boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top = -\boldsymbol{\Omega}\}$；对每个反对称矩阵 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$，曲线 $t \mapsto \exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bQ$ 落在 $O(n)$ 中，并以速度 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$ 经过 $\bQ$。

$O(n)$ is the level set $\{\bQ : \bQ\bQ^\top = \bI\}$ of the map $\bQ \mapsto \bQ\bQ^\top$, whose derivative $\dot\bQ \mapsto \dot\bQ\bQ^\top + \bQ\dot\bQ^\top$ maps onto the symmetric matrices (given a symmetric $\bS$, take $\dot\bQ = \tfrac12\bS\bQ$). By the regular value theorem, $O(n)$ is a smooth manifold whose tangent space at $\bQ$ is the kernel of this derivative. Writing $\dot\bQ = \boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$, the kernel condition becomes $\boldsymbol{\Omega} + \boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top = \bzero$. A skew-symmetric matrix is determined by its $n(n-1)/2$ entries above the diagonal, which gives the dimension. Finally, $\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \exp(-t\boldsymbol{\Omega})\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \bI$, and the derivative at $t = 0$ is $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$.

$O(n)$ 是映射 $\bQ \mapsto \bQ\bQ^\top$ 的水平集 $\{\bQ : \bQ\bQ^\top = \bI\}$，该映射的导数 $\dot\bQ \mapsto \dot\bQ\bQ^\top + \bQ\dot\bQ^\top$ 映满全体对称矩阵（对给定的对称矩阵 $\bS$，取 $\dot\bQ = \tfrac12\bS\bQ$ 即可）。由正则值定理，$O(n)$ 是光滑流形，它在 $\bQ$ 处的切空间就是这个导数的核。记 $\dot\bQ = \boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$，核的条件就变成 $\boldsymbol{\Omega} + \boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top = \bzero$。反对称矩阵由对角线以上的 $n(n-1)/2$ 个元素确定，由此得到维数。最后，$\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \exp(-t\boldsymbol{\Omega})\exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \bI$，且它在 $t = 0$ 处的导数为 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$。

For $n = 2$, $\exp\bigl(\theta\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\bigr) = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, the rotation by $\theta$: one parameter, as $n(n-1)/2 = 1$ predicts. In general every rotation is the exponential of a skew-symmetric matrix. An orthogonal change of basis $\bB$ brings any orthogonal $\bQ$ to a block-diagonal form with $2 \times 2$ blocks $\exp(\theta_k\bJ)$, $\bJ = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, and diagonal entries $\pm 1$ (Horn & Johnson, 2013). When $\det\bQ = 1$ the entries $-1$ come in pairs, each pair forming a block $\exp(\pi\bJ) = -\bI_2$, so $\bQ = \exp(\boldsymbol{\Omega})$ with the skew-symmetric matrix $\boldsymbol{\Omega} = \bB\,\diag(\theta_1\bJ, \dots, \theta_k\bJ, 0, \dots, 0)\,\bB^\top$. Conversely, $\det\exp(\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = e^{\tr\boldsymbol{\Omega}} = 1$.

当 $n = 2$ 时，$\exp\bigl(\theta\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\bigr) = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，即旋转 $\theta$ 角：只有一个参数，与 $n(n-1)/2 = 1$ 一致。一般地，每个旋转都是某个反对称矩阵的指数。通过正交基变换 $\bB$，任何正交矩阵 $\bQ$ 都可以化为分块对角形式，其中的 $2 \times 2$ 块为 $\exp(\theta_k\bJ)$，$\bJ = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，其余对角元为 $\pm 1$（Horn & Johnson, 2013）。当 $\det\bQ = 1$ 时，对角元 $-1$ 成对出现，每一对构成一个块 $\exp(\pi\bJ) = -\bI_2$，于是 $\bQ = \exp(\boldsymbol{\Omega})$，其中 $\boldsymbol{\Omega} = \bB\,\diag(\theta_1\bJ, \dots, \theta_k\bJ, 0, \dots, 0)\,\bB^\top$ 是反对称矩阵。反之，$\det\exp(\boldsymbol{\Omega}) = e^{\tr\boldsymbol{\Omega}} = 1$。

Givens rotations. The Givens rotation $\bG(i, j, \theta)$ equals the identity except for the entries $g_{ii} = g_{jj} = \cos\theta$, $g_{ij} = -\sin\theta$ and $g_{ji} = \sin\theta$; it rotates the $(i, j)$ coordinate plane by $\theta$ and leaves the other coordinates unchanged. There are $n(n-1)/2$ such planes, matching the dimension of $O(n)$. Every orthogonal matrix is a product of at most $n(n-1)/2$ Givens rotations and a diagonal matrix of signs: applying Givens rotations to zero the entries below the diagonal of $\bQ$ column by column, as in the Givens QR factorization (Golub & Van Loan, 2013), leaves an upper triangular orthogonal matrix, which must be diagonal with entries $\pm 1$. Jacobi-type algorithms such as JADE () search over $O(n)$ by sweeping through the planes and optimizing one angle at a time.

Givens 旋转。Givens 旋转 $\bG(i, j, \theta)$ 除 $g_{ii} = g_{jj} = \cos\theta$、$g_{ij} = -\sin\theta$、$g_{ji} = \sin\theta$ 之外与单位阵相同；它把 $(i, j)$ 坐标平面旋转 $\theta$ 角，而保持其余坐标不变。这样的平面共有 $n(n-1)/2$ 个，恰好等于 $O(n)$ 的维数。每个正交矩阵都是至多 $n(n-1)/2$ 个 Givens 旋转与一个符号对角矩阵的乘积：像用 Givens 旋转做 QR 分解那样（Golub & Van Loan, 2013），逐列消去 $\bQ$ 对角线以下的元素，剩下一个上三角的正交矩阵，它必然是对角元为 $\pm 1$ 的对角矩阵。JADE（）等 Jacobi 型算法在 $O(n)$ 中搜索的方式，就是依次扫过各个平面，每次只优化一个角度。

Permutation matrices. For a permutation $\pi$ of $\{1, \dots, n\}$, the permutation matrix $\bPi$ has entries $\pi_{ij} = 1$ if $i = \pi(j)$ and $0$ otherwise, so $(\bPi\bs)_{\pi(j)} = s_j$: it moves component $j$ to position $\pi(j)$. Permutation matrices are orthogonal, and so are the signed permutation matrices $\bPi\bD$ with $\bD = \diag(\pm 1)$. These are the only orthogonal scaled permutations.

置换矩阵。对 $\{1, \dots, n\}$ 的置换 $\pi$，置换矩阵 $\bPi$ 的元素为：当 $i = \pi(j)$ 时 $\pi_{ij} = 1$，否则为 $0$。于是 $(\bPi\bs)_{\pi(j)} = s_j$：它把第 $j$ 个分量移到第 $\pi(j)$ 个位置。置换矩阵是正交的，带符号的置换矩阵 $\bPi\bD$（$\bD = \diag(\pm 1)$）也是正交的，而且它们是仅有的正交的带尺度置换矩阵。

Let $\bPi$ be a permutation matrix and $\bD$ a diagonal matrix. Then $\bPi\bD$ is orthogonal if and only if $\bD = \diag(\pm 1)$. The signed permutation matrices form a subgroup of $O(n)$ with $2^n n!$ elements.

设 $\bPi$ 为置换矩阵，$\bD$ 为对角矩阵。则 $\bPi\bD$ 正交当且仅当 $\bD = \diag(\pm 1)$。带符号的置换矩阵构成 $O(n)$ 的一个子群，共有 $2^n n!$ 个元素。

$(\bPi\bD)^\top(\bPi\bD) = \bD\bPi^\top\bPi\bD = \bD^2$, which equals $\bI$ exactly when every $d_i^2 = 1$. For two signed permutations, $\bD\bPi' = \bPi'(\bPi'^\top\bD\bPi')$ and $\bPi'^\top\bD\bPi'$ is again a diagonal sign matrix, so the product $(\bPi\bD)(\bPi'\bD')$ is a signed permutation; so is the inverse $(\bPi\bD)^\top = \bPi^\top(\bPi\bD\bPi^\top)$. Distinct pairs $(\bPi, \bD)$ give distinct matrices, because the position of the nonzero entry in each column determines $\bPi$ and its sign determines $\bD$; hence there are $n!\cdot 2^n$ of them.

$(\bPi\bD)^\top(\bPi\bD) = \bD\bPi^\top\bPi\bD = \bD^2$，它等于 $\bI$ 当且仅当每个 $d_i^2 = 1$。对两个带符号置换矩阵，$\bD\bPi' = \bPi'(\bPi'^\top\bD\bPi')$，而 $\bPi'^\top\bD\bPi'$ 仍是对角符号矩阵，所以乘积 $(\bPi\bD)(\bPi'\bD')$ 仍是带符号置换矩阵；逆矩阵 $(\bPi\bD)^\top = \bPi^\top(\bPi\bD\bPi^\top)$ 也是。不同的 $(\bPi, \bD)$ 给出不同的矩阵，因为每列非零元的位置确定 $\bPi$，其符号确定 $\bD$；因此共有 $n!\cdot 2^n$ 个。

In ICA, separation means that the global matrix is a scaled permutation $\bPi\bD$ (). After whitening the global matrix is orthogonal (), and the lemma shows that the scaling is then reduced to signs: the solutions form the finite group of signed permutations.

在 ICA 中，分离意味着全局矩阵是带尺度的置换矩阵 $\bPi\bD$（）。白化之后全局矩阵是正交的（），由引理可知，此时尺度只剩下符号：所有解构成带符号置换矩阵这个有限群。

Symmetric matrices and matrix functions对称矩阵与矩阵函数

Spectral theorem. Every symmetric $\bC \in \R^{n \times n}$ can be written $\bC = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top = \sum_i \lambda_i\be_i\be_i^\top$, with $\mathbf{E}$ orthogonal with columns $\be_i$ and $\bLambda = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$ real (Horn & Johnson, 2013). The columns are orthonormal eigenvectors, $\bC\be_i = \lambda_i\be_i$. If the eigenvalues are distinct, each eigenvector is unique up to sign; for a repeated eigenvalue, any orthonormal basis of its eigenspace can be used.

谱定理。每个对称矩阵 $\bC \in \R^{n \times n}$ 都可以写成 $\bC = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top = \sum_i \lambda_i\be_i\be_i^\top$，其中 $\mathbf{E}$ 是以 $\be_i$ 为列的正交矩阵，$\bLambda = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$ 为实对角矩阵（Horn & Johnson, 2013）。各列是标准正交的特征向量，$\bC\be_i = \lambda_i\be_i$。若特征值互不相同，则每个特征向量在相差一个符号的意义下唯一；对重特征值，可以取其特征子空间的任意一组标准正交基。

Definiteness. $\bC$ is positive semidefinite, $\bC \succeq \bzero$, if $\bv^\top\bC\bv \ge 0$ for all $\bv$, equivalently if all $\lambda_i \ge 0$; it is positive definite, $\bC \succ \bzero$, if $\bv^\top\bC\bv > 0$ for all $\bv \ne \bzero$, equivalently if all $\lambda_i > 0$. Covariance matrices are positive semidefinite, since $\bv^\top\bC_{\bx}\bv = \Var(\bv^\top\bx)$. A positive definite matrix has a unique Cholesky factorization $\bC = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$ with $\mathbf{L}$ lower triangular with a positive diagonal (Golub & Van Loan, 2013).

正定性。若对所有 $\bv$ 都有 $\bv^\top\bC\bv \ge 0$，等价地，所有 $\lambda_i \ge 0$，则称 $\bC$ 半正定，记作 $\bC \succeq \bzero$；若对所有 $\bv \ne \bzero$ 都有 $\bv^\top\bC\bv > 0$，等价地，所有 $\lambda_i > 0$，则称 $\bC$ 正定，记作 $\bC \succ \bzero$。协方差矩阵是半正定的，因为 $\bv^\top\bC_{\bx}\bv = \Var(\bv^\top\bx)$。正定矩阵有唯一的 Cholesky 分解 $\bC = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$，其中 $\mathbf{L}$ 是对角元为正的下三角矩阵（Golub & Van Loan, 2013）。

Rayleigh quotient. For a unit vector $\bw$, $\bw^\top\bC\bw = \sum_i \lambda_i(\be_i^\top\bw)^2$ is an average of the eigenvalues with weights $(\be_i^\top\bw)^2$ that sum to one. Hence $\max_{\|\bw\| = 1}\bw^\top\bC\bw = \lambda_{\max}$ and $\min_{\|\bw\| = 1}\bw^\top\bC\bw = \lambda_{\min}$, attained at the corresponding eigenvectors. This variational characterization is the basis of PCA, and revisits it as an optimization problem on the sphere.

Rayleigh 商。对单位向量 $\bw$，$\bw^\top\bC\bw = \sum_i \lambda_i(\be_i^\top\bw)^2$ 是各特征值以 $(\be_i^\top\bw)^2$ 为权重的加权平均，权重之和为一。因此 $\max_{\|\bw\| = 1}\bw^\top\bC\bw = \lambda_{\max}$，$\min_{\|\bw\| = 1}\bw^\top\bC\bw = \lambda_{\min}$，分别在相应的特征向量处取到。这一变分刻画是 PCA 的基础，还会把它作为球面上的优化问题重新讨论。

Matrix functions. For a function $f$ defined on the eigenvalues, $f(\bC) = \mathbf{E}f(\bLambda)\mathbf{E}^\top = \sum_i f(\lambda_i)\be_i\be_i^\top$. The result does not depend on the choice of eigenvectors, because $f(\bC) = p(\bC)$ for any polynomial $p$ with $p(\lambda_i) = f(\lambda_i)$ for all $i$. Examples are powers, $\exp(\bC)$, $\log\bC$ for $\bC \succ \bzero$, and square roots. Matrix functions commute with orthogonal similarity: $f(\bQ\bC\bQ^\top) = \bQ f(\bC)\bQ^\top$.

矩阵函数。对定义在各特征值上的函数 $f$，令 $f(\bC) = \mathbf{E}f(\bLambda)\mathbf{E}^\top = \sum_i f(\lambda_i)\be_i\be_i^\top$。这个定义与特征向量的选取无关，因为对任何满足 $p(\lambda_i) = f(\lambda_i)$（对所有 $i$）的多项式 $p$，都有 $f(\bC) = p(\bC)$。例子有幂、$\exp(\bC)$、$\bC \succ \bzero$ 时的 $\log\bC$，以及平方根。矩阵函数与正交相似变换可交换：$f(\bQ\bC\bQ^\top) = \bQ f(\bC)\bQ^\top$。

Every positive semidefinite $\bC$ has a unique positive semidefinite square root, that is, a matrix $\bB \succeq \bzero$ with $\bB^2 = \bC$, namely $\bC^{1/2} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$. If $\bC \succ \bzero$, then $\bC^{-1/2} = (\bC^{1/2})^{-1} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$ and $\bC^{-1/2}\bC\,\bC^{-1/2} = \bI$.

每个半正定矩阵 $\bC$ 都有唯一的半正定平方根，即满足 $\bB^2 = \bC$ 的矩阵 $\bB \succeq \bzero$，它就是 $\bC^{1/2} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$。若 $\bC \succ \bzero$，则 $\bC^{-1/2} = (\bC^{1/2})^{-1} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$，且 $\bC^{-1/2}\bC\,\bC^{-1/2} = \bI$。

$\mathbf{E}\bLambda^{1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$ is positive semidefinite and its square is $\mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top = \bC$. For uniqueness, let $\bB \succeq \bzero$ with $\bB^2 = \bC$. Then $\bB$ commutes with $\bC = \bB^2$, so it maps each eigenspace of $\bC$ into itself: if $\bC\bv = \lambda\bv$, then $\bC(\bB\bv) = \bB\bC\bv = \lambda\bB\bv$. Restricted to the eigenspace of $\lambda$, $\bB$ is symmetric and positive semidefinite with square $\lambda\bI$, so its eigenvalues are nonnegative square roots of $\lambda$, and it equals $\sqrt\lambda\,\bI$ there. Hence $\bB = \sum_i \sqrt{\lambda_i}\,\be_i\be_i^\top$. The statements about $\bC^{-1/2}$ follow from the eigendecomposition.

$\mathbf{E}\bLambda^{1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$ 是半正定的，且其平方为 $\mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top = \bC$。再证唯一性：设 $\bB \succeq \bzero$ 且 $\bB^2 = \bC$。则 $\bB$ 与 $\bC = \bB^2$ 可交换，所以 $\bB$ 把 $\bC$ 的每个特征子空间映到自身：若 $\bC\bv = \lambda\bv$，则 $\bC(\bB\bv) = \bB\bC\bv = \lambda\bB\bv$。把 $\bB$ 限制在特征值 $\lambda$ 的特征子空间上，它是对称半正定的，且平方为 $\lambda\bI$，所以它的特征值都是 $\lambda$ 的非负平方根，从而它在该子空间上等于 $\sqrt\lambda\,\bI$。因此 $\bB = \sum_i \sqrt{\lambda_i}\,\be_i\be_i^\top$。关于 $\bC^{-1/2}$ 的结论由特征分解直接得到。

Non-symmetric square roots, that is, matrices $\bB$ with $\bB\bB^\top = \bC$ such as the Cholesky factor, are not unique. If $\bC \succ \bzero$ and $\bB\bB^\top = \bB'\bB'^\top = \bC$, then $\bQ = \bB^{-1}\bB'$ satisfies $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bB^{-1}\bC\bB^{-\top} = \bI$, so $\bB' = \bB\bQ$ with $\bQ$ orthogonal, and every such $\bB\bQ$ works. Inverting gives the whitening matrices of .

非对称的平方根，即满足 $\bB\bB^\top = \bC$ 的矩阵 $\bB$（例如 Cholesky 因子），并不唯一。若 $\bC \succ \bzero$ 且 $\bB\bB^\top = \bB'\bB'^\top = \bC$，则 $\bQ = \bB^{-1}\bB'$ 满足 $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bB^{-1}\bC\bB^{-\top} = \bI$，所以 $\bB' = \bB\bQ$，$\bQ$ 正交；反之每个这样的 $\bB\bQ$ 都满足要求。取逆即得中的白化矩阵。

The SVD and the polar decomposition奇异值分解与极分解

Every $\bW \in \R^{n \times m}$ has a singular value decomposition (SVD) $\bW = \mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top$, with $\mathbf{L} \in O(n)$, $\bR \in O(m)$, and $\bSigma \in \R^{n \times m}$ zero except for the diagonal entries $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots \ge 0$, the singular values (Golub & Van Loan, 2013). We call the factors $\mathbf{L}$ and $\bR$, for left and right, to keep $\bU$ and $\bV$ for the rotation and the whitening matrix of the main text. The $\sigma_i^2$ are the eigenvalues of $\bW\bW^\top$ and of $\bW^\top\bW$, whose eigenvectors are the columns of $\mathbf{L}$ and of $\bR$. A square $\bW$ is invertible exactly when all its singular values are positive.

每个 $\bW \in \R^{n \times m}$ 都有奇异值分解（SVD）$\bW = \mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top$，其中 $\mathbf{L} \in O(n)$，$\bR \in O(m)$，$\bSigma \in \R^{n \times m}$ 除对角元 $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots \ge 0$（即奇异值）外全为零（Golub & Van Loan, 2013）。我们把两个因子记作 $\mathbf{L}$ 与 $\bR$（取 left 与 right 之意），以便把 $\bU$ 与 $\bV$ 留给正文中的旋转与白化矩阵。$\sigma_i^2$ 是 $\bW\bW^\top$ 与 $\bW^\top\bW$ 的特征值，相应的特征向量分别是 $\mathbf{L}$ 与 $\bR$ 的各列。方阵 $\bW$ 可逆，当且仅当它的奇异值全为正。

Polar decomposition. An invertible square $\bW$ factors as

极分解。可逆方阵 $\bW$ 可以分解为

$$\bW = (\bW\bW^\top)^{1/2}\,\bQ_{\bW}, \qquad \bQ_{\bW} = (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\,\bW = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top,$$

where $(\bW\bW^\top)^{1/2} = \mathbf{L}\bSigma\mathbf{L}^\top$ is positive definite and $\bQ_{\bW}$ is orthogonal; indeed $(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW = \mathbf{L}\bSigma^{-1}\mathbf{L}^\top\mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$. This is the matrix analogue of writing a nonzero complex number as a modulus times a phase. The same orthogonal factor appears on the other side, $\bW = \bQ_{\bW}(\bW^\top\bW)^{1/2}$. A singular $\bW$ also has polar decompositions, but its orthogonal factor is not unique.

其中 $(\bW\bW^\top)^{1/2} = \mathbf{L}\bSigma\mathbf{L}^\top$ 正定，$\bQ_{\bW}$ 正交；事实上 $(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW = \mathbf{L}\bSigma^{-1}\mathbf{L}^\top\mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$。这相当于把非零复数写成模乘以相位的矩阵版本。同一个正交因子也出现在另一侧：$\bW = \bQ_{\bW}(\bW^\top\bW)^{1/2}$。奇异的 $\bW$ 也有极分解，但其正交因子不唯一。

Let $\bW \in \R^{n \times n}$ be invertible. Then $(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$ is the unique orthogonal matrix closest to $\bW$ in Frobenius norm: $\|\bW - \bQ\|_F > \|\bW - (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW\|_F$ for every other orthogonal $\bQ$.

设 $\bW \in \R^{n \times n}$ 可逆。则 $(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$ 是在 Frobenius 范数下离 $\bW$ 最近的唯一正交矩阵：对其他任何正交矩阵 $\bQ$，$\|\bW - \bQ\|_F > \|\bW - (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW\|_F$。

For orthogonal $\bQ$, $\|\bW - \bQ\|_F^2 = \|\bW\|_F^2 + n - 2\tr(\bQ^\top\bW)$, so we must maximize $\tr(\bQ^\top\bW) = \tr(\bQ^\top\mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top) = \tr(\bM\bSigma)$ with $\bM = \bR^\top\bQ^\top\mathbf{L}$, which is orthogonal. Then $\tr(\bM\bSigma) = \sum_i m_{ii}\sigma_i \le \sum_i \sigma_i$, because each column of $\bM$ is a unit vector and so $|m_{ii}| \le 1$. Since every $\sigma_i > 0$, equality holds if and only if $m_{ii} = 1$ for all $i$; a unit column with $i$-th entry 1 is the $i$-th column of the identity, so this means $\bM = \bI$, that is, $\bQ = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$. By , $\mathbf{L}\bR^\top = (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$.

对正交矩阵 $\bQ$，$\|\bW - \bQ\|_F^2 = \|\bW\|_F^2 + n - 2\tr(\bQ^\top\bW)$，所以问题化为最大化 $\tr(\bQ^\top\bW) = \tr(\bQ^\top\mathbf{L}\bSigma\bR^\top) = \tr(\bM\bSigma)$，其中 $\bM = \bR^\top\bQ^\top\mathbf{L}$ 是正交矩阵。于是 $\tr(\bM\bSigma) = \sum_i m_{ii}\sigma_i \le \sum_i \sigma_i$，因为 $\bM$ 的每一列都是单位向量，所以 $|m_{ii}| \le 1$。由于所有 $\sigma_i > 0$，等号成立当且仅当对所有 $i$ 都有 $m_{ii} = 1$；第 $i$ 个分量为 1 的单位列向量就是单位阵的第 $i$ 列，所以这意味着 $\bM = \bI$，即 $\bQ = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$。由 ，$\mathbf{L}\bR^\top = (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$。

Uses in ICA. Symmetric FastICA orthogonalizes its current estimate by $\bW \leftarrow (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$ (), which by the proposition is the orthogonal matrix closest to $\bW$. It treats all rows alike: since matrix functions commute with orthogonal similarity, replacing $\bW$ by $\bPi\bW$ replaces the result by $\bPi(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$, whereas Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization depends on the order of the rows. ZCA whitening produces the whitened mixing matrix $(\bA\bA^\top)^{-1/2}\bA$, the polar factor of $\bA$ (). Numerically, $\bQ_{\bW}$ is computed from the SVD, or by iterations such as Newton's iteration $\bX \leftarrow (\bX + \bX^{-\top})/2$ started at $\bX = \bW$ (Higham, 1986). Applied to $\bB\bA^\top$ in place of $\bW$, the same argument solves the orthogonal Procrustes problem $\min_{\bQ \in O(n)}\|\bQ\bA - \bB\|_F$.

在 ICA 中的应用。对称 FastICA 用 $\bW \leftarrow (\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$ 对当前估计作正交化（），由命题可知，这正是离 $\bW$ 最近的正交矩阵。它对各行一视同仁：由于矩阵函数与正交相似变换可交换，把 $\bW$ 换成 $\bPi\bW$，结果就变成 $\bPi(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW$；而 Gram–Schmidt 正交化的结果依赖于各行的次序。ZCA 白化给出的白化混合矩阵 $(\bA\bA^\top)^{-1/2}\bA$ 正是 $\bA$ 的极因子（）。数值上，$\bQ_{\bW}$ 可由 SVD 求得，也可以用迭代法计算，例如从 $\bX = \bW$ 出发的 Newton 迭代 $\bX \leftarrow (\bX + \bX^{-\top})/2$（Higham, 1986）。把 $\bW$ 换成 $\bB\bA^\top$，同样的论证还可以解决正交 Procrustes 问题 $\min_{\bQ \in O(n)}\|\bQ\bA - \bB\|_F$。

Kronecker products, vec and tensorsKronecker 积、vec 与张量

Kronecker products. For $\bA \in \R^{p \times q}$ and $\bB \in \R^{r \times s}$, the Kronecker product $\bA \otimes \bB \in \R^{pr \times qs}$ is the block matrix whose $(i, j)$ block is $a_{ij}\bB$. It satisfies $(\bA \otimes \bB)(\bC \otimes \bD) = \bA\bC \otimes \bB\bD$ whenever the products exist, $(\bA \otimes \bB)^\top = \bA^\top \otimes \bB^\top$, and $(\bA \otimes \bB)^{-1} = \bA^{-1} \otimes \bB^{-1}$. For square $\bA$ ($p \times p$) and $\bB$ ($r \times r$), the eigenvalues of $\bA \otimes \bB$ are the products of an eigenvalue of $\bA$ and an eigenvalue of $\bB$, so $\tr(\bA \otimes \bB) = \tr\bA\,\tr\bB$ and $\det(\bA \otimes \bB) = (\det\bA)^r(\det\bB)^p$. By the first two rules, the Kronecker product of orthogonal matrices is orthogonal.

Kronecker 积。对 $\bA \in \R^{p \times q}$ 与 $\bB \in \R^{r \times s}$，Kronecker 积 $\bA \otimes \bB \in \R^{pr \times qs}$ 是第 $(i, j)$ 块为 $a_{ij}\bB$ 的分块矩阵。它满足：只要乘积有意义，就有 $(\bA \otimes \bB)(\bC \otimes \bD) = \bA\bC \otimes \bB\bD$；$(\bA \otimes \bB)^\top = \bA^\top \otimes \bB^\top$；$(\bA \otimes \bB)^{-1} = \bA^{-1} \otimes \bB^{-1}$。对方阵 $\bA$（$p \times p$）与 $\bB$（$r \times r$），$\bA \otimes \bB$ 的特征值是 $\bA$ 的一个特征值与 $\bB$ 的一个特征值之积，所以 $\tr(\bA \otimes \bB) = \tr\bA\,\tr\bB$，$\det(\bA \otimes \bB) = (\det\bA)^r(\det\bB)^p$。由前两条规则，正交矩阵的 Kronecker 积仍是正交矩阵。

The vec operator. $\vecop(\bX)$ stacks the columns of $\bX$ into one long vector. The key identity is

vec 算子。$\vecop(\bX)$ 把 $\bX$ 的各列依次叠成一个长向量。关键的恒等式是

$$\vecop(\bA\bX\bB) = (\bB^\top \otimes \bA)\,\vecop(\bX).$$

To see it, note that column $k$ of $\bA\bX\bB$ is $\sum_j b_{jk}\bA\bx_j$, where $\bx_j$ is column $j$ of $\bX$; this is block $k$ of $(\bB^\top \otimes \bA)\vecop(\bX)$, whose $(k, j)$ block is $b_{jk}\bA$. Special cases are $\vecop(\ba\bb^\top) = \bb \otimes \ba$ and $\tr(\bA^\top\bB) = \vecop(\bA)^\top\vecop(\bB)$. In NumPy, which stores arrays by rows, $\vecop(\bX)$ is X.reshape(-1, order="F"); flattening by rows instead gives the identity with $\bA \otimes \bB^\top$ in place of $\bB^\top \otimes \bA$.

验证如下：$\bA\bX\bB$ 的第 $k$ 列为 $\sum_j b_{jk}\bA\bx_j$，其中 $\bx_j$ 是 $\bX$ 的第 $j$ 列；这正是 $(\bB^\top \otimes \bA)\vecop(\bX)$ 的第 $k$ 块，因为 $\bB^\top \otimes \bA$ 的第 $(k, j)$ 块为 $b_{jk}\bA$。特例有 $\vecop(\ba\bb^\top) = \bb \otimes \ba$ 与 $\tr(\bA^\top\bB) = \vecop(\bA)^\top\vecop(\bB)$。NumPy 按行存储数组，$\vecop(\bX)$ 对应 X.reshape(-1, order="F")；若按行展平，恒等式中的 $\bB^\top \otimes \bA$ 要换成 $\bA \otimes \bB^\top$。

Tensors. An order-$k$ tensor is a $k$-way array $\mathcal{T} = (t_{i_1 \dots i_k})$. The outer product of vectors $\ba, \bb, \mathbf{c}, \mathbf{d}$ is the order-4 tensor $\ba \circ \bb \circ \mathbf{c} \circ \mathbf{d}$ with entries $a_ib_jc_kd_l$; tensors of this form have rank one. A tensor is symmetric if its entries are invariant under permutations of the indices. A CP (canonical polyadic) decomposition writes $\mathcal{T} = \sum_{r=1}^R \lambda_r\,\ba_r \circ \bb_r \circ \mathbf{c}_r \circ \mathbf{d}_r$, and the smallest such $R$ is the CP rank. Tensor rank behaves differently from matrix rank. It can exceed every dimension of the array, computing it is NP-hard in general (Hillar & Lim, 2013), and the CP decomposition is often essentially unique, whereas a low-rank matrix factorization is unique only up to an invertible linear transformation (Kolda & Bader, 2009). Uniqueness conditions, such as Kruskal's, are discussed in .

张量。$k$ 阶张量是一个 $k$ 维数组 $\mathcal{T} = (t_{i_1 \dots i_k})$。向量 $\ba, \bb, \mathbf{c}, \mathbf{d}$ 的外积是四阶张量 $\ba \circ \bb \circ \mathbf{c} \circ \mathbf{d}$，其元素为 $a_ib_jc_kd_l$；这种形式的张量秩为一。若张量的元素在下标的任意置换下不变，则称它是对称的。CP（canonical polyadic）分解把张量写成 $\mathcal{T} = \sum_{r=1}^R \lambda_r\,\ba_r \circ \bb_r \circ \mathbf{c}_r \circ \mathbf{d}_r$，这样的 $R$ 的最小值称为 CP 秩。张量秩与矩阵秩的性质很不一样：它可以超过数组的每一维，一般情形下计算它是 NP 难的（Hillar & Lim, 2013），而且 CP 分解常常在本质上唯一，而低秩矩阵分解只在相差一个可逆线性变换的意义下唯一（Kolda & Bader, 2009）。Kruskal 条件等唯一性条件见。

Multilinear transformation. For a matrix $\bM$ with $n$ columns and an order-4 tensor $\mathcal{T}$ with all dimensions equal to $n$, define

多重线性变换。对有 $n$ 列的矩阵 $\bM$ 与各维都等于 $n$ 的四阶张量 $\mathcal{T}$，定义

$$(\mathcal{T} \times_1 \bM \times_2 \bM \times_3 \bM \times_4 \bM)_{ijkl} = \sum_{p,q,r,t} m_{ip}\,m_{jq}\,m_{kr}\,m_{lt}\,t_{pqrt}.$$

It maps the rank-one tensor $\ba \circ \ba \circ \ba \circ \ba$ to $(\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba)$, and for orthogonal $\bM$ it preserves the Frobenius norm $\|\mathcal{T}\|_F^2 = \sum_{ijkl}t_{ijkl}^2$ (). The fourth-order cumulant tensor $\mathcal{K}_{\bx}$ of a random vector, with entries $\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l)$, is symmetric, and by multilinearity () $\mathcal{K}_{\bM\bx} = \mathcal{K}_{\bx} \times_1 \bM \times_2 \bM \times_3 \bM \times_4 \bM$. For independent sources, $\mathcal{K}_{\bs}$ is diagonal, its only nonzero entries being $(\mathcal{K}_{\bs})_{pppp} = \kappa_p$. Hence, for $\bx = \bA\bs$,

它把秩一张量 $\ba \circ \ba \circ \ba \circ \ba$ 映为 $(\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba) \circ (\bM\ba)$；当 $\bM$ 正交时，它保持 Frobenius 范数 $\|\mathcal{T}\|_F^2 = \sum_{ijkl}t_{ijkl}^2$ 不变（）。随机向量的四阶累积量张量 $\mathcal{K}_{\bx}$ 以 $\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l)$ 为元素，它是对称张量；由多重线性（），$\mathcal{K}_{\bM\bx} = \mathcal{K}_{\bx} \times_1 \bM \times_2 \bM \times_3 \bM \times_4 \bM$。对独立的源，$\mathcal{K}_{\bs}$ 是对角张量，唯一的非零元为 $(\mathcal{K}_{\bs})_{pppp} = \kappa_p$。因此对 $\bx = \bA\bs$，

$$\mathcal{K}_{\bx} = \sum_{p=1}^n \kappa_p\,\ba_p \circ \ba_p \circ \ba_p \circ \ba_p,$$

a symmetric CP decomposition of rank at most $n$ whose factors are the columns of $\bA$; this is in tensor form. Arranging the entries as an $m^2 \times m^2$ matrix, with rows indexed by $(i, j)$ and columns by $(k, l)$, gives $\sum_p \kappa_p\,\vecop(\ba_p\ba_p^\top)\vecop(\ba_p\ba_p^\top)^\top$, a symmetric matrix of rank at most $n$. For whitened data the factors are orthogonal, which JADE exploits (); with more sources than sensors the rank exceeds the dimension, which only tensors allow ().

这是一个秩至多为 $n$ 的对称 CP 分解，其因子就是 $\bA$ 的各列；它正是 的张量形式。把各元素排成 $m^2 \times m^2$ 的矩阵，行以 $(i, j)$ 为指标、列以 $(k, l)$ 为指标，得到 $\sum_p \kappa_p\,\vecop(\ba_p\ba_p^\top)\vecop(\ba_p\ba_p^\top)^\top$，这是秩至多为 $n$ 的对称矩阵。对白化后的数据，这些因子相互正交，JADE 正是利用了这一点（）；当源多于传感器时，秩超过了维数，而这只有张量才允许（）。

Matrix calculus矩阵微积分

For a scalar function $f$ of a matrix $\bW \in \R^{n \times m}$, the gradient $\partial f/\partial\bW$ is the $n \times m$ matrix of partial derivatives $\partial f/\partial w_{ij}$. The most reliable way to compute it is through the differential: if $df = \tr(\bG^\top d\bW)$ for every direction $d\bW$, then $\partial f/\partial\bW = \bG$ (Magnus & Neudecker, 2019). The basic rules are

对矩阵 $\bW \in \R^{n \times m}$ 的标量函数 $f$，梯度 $\partial f/\partial\bW$ 是由偏导数 $\partial f/\partial w_{ij}$ 构成的 $n \times m$ 矩阵。最可靠的计算方法是通过微分：若对每个方向 $d\bW$ 都有 $df = \tr(\bG^\top d\bW)$，则 $\partial f/\partial\bW = \bG$（Magnus & Neudecker, 2019）。基本规则如下：

$$\begin{aligned} d\,\tr(\bA\bW) &= \tr(\bA\,d\bW), & \frac{\partial\,\tr(\bA\bW)}{\partial\bW} &= \bA^\top, \\[6pt] d(\ba^\top\bW\bb) &= \tr(\bb\ba^\top d\bW), & \frac{\partial\,(\ba^\top\bW\bb)}{\partial\bW} &= \ba\bb^\top, \\[6pt] d\,\tr(\bW^\top\bM\bW) &= \tr\bigl(\bW^\top(\bM + \bM^\top)\,d\bW\bigr), & \frac{\partial\,\tr(\bW^\top\bM\bW)}{\partial\bW} &= (\bM + \bM^\top)\bW, \\[6pt] d(\bW^{-1}) &= -\bW^{-1}(d\bW)\,\bW^{-1}. \end{aligned}$$

The last rule follows by differentiating $\bW\bW^{-1} = \bI$. The determinant, which brings the Jacobian of $\by = \bW\bx$ into every likelihood and entropy of this book, has a particularly simple gradient.

最后一条由对 $\bW\bW^{-1} = \bI$ 求微分得到。行列式把变换 $\by = \bW\bx$ 的 Jacobi 因子带进了本书中的每个似然与熵，而它的梯度格外简单。

For invertible $\bW$, $d\log|\det\bW| = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW)$, hence $\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$.

对可逆的 $\bW$，$d\log|\det\bW| = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW)$，因此 $\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$。

$\det(\bW + d\bW) = \det\bW\,\det(\bI + \bW^{-1}d\bW)$, and $\det(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}) = 1 + \tr\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O(\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}\|^2)$: in the expansion of the determinant, the product of the diagonal entries contributes $1 + \tr\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O(\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}\|^2)$, and every other term contains at least two off-diagonal entries of $\boldsymbol{\Delta}$. Hence $\log|\det(\bW + d\bW)| = \log|\det\bW| + \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) + O(\|d\bW\|^2)$. Writing $\tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) = \tr\bigl((\bW^{-\top})^\top d\bW\bigr)$ identifies the gradient.

$\det(\bW + d\bW) = \det\bW\,\det(\bI + \bW^{-1}d\bW)$，而 $\det(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}) = 1 + \tr\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O(\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}\|^2)$：在行列式的展开式中，对角元之积贡献 $1 + \tr\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O(\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}\|^2)$，其余每一项都至少含有 $\boldsymbol{\Delta}$ 的两个非对角元。因此 $\log|\det(\bW + d\bW)| = \log|\det\bW| + \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) + O(\|d\bW\|^2)$。把 $\tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW)$ 写成 $\tr\bigl((\bW^{-\top})^\top d\bW\bigr)$，即得梯度。

Let $f(\bW) = \log|\det\bW| + \sum_i \E[\log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx)]$, where $\bw_i^\top$ is row $i$ of $\bW$, $\by = \bW\bx$, and $\psi_i = -p_i'/p_i$ is the score function of source $i$. Then $df = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) - \E\bigl[\sum_i \psi_i(y_i)\,d\bw_i^\top\bx\bigr] = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) - \tr\bigl(\E[\bx\bpsi(\by)^\top]\,d\bW\bigr)$, so

设 $f(\bW) = \log|\det\bW| + \sum_i \E[\log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx)]$，其中 $\bw_i^\top$ 是 $\bW$ 的第 $i$ 行，$\by = \bW\bx$，$\psi_i = -p_i'/p_i$ 是源 $i$ 的得分函数。则 $df = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) - \E\bigl[\sum_i \psi_i(y_i)\,d\bw_i^\top\bx\bigr] = \tr(\bW^{-1}d\bW) - \tr\bigl(\E[\bx\bpsi(\by)^\top]\,d\bW\bigr)$，所以

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial\bW} = \bW^{-\top} - \E\bigl[\bpsi(\by)\,\bx^\top\bigr], \qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial\bW}\,\bW^\top\bW = \bigl(\bI - \E[\bpsi(\by)\,\by^\top]\bigr)\bW.$$

The second expression, the gradient multiplied on the right by the positive definite matrix $\bW^\top\bW$, is the relative or natural gradient of ; it is still an ascent direction, because $\langle\bG, \bG\bW^\top\bW\rangle = \|\bG\bW^\top\|_F^2 \ge 0$ for $\bG = \partial f/\partial\bW$.

第二个表达式，即右乘正定矩阵 $\bW^\top\bW$ 之后的梯度，就是中的相对梯度（自然梯度）；它仍是上升方向，因为对 $\bG = \partial f/\partial\bW$ 有 $\langle\bG, \bG\bW^\top\bW\rangle = \|\bG\bW^\top\|_F^2 \ge 0$。

Formulas like these are easy to get wrong by a transpose, and easy to check. The script below verifies , by central differences, and against 20,000 random orthogonal matrices drawn uniformly from $O(4)$.

这类公式很容易在转置上出错，也很容易检验。下面的脚本验证 ，用中心差分验证，并用从 $O(4)$ 中均匀抽取的 20000 个随机正交矩阵检验。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)

# vec(AXB) = (B^T kron A) vec(X), with vec stacking columns
A, X, B = (rng.standard_normal(s) for s in [(3, 4), (4, 5), (5, 2)])
vec = lambda M: M.reshape(-1, order="F")
print(np.allclose(vec(A @ X @ B), np.kron(B.T, A) @ vec(X)))

# gradient of log|det W| by central differences
W, h = rng.standard_normal((4, 4)), 1e-6
logdet = lambda M: np.linalg.slogdet(M)[1]
G = np.zeros((4, 4))
for i in range(4):
    for j in range(4):
        D = np.zeros((4, 4)); D[i, j] = h
        G[i, j] = (logdet(W + D) - logdet(W - D)) / (2 * h)
print(f"{np.abs(G - np.linalg.inv(W).T).max():.1e}")

# closest orthogonal matrix: L R^T = (W W^T)^(-1/2) W
L, sv, Rt = np.linalg.svd(W)
d, E = np.linalg.eigh(W @ W.T)
print(np.allclose(L @ Rt, E @ np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T @ W))
def random_orthogonal(n):                 # uniformly distributed on O(n)
    Q, R = np.linalg.qr(rng.standard_normal((n, n)))
    return Q * np.sign(np.diag(R))
dist = min(np.linalg.norm(W - random_orthogonal(4)) for _ in range(20000))
print(f"{np.linalg.norm(W - L @ Rt):.3f} {dist:.3f}")

It prints True, a finite-difference error of 5.7e-08, True, and the distances 2.886 from $\bW$ to its polar factor and 3.047 to the best of the random orthogonal matrices.

Optimization on the sphere and the orthogonal group球面与正交群上的优化

Many ICA contrasts are optimized under constraints. One-unit methods search over unit vectors $\bw$ (, ), and methods for whitened data search over orthogonal matrices. Both constraint sets are smooth manifolds, and the same three ingredients recur: first-order (Lagrange) conditions, the Riemannian gradient, and retractions, which map a step taken in the tangent space back onto the manifold (Edelman, Arias & Smith, 1998; Absil, Mahony & Sepulchre, 2008).

许多 ICA 对比函数都是在约束下优化的。单单元方法在单位向量 $\bw$ 中搜索（，），针对白化数据的方法则在正交矩阵中搜索。这两个约束集都是光滑流形，反复出现的是同样三样东西：一阶（拉格朗日）条件、黎曼梯度，以及把在切空间中迈出的一步拉回流形的收缩映射（retraction）（Edelman, Arias & Smith, 1998; Absil, Mahony & Sepulchre, 2008）。

Let $f$ be twice continuously differentiable on $\R^n$, let $\bw$ be a unit vector, and write $\lambda = \bw^\top\nabla f(\bw)$. (a) If $\bw$ is a local maximum or minimum of $f$ on the unit sphere, then $\nabla f(\bw) = \lambda\bw$; equivalently, the Riemannian gradient $(\bI - \bw\bw^\top)\nabla f(\bw)$ vanishes. (b) At such a critical point, if $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv < 0$ for every nonzero $\bv$ with $\bw^\top\bv = 0$, then $\bw$ is a strict local maximum on the sphere; if $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv > 0$ for some such $\bv$, it is not a local maximum.

设 $f$ 在 $\R^n$ 上二阶连续可微，$\bw$ 是单位向量，记 $\lambda = \bw^\top\nabla f(\bw)$。（a）若 $\bw$ 是 $f$ 在单位球面上的局部极大点或极小点，则 $\nabla f(\bw) = \lambda\bw$；等价地，黎曼梯度 $(\bI - \bw\bw^\top)\nabla f(\bw)$ 为零。（b）在这样的临界点处，若对每个满足 $\bw^\top\bv = 0$ 的非零 $\bv$ 都有 $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv < 0$，则 $\bw$ 是球面上的严格局部极大点；若对某个这样的 $\bv$ 有 $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv > 0$，则它不是局部极大点。

For a unit vector $\bv \perp \bw$, the curve $\bw(t) = \cos t\,\bw + \sin t\,\bv$ stays on the sphere, with $\bw(0) = \bw$, $\bw'(0) = \bv$ and $\bw''(0) = -\bw$. Hence the first derivative of $f(\bw(t))$ at $t = 0$ is $\nabla f(\bw)^\top\bv$, and the second is $\bv^\top\nabla^2 f(\bw)\bv - \nabla f(\bw)^\top\bw$. At a local extremum the first derivative vanishes for every tangent $\bv$, so $\nabla f(\bw)$ is parallel to $\bw$, which is (a). At a critical point the second derivative equals $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv$. If it is positive for some $\bv$, $f$ increases along that curve, so $\bw$ is not a local maximum. Every point of the sphere near $\bw$ has the form $\bw(t)$ for a small $t$ and some unit $\bv \perp \bw$, and by Taylor's theorem $f(\bw(t)) = f(\bw) + \tfrac{t^2}{2}\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv + o(t^2)$ uniformly in $\bv$, because the unit tangent vectors form a compact set; a negative definite form therefore gives $f(\bw(t)) < f(\bw)$ for small $t \ne 0$.

对与 $\bw$ 正交的单位向量 $\bv$，曲线 $\bw(t) = \cos t\,\bw + \sin t\,\bv$ 始终在球面上，且 $\bw(0) = \bw$，$\bw'(0) = \bv$，$\bw''(0) = -\bw$。因此 $f(\bw(t))$ 在 $t = 0$ 处的一阶导数为 $\nabla f(\bw)^\top\bv$，二阶导数为 $\bv^\top\nabla^2 f(\bw)\bv - \nabla f(\bw)^\top\bw$。在局部极值点，一阶导数对每个切向量 $\bv$ 都为零，所以 $\nabla f(\bw)$ 与 $\bw$ 平行，这就是（a）。在临界点处，二阶导数等于 $\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv$。若它对某个 $\bv$ 为正，则 $f$ 沿这条曲线增大，所以 $\bw$ 不是局部极大点。球面上靠近 $\bw$ 的每一点都可以写成 $\bw(t)$ 的形式（$t$ 很小，$\bv \perp \bw$ 为单位向量）；由于单位切向量构成紧集，由 Taylor 定理，$f(\bw(t)) = f(\bw) + \tfrac{t^2}{2}\bv^\top(\nabla^2 f(\bw) - \lambda\bI)\bv + o(t^2)$ 关于 $\bv$ 一致成立，因此当这个二次型负定时，对很小的 $t \ne 0$ 有 $f(\bw(t)) < f(\bw)$。

For example, $f(\bw) = \bw^\top\bC\bw$ with $\bC$ symmetric has $\nabla f = 2\bC\bw$ and $\nabla^2 f = 2\bC$, so its critical points on the sphere are the unit eigenvectors, with $\lambda = 2\lambda_k$ at $\be_k$. Suppose $\lambda_1 > \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n$. At $\be_1$, the form $2\bv^\top(\bC - \lambda_1\bI)\bv$ is negative for every nonzero $\bv \perp \be_1$, so $\be_1$ is a strict local maximum. At $\be_k$ with $k \ge 2$, the tangent direction $\bv = \be_1$ gives $2(\lambda_1 - \lambda_k) > 0$, so $\be_k$ is not a local maximum. The only local maxima are $\pm\be_1$: PCA has no spurious local optima. carries out the same analysis for kurtosis ().

举例来说，对对称矩阵 $\bC$，$f(\bw) = \bw^\top\bC\bw$ 有 $\nabla f = 2\bC\bw$ 与 $\nabla^2 f = 2\bC$，所以它在球面上的临界点就是单位特征向量，在 $\be_k$ 处 $\lambda = 2\lambda_k$。设 $\lambda_1 > \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n$。在 $\be_1$ 处，二次型 $2\bv^\top(\bC - \lambda_1\bI)\bv$ 对每个与 $\be_1$ 正交的非零 $\bv$ 都为负，所以 $\be_1$ 是严格局部极大点。在 $\be_k$（$k \ge 2$）处，取切方向 $\bv = \be_1$ 得 $2(\lambda_1 - \lambda_k) > 0$，所以 $\be_k$ 不是局部极大点。唯一的局部极大点是 $\pm\be_1$：PCA 没有虚假的局部最优。对峭度作了同样的分析（）。

The vector $(\bI - \bw\bw^\top)\nabla f(\bw)$, the projection of the gradient onto the tangent space $\{\bv : \bw^\top\bv = 0\}$, is the Riemannian gradient of $f$ on the sphere. Gradient ascent takes a step $\mu\bv$ along it and maps back by normalization, $\bw \leftarrow (\bw + \mu\bv)/\|\bw + \mu\bv\|$, the simplest retraction; the normalization step of FastICA has the same form.

向量 $(\bI - \bw\bw^\top)\nabla f(\bw)$ 是梯度在切空间 $\{\bv : \bw^\top\bv = 0\}$ 上的投影，称为 $f$ 在球面上的黎曼梯度。梯度上升沿它走一步 $\mu\bv$，再通过归一化拉回球面：$\bw \leftarrow (\bw + \mu\bv)/\|\bw + \mu\bv\|$，这是最简单的收缩映射；FastICA 的归一化步骤也是这种形式。

正交群。设 $f$ 是 $\bU \in \R^{n \times n}$ 的光滑函数，欧氏梯度为 $\bG = \partial f/\partial\bU$。由，$O(n)$ 在 $\bU$ 处的切方向为 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bU$（$\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 反对称），$f$ 沿 $t \mapsto \exp(t\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bU$ 在 $t = 0$ 处的导数为 $\langle\bG, \boldsymbol{\Omega}\bU\rangle = \langle\bG\bU^\top, \boldsymbol{\Omega}\rangle = \langle\operatorname{skew}(\bG\bU^\top), \boldsymbol{\Omega}\rangle$，因为 $\bG\bU^\top$ 的对称部分与 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 正交（）。因此

$$\operatorname{grad} f(\bU) = \operatorname{skew}(\bG\bU^\top)\,\bU$$

is the Riemannian gradient, and $\bU$ is a critical point of $f$ on $O(n)$ if and only if $\bG\bU^\top$ is symmetric. This is the Lagrange condition for the constraint $\bU\bU^\top = \bI$, whose multiplier is a symmetric matrix $\bLambda$ with $\bG = \bLambda\bU$. Retractions include the matrix exponential $\bU \leftarrow \exp(\mu\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bU$, the Cayley transform $\bU \leftarrow (\bI - \tfrac{\mu}{2}\boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \tfrac{\mu}{2}\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bU$ (), and the polar factor of $\bU + \mu\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bU$, that is, symmetric orthogonalization (). 就是黎曼梯度；$\bU$ 是 $f$ 在 $O(n)$ 上的临界点，当且仅当 $\bG\bU^\top$ 对称。这正是约束 $\bU\bU^\top = \bI$ 下的拉格朗日条件，其乘子是满足 $\bG = \bLambda\bU$ 的对称矩阵 $\bLambda$。常用的收缩映射有矩阵指数 $\bU \leftarrow \exp(\mu\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bU$、Cayley 变换 $\bU \leftarrow (\bI - \tfrac{\mu}{2}\boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \tfrac{\mu}{2}\boldsymbol{\Omega})\bU$（），以及 $\bU + \mu\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bU$ 的极因子，即对称正交化（）。在二维情形，它们都只是改变旋转角。

Asymptotic statistics渐近统计

Part IV and several other chapters make statements about large samples. We write $X_T = O_P(r_T)$ if $X_T/r_T$ is bounded in probability, that is, for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is an $M$ with $P(|X_T/r_T| > M) < \varepsilon$ for all $T$, and $X_T = o_P(r_T)$ if $X_T/r_T \to 0$ in probability. By the central limit theorem, the average of $T$ i.i.d. random vectors with mean $\bmu$ and covariance $\bSigma$ satisfies $\sqrt T(\bar\bx - \bmu) \to \Ncal(\bzero, \bSigma)$ in distribution, so $\bar\bx - \bmu = O_P(T^{-1/2})$. Slutsky's lemma allows us to add $o_P(1)$ terms to a sequence that converges in distribution, or to multiply it by a sequence that converges in probability to a constant, without changing the limit beyond the obvious (van der Vaart, 1998).

第四部分以及其他若干章都涉及大样本下的结论。若 $X_T/r_T$ 依概率有界，即对任意 $\varepsilon > 0$，存在 $M$ 使对所有 $T$ 都有 $P(|X_T/r_T| > M) < \varepsilon$，就记 $X_T = O_P(r_T)$；若 $X_T/r_T$ 依概率收敛到 0，就记 $X_T = o_P(r_T)$。由中心极限定理，$T$ 个均值为 $\bmu$、协方差为 $\bSigma$ 的独立同分布随机向量的平均满足 $\sqrt T(\bar\bx - \bmu) \to \Ncal(\bzero, \bSigma)$（依分布），所以 $\bar\bx - \bmu = O_P(T^{-1/2})$。Slutsky 引理允许我们给依分布收敛的序列加上 $o_P(1)$ 项，或乘以依概率收敛到常数的序列，而极限只发生显然的变化（van der Vaart, 1998）。

Let $\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta) \to \Ncal(\bzero, \bSigma)$ in distribution, and let $g$ be a real function differentiable at $\btheta$. Then $\sqrt T\bigl(g(\hat\btheta_T) - g(\btheta)\bigr) \to \Ncal\bigl(0,\ \nabla g(\btheta)^\top\bSigma\,\nabla g(\btheta)\bigr)$. For a vector-valued $g$, replace $\nabla g(\btheta)^\top$ by the Jacobian matrix of $g$ at $\btheta$.

设 $\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta)$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(\bzero, \bSigma)$，$g$ 是在 $\btheta$ 处可微的实函数。则 $\sqrt T\bigl(g(\hat\btheta_T) - g(\btheta)\bigr) \to \Ncal\bigl(0,\ \nabla g(\btheta)^\top\bSigma\,\nabla g(\btheta)\bigr)$。若 $g$ 取向量值，把 $\nabla g(\btheta)^\top$ 换成 $g$ 在 $\btheta$ 处的 Jacobi 矩阵即可。

Differentiability gives $g(\hat\btheta_T) - g(\btheta) = \nabla g(\btheta)^\top(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta) + o_P(\|\hat\btheta_T - \btheta\|)$, and $\|\hat\btheta_T - \btheta\| = O_P(T^{-1/2})$. Multiply by $\sqrt T$: the remainder becomes $o_P(1)$, and the linear term converges to $\Ncal(0, \nabla g^\top\bSigma\nabla g)$. Slutsky's lemma completes the proof.

由可微性，$g(\hat\btheta_T) - g(\btheta) = \nabla g(\btheta)^\top(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta) + o_P(\|\hat\btheta_T - \btheta\|)$，且 $\|\hat\btheta_T - \btheta\| = O_P(T^{-1/2})$。乘以 $\sqrt T$ 后，余项变为 $o_P(1)$，线性项收敛到 $\Ncal(0, \nabla g^\top\bSigma\nabla g)$。由 Slutsky 引理即得结论。

Let $y$ have zero mean and moments $\mu_j = \E[y^j]$ up to order 8, and let $\hat k = \hat m_4 - 3\hat m_2^2$ with $\hat m_j = \frac1T\sum_t y(t)^j$. M-estimators. Most ICA estimators are defined as solutions of estimating equations, such as $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\,y_j] = 0$ for $i \ne j$ in , with the expectation replaced by a sample average. Their asymptotic covariance has a standard form.

M 估计量。大多数 ICA 估计量都定义为估计方程的解，例如中的 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\,y_j] = 0$（$i \ne j$），其中期望换成样本平均。它们的渐近协方差有标准的形式。

Let $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$ be i.i.d., and let $\hat\btheta_T$ solve $\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta) = \bzero$, where $\E[\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)] = \bzero$. Assume that $\hat\btheta_T \to \btheta_0$ in probability, that $\mathbf{h}$ is twice continuously differentiable in $\btheta$ with first and second derivatives dominated near $\btheta_0$ by an integrable function of $\bx$, that $\bH = \E[\partial\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)/\partial\btheta^\top]$ is invertible, and that $\bB = \E[\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)\,\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)^\top]$ is finite. Then $\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0) \to \Ncal(\bzero,\ \bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top})$.

设 $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$ 独立同分布，$\hat\btheta_T$ 是方程 $\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta) = \bzero$ 的解，且 $\E[\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)] = \bzero$。假设 $\hat\btheta_T$ 依概率收敛到 $\btheta_0$；$\mathbf{h}$ 关于 $\btheta$ 二阶连续可微，且其一阶与二阶导数在 $\btheta_0$ 附近被某个关于 $\bx$ 可积的函数控制；$\bH = \E[\partial\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)/\partial\btheta^\top]$ 可逆；$\bB = \E[\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)\,\mathbf{h}(\bx, \btheta_0)^\top]$ 有限。则 $\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0) \to \Ncal(\bzero,\ \bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top})$。

(Sketch.) A Taylor expansion of the estimating equations around $\btheta_0$ gives $\bzero = \frac1T\sum_t \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta_0) + \hat\bH_T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0)$, where $\hat\bH_T$ is an average of derivatives evaluated at points between $\hat\btheta_T$ and $\btheta_0$; by the law of large numbers and the domination condition, $\hat\bH_T \to \bH$ in probability. Hence $\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0) = -\hat\bH_T^{-1}\,T^{-1/2}\sum_t \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta_0)$ with probability tending to one, and the central limit theorem and Slutsky's lemma give the limit. （证明概要。）把估计方程在 $\btheta_0$ 附近作 Taylor 展开，得 $\bzero = \frac1T\sum_t \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta_0) + \hat\bH_T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0)$，其中 $\hat\bH_T$ 是在 $\hat\btheta_T$ 与 $\btheta_0$ 之间的点处取值的导数的平均；由大数定律与控制条件，$\hat\bH_T$ 依概率收敛到 $\bH$。因此，在概率趋于一的事件上，$\sqrt T(\hat\btheta_T - \btheta_0) = -\hat\bH_T^{-1}\,T^{-1/2}\sum_t \mathbf{h}(\bx(t), \btheta_0)$，再由中心极限定理与 Slutsky 引理即得极限。细节以及更弱的条件见 van der Vaart（1998）第 5 章。

The covariance $\bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top}$ is called the sandwich. evaluates it for ICA estimators (), and works out the simplest case, the estimation of a location parameter.

协方差 $\bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top}$ 称为三明治（sandwich）协方差。对 ICA 估计量计算了它（），则处理最简单的情形，即位置参数的估计。

Fisher information and the Cramér–Rao bound. For a family of densities $p(\bx; \btheta)$, the Fisher information is $\mathcal{I}(\btheta) = \E\bigl[\nabla_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)\,\nabla_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)^\top\bigr]$, which equals $-\E[\nabla^2_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)]$ under regularity conditions. The Cramér–Rao bound states that, under regularity conditions, every unbiased estimator based on $T$ i.i.d. observations satisfies $\Cov(\hat\btheta) \succeq (T\,\mathcal{I}(\btheta))^{-1}$; the maximum-likelihood estimator is asymptotically normal with covariance $\mathcal{I}(\btheta)^{-1}/T$, so it attains the bound asymptotically (Lehmann & Casella, 1998; van der Vaart, 1998). In the sandwich formula, maximum likelihood takes $\mathbf{h}$ to be the score, so that $\bH = -\mathcal{I}$, $\bB = \mathcal{I}$ and $\bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top} = \mathcal{I}^{-1}$. For ICA, derives the bound for the gain matrix ().

Fisher 信息与 Cramér–Rao 界。对密度族 $p(\bx; \btheta)$，Fisher 信息定义为 $\mathcal{I}(\btheta) = \E\bigl[\nabla_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)\,\nabla_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)^\top\bigr]$，在正则条件下它等于 $-\E[\nabla^2_{\btheta}\log p(\bx; \btheta)]$。Cramér–Rao 界是说：在正则条件下，基于 $T$ 个独立同分布观测的任何无偏估计量都满足 $\Cov(\hat\btheta) \succeq (T\,\mathcal{I}(\btheta))^{-1}$；最大似然估计量渐近正态，协方差为 $\mathcal{I}(\btheta)^{-1}/T$，所以它渐近地达到这个界（Lehmann & Casella, 1998; van der Vaart, 1998）。在三明治公式中，最大似然取 $\mathbf{h}$ 为得分，于是 $\bH = -\mathcal{I}$，$\bB = \mathcal{I}$，$\bH^{-1}\bB\bH^{-\top} = \mathcal{I}^{-1}$。对 ICA，推导了增益矩阵的这个界（）。

For a location family $p(x - \theta)$, the score with respect to $\theta$ is $-p'(x - \theta)/p(x - \theta) = \psi(x - \theta)$, where $\psi = -p'/p$ is the score function in the book's notation, and the Fisher information is $\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2]$. For the standard Gaussian, $\psi(u) = u$ and $\mathcal{J} = 1$; for the unit-variance Laplace law, $\psi(u) = \sqrt2\,\sign(u)$ and $\mathcal{J} = 2$. Among laws of a given variance, the Gaussian carries the least information.

对位置族 $p(x - \theta)$，关于 $\theta$ 的得分为 $-p'(x - \theta)/p(x - \theta) = \psi(x - \theta)$，其中 $\psi = -p'/p$ 即本书记号中的得分函数，Fisher 信息为 $\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2]$。对标准高斯分布，$\psi(u) = u$，$\mathcal{J} = 1$；对单位方差的拉普拉斯分布，$\psi(u) = \sqrt2\,\sign(u)$，$\mathcal{J} = 2$。在方差给定的分布中，高斯分布携带的信息最少。

Let $s$ have zero mean, unit variance and a continuously differentiable density $p > 0$ with score $\psi = -p'/p$, $\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2] < \infty$, and $u\,p(u) \to 0$ as $|u| \to \infty$. Then $\mathcal{J} \ge 1$, with equality if and only if $s \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$.

设 $s$ 均值为零、方差为一，具有连续可微的密度 $p > 0$，其得分为 $\psi = -p'/p$，$\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2] < \infty$，并且当 $|u| \to \infty$ 时 $u\,p(u) \to 0$。则 $\mathcal{J} \ge 1$，等号成立当且仅当 $s \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$。

Integrating by parts, $\E[s\,\psi(s)] = -\int u\,p'(u)\,du = \bigl[-u\,p(u)\bigr]_{-\infty}^{\infty} + \int p(u)\,du = 1$; the integral converges absolutely because $\E|s\,\psi(s)| \le (\E[s^2]\,\mathcal{J})^{1/2}$. By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, $1 = (\E[s\,\psi(s)])^2 \le \E[s^2]\,\E[\psi(s)^2] = \mathcal{J}$. Equality holds if and only if $\psi(s) = c\,s$ almost surely for a constant $c$; since $\psi$ is continuous and $p > 0$, this means $\psi(u) = cu$ for all $u$, so $(\log p)' = -cu$ and $p$ is a Gaussian density with variance $1/c$. Unit variance forces $c = 1$.

分部积分得 $\E[s\,\psi(s)] = -\int u\,p'(u)\,du = \bigl[-u\,p(u)\bigr]_{-\infty}^{\infty} + \int p(u)\,du = 1$；由于 $\E|s\,\psi(s)| \le (\E[s^2]\,\mathcal{J})^{1/2}$，这个积分绝对收敛。由 Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式，$1 = (\E[s\,\psi(s)])^2 \le \E[s^2]\,\E[\psi(s)^2] = \mathcal{J}$。等号成立当且仅当存在常数 $c$ 使 $\psi(s) = c\,s$ 几乎必然成立；由于 $\psi$ 连续且 $p > 0$，这意味着对所有 $u$ 有 $\psi(u) = cu$，于是 $(\log p)' = -cu$，$p$ 是方差为 $1/c$ 的高斯密度。单位方差迫使 $c = 1$。

The same integration by parts applies to absolutely continuous densities such as the Laplace density, so the inequality holds for them too. Consequently, for two unit-variance sources $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j \ge 1$, with equality only when both are Gaussian, which is why the conditions $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j > 1$ in and fail only for pairs of Gaussian sources.

同样的分部积分也适用于拉普拉斯密度这样的绝对连续密度，所以不等式对它们同样成立。因此，对两个单位方差的源有 $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j \ge 1$，仅当二者都是高斯的时取等号；这就是为什么与中的条件 $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j > 1$ 只对一对高斯源才不成立。

  • $O(n)$ has dimension $n(n-1)/2$, and its tangent directions at $\bQ$ are $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$ with $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ skew-symmetric.   • A positive semidefinite matrix has a unique positive semidefinite square root, $\bC^{1/2} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$; non-symmetric square roots differ by orthogonal factors.
  • The polar factor $(\bW\bW^\top)^{-1/2}\bW = \mathbf{L}\bR^\top$ is the orthogonal matrix closest to $\bW$; it is what symmetric orthogonalization computes.
  • $\vecop(\bA\bX\bB) = (\bB^\top \otimes \bA)\vecop(\bX)$. Cumulant tensors transform multilinearly, and for $\bx = \bA\bs$ the fourth-order cumulant tensor is a symmetric CP decomposition along the columns of $\bA$.
  • Compute gradients through differentials; in particular $\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$.
  • On the sphere, critical points satisfy $\nabla f(\bw) = \lambda\bw$ and the Riemannian gradient is $(\bI - \bw\bw^\top)\nabla f$; on $O(n)$ it is $\operatorname{skew}(\bG\bU^\top)\bU$.
  • The delta method, the sandwich covariance of M-estimators and the Cramér–Rao bound are the tools of Part IV; a unit-variance law has Fisher information $\mathcal{J} \ge 1$, with equality only for the Gaussian.
  • $O(n)$ 的维数为 $n(n-1)/2$，在 $\bQ$ 处的切方向为 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bQ$（$\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 反对称）。Givens 旋转生成 $SO(n)$，再乘上一个符号对角矩阵即可得到 $O(n)$ 的任意元素。带尺度的置换矩阵仅当尺度为 $\pm 1$ 时才是正交的。

  • 通过微分来计算梯度；特别地，$\partial\log|\det\bW|/\partial\bW = \bW^{-\top}$。

Let $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ be skew-symmetric. Show that $\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ is invertible and that the Cayley transform $\bQ = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})$ is orthogonal. For $n = 2$ and $\boldsymbol{\Omega} = \tfrac{\theta}{2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, which rotation is $\bQ$?

设 $\boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 反对称。证明 $\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 可逆，并且 Cayley 变换 $\bQ = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})$ 是正交矩阵。当 $n = 2$、$\boldsymbol{\Omega} = \tfrac{\theta}{2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ 时，$\bQ$ 是哪个旋转？

For every $\bv$, $\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bv$ is a scalar equal to its transpose $\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top\bv = -\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bv$, so it is zero. Hence $\bv^\top(\bI \mp \boldsymbol{\Omega})\bv = \|\bv\|^2 > 0$ for $\bv \ne \bzero$, and both $\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ and $\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ are invertible. Since $(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ and $(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top = \bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$, we get $\bQ^\top = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$. The matrices $(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$ and $(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$ commute, being functions of the same matrix, so $\bQ^\top\bQ = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}) = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \bI$. For $n = 2$, write $a = \theta/2$; then $\bQ = \frac{1}{1 + a^2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 - a^2 & -2a \\ 2a & 1 - a^2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, the rotation by the angle $2\arctan(\theta/2)$, since $\cos\phi = (1 - a^2)/(1 + a^2)$ and $\sin\phi = 2a/(1 + a^2)$ for $\phi = 2\arctan a$. For small $\theta$ the angle is $\theta + O(\theta^3)$, so the Cayley transform agrees with the exponential to first order, as a retraction should.

对任意 $\bv$，标量 $\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bv$ 等于它的转置 $\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}^\top\bv = -\bv^\top\boldsymbol{\Omega}\bv$，所以为零。于是对 $\bv \ne \bzero$ 有 $\bv^\top(\bI \mp \boldsymbol{\Omega})\bv = \|\bv\|^2 > 0$，$\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 与 $\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}$ 都可逆。由于 $(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}$，$(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^\top = \bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega}$，得 $\bQ^\top = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$。$(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$ 与 $(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}$ 都是同一个矩阵的函数，因而可交换，所以 $\bQ^\top\bQ = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}) = (\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})(\bI - \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega})^{-1}(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Omega}) = \bI$。当 $n = 2$ 时，记 $a = \theta/2$，则 $\bQ = \frac{1}{1 + a^2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 - a^2 & -2a \\ 2a & 1 - a^2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$；由于对 $\phi = 2\arctan a$ 有 $\cos\phi = (1 - a^2)/(1 + a^2)$、$\sin\phi = 2a/(1 + a^2)$，它是旋转 $2\arctan(\theta/2)$ 角的矩阵。当 $\theta$ 很小时这个角为 $\theta + O(\theta^3)$，所以 Cayley 变换与矩阵指数在一阶上一致，这正是收缩映射应有的性质。

Let $\bz$ be white, let $G$ be a smooth function with derivative $g$, and let $f(\bw) = \E[G(\bw^\top\bz)]$; assume that the expectations exist and that differentiation under the expectation is allowed. Show that the critical points of $f$ on the unit sphere satisfy $\E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \beta\bw$ with $\beta = \E[(\bw^\top\bz)\,g(\bw^\top\bz)]$. What happens for $G(u) = u^2$?

设 $\bz$ 是白的，$G$ 是光滑函数、导数为 $g$，令 $f(\bw) = \E[G(\bw^\top\bz)]$；假设各期望存在，且可以在期望号下求导。证明 $f$ 在单位球面上的临界点满足 $\E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \beta\bw$，其中 $\beta = \E[(\bw^\top\bz)\,g(\bw^\top\bz)]$。当 $G(u) = u^2$ 时会怎样？

Differentiating under the expectation, $\nabla f(\bw) = \E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)]$. By (a), a critical point satisfies $\nabla f(\bw) = \lambda\bw$ with $\lambda = \bw^\top\nabla f(\bw) = \E[(\bw^\top\bz)\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \beta$. This is the equation from which FastICA starts (). For $G(u) = u^2$, $g(u) = 2u$ and $\nabla f(\bw) = 2\E[\bz\bz^\top]\bw = 2\bw$ because $\bz$ is white, so every unit vector is a critical point: $f \equiv 1$ on the sphere. Arrange the entries of $\mathcal{T}$ in a vector $\bt$, with the first index running fastest. Split $\|\mathcal{K}_{\by}\|_F^2$ into the diagonal entries, $\cum(y_i, y_i, y_i, y_i)^2 = \kurt(y_i)^2$, and the squared cross-cumulants. Independent outputs have zero cross-cumulants (), so at a separating rotation the whole norm sits on the diagonal and $\sum_i\kurt(y_i)^2$ attains its largest possible value $\|\mathcal{K}_{\bz}\|_F^2$.

把 $\mathcal{T}$ 的元素按第一个下标变化最快的次序排成向量 $\bt$。变换后的元素 $\sum_{pqrt} q_{ip}q_{jq}q_{kr}q_{lt}\,t_{pqrt}$ 构成向量 $(\bQ \otimes \bQ \otimes \bQ \otimes \bQ)\bt$。由中的规则，正交矩阵的 Kronecker 积仍是正交矩阵，所以 $\bt$ 的欧氏范数，也就是 $\mathcal{T}$ 的 Frobenius 范数，保持不变。对 $\by = \bU\bz$，由多重线性得 $\mathcal{K}_{\by} = \mathcal{K}_{\bz} \times_1 \bU \times_2 \bU \times_3 \bU \times_4 \bU$，所以对每个 $\bU$ 都有 $\|\mathcal{K}_{\by}\|_F = \|\mathcal{K}_{\bz}\|_F$。把 $\|\mathcal{K}_{\by}\|_F^2$ 分成对角元部分 $\cum(y_i, y_i, y_i, y_i)^2 = \kurt(y_i)^2$ 与交叉累积量的平方两部分。二者之和固定，所以最大化前者等价于最小化后者。独立的输出没有交叉累积量（），所以在实现分离的旋转处，全部范数都集中在对角元上，$\sum_i\kurt(y_i)^2$ 达到可能的最大值 $\|\mathcal{K}_{\bz}\|_F^2$。
