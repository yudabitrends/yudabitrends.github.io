A three-sensor calculation三个传感器的手算

  1. Suppose a centered $3\times100$ data matrix has covariance eigenvalues $9,4,1$. Rows are sensors, columns are observations; these three numbers describe variance along PCA directions.
  2. For the candidate $k=2$, PPCA with isotropic noise estimates the noise variance by the discarded average: just $1$. The two retained signal eigenvalues after noise subtraction are $9-1=8$ and $4-1=3$.
  3. For the different candidate $k=1$, the discarded average is $(4+1)/2=2.5$. The procedure now treats the second direction as part of the residual noise. Comparing candidates therefore changes both dimension and the fitted noise level.
  1. 设中心化后的数据矩阵尺寸为 $3\times100$，协方差特征值为 $9,4,1$。行是传感器，列是观测；这三个数描述各 PCA 方向上的方差。
  2. 候选阶数为 $k=2$ 时，各向同性噪声 PPCA 用舍弃特征值的平均估计噪声方差，此处就是 $1$。减去噪声后，两个保留的信号特征值为 $9-1=8$ 与 $4-1=3$。
  3. 换成候选阶数 $k=1$，舍弃特征值的平均变成 $(4+1)/2=2.5$，第二个方向被归入残余噪声。因此，比较候选模型时，维数与拟合噪声水平都会改变。

This arithmetic does not prove that two physical sources exist. It is conditional on the covariance model; sample dependence, weak signals and unequal sensor noise can change the interpretation. Fit penalties and scientific checks are still needed.

这段算术不能证明存在两个真实物理源。它以该协方差模型为前提；样本相关、弱信号和不同传感器的噪声不等都会改变解释，仍需结合拟合惩罚与科学检查。

Beyond the noiseless square model超越无噪声的方阵模型

The theory of the previous chapters assumes that $\bx = \bA\bs$ holds exactly and that the number of sources is known. Real recordings violate this in three ways that matter. Sensors add noise, so the data follow $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$. The number of sources is unknown and usually much smaller than the number of sensors: a 64-channel EEG recording or an fMRI data set with hundreds of time points is reduced to a few dozen components, and someone has to decide how many. Finally, ICA algorithms are iterative and randomly initialized and their contrasts have several local optima, so two runs on the same data can return different components.

前面各章的理论假设 $\bx = \bA\bs$ 严格成立，而且源的个数已知。真实数据在三个要紧的方面违背这些假设。传感器会引入噪声，所以数据服从 $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$。源的个数未知，而且通常远小于传感器个数：一份 64 导联的 EEG 记录，或一份有几百个时间点的 fMRI 数据，往往被降到几十个成分，而降到多少需要有人决定。最后，ICA 算法是迭代的、随机初始化的，其对比函数有多个局部最优，所以在同一份数据上运行两次，可能得到不同的成分。

This chapter takes up the three questions in turn: what noise does to ICA (), how to choose the number of components and what happens when it is wrong ( and ), and how to judge whether an estimated component can be trusted ( and ).

本章依次讨论这三个问题：噪声对 ICA 有什么影响（）；如何选择成分个数，以及选错了会怎样（与）；如何判断一个估计出的成分是否可信（与）。

Noisy ICA含噪 ICA

The noisy model is

含噪模型为

$$\bx = \bA\bs + \bn, \qquad \bn \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \sigma^2\bI),$$

with $\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ of full column rank ($m \ge n$), sources as in with unit variance, and noise independent of the sources. Isotropic noise is the standard assumption; noise with a known positive-definite covariance $\bSigma_{\bn}$ is reduced to it by replacing $\bx$ with $\bSigma_{\bn}^{-1/2}\bx$. The covariance of the data is $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI$. If $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n > 0$ are the nonzero eigenvalues of $\bA\bA^\top$, the eigenvalues of $\bC_{\bx}$ are

其中 $\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ 列满秩（$m \ge n$），源满足且方差为一，噪声与源独立。各向同性噪声是标准假设；若噪声协方差 $\bSigma_{\bn}$ 已知且正定，用 $\bSigma_{\bn}^{-1/2}\bx$ 代替 $\bx$ 即可化为这种情形。数据的协方差为 $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI$。若 $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n > 0$ 是 $\bA\bA^\top$ 的非零特征值，则 $\bC_{\bx}$ 的特征值为

$$\lambda_k = d_k + \sigma^2 \quad (k \le n), \qquad \lambda_k = \sigma^2 \quad (n < k \le m),$$

and the $n$ leading eigenvectors span the column space of $\bA$. This is why PCA reduction () keeps the signal, and it is the starting point of order selection. Four consequences of noise follow.

并且前 $n$ 个特征向量张成 $\bA$ 的列空间。这就是 PCA 降维（）能保留信号的原因，也是选择阶数的出发点。噪声带来以下四个后果。

Whitening is biased白化有偏

Let $\bU_n$ hold the $n$ leading eigenvectors of $\bC_{\bx}$ and $\bLambda_n = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$, and let $\bV = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bU_n^\top$ with $\bQ$ orthogonal (for $m = n$ this is every whitening matrix). Then

设 $\bU_n$ 由 $\bC_{\bx}$ 的前 $n$ 个特征向量组成，$\bLambda_n = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$，并设 $\bV = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bU_n^\top$，其中 $\bQ$ 为正交矩阵（$m = n$ 时这就是全部白化矩阵）。则

$$(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bI - \sigma^2\,\bV\bV^\top,$$

so the singular values of $\bV\bA$ are $\sqrt{1 - \sigma^2/\lambda_k}$, $k = 1, \dots, n$, and $\bV\bA$ is not orthogonal when $\sigma^2 > 0$. The bias-corrected matrix $\bV_{\mathrm c} = \bQ(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\bU_n^\top$ makes $\bV_{\mathrm c}\bA$ orthogonal.

因此 $\bV\bA$ 的奇异值为 $\sqrt{1 - \sigma^2/\lambda_k}$（$k = 1, \dots, n$），当 $\sigma^2 > 0$ 时 $\bV\bA$ 不是正交矩阵。经偏差校正的矩阵 $\bV_{\mathrm c} = \bQ(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\bU_n^\top$ 则使 $\bV_{\mathrm c}\bA$ 成为正交矩阵。

Since $\bA\bA^\top = \bC_{\bx} - \sigma^2\bI$ has its column space spanned by $\bU_n$, $\bA\bA^\top = \bU_n(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bU_n^\top$. Hence $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1/2}(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bQ^\top = \bI - \sigma^2\bQ\bLambda_n^{-1}\bQ^\top$, and $\bV\bV^\top = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1}\bQ^\top$ has eigenvalues $1/\lambda_k$. Replacing $\bLambda_n^{-1/2}$ by $(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}$ gives $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$.

由于 $\bA\bA^\top = \bC_{\bx} - \sigma^2\bI$ 的列空间由 $\bU_n$ 张成，有 $\bA\bA^\top = \bU_n(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bU_n^\top$。因此 $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1/2}(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bQ^\top = \bI - \sigma^2\bQ\bLambda_n^{-1}\bQ^\top$，而 $\bV\bV^\top = \bQ\bLambda_n^{-1}\bQ^\top$ 的特征值为 $1/\lambda_k$。把 $\bLambda_n^{-1/2}$ 换成 $(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}$ 就得到 $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$。

After ordinary whitening, an algorithm that searches over rotations therefore cannot, in general, reach a separating matrix. A rotation can turn $\bV\bA$ into a scaled permutation matrix only if the columns of $\bV\bA$ are orthogonal; since $(\bV\bA)^\top(\bV\bA) = (\bA^\top\bA + \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}\bA^\top\bA$, this happens exactly when the columns of $\bA$ are orthogonal. The distortion is of relative size $\sigma^2/\lambda_k$ and is largest along the weakest signal direction. Bias correction requires $\sigma^2$, which can be estimated from the discarded eigenvalues when $m > n$ (see probabilistic PCA below). Unlike the noiseless case, equivariance is also lost: the outputs $\bW\bx = \bP\bs + \bW\bn$ contain a noise term that depends on $\bW$ and hence on $\bA$.

因此，在普通白化之后，在旋转中搜索的算法一般无法到达分离矩阵。只有当 $\bV\bA$ 的各列相互正交时，旋转才能把它变成带尺度的置换矩阵；而 $(\bV\bA)^\top(\bV\bA) = (\bA^\top\bA + \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}\bA^\top\bA$，所以这恰好发生在 $\bA$ 的各列相互正交的时候。畸变的相对大小为 $\sigma^2/\lambda_k$，沿最弱的信号方向最大。偏差校正需要知道 $\sigma^2$；当 $m > n$ 时，可以由被舍弃的特征值估计它（见下文的概率 PCA）。与无噪声情形不同，等变性也不再成立：输出 $\bW\bx = \bP\bs + \bW\bn$ 含有依赖于 $\bW$、从而依赖于 $\bA$ 的噪声项。

Higher-order cumulants ignore Gaussian noise高阶累积量对高斯噪声“视而不见”

Cumulants are additive for independent summands and vanish beyond order two for Gaussian variables (). Hence the fourth-order cumulants of noisy data are those of the noiseless mixture,

独立随机变量之和的累积量等于各自累积量之和，而高斯变量二阶以上的累积量全为零（）。因此含噪数据的四阶累积量与无噪声混合的完全相同：

$$\cum(x_i, x_j, x_k, x_l) = \sum_{p=1}^n \kappa_p\,a_{ip}a_{jp}a_{kp}a_{lp}.$$

After bias-corrected whitening $\bz=\bV_{\mathrm c}\bx$, a unit vector $\bw$ gives $\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)=\sum_p q_p^4\kappa_p$, with $\bq=(\bV_{\mathrm c}\bA)^\top\bw$ a unit vector. Thus nonzero-kurtosis source directions remain the maxima of $|\kurt|$ (). Correctly formed cumulant methods can be consistent when their cumulants exist, the noise correction is consistently estimated and their identifiability assumptions hold; zero-kurtosis non-Gaussian sources remain a limitation of fourth-order methods. Naively applying a noiseless $\log\cosh$ contrast or noiseless likelihood to noisy data is generally biased. This does not mean that a correctly specified noisy likelihood is biased. Hyvärinen (1999) developed Gaussian-moment corrections and a modified FastICA for this setting.

偏差校正白化后，$\bz=\bV_{\mathrm c}\bx$，单位向量 $\bw$ 满足 $\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)=\sum_p q_p^4\kappa_p$，其中 $\bq=(\bV_{\mathrm c}\bA)^\top\bw$ 也是单位向量。因此，非零峭度源的方向仍是 $|\kurt|$ 的极大点（）。在所需累积量存在、噪声校正可一致估计且可辨识性条件成立时，正确计算累积量的方法可以一致；零峭度的非高斯源仍是四阶方法的局限。把无噪 $\log\cosh$ 对比函数或无噪似然直接用于含噪数据，一般有偏，但这不表示正确指定的含噪似然也有偏。Hyvärinen（1999）针对这种情形提出了高斯矩校正与修正的 FastICA。

Correct the cumulant, not only the whitening不仅要校正白化，还要正确计算累积量

Bias-corrected whitening makes the signal covariance the identity, but not the covariance of the observed data. For $\bz=\bV_{\mathrm c}\bx$, the observed covariance is $\bC_z=\bI+\sigma^2\bV_{\mathrm c}\bV_{\mathrm c}^\top$. Thus a fourth-cumulant implementation must subtract products of the actual observed covariances:

偏差校正白化使信号协方差成为单位阵，却不会使观测数据的协方差成为单位阵。对 $\bz=\bV_{\mathrm c}\bx$，观测协方差为 $\bC_z=\bI+\sigma^2\bV_{\mathrm c}\bV_{\mathrm c}^\top$。因此四阶累积量的实现必须减去实际观测协方差的乘积：

$$\cum(z_i,z_j,z_k,z_l)=\E[z_i z_j z_k z_l]-(C_z)_{ij}(C_z)_{kl}-(C_z)_{ik}(C_z)_{jl}-(C_z)_{il}(C_z)_{jk}.$$

In particular, $\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)=\E[(\bw^\top\bz)^4]-3(\bw^\top\bC_z\bw)^2$, not the fourth moment minus 3. Feeding these data into code that silently assumes identity total covariance breaks noise invariance. A small or negative estimate $\ell_k-\hat\sigma^2$ warns of order or noise-model uncertainty; a numerical floor prevents overflow but does not identify a buried source. Correcting preprocessing alone does not turn every noiseless ICA implementation into a consistent noisy-ICA method (Hyvärinen, 1999).

特别地，$\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)=\E[(\bw^\top\bz)^4]-3(\bw^\top\bC_z\bw)^2$，并非四阶矩减 3。若程序暗中假定总协方差是单位阵，直接输入这种数据就会破坏噪声不变性。估计值 $\ell_k-\hat\sigma^2$ 很小或为负时，意味着阶数或噪声模型存在不确定性；数值截断能防止溢出，却不能辨识淹没在噪声中的源。仅校正预处理，并不能让所有无噪 ICA 实现自动成为一致的含噪 ICA 方法（Hyvärinen, 1999）。

The sources cannot be recovered exactly源无法精确恢复

Even with $\bA$ known, noise cannot be removed. For $m = n$ the unmixed outputs $\bA^{-1}\bx = \bs + \bA^{-1}\bn$ carry noise with covariance $\sigma^2(\bA^\top\bA)^{-1}$, which is large when $\bA$ is ill-conditioned. The linear estimator with the smallest mean squared error is $\hat\bs = \bA^\top(\bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}\bx$, which accepts a bias (shrinkage toward zero) in exchange for less noise; the posterior mean using the source densities has no greater mean squared error and can improve on this linear estimate (Hyvärinen, Karhunen & Oja, 2001). Noisy ICA thus splits into two tasks: estimating $\bA$, which can be done consistently, and estimating $\bs$, which can only be done approximately.

即使 $\bA$ 已知，噪声也无法去除。$m = n$ 时，解混输出 $\bA^{-1}\bx = \bs + \bA^{-1}\bn$ 所含噪声的协方差为 $\sigma^2(\bA^\top\bA)^{-1}$，$\bA$ 病态时它很大。均方误差最小的线性估计量是 $\hat\bs = \bA^\top(\bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}\bx$，它以一定的偏差（向零收缩）换取较小的噪声；使用源密度得到的后验均值，其均方误差不会更大，并且可能优于这一线性估计（Hyvärinen, Karhunen & Oja, 2001）。因此含噪 ICA 分为两个任务：估计 $\bA$，这可以相合地做到；估计 $\bs$，这只能近似地做到。

Probabilistic PCA概率 PCA

Probabilistic PCA (Tipping & Bishop, 1999) is the model $\bx = \bF\mathbf{f} + \bn$ with $k$ Gaussian factors $\mathbf{f} \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$ and $\bn \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \sigma^2\bI)$. Let $\ell_1 \ge \dots \ge \ell_m$ be the eigenvalues of the sample covariance, $\bU_k$ the corresponding $k$ leading eigenvectors and $\mathbf{L}_k = \diag(\ell_1, \dots, \ell_k)$. The maximum-likelihood estimates are

概率 PCA（Tipping & Bishop, 1999）是模型 $\bx = \bF\mathbf{f} + \bn$，其中 $k$ 个因子 $\mathbf{f} \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$ 为高斯分布，$\bn \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \sigma^2\bI)$。设 $\ell_1 \ge \dots \ge \ell_m$ 为样本协方差的特征值，$\bU_k$ 为相应的前 $k$ 个特征向量，$\mathbf{L}_k = \diag(\ell_1, \dots, \ell_k)$。最大似然估计为

$$\hat\sigma^2 = \frac{1}{m - k}\sum_{j=k+1}^m \ell_j, \qquad \hat\bF = \bU_k\bigl(\mathbf{L}_k - \hat\sigma^2\bI\bigr)^{1/2}\bR,$$

where $\bR$ is an arbitrary orthogonal matrix. The noise variance is the average of the discarded eigenvalues, and the factor loadings are determined only up to the rotation $\bR$, because Gaussian factors can be rotated freely. That rotation is exactly what ICA determines when the factors are non-Gaussian. A noisy-ICA pipeline therefore estimates the signal subspace and $\sigma^2$ by probabilistic PCA, whitens with the bias-corrected matrix $(\mathbf{L}_k - \hat\sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\bU_k^\top$, and finds the rotation by ICA; probabilistic ICA for fMRI (Beckmann & Smith, 2004) follows this route ().

其中 $\bR$ 是任意正交矩阵。噪声方差就是被舍弃特征值的平均；因子载荷只能确定到旋转 $\bR$，因为高斯因子可以自由旋转。而当因子非高斯时，这个旋转恰好由 ICA 确定。因此含噪 ICA 的流程是：用概率 PCA 估计信号子空间和 $\sigma^2$，用经偏差校正的矩阵 $(\mathbf{L}_k - \hat\sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\bU_k^\top$ 白化，再用 ICA 求出旋转。fMRI 中的概率 ICA（Beckmann & Smith, 2004）走的就是这条路（）。

Choosing the number of components选择成分个数

Reading the criteria. Think of AIC or MDL as a scoreboard with one row per candidate $k$. Better covariance fit lowers one part of the score; more adjustable parameters raise another. A minimum chooses a candidate within this family, rather than counting biological objects directly.

怎样读准则。可以把 AIC 或 MDL 想成一张评分表，每行对应一个候选 $k$。协方差拟合更好会降低一部分分数，可调参数更多则会增加另一部分。最小分数选的是这个模型族中的候选模型，并不是直接清点生物对象。

When $m > n$, ICA is run on the $k$-dimensional principal subspace, and $k$, the model order, must be chosen. In the population, shows $n$ eigenvalues above a flat floor $\sigma^2$. In a sample the noise eigenvalues spread around $\sigma^2$, the more so the larger $m/T$, and a weak signal eigenvalue can disappear among them. A principled choice compares, for each candidate $k$, how well the “$k$ signals plus white noise” covariance fits the data against how many parameters it uses.

当 $m > n$ 时，ICA 在 $k$ 维主子空间上运行，而 $k$，即模型阶数，必须选定。在总体层面， 显示有 $n$ 个特征值高出平坦的噪声底 $\sigma^2$。在样本中，噪声特征值会在 $\sigma^2$ 周围散开，$m/T$ 越大散得越开，较弱的信号特征值可能淹没其中。有原则的选择方法是：对每个候选的 $k$，权衡“$k$ 个信号加白噪声”这一协方差模型对数据的拟合程度与它所用的参数个数。

The maximum-likelihood fit of that model is the probabilistic PCA solution, and its maximized Gaussian log-likelihood depends on the eigenvalues only through the ratio of the geometric to the arithmetic mean of the $m - k$ smallest ones ():

这个模型的最大似然拟合就是概率 PCA 的解，其最大化后的高斯对数似然只通过最小的 $m - k$ 个特征值的几何平均与算术平均之比依赖于特征值（）：

$$\log\frac{L_k}{L_m} = \frac{T}{2}\,(m - k)\,\log\frac{\bigl(\prod_{j=k+1}^m \ell_j\bigr)^{1/(m-k)}}{\frac{1}{m-k}\sum_{j=k+1}^m \ell_j}, \qquad k < m.$$

Here $L_k$ is the maximized likelihood of the model with $k$ signals and $L_m$ that of an unrestricted covariance, which does not depend on $k$. The logarithm on the right is at most zero and vanishes only when the $m - k$ smallest eigenvalues are equal. The model has $p(k) = mk - k(k-1)/2 + 1$ free parameters ($k$ signal eigenvalues, $\sigma^2$, and $k$ orthonormal eigenvectors in $\R^m$). The order is chosen by minimizing

其中 $L_k$ 是含 $k$ 个信号的模型的最大似然值，$L_m$ 是协方差不受限制时的最大似然值，后者与 $k$ 无关。右边的对数不大于零，且仅当最小的 $m - k$ 个特征值全部相等时为零。该模型有 $p(k) = mk - k(k-1)/2 + 1$ 个自由参数（$k$ 个信号特征值、$\sigma^2$，以及 $\R^m$ 中的 $k$ 个标准正交特征向量）。阶数通过最小化下列准则来选择：

$$\mathrm{AIC}(k) = -2\log\frac{L_k}{L_m} + 2\,p(k), \qquad \mathrm{MDL}(k) = -\log\frac{L_k}{L_m} + \tfrac12\,p(k)\log T,$$

the criteria of Akaike (1974) and of Rissanen (1978), written relative to $L_m$, which shifts them by a constant and does not change the minimizer; MDL coincides with half the Bayesian information criterion of Schwarz (1978). Wax and Kailath (1985) derived these eigenvalue forms for complex-valued array data, where the likelihood term carries $T$ instead of $T/2$ and the parameter count is $k(2m - k)$; the forms above are the real-valued analogues. Because its penalty grows with $\log T$, MDL is consistent for i.i.d. Gaussian data, whereas AIC tends to overestimate the order (Wax & Kailath, 1985). Minka (2000) proposed a Bayesian alternative, a Laplace approximation to the evidence of the probabilistic PCA model, which outperformed cross-validation and other methods in his simulations; it is used in probabilistic ICA (Beckmann & Smith, 2004).

这分别是 Akaike（1974）和 Rissanen（1978）的准则，这里写成相对于 $L_m$ 的形式，只差一个常数，不改变最小点；MDL 恰为 Schwarz（1978）的贝叶斯信息准则的一半。Wax 和 Kailath（1985）针对复值阵列数据推导了这些基于特征值的形式，那里似然项中是 $T$ 而不是 $T/2$，参数个数为 $k(2m - k)$；上面的形式是相应的实值版本。由于 MDL 的惩罚项随 $\log T$ 增长，它对独立同分布的高斯数据是相合的，而 AIC 往往高估阶数（Wax & Kailath, 1985）。Minka（2000）提出了一个贝叶斯替代方案：用 Laplace 近似计算概率 PCA 模型的证据（evidence），在他的模拟中优于交叉验证和其他方法；概率 ICA 采用了这一方法（Beckmann & Smith, 2004）。

Dependent samples相依样本

All of these criteria treat the $T$ samples as independent. Temporally or spatially correlated samples carry less information than their number suggests: the sample covariance fluctuates more, the noise eigenvalues spread more, and the criteria read the spread as signal and overestimate the order. Li, Adalı and Calhoun (2007) proposed to subsample the data so that the retained samples are effectively independent, choosing the subsampling depth from an entropy-rate measure of the dependence, and to apply the criteria to the subsampled data. In the GIFT toolbox, which implements this method, the eigenvalues of the subsampled data are further divided by the corresponding quantiles of the Marčenko–Pastur law () for the effective sample size, and the order is taken at the first local minimum of MDL rather than at its global minimum. The script below shows the effect with $n = 8$ Laplace sources in $m = 30$ sensors, where sources and noise are either white or first-order autoregressive with coefficient $\phi = 0.9$.

上述准则都把 $T$ 个样本当作相互独立。时间上或空间上相关的样本所含的信息比其个数所暗示的要少：样本协方差波动更大，噪声特征值散得更开，而准则会把这种散开误读为信号，从而高估阶数。Li、Adalı 和 Calhoun（2007）提出对数据降采样，使保留下来的样本近似独立（降采样的间隔由衡量相依程度的熵率指标确定），再对降采样后的数据应用这些准则。在实现了这一方法的 GIFT 工具箱中，降采样数据的特征值还要再除以按有效样本量计算的 Marčenko–Pastur 分布（）的相应分位数，阶数取 MDL 的第一个局部极小点，而不是全局极小点。下面的脚本用 $m = 30$ 个传感器中的 $n = 8$ 个拉普拉斯源演示这一效应，源和噪声或者是白的，或者是系数 $\phi = 0.9$ 的一阶自回归过程。

import numpy as np

def order_criteria(X):
    """Orders chosen by AIC and MDL from the sample covariance eigenvalues."""
    m, T = X.shape
    if m < 2 or T <= m or not np.isfinite(X).all():
        raise ValueError("Need finite data with T > m >= 2.")
    ell = np.sort(np.linalg.eigvalsh(np.cov(X)))[::-1]
    if ell[-1] <= np.finfo(float).eps * m * ell[0]:
        raise ValueError("Covariance is numerically rank deficient.")
    aic, mdl = [], []
    for k in range(m):
        tail = ell[k:]                      # the m - k smallest eigenvalues
        loglr = (m - k) * (np.mean(np.log(tail)) - np.log(np.mean(tail)))
        p = m * k - k * (k - 1) / 2 + 1     # free parameters of the model
        aic.append(-T * loglr + 2 * p)
        mdl.append(-T / 2 * loglr + p / 2 * np.log(T))
    return int(np.argmin(aic)), int(np.argmin(mdl))

def ar1(E, phi):
    """Turn each row of white noise E into a unit-variance AR(1) series."""
    Y = np.empty_like(E)
    Y[:, 0] = E[:, 0]
    for t in range(1, E.shape[1]):
        Y[:, t] = phi * Y[:, t - 1] + np.sqrt(1 - phi ** 2) * E[:, t]
    return Y

rng = np.random.default_rng(1)
m, n, T = 30, 8, 5000
A = rng.normal(size=(m, n))
S = rng.laplace(0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), (n, T))
N = rng.normal(size=(m, T))
for phi in [0.0, 0.9]:
    X = A @ ar1(S, phi) + ar1(N, phi)
    print(f"phi = {phi}: all samples (AIC, MDL) = {order_criteria(X)}, "
          f"every 10th sample = {order_criteria(X[:, ::10])}")

样本独立时，MDL 找到了全部八个源，AIC 多估了一个。样本相依时两者都失败了，AIC 甚至选了允许的最大阶数。每十个样本保留一个，尽管丢掉了 90% 的数据，却恢复了独立样本时的结果（MDL 为 8，AIC 为 9）；10 接近于这种相依性使两个 AR(1) 序列之间样本协方差的方差放大的倍数 $(1 + \phi^2)/(1 - \phi^2) \approx 9.5$。说明了原因：相依数据的噪声特征值构成倾斜的而不是平坦的“底”，MDL 的最小值远在 $k = 8$ 之后。

Too few or too many components: merged and split components成分过少或过多：合并与分裂

A wrong order has characteristic consequences. If $k < n$, PCA keeps the $k$ directions of largest variance and discards directions that still contain signal; the sources that live partly in the discarded directions cannot be separated and appear as merged components, mixtures of two or more sources. The typical victims are sources with nearly collinear mixing columns. Their sum direction has large variance and their difference direction small variance, proportional to $1 - \cos\theta$ for columns of equal norm at angle $\theta$ (), so the difference is the first thing to be discarded. In fMRI terms, two regions with similar time courses are found as one network.

阶数选错会带来典型的后果。若 $k < n$，PCA 只保留方差最大的 $k$ 个方向，丢掉了仍含信号的方向；部分落在被丢弃方向上的那些源无法分开，表现为合并的成分，即两个或更多源的混合。最典型的受害者是混合列几乎共线的源：它们的和方向方差大，差方向方差小，对夹角为 $0 < \theta < \pi/2$、范数相等的两列，后者与 $1 - \cos\theta$ 成正比（），所以差方向最先被丢掉。用 fMRI 的话说，时间过程相近的两个脑区会被当成一个网络。

If $k > n$, the extra retained dimensions contain mostly noise. In a well-specified, sufficiently sampled noise-aware analysis, additional directions may appear as nearly Gaussian components. Ordinary noise-free ICA, however, is not guaranteed to leave the signal components unchanged: whitening bias and a non-identifiable Gaussian subspace remain. Two further problems are common. Components merged at a lower order may split at a higher one; whether the parts are distinct sources or fragments of one process cannot be decided by ICA alone. When $k$ is large relative to $T$, ICA may also overfit, returning components concentrated on a few samples whose apparent non-Gaussianity is created by the fit itself (Särelä & Vigário, 2003). On the fMRI data studied by Li, Adalı and Calhoun (2007), overestimating the order reduced component stability and the accuracy of task-related activation estimates.

若 $k > n$，额外保留的维度主要含噪声。在噪声模型正确且样本充分的分析中，这些方向可能表现为近高斯成分；但不能保证普通无噪 ICA 会让信号成分保持不变：白化偏差与不可辨识的高斯子空间仍然存在。另有两个常见问题。低阶数下合并的成分可能在高阶数下分裂，而分裂后的部分究竟是不同源还是同一过程的片段，仅靠 ICA 无法判断。当 $k$ 相对于 $T$ 过大时，ICA 还可能过拟合，得到集中在少数样本上的成分，其表面上的非高斯性是拟合本身制造的（Särelä & Vigário, 2003）。在 Li、Adalı 与 Calhoun（2007）研究的 fMRI 数据中，高估阶数降低了成分稳定性与任务相关激活估计的准确性。

An exact finite-source model has a true source count. In real data, however, that model may only approximate a hierarchy of processes, so there need not be one scientifically preferred order: the fitted order also sets the scale of the description. Run ICA at several plausible orders, track how components merge and split, and report both the selection criterion and its assumptions. discusses the consequences for brain networks.

精确的有限源模型有真实源数。但真实数据中的层级过程可能只能被该模型近似描述，因此未必存在唯一具有科学意义的最佳阶数：拟合阶数也决定描述的尺度。应在若干合理阶数下运行 ICA，追踪成分的合并与分裂，并同时报告选择准则及其假设。讨论这一点对脑网络的影响。

Reliability across runs: ICASSO and RAICAR多次运行的可靠性：ICASSO 与 RAICAR

An ICA estimate depends on more than the model. The starting point decides which local optimum the algorithm reaches, and the finite sample perturbs every optimum (). A component that appears in every run is more trustworthy than one that appears once. ICASSO (Himberg, Hyvärinen & Esposito, 2004) turns this observation into a procedure.

ICA 的估计结果不只取决于模型。起点决定了算法到达哪个局部最优，有限样本又会扰动每个最优点（）。每次运行都出现的成分，比只出现一次的成分更可信。ICASSO（Himberg, Hyvärinen & Esposito, 2004）把这一观察变成了一套流程。

  1. Run ICA $R$ times, each with a new random initialization and, optionally, on a bootstrap resample of the data (columns of $\bX$ drawn with replacement). Collect all estimated components, $R \times k$ in total.
  2. For every pair of estimates $a, b$ compute the similarity $\sigma_{ab} = |r_{ab}|$, the absolute correlation between the two estimated components (rows of $\hat\bW\bX$: time series in temporal ICA, spatial maps in spatial ICA); with unmixing vectors $\bw_a, \bw_b$ it equals
    $$\sigma_{ab}=\frac{|\bw_a^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_b|}{\sqrt{(\bw_a^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_a)(\bw_b^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_b)}}.$$
  3. Cluster the estimates by agglomerative hierarchical clustering with average linkage on the dissimilarity $1 - \sigma_{ab}$, and cut the tree into $k$ clusters.
  4. For each cluster $C$ compute the quality index and choose as its representative (the centrotype) the estimate with the largest total similarity to the other members of $C$.
  5. Report the centrotypes ranked by $I_q$; compact and isolated clusters ($I_q$ near 1) indicate reliable components.
  1. 运行 ICA $R$ 次，每次使用新的随机初始化，也可以在数据的自助重抽样样本（有放回地抽取 $\bX$ 的列）上运行。收集全部估计成分，共 $R \times k$ 个。
  2. 对每一对估计 $a, b$ 计算相似度 $\sigma_{ab} = |r_{ab}|$，即两个估计成分（$\hat\bW\bX$ 的行：时间 ICA 中是时间序列，空间 ICA 中是空间图）之间相关系数的绝对值；若解混向量为 $\bw_a, \bw_b$，它等于
    $$\sigma_{ab}=\frac{|\bw_a^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_b|}{\sqrt{(\bw_a^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_a)(\bw_b^\top\bC_{\bx}\bw_b)}}.$$
  3. 以 $1 - \sigma_{ab}$ 为相异度，用平均连接的凝聚式层次聚类对这些估计聚类，并把聚类树切成 $k$ 个簇。
  4. 对每个簇 $C$ 计算质量指标 ，并选出与簇内其他成员总相似度最大的估计作为代表（称为中心型，centrotype）。
  5. 按 $I_q$ 排序报告各中心型；紧凑且孤立的簇（$I_q$ 接近 1）表示可靠的成分。

The quality index of a cluster $C$ with complement $C^c$ is the average similarity inside the cluster minus the average similarity to the estimates outside it:

簇 $C$（其补集为 $C^c$）的质量指标，是簇内的平均相似度减去它与簇外估计的平均相似度：

$$I_q(C) = \frac{1}{|C|^2}\sum_{a, b \in C}\sigma_{ab} \;-\; \frac{1}{|C|\,|C^c|}\sum_{a \in C}\sum_{b \in C^c}\sigma_{ab}.$$

ICASSO also projects the estimates to two dimensions for visual inspection. RAICAR (Yang, LaConte, Weng & Hu, 2008) follows the same idea with a different matching step: it runs ICA many times, aligns the components across runs from the matrix of absolute cross-correlations between runs, ranks the aligned components by a reproducibility index computed from the absolute correlations among their aligned realizations, and averages the aligned estimates. Both methods reuse the same data. Split-half reproducibility asks a stronger question: split the data into two halves (two groups of subjects, two sessions, or the two halves of a long recording), run ICA on each, match the components between the halves by maximizing the total absolute correlation over permutations, and report the matched correlations. A component that reappears in independent data is closer to a scientific replication than one that reappears in reruns.

ICASSO 还把这些估计投影到二维平面上，便于目视检查。RAICAR（Yang, LaConte, Weng & Hu, 2008）遵循同样的思路，只是配对步骤不同：它多次运行 ICA，利用各次运行之间的相关系数绝对值矩阵把成分对齐，按可重复性指标给对齐后的成分排序（该指标由同一成分各次实现之间相关系数的绝对值算出），再对对齐后的估计取平均。这两种方法用的都是同一份数据。分半可重复性问的是更强的问题：把数据分成两半（两组被试、两次扫描，或长记录的前后两半），分别做 ICA，通过在所有置换中最大化总的相关绝对值来配对两半的成分，然后报告配对后的相关系数。在独立数据中重新出现的成分，比在重复运行中重新出现的成分，更接近科学意义上的重复验证。

The spread of a component across bootstrap runs is an empirical version of the sampling variability studied in . It is large for nearly Gaussian sources, whose asymptotic variance is at least $1/(\mathcal{J} - 1)$ for one-unit estimators (), and it grows as $T$ decreases. A low stability index can therefore be predicted, in part, from the source distributions and the sample size.

一个成分在各次自助重抽样运行之间的离散程度，是所研究的抽样变异性的经验版本。对接近高斯的源它很大，因为单单元估计量的渐近方差至少为 $1/(\mathcal{J} - 1)$（）；样本量 $T$ 越小，它也越大。所以较低的稳定性指标，在一定程度上可以由源的分布和样本量预先估计出来。

What a stability index does and does not certify稳定性指标能说明什么、不能说明什么

A high stability index is evidence that similar components recur under the perturbations actually tested. It does not prove global optimality, unbiasedness or robustness to untested perturbations; identical initialization, preprocessing or optimization failure can also make a wrong result repeatable.

较高的稳定性指标说明相似成分会在实际测试的扰动下反复出现。它不能证明全局最优、无偏，或对未测试扰动的稳健性；相同的初始化、预处理或优化失败，也可能让错误结果重复出现。

  • Stable is not unbiased. A biased estimator is stable and wrong. Noise-biased whitening () produces reproducible but distorted components, and an order that is too low produces perfectly stable merged components ().
  • Stable is not meaningful. In fMRI, head motion and physiological artifacts are among the most stable components of all; stability says nothing about their origin.
  • Stable is not independent. ICA returns maximally independent components whether or not the data were generated by independent sources, and a dependent structure can be reproduced as reliably as an independent one.
  • Unstable is not always bad. If two sources are nearly Gaussian, or more generally if the contrast is nearly flat within a subspace, each run returns a different rotation inside a subspace that is itself stable (). The individual components then have low stability while the subspace they span is reliable; comparing subspaces across runs, rather than single components, reveals this ().
  • 稳定不等于无偏。有偏的估计量可以既稳定又错误。受噪声影响的白化（）给出可重复但有畸变的成分，阶数过低则给出完全稳定的合并成分（）。
  • 稳定不等于有意义。在 fMRI 中，头动和生理伪迹恰恰是最稳定的成分之一；稳定性对它们的来源一无所知。
  • 稳定不等于独立。无论数据是否由独立源生成，ICA 都会返回“尽可能独立”的成分；相依的结构也可以和独立的结构一样被稳定地重现。
  • 不稳定未必是坏事。如果两个源接近高斯，或者更一般地，对比函数在某个子空间内几乎是平的，那么每次运行都会在这个本身稳定的子空间内给出不同的旋转（）。此时单个成分的稳定性很低，而它们张成的子空间是可靠的；在各次运行之间比较子空间而不是单个成分，就能看出这一点（）。

Stability is useful evidence, not a logical certificate of validity. Low stability may call for a subspace interpretation or indicate insufficient information, rather than prove that no source exists. Interpretation also needs agreement with the noise and order diagnostics, replication on independent data and knowledge of the domain.

稳定性是有用的证据，而不是有效性的逻辑证明。低稳定性可能提示应解释子空间，或说明信息不足，而非证明不存在真实源。解释还需要与噪声和阶数诊断一致、在独立数据中复现，并结合领域知识。

Resampling and reporting uncertainty重采样与不确定性报告

After sample centering, a sample covariance has rank at most $\min(m,T-1)$. The eigenvalue likelihood above requires positive eigenvalues; when $T\le m$, the unrestricted covariance likelihood is singular and code must not take logarithms of zeros. Reducing to a justified rank, collecting more observations or fitting a regularized model changes the analysis and must be reported. Fixed-dimension consistency statements do not automatically apply when $m/T$ stays large.

经样本中心化后，样本协方差的秩至多为 $\min(m,T-1)$。上面的特征值似然要求特征值为正；当 $T\le m$ 时，无约束协方差似然是奇异的，程序不能对零取对数。按合理依据降秩、增加观测或改用正则化模型，都会改变分析，必须报告。维数固定时的一致性结论，也不能自动推广到 $m/T$ 始终很大的情形。

For dependent recordings, an ordinary column bootstrap destroys dependence and can understate uncertainty. Resample independent subjects or sessions when those are the sampling units, or use blocks that preserve the relevant dependence. Refit centering, PCA, order selection and ICA inside each resample when the target is uncertainty of the full pipeline. Compare maps on a common original voxel grid, or apply estimated unmixing matrices to a common evaluation recording: correlating differently resampled columns by their positions has no meaning. Report the resampling unit, block length, converged-run fraction, one-to-one component matching rule and stability across nearby orders. A stability score conditional on a fixed order is not uncertainty about that order.

Key points

本章要点

  • Gaussian noise biases whitening: $\bV\bA$ has singular values $\sqrt{1 - \sigma^2/\lambda_k}$. Bias-corrected whitening needs $\sigma^2$, which probabilistic PCA estimates as the mean of the discarded eigenvalues when $n < m$.
  • Existing cumulants of order three and higher ignore independent Gaussian noise; with corrected whitening and suitable moment and identifiability conditions, cumulant methods can identify $\bA$; the sources themselves can only be estimated approximately.
  • AIC, MDL and Minka's evidence choose the order from the eigenvalues of the covariance, assuming independent samples. MDL is consistent and AIC tends to overestimate; dependence can cause overestimation, and suitable subsampling can reduce that bias.
  • Too few components merge sources, nearly collinear ones first; too many add noise components and, when samples are scarce, overfitted spikes. Splits at higher orders must be interpreted, not taken for granted.
  • ICASSO, RAICAR and split-half analyses measure reproducibility. Stability alone does not establish validity, and unstable single components can span a stable subspace.
  • 高斯噪声使白化有偏：$\bV\bA$ 的奇异值为 $\sqrt{1 - \sigma^2/\lambda_k}$。偏差校正的白化需要 $\sigma^2$，当 $n < m$ 时，概率 PCA 用被舍弃特征值的平均来估计它。
  • 存在的三阶及以上累积量不受独立高斯噪声影响；在校正白化且适当的矩与可辨识性条件成立时，累积量方法可以辨识 $\bA$；源本身则只能近似估计。
  • AIC、MDL 和 Minka 的证据方法都由协方差的特征值选择阶数，并假设样本相互独立。MDL 相合，AIC 往往高估；样本相依可能引起高估，适当的子采样可以减轻这一偏差。
  • 成分过少会合并源，混合列几乎共线的源最先被合并；成分过多会多出噪声成分，样本稀少时还会出现过拟合的尖峰。高阶时的分裂需要解释，不能想当然地接受。
  • ICASSO、RAICAR 和分半分析衡量的是可重复性。稳定性本身不能证明有效性；不稳定的单个成分可能张成一个稳定的子空间。

Any rotation of the two Gaussian components leaves them independent and Gaussian (), so the contrast is flat within their plane, and each run returns a rotation determined by the initialization and by sampling noise.
