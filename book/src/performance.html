How accurate can separation be?分离能做到多精确？

An ICA algorithm computes its estimate from a finite sample, so even when the model holds exactly, each output keeps a small amount of the other sources. The script of printed $\bW\bA \approx \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0.03 & 1.00 \\ -1.00 & 0.02 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$: separation succeeded, but the entries $0.03$ and $0.02$ are not zero, and they would change with a new sample. This chapter quantifies such residual errors. How large are they? How do they depend on the sample size $T$, on the distributions of the sources and on the algorithm? Is there a limit that no algorithm can beat?

The answers are asymptotic: they describe the leading behaviour of the errors as $T \to \infty$, and in practice they are accurate already for moderate $T$. Three facts organize the chapter. For the usual estimators the distribution of the errors does not depend on the mixing matrix (). The errors shrink like $1/\sqrt{T}$, with constants that can be computed in closed form; for one-unit FastICA the constant is the asymptotic variance $V_g$ of Hyvärinen (1997; 1999), and the best nonlinearity is the score function of the source (). Finally, the Cramér–Rao bound (Tichavský, Koldovský & Oja, 2006) gives the smallest variance that any unbiased estimator can achieve; it is governed by the Fisher information of the sources and becomes infinite when two sources are Gaussian ().

这些答案都是渐近的：它们描述 $T \to \infty$ 时误差的主导行为，而实践中 $T$ 不太大时就已相当准确。本章围绕三个事实展开。对常用的估计量，误差的分布与混合矩阵无关（）。误差以 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 的速度缩小，其常数可以写成闭式：对单单元 FastICA，这个常数就是 Hyvärinen（1997; 1999）给出的渐近方差 $V_g$，而最优的非线性函数是源的得分函数（）。最后，Cramér–Rao 界（Tichavský, Koldovský & Oja, 2006）给出任何无偏估计量所能达到的最小方差；它由源的 Fisher 信息决定，两个源都是高斯时变为无穷大（）。

Throughout the chapter the model is square ($m = n$, $\bA$ invertible), the sources satisfy with zero mean and unit variance, and their densities $p_i$ are smooth enough for every expectation below to exist and for differentiation under the integral sign. $$\E[f'(s)] = \E[\psi(s)\,f(s)].$$

With $f = 1$, $f(s) = s$ and $f = \psi$ it yields $\E[\psi(s)] = 0$, $\E[s\,\psi(s)] = 1$ and $\E[\psi'(s)] = \mathcal{J}$. For a standard Gaussian $\psi(s) = s$, so $\mathcal{J} = 1$; every other unit-variance law has $\mathcal{J} > 1$, because $\E[(\psi(s) - s)^2] = \mathcal{J} - 2 + 1 = \mathcal{J} - 1$ vanishes only when $\psi(s) = s$.

The gain matrix and the interference-to-signal ratio增益矩阵与干扰信号比

Let $\hat\bW$ be an estimate of the unmixing matrix computed from $T$ samples. Its quality is judged not by $\hat\bW$ itself, which depends on the arbitrary scaling of the outputs, but by what it does to the sources.

设 $\hat\bW$ 是由 $T$ 个样本算出的解混矩阵估计。评价它的好坏，不看 $\hat\bW$ 本身（它依赖于输出的任意尺度），而看它对源做了什么。

The gain matrix of $\hat\bW$ is $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$, so that the outputs are $\hat\by = \hat\bP\bs$. Order the outputs so that output $i$ estimates source $i$, and let $\hat\bD = \diag(\hat P_{11}, \dots, \hat P_{nn})$. The gain error is the matrix $\bepsilon$ with zero diagonal defined by

$\hat\bW$ 的增益矩阵是 $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$，于是输出为 $\hat\by = \hat\bP\bs$。把输出排好顺序，使第 $i$ 个输出估计第 $i$ 个源，并记 $\hat\bD = \diag(\hat P_{11}, \dots, \hat P_{nn})$。增益误差是对角元为零的矩阵 $\bepsilon$，定义为

$$\hat\bD^{-1}\hat\bP = \bI + \bepsilon, \qquad \varepsilon_{ij} = \frac{\hat P_{ij}}{\hat P_{ii}} \quad (i \ne j).$$

Output $i$ is then $\hat P_{ii}\bigl(s_i + \sum_{j \ne i}\varepsilon_{ij}s_j\bigr)$: after the unrecoverable scale $\hat P_{ii}$ has been divided out, $\varepsilon_{ij}$ is the amount of source $j$ that leaks into the estimate of source $i$. Perfect separation means $\bepsilon = \bzero$.

于是第 $i$ 个输出等于 $\hat P_{ii}\bigl(s_i + \sum_{j \ne i}\varepsilon_{ij}s_j\bigr)$：除掉无法恢复的尺度 $\hat P_{ii}$ 之后，$\varepsilon_{ij}$ 就是源 $j$ 漏进源 $i$ 的估计中的分量。完全分离意味着 $\bepsilon = \bzero$。

The interference-to-signal ratio of source $j$ in output $i$ is $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$, $i \ne j$, where the expectation is over the random sample. The total ISR of output $i$ is $\sum_{j \ne i}\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}$.

源 $j$ 在输出 $i$ 中的干扰信号比（ISR）定义为 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$（$i \ne j$），期望对随机样本求取。输出 $i$ 的总 ISR 为 $\sum_{j \ne i}\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}$。

Because the sources have unit variance, $\varepsilon_{ij}^2$ is the ratio of the power that source $j$ contributes to output $i$ to the power of the wanted source in that output. ISRs are often quoted in decibels, $10\log_{10}\mathrm{ISR}$; the reciprocal is the signal-to-interference ratio (SIR). In a simulation $\bA$ is known, so $\bepsilon$ can be computed in every run and the ISR estimated by averaging $\varepsilon_{ij}^2$ over runs. For the estimators of this chapter, $\sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon_{ij}$ converges in distribution to a normal law with mean zero and some variance $v_{ij}$, the asymptotic variance. As is customary, we then use $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} \approx v_{ij}/T$, replacing the exact mean square by its asymptotic value; checks this approximation numerically. The constant $v_{ij}$ measures the statistical efficiency of a method independently of $T$.

由于源的方差为一，$\varepsilon_{ij}^2$ 就是源 $j$ 在输出 $i$ 中贡献的功率与目标源功率之比。ISR 常以分贝表示，即 $10\log_{10}\mathrm{ISR}$；其倒数是信干比（SIR）。做模拟时 $\bA$ 已知，因此每次运行都能算出 $\bepsilon$，再对多次运行的 $\varepsilon_{ij}^2$ 求平均即可估计 ISR。对本章讨论的估计量，$\sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon_{ij}$ 依分布收敛到均值为零、方差为某个 $v_{ij}$ 的正态分布，$v_{ij}$ 称为渐近方差。按惯例，我们用 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} \approx v_{ij}/T$，即以渐近值代替精确的均方；用数值方法检验这一近似。常数 $v_{ij}$ 衡量的是方法本身的统计效率，与 $T$ 无关。

Why the mixing matrix does not matter为什么混合矩阵无关紧要

Most ICA estimators are defined by equations that involve the data only through the outputs. Maximum likelihood and Infomax solve $\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top] = \bI$; the fixed points of FastICA satisfy whitening and fixed-point conditions on $\by$; JADE optimizes cumulants of $\by$. Such estimators are equivariant (Cardoso & Laheld, 1996; see also ), and equivariance has a strong consequence for performance.

大多数 ICA 估计量由一组只通过输出依赖数据的方程定义。最大似然与 Infomax 求解 $\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top] = \bI$；FastICA 的不动点满足关于 $\by$ 的白化条件和不动点条件；JADE 优化的是 $\by$ 的累积量。这样的估计量具有等变性（Cardoso & Laheld, 1996；另见），而等变性对性能有一个很强的推论。

Suppose $\hat\bW$ solves equations $\mathbf{H}(\hat\bW\bX) = \bzero$ that depend on the data matrix $\bX$ only through the outputs $\hat\bW\bX$, and that when several solutions exist, the one returned is chosen by a rule that also depends only on the outputs. Then the gain matrix $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$ obtained from $\bX = \bA\bS$ equals the estimate obtained from the sources $\bS$ themselves. In particular, the distributions of $\hat\bP$, $\bepsilon$ and every ISR are the same for all invertible $\bA$.

设 $\hat\bW$ 是方程 $\mathbf{H}(\hat\bW\bX) = \bzero$ 的解，这组方程只通过输出 $\hat\bW\bX$ 依赖数据矩阵 $\bX$；若有多个解，则返回哪一个也只由输出决定。那么由 $\bX = \bA\bS$ 得到的增益矩阵 $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$，等于直接用源 $\bS$ 算出的估计。特别地，对所有可逆的 $\bA$，$\hat\bP$、$\bepsilon$ 以及每个 ISR 的分布都相同。

Let $\bW_{\bS}$ be the solution returned for the data $\bS$. The matrix $\bW_{\bS}\bA^{-1}$ produces from $\bX = \bA\bS$ exactly the same outputs, $(\bW_{\bS}\bA^{-1})(\bA\bS) = \bW_{\bS}\bS$, so it solves the equations for $\bX$ and is the one returned. Hence $\hat\bW(\bA\bS) = \hat\bW(\bS)\,\bA^{-1}$ and $\hat\bP = \hat\bW(\bS)$, which does not involve $\bA$.

记 $\bW_{\bS}$ 为对数据 $\bS$ 返回的解。矩阵 $\bW_{\bS}\bA^{-1}$ 作用于 $\bX = \bA\bS$ 时给出完全相同的输出，$(\bW_{\bS}\bA^{-1})(\bA\bS) = \bW_{\bS}\bS$，因此它是关于 $\bX$ 的方程的解，而且就是被返回的那一个。于是 $\hat\bW(\bA\bS) = \hat\bW(\bS)\,\bA^{-1}$，从而 $\hat\bP = \hat\bW(\bS)$，与 $\bA$ 无关。

Simulation studies can therefore take $\bA = \bI$ without loss of generality, and an ill-conditioned mixing matrix does not by itself make an equivariant estimator less accurate. What can depend on $\bA$ is the path of an iterative algorithm that is not itself equivariant, such as plain gradient ascent with a fixed step size, and the numerical round-off when $\bA$ is nearly singular.

因此，模拟研究可以不失一般性地取 $\bA = \bI$；混合矩阵病态本身也不会降低等变估计量的精度。可能依赖于 $\bA$ 的，是本身不具等变性的迭代算法的收敛路径（例如固定步长的普通梯度上升），以及 $\bA$ 接近奇异时的数值舍入误差。

Amari's performance indexAmari 性能指标

The ISR requires the outputs to be matched to the sources and the scales to be removed. Amari, Cichocki and Yang (1996) proposed a single number that needs no matching of outputs to sources. It does depend on the scales of the outputs, which are therefore normalized first (see the invariance property below). For an $n \times n$ gain matrix $\bP = (p_{ij})$,

计算 ISR 需要先把输出与源配对，并除去尺度。Amari、Cichocki 和 Yang（1996）提出了一个无需把输出与源配对的单一指标。不过它依赖于输出的尺度，因此要先把输出归一化（见下面的不变性）。对 $n \times n$ 的增益矩阵 $\bP = (p_{ij})$，定义

$$E(\bP) = \sum_{i=1}^n\Bigl(\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{|p_{ij}|}{\max_k|p_{ik}|} - 1\Bigr) + \sum_{j=1}^n\Bigl(\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{|p_{ij}|}{\max_k|p_{kj}|} - 1\Bigr).$$

Its main properties follow directly from the definition.

它的主要性质可以直接从定义得出。

  • Zero exactly at separation. Each bracket is a sum of ratios in $[0, 1]$, one of which equals $1$, so it is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when its row (or column) has a single nonzero entry. Hence $E(\bP) \ge 0$, with equality if and only if $\bP = \bPi\bD$ as in .
  • Range. Each bracket is at most $n - 1$, so $0 \le E(\bP) \le 2n(n-1)$; the normalized index $E/(2n(n-1))$ lies in $[0, 1]$.
  • Invariance. $E$ is unchanged by permutations of the rows or of the columns. A row bracket is unchanged when its row is rescaled and a column bracket when its column is rescaled, but rescaling a row changes the column brackets. In practice $E$ is computed after normalizing every output to unit variance, that is, every row of $\hat\bP$ to unit Euclidean norm (the sources having unit variance).
  • Relation to the ISR. With the rows normalized and the outputs matched to the sources, $|\hat P_{ii}| = 1 + O(\|\bepsilon\|^2)$ and $E(\hat\bP) = 2\sum_{i \ne j}|\varepsilon_{ij}| + O(\|\bepsilon\|^2)$. If $\sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon_{ij}$ is approximately normal, $\E|\varepsilon_{ij}| \approx \sqrt{2\,\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}/\pi}$, so $\E[E(\hat\bP)] \approx 2\sqrt{2/\pi}\,\sum_{i \ne j}\sqrt{\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}}$. The Amari index decreases like $1/\sqrt{T}$, the ISR like $1/T$: halving the index takes four times as many samples.
  • 恰在分离时为零。每个括号都是若干个 $[0, 1]$ 中的比值之和，其中恰有一个等于 $1$，所以它非负，并且当且仅当该行（或该列）只有一个非零元时为零。因此 $E(\bP) \ge 0$，等号成立当且仅当 $\bP = \bPi\bD$，与一致。
  • 取值范围。每个括号至多为 $n - 1$，所以 $0 \le E(\bP) \le 2n(n-1)$；归一化指标 $E/(2n(n-1))$ 落在 $[0, 1]$ 中。
  • 不变性。对行或列作置换，$E$ 不变。某一行乘以常数时，该行的括号不变；某一列乘以常数时，该列的括号不变；但对行作缩放会改变各列的括号。实践中先把每个输出归一化为单位方差，即把 $\hat\bP$ 的每一行归一化为单位欧氏范数（源的方差为一），再计算 $E$。
  • 与 ISR 的关系。行归一化且输出与源配好对之后，$|\hat P_{ii}| = 1 + O(\|\bepsilon\|^2)$，并且 $E(\hat\bP) = 2\sum_{i \ne j}|\varepsilon_{ij}| + O(\|\bepsilon\|^2)$。若 $\sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon_{ij}$ 近似服从正态分布，则 $\E|\varepsilon_{ij}| \approx \sqrt{2\,\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}/\pi}$，于是 $\E[E(\hat\bP)] \approx 2\sqrt{2/\pi}\,\sum_{i \ne j}\sqrt{\mathrm{ISR}_{ij}}$。Amari 指标以 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 的速度下降，ISR 则以 $1/T$ 的速度下降：要把指标减半，需要四倍的样本。

Take the gain matrix printed in , $\hat\bP = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0.03 & 1.00 \\ -1.00 & 0.02 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, whose rows already have norm $1.00$ to two decimals. The row brackets are $0.03$ and $0.02$, the column brackets $0.03$ and $0.02$, so $E(\hat\bP) = 0.10$ and the normalized index is $0.025$. Output 1 estimates $s_2$ and output 2 estimates $s_1$. Reordering the outputs accordingly gives $\varepsilon_{12} = 0.02/(-1.00)$ (source 2 in the estimate of source 1) and $\varepsilon_{21} = 0.03/1.00$ (source 1 in the estimate of source 2). The realized interference-to-signal ratios are $\varepsilon_{12}^2 = 4 \times 10^{-4}$ ($-34.0$ dB) and $\varepsilon_{21}^2 = 9 \times 10^{-4}$ ($-30.5$ dB), and indeed $2(|\varepsilon_{12}| + |\varepsilon_{21}|) = 0.10 = E(\hat\bP)$.

取中输出的增益矩阵 $\hat\bP = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0.03 & 1.00 \\ -1.00 & 0.02 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，保留两位小数时它的各行范数已经是 $1.00$。两个行括号分别是 $0.03$ 和 $0.02$，两个列括号也是 $0.03$ 和 $0.02$，所以 $E(\hat\bP) = 0.10$，归一化指标为 $0.025$。输出 1 估计的是 $s_2$，输出 2 估计的是 $s_1$。相应地重排输出，得到 $\varepsilon_{12} = 0.02/(-1.00)$（源 2 漏进源 1 的估计中）和 $\varepsilon_{21} = 0.03/1.00$（源 1 漏进源 2 的估计中）。这次实现的干扰信号比为 $\varepsilon_{12}^2 = 4 \times 10^{-4}$（$-34.0$ dB）和 $\varepsilon_{21}^2 = 9 \times 10^{-4}$（$-30.5$ dB），并且确有 $2(|\varepsilon_{12}| + |\varepsilon_{21}|) = 0.10 = E(\hat\bP)$。

Most ICA estimators are solutions of estimating equations $\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T \mathbf{F}(\bx(t); \btheta) = \bzero$ whose population version $\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta)] = \bzero$ holds at the true parameter $\btheta_0$; here $\btheta$ collects the entries of $\bW$ and $\btheta_0$ is a separating matrix. The theory of such M-estimators (; van der Vaart, 1998) states that if $\btheta_0$ is an isolated root, $\mathbf{F}$ is smooth enough and $\bM = \E[\partial\mathbf{F}/\partial\btheta^\top]$ is invertible at $\btheta_0$, then the root $\hat\btheta$ near $\btheta_0$ is consistent and

大多数 ICA 估计量都是估计方程 $\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T \mathbf{F}(\bx(t); \btheta) = \bzero$ 的解，其总体形式 $\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta)] = \bzero$ 在真实参数 $\btheta_0$ 处成立；这里 $\btheta$ 由 $\bW$ 的元素组成，$\btheta_0$ 是一个分离矩阵。这类 M 估计量的理论（；van der Vaart, 1998）指出：若 $\btheta_0$ 是孤立根，$\mathbf{F}$ 足够光滑，且 $\bM = \E[\partial\mathbf{F}/\partial\btheta^\top]$ 在 $\btheta_0$ 处可逆，则 $\btheta_0$ 附近的根 $\hat\btheta$ 是相合的，并且

$$\sqrt{T}\,(\hat\btheta - \btheta_0) \ \xrightarrow{d}\ \Ncal\bigl(\bzero,\ \bM^{-1}\bSigma\bM^{-\top}\bigr), \qquad \bSigma = \E\bigl[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)\,\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)^\top\bigr].$$

The argument is a first-order expansion, $\bzero = \hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \hat\btheta)] \approx \hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)] + \bM(\hat\btheta - \btheta_0)$, followed by the central limit theorem for $\hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)]$. The errors are therefore of order $1/\sqrt{T}$ and the ISR of order $1/T$. For ICA the expansion has a special structure: it splits into separate $2 \times 2$ problems, one for each pair of sources.

论证就是一阶展开 $\bzero = \hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \hat\btheta)] \approx \hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)] + \bM(\hat\btheta - \btheta_0)$，再对 $\hat\E[\mathbf{F}(\bx; \btheta_0)]$ 应用中心极限定理。因此误差是 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 量级，ISR 是 $1/T$ 量级。对 ICA 而言，这个展开有特殊的结构：它分解为互不相干的若干个 $2 \times 2$ 问题，每对源一个。

The price of exact decorrelation严格去相关的代价

Many algorithms whiten with the sample covariance and then rotate (symmetric FastICA, JADE). Their outputs are exactly decorrelated in the sample, $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \bI$, and this constraint alone fixes half of the error.

许多算法先用样本协方差白化、再做旋转（对称 FastICA、JADE）。它们的输出在样本上严格去相关，即 $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \bI$，而仅这一约束就确定了误差的一半。

Suppose the outputs satisfy $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \bI$ and, after their order and signs have been fixed, $\hat\bP - \bI = O_p(T^{-1/2})$. Then for $i \ne j$, $\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji} = -\hat\E[s_is_j] + o_p(T^{-1/2})$, so $\sqrt{T}(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ converges in distribution to $\Ncal(0, 1)$. Consequently, if $(\sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ji})$ converges in distribution to a limit with variances $v_{ij}$ and $v_{ji}$, then $v_{ij} + v_{ji} \ge \frac12$; to first order, $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$, whatever the source distributions and the rotation criterion.

设输出满足 $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \bI$，并且固定输出的顺序与符号之后 $\hat\bP - \bI = O_p(T^{-1/2})$。则对 $i \ne j$ 有 $\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji} = -\hat\E[s_is_j] + o_p(T^{-1/2})$，从而 $\sqrt{T}(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(0, 1)$。因此，若 $(\sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ji})$ 依分布收敛到某个方差分别为 $v_{ij}$ 与 $v_{ji}$ 的极限，则 $v_{ij} + v_{ji} \ge \frac12$；在一阶近似下，$\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$，无论源的分布和旋转准则如何。

We have $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top$ with $\hat\bC_{\bs} = \hat\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}$. For $i \ne j$, $\Delta_{ij}$ is the average of the i.i.d. zero-mean terms $s_i(t)s_j(t)$, whose variance is $\E[s_i^2s_j^2] = 1$, so $\sqrt{T}\,\Delta_{ij}$ converges in distribution to $\Ncal(0, 1)$; by the law of large numbers $\boldsymbol{\Delta} \to \bzero$ in probability. Write $\hat\bP = (\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)$ with $\boldsymbol{\delta} = \hat\bD - \bI$ diagonal; by hypothesis $\boldsymbol{\delta}$ and $\bepsilon$ are $O_p(T^{-1/2})$. Expanding $(\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)^\top(\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta}) = \bI$ gives $2\boldsymbol{\delta} + \bepsilon + \bepsilon^\top + \boldsymbol{\Delta} = o_p(T^{-1/2})$, since every other term is a product of a factor of order $T^{-1/2}$ and a factor tending to zero. The off-diagonal part is the first claim. Finally, if $(\sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ji})$ converges to $(X, Y)$, then $X + Y \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$, and $1 = \Var(X + Y) \le 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$ because $\Var(X + Y) + \Var(X - Y) = 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$.

我们有 $\hat\E[\hat\by\hat\by^\top] = \hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top$，其中 $\hat\bC_{\bs} = \hat\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}$。对 $i \ne j$，$\Delta_{ij}$ 是独立同分布、均值为零的项 $s_i(t)s_j(t)$ 的平均，这些项的方差为 $\E[s_i^2s_j^2] = 1$，所以 $\sqrt{T}\,\Delta_{ij}$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(0, 1)$；由大数定律，$\boldsymbol{\Delta}$ 依概率收敛到 $\bzero$。把 $\hat\bP$ 写成 $(\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)$，其中 $\boldsymbol{\delta} = \hat\bD - \bI$ 为对角矩阵；由假设，$\boldsymbol{\delta}$ 与 $\bepsilon$ 都是 $O_p(T^{-1/2})$。展开 $(\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)^\top(\bI + \boldsymbol{\delta}) = \bI$，得 $2\boldsymbol{\delta} + \bepsilon + \bepsilon^\top + \boldsymbol{\Delta} = o_p(T^{-1/2})$，因为其余每一项都是一个 $T^{-1/2}$ 量级的因子与一个趋于零的因子之积。其非对角部分就是第一个结论。最后，若 $(\sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt{T}\varepsilon_{ji})$ 依分布收敛到 $(X, Y)$，则 $X + Y \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$；又因为 $\Var(X + Y) + \Var(X - Y) = 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$，所以 $1 = \Var(X + Y) \le 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$。

One-unit estimators and the optimal nonlinearity单单元估计量与最优非线性函数

One-unit FastICA () centers the data, whitens them with the sample covariance, $\bz = \hat\bV\bx$, and returns a unit vector $\bw$ with $\hat\E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \lambda\bw$ for some scalar $\lambda$: the Lagrange condition for an extremum of $\hat\E[G(\bw^\top\bz)]$ on the sphere, which the update solves. Because the sources have zero mean, we first analyse the estimator without the centering step and then state what centering changes. Suppose the solution estimates source $i$, and write $y = \bw^\top\bz = \bq^\top\bs$ with $\bq = (\hat\bV\bA)^\top\bw$, the row of the gain matrix that produces $y$. The gain errors of this output are $\varepsilon_k = q_k/q_i$, $k \ne i$.

单单元 FastICA（）先把数据中心化，再用样本协方差白化，$\bz = \hat\bV\bx$，然后返回一个满足 $\hat\E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \lambda\bw$（$\lambda$ 为某个标量）的单位向量 $\bw$：这是 $\hat\E[G(\bw^\top\bz)]$ 在球面上取极值的 Lagrange 条件，更新式 求解的正是它。由于源的均值为零，我们先分析不做中心化的估计量，再说明中心化带来的变化。设这个解估计的是源 $i$，记 $y = \bw^\top\bz = \bq^\top\bs$，其中 $\bq = (\hat\bV\bA)^\top\bw$ 是增益矩阵中产生 $y$ 的那一行。这个输出的增益误差为 $\varepsilon_k = q_k/q_i$，$k \ne i$。

Let $g$ be differentiable, let the expectations below exist, and let $\E[s_ig(s_i) - g'(s_i)] \ne 0$. For the solution $\bw$ of $\hat\E[\bz\,g(\bw^\top\bz)] = \lambda\bw$, $\|\bw\| = 1$, near the direction of source $i$, and for every $k \ne i$, $\sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon_k$ is asymptotically normal with mean zero and variance

$$V_g = \frac{\E[g^2(s_i)] - \bigl(\E[s_i\,g(s_i)]\bigr)^2}{\bigl(\E[s_i\,g(s_i) - g'(s_i)]\bigr)^2}.$$

The variance depends on the distribution of $s_i$ alone: not on the other sources and, by , not on $\bA$ (Hyvärinen, 1997; 1999). If the data are centered with the sample mean before whitening, as in , the same holds with $\E[g^2(s_i)]$ in the numerator replaced by $\Var g(s_i) = \E[g^2(s_i)] - (\E[g(s_i)])^2$ (Wei, 2015).

Limit. The right side is an average of i.i.d. zero-mean terms with variance $\E[s_k^2]\,\E[(g(s_1) - \mu s_1)^2] = \E[g^2(s_1)] - \mu^2$, using $\E[s_1g(s_1)] = \mu$ and $\E[s_1^2] = 1$. The central limit theorem and division by $\mu - \E[g'(s_1)]$ give the result.

Centering. If the data are centered, the argument applies with $\bs$ replaced by $\bs - \bar\bs$, where $\bar\bs$ is the sample mean of the sources. To first order the only change is that $\hat\E[s_k\,g(s_1)]$ gains the term $-\bar s_k\,\E[g(s_1)]$. 中心化。若先把数据中心化，只需把 $\bs$ 换成 $\bs - \bar\bs$，上述论证依然成立，其中 $\bar\bs$ 是源的样本均值。在一阶近似下，唯一的变化是 $\hat\E[s_k\,g(s_1)]$ 多出一项 $-\bar s_k\,\E[g(s_1)]$。于是右边的 $g(s_1)$ 换成了 $g(s_1) - \E[g(s_1)]$，方差变为 $\E[g^2(s_1)] - \mu^2 - (\E[g(s_1)])^2$。

Three features of matter in practice. The denominator is the quantity $\E[s\,g(s) - g'(s)]$ that decides whether the source direction is an extremum of the FastICA contrast (); for a Gaussian source with $f = g$ gives $\E[s\,g(s)] = \E[g'(s)]$, so the variance is infinite. Second, $V_g$ does not change when $g$ is multiplied by a nonzero constant or when a multiple of $s$ is added to $g$: only the nonlinear part of $g$ matters. Checking the theory by simulation用模拟检验理论

The script below estimates $T \cdot \mathrm{ISR}$ for one-unit FastICA on a Laplace source mixed with two uniform sources, for $g = \tanh$ and $g(u) = u^3$, and compares it with $V_g$ computed by Monte Carlo integration. Every run draws new sources and whitens with the sample covariance; the iteration starts at the true direction, so that the run measures the accuracy of the fixed point near source 1 rather than the chance of converging to another source.

下面的脚本对一个拉普拉斯源与两个均匀源的混合运行单单元 FastICA，分别取 $g = \tanh$ 和 $g(u) = u^3$，估计 $T \cdot \mathrm{ISR}$，并与用蒙特卡洛积分算出的 $V_g$ 比较。每次运行都重新抽取源，并用样本协方差白化；迭代从真实方向出发，因此测得的是源 1 附近那个不动点的精度，而不是收敛到其他源的概率。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
nonlin = {"tanh": (np.tanh, lambda u: 1 - np.tanh(u) ** 2),
          "pow3": (lambda u: u ** 3, lambda u: 3 * u ** 2)}
s = rng.laplace(0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), 10**7)     # unit-variance Laplace source

n, T, runs = 3, 5000, 2000
A = rng.normal(size=(n, n))
for name, (g, dg) in nonlin.items():
    # predicted asymptotic variance V_g, expectations by Monte Carlo
    Esg, Edg = np.mean(s * g(s)), np.mean(dg(s))
    V_g = (np.mean(g(s) ** 2) - Esg ** 2) / (Esg - Edg) ** 2
    eps2 = []
    for r in range(runs):
        S = np.vstack([rng.laplace(0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), T),  # target
                       rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), (2, T))])
        X = A @ S
        X -= X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
        d, E = np.linalg.eigh(X @ X.T / T)
        V = E @ np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T            # sample whitening
        Z = V @ X
        w = np.linalg.solve((V @ A).T, np.eye(n)[0])  # start at target
        w /= np.linalg.norm(w)
        for it in range(200):                       # one-unit FastICA
            y = w @ Z
            w_new = Z @ g(y) / T - np.mean(dg(y)) * w
            w_new /= np.linalg.norm(w_new)
            done = 1 - abs(w_new @ w) < 1e-12
            w = w_new
            if done:
                break
        q = w @ V @ A                               # row of gain matrix
        eps2.extend((q[1:] / q[0]) ** 2)
    est = T * np.mean(eps2)
    se = T * np.std(eps2) / np.sqrt(len(eps2))
    print(f"{name}: predicted V_g = {V_g:.2f}, "
          f"observed T*ISR = {est:.2f} +/- {2 * se:.2f}")

The Cramér–Rao boundCramér–Rao 界

The variances computed so far belong to particular estimators. The Cramér–Rao bound limits all of them. Suppose the source densities are known, so that the unmixing matrix is the only unknown, and parametrize it around the truth as $\bW = (\bI + \mathbf{E})\bA^{-1}$. The gain matrix is then $\bP = \bI + \mathbf{E}$: the off-diagonal entries $E_{ij}$ are the separation errors and the diagonal entries are scale parameters. By the change of variables behind , one observation has log-density

前面算出的方差都属于具体的估计量，Cramér–Rao 界则对所有估计量都成立。假设源密度已知，唯一的未知量是解混矩阵，并在真值附近把它参数化为 $\bW = (\bI + \mathbf{E})\bA^{-1}$。于是增益矩阵为 $\bP = \bI + \mathbf{E}$：非对角元 $E_{ij}$ 就是分离误差，对角元是尺度参数。由 背后的变量替换，单个观测的对数密度为

$$\log p(\bx; \mathbf{E}) = \log|\det(\bI + \mathbf{E})| - \log|\det\bA| + \sum_{i=1}^n\log p_i(y_i), \qquad \by = (\bI + \mathbf{E})\,\bs.$$

Assume the standing assumptions, with source densities $p_i$ that are absolutely continuous and positive on $\R$, have finite Fisher information $\mathcal{J}_i$, and satisfy the usual regularity conditions of the Cramér–Rao inequality. At $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ the Fisher information matrix of one observation is block diagonal: the diagonal (scale) parameters form one block, and each pair $(E_{ij}, E_{ji})$, $i < j$, forms the block

在本章的基本假设下，设源密度 $p_i$ 在 $\R$ 上绝对连续且处处为正，Fisher 信息 $\mathcal{J}_i$ 有限，并满足 Cramér–Rao 不等式通常的正则条件。则在 $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ 处，单个观测的 Fisher 信息矩阵是分块对角的：对角（尺度）参数自成一块，每一对 $(E_{ij}, E_{ji})$（$i < j$）构成一块

$$\mathbf{F}_{ij} = \begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{J}_i & 1 \\ 1 & \mathcal{J}_j \end{pmatrix}.$$

If $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j > 1$, every estimator $\hat{\mathbf{E}}$ that is unbiased in a neighbourhood of $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ and uses $T$ i.i.d. observations satisfies

若 $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j > 1$，则任何在 $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ 的某个邻域内无偏、并使用 $T$ 个独立同分布观测的估计量 $\hat{\mathbf{E}}$ 都满足

$$\Var(\hat E_{ij}) \ \ge\ \mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ij}) = \frac{1}{T}\,\frac{\mathcal{J}_j}{\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1}.$$

To first order $\hat E_{ij}$ is the gain error $\varepsilon_{ij}$ of the estimate $\hat\bW = (\bI + \hat{\mathbf{E}})\bA^{-1}$ (Tichavský, Koldovský & Oja, 2006; see also Pham & Garat, 1997).

在一阶近似下，$\hat E_{ij}$ 就是估计 $\hat\bW = (\bI + \hat{\mathbf{E}})\bA^{-1}$ 的增益误差 $\varepsilon_{ij}$（Tichavský, Koldovský & Oja, 2006；另见 Pham & Garat, 1997）。

Since $\partial\log|\det(\bI + \mathbf{E})|/\partial E_{ij} = [(\bI + \mathbf{E})^{-\top}]_{ij}$ () and $\partial\log p_i(y_i)/\partial E_{ij} = (p_i'/p_i)(y_i)\,s_j$, the score for $E_{ij}$ at $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ is $\Gamma_{ij} = \delta_{ij} - \psi_i(s_i)\,s_j$. For $i \ne j$ and $k \ne l$, $\E[\Gamma_{ij}\Gamma_{kl}] = \E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j\,\psi_k(s_k)s_l]$. By independence this factorizes over the sources, and any factor of the form $\E[s_l]$ or $\E[\psi_k(s_k)]$ vanishes (the latter by with $f = 1$). The only nonzero cases are $(k, l) = (i, j)$, giving $\E[\psi_i^2(s_i)]\,\E[s_j^2] = \mathcal{J}_i$, and $(k, l) = (j, i)$, giving $\E[\psi_i(s_i)s_i]\,\E[\psi_j(s_j)s_j] = 1$. In the same way $\E[\Gamma_{ij}\Gamma_{kk}] = -\E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j] + \E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j\,\psi_k(s_k)s_k] = 0$ for $i \ne j$, because every term contains $\E[s_j]$ or $\E[\psi_i(s_i)]$. This proves the block structure. For $T$ i.i.d. observations the information is $T$ times larger, and the Cramér–Rao inequality bounds $\Var(\hat E_{ij})$ by the corresponding diagonal entry of the inverse, $[(T\mathbf{F}_{ij})^{-1}]_{11}$, where

由于 $\partial\log|\det(\bI + \mathbf{E})|/\partial E_{ij} = [(\bI + \mathbf{E})^{-\top}]_{ij}$（），且 $\partial\log p_i(y_i)/\partial E_{ij} = (p_i'/p_i)(y_i)\,s_j$，在 $\mathbf{E} = \bzero$ 处 $E_{ij}$ 的得分为 $\Gamma_{ij} = \delta_{ij} - \psi_i(s_i)\,s_j$。对 $i \ne j$、$k \ne l$，有 $\E[\Gamma_{ij}\Gamma_{kl}] = \E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j\,\psi_k(s_k)s_l]$。由独立性，这个期望按源分解为乘积，而形如 $\E[s_l]$ 或 $\E[\psi_k(s_k)]$ 的因子都为零（后者由 取 $f = 1$ 得到）。只有两种情形不为零：$(k, l) = (i, j)$ 给出 $\E[\psi_i^2(s_i)]\,\E[s_j^2] = \mathcal{J}_i$，$(k, l) = (j, i)$ 给出 $\E[\psi_i(s_i)s_i]\,\E[\psi_j(s_j)s_j] = 1$。同理，对 $i \ne j$ 有 $\E[\Gamma_{ij}\Gamma_{kk}] = -\E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j] + \E[\psi_i(s_i)s_j\,\psi_k(s_k)s_k] = 0$，因为每一项都含有 $\E[s_j]$ 或 $\E[\psi_i(s_i)]$。分块结构由此得证。$T$ 个独立同分布观测的信息量是单个观测的 $T$ 倍，Cramér–Rao 不等式用逆矩阵的相应对角元 $[(T\mathbf{F}_{ij})^{-1}]_{11}$ 给出 $\Var(\hat E_{ij})$ 的下界，其中

$$\mathbf{F}_{ij}^{-1} = \frac{1}{\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1}\begin{pmatrix} \mathcal{J}_j & -1 \\ -1 & \mathcal{J}_i \end{pmatrix}.$$

Finally $\varepsilon_{ij} = \hat E_{ij}/(1 + \hat E_{ii})$ agrees with $\hat E_{ij}$ to first order.

最后，$\varepsilon_{ij} = \hat E_{ij}/(1 + \hat E_{ii})$ 在一阶近似下与 $\hat E_{ij}$ 相同。

Practical estimators are biased at finite $T$, so the bound is used in its asymptotic sense: under standard regularity conditions, no regular estimator that is consistent and asymptotically normal has an asymptotic variance $v_{ij}$ below $\mathcal{J}_j/(\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1)$ (van der Vaart, 1998). Comparing with shows that maximum likelihood with the true scores attains the bound.

实际的估计量在有限 $T$ 下是有偏的，因此这个界按渐近意义使用：在标准的正则条件下，任何相合且渐近正态的正则估计量，其渐近方差 $v_{ij}$ 都不会低于 $\mathcal{J}_j/(\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1)$（van der Vaart, 1998）。把 与比较可知，用真实得分函数的最大似然达到了这个界。

What the bound says这个界说明了什么

  • Two Gaussian sources. If $\mathcal{J}_i = \mathcal{J}_j = 1$, the block is singular and the bound is infinite: the data carry no information about the rotation within the pair, as shows from the identifiability side.
  • One Gaussian source. If $\mathcal{J}_j = 1 < \mathcal{J}_i$, then $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ij}) = 1/(T(\mathcal{J}_i - 1))$ but $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ji}) = \mathcal{J}_i/(T(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)) > 1/T$. The non-Gaussian source is located by its non-Gaussianity; the Gaussian output can only be found as the direction uncorrelated with it, and it inherits the sampling error of the covariance ().
  • Nearly Gaussian sources. For two sources with the same law the bound is $\mathcal{J}/(T(\mathcal{J}^2 - 1))$. With $T = 1000$ it is $6.7 \times 10^{-4}$ ($-31.8$ dB) for Laplace sources ($\mathcal{J} = 2$) but $5.4 \times 10^{-3}$ ($-22.7$ dB) for logistic sources ($\mathcal{J} \approx 1.097$): identifiable is not the same as easy to estimate ().
  • Strongly non-Gaussian sources. As $\mathcal{J}_j \to \infty$, $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ij}) \to 1/(T\mathcal{J}_i)$ and $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ji}) \to 0$.
  • Bounded sources. A uniform density jumps at the ends of its support, the regularity conditions fail, and the bound is not defined (Tichavský et al., 2006). Smooth densities that approach the uniform law, such as the generalized Gaussian with large exponent in , have $\mathcal{J} \to \infty$, which correctly suggests that such sources are easy to separate.
  • 两个高斯源。若 $\mathcal{J}_i = \mathcal{J}_j = 1$，则分块 奇异，界为无穷大：数据中没有关于这一对源内部旋转的任何信息，这与从可辨识性角度得到的结论一致。
  • 一个高斯源。若 $\mathcal{J}_j = 1 < \mathcal{J}_i$，则 $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ij}) = 1/(T(\mathcal{J}_i - 1))$，但 $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ji}) = \mathcal{J}_i/(T(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)) > 1/T$。非高斯源靠它的非高斯性定位；高斯输出只能作为与之不相关的方向来确定，因而继承了协方差的抽样误差（）。
  • 接近高斯的源。对同分布的两个源，界为 $\mathcal{J}/(T(\mathcal{J}^2 - 1))$。取 $T = 1000$，拉普拉斯源（$\mathcal{J} = 2$）的界为 $6.7 \times 10^{-4}$（$-31.8$ dB），逻辑斯谛源（$\mathcal{J} \approx 1.097$）的界却是 $5.4 \times 10^{-3}$（$-22.7$ dB）：可辨识并不等于容易估计（）。
  • 强非高斯的源。当 $\mathcal{J}_j \to \infty$ 时，$\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ij}) \to 1/(T\mathcal{J}_i)$，而 $\mathrm{CRB}(\varepsilon_{ji}) \to 0$。
  • 有界的源。均匀分布的密度在支撑集端点处跳跃，正则条件不成立，这个界没有定义（Tichavský et al., 2006）。逼近均匀分布的光滑密度（例如中指数很大的广义高斯分布）的 $\mathcal{J} \to \infty$，这正确地提示了这类源很容易分离。

The densities are unknown in practice, but each off-diagonal score $\psi_i(s_i)s_j$ is uncorrelated with every zero-mean function of a single source, so perturbing the marginal densities carries no first-order information about $E_{ij}$; this is why the same bound remains the benchmark when the densities must be learned from the data (see Amari & Cardoso, 1997, for the semiparametric theory of ICA estimating functions). Other estimators fall short of the bound, for reasons that can be identified.

用真实得分函数的最大似然可以达到（）。实践中密度是未知的；但每个非对角得分 $\psi_i(s_i)s_j$ 都与任何只依赖单个源的零均值函数不相关，因此扰动边缘密度不会带来关于 $E_{ij}$ 的一阶信息；这就是为什么密度需要从数据中学习时，这个界仍然是衡量标准（ICA 估计函数的半参数理论见 Amari & Cardoso, 1997）。其他估计量达不到这个界，而原因都可以说清楚。

For $k \ne i$, the optimal one-unit variance and the bound satisfy $\dfrac{1}{\mathcal{J}_i - 1} \ge \dfrac{\mathcal{J}_k}{\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_k - 1}$, with equality if and only if $\mathcal{J}_k = 1$.

对 $k \ne i$，单单元估计量的最优方差与这个界满足 $\dfrac{1}{\mathcal{J}_i - 1} \ge \dfrac{\mathcal{J}_k}{\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_k - 1}$，等号成立当且仅当 $\mathcal{J}_k = 1$。

Both denominators are positive, and cross-multiplying gives $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_k - 1 \ge \mathcal{J}_k(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)$, that is, $\mathcal{J}_k \ge 1$, with equality exactly when $\mathcal{J}_k = 1$.

两个分母都为正，交叉相乘得 $\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_k - 1 \ge \mathcal{J}_k(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)$，即 $\mathcal{J}_k \ge 1$，等号恰在 $\mathcal{J}_k = 1$ 时成立。

A one-unit contrast looks only at the distribution of its own output and ignores the non-Gaussianity of the interfering sources, which maximum likelihood exploits. Estimators with exactly decorrelated outputs pay a different price. 单单元对比函数只看自身输出的分布，忽略了干扰源的非高斯性，而最大似然利用了它。输出严格去相关的估计量付出的是另一种代价。由，$\sqrt{T}(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ 渐近服从 $\Ncal(0, 1)$，所以在一阶近似下这个和的方差为 $1/T$，而这个和的方差的界 $(1, 1)\,\mathbf{F}_{ij}^{-1}(1, 1)^\top/T$ 等于

$$\frac{1}{T}\,\frac{\mathcal{J}_i + \mathcal{J}_j - 2}{\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1} \ \le\ \frac{1}{T},$$

since the difference of the two sides is proportional to $(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)(\mathcal{J}_j - 1) \ge 0$, with equality only when one of the sources is Gaussian. Hence no estimator with exactly decorrelated outputs is asymptotically efficient for a pair of non-Gaussian sources; this limitation of prewhitened (orthogonal) methods was analysed by Cardoso (1994). 因为两边之差与 $(\mathcal{J}_i - 1)(\mathcal{J}_j - 1) \ge 0$ 成正比，只有当其中一个源是高斯时才取等号。因此，对一对非高斯源，输出严格去相关的估计量都不是渐近有效的；Cardoso（1994）分析过预白化（正交）方法的这一局限。对称 FastICA（）同时具有这两种局限：它对所有输出使用同一个非线性函数 $g$，并使输出严格去相关。Tichavský、Koldovský 和 Oja（2006）推导了它的渐近精度（Koldovský, Tichavský & Oja, 2006 的式 (10) 重述了这一结果）：对 $k \ne l$，

$$T\cdot\mathrm{ISR}_{kl} \ \to\ \frac{\gamma_k + \gamma_l + \tau_l^2}{(\tau_k + \tau_l)^2}, \qquad \gamma_k = \beta_k - \mu_k^2, \quad \tau_k = |\mu_k - \rho_k|,$$

Key points

本章要点

  • The gain error $\varepsilon_{ij} = \hat P_{ij}/\hat P_{ii}$ measures how much of source $j$ leaks into the estimate of source $i$; $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$. Once the outputs are normalized, Amari's index summarizes $\hat\bP$ without matching outputs to sources; it decreases like $1/\sqrt{T}$.
  • For equivariant estimators the distribution of the errors does not depend on the mixing matrix, only on the sources and the method.
  • Estimators defined by estimating equations have errors of order $1/\sqrt{T}$; for ICA the asymptotic variance decouples into pairs of sources, and exact decorrelation makes $\sqrt{T}(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ asymptotically $\Ncal(0, 1)$.
  • One-unit FastICA has asymptotic variance $V_g$, which depends only on the estimated source (and, for skewed sources, on whether the data are centered); it is minimized by the score function, with value $1/(\mathcal{J} - 1)$.
  • The Cramér–Rao bound is $\mathcal{J}_j/(T(\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1))$: infinite for two Gaussian sources, large for nearly Gaussian ones, attained by maximum likelihood with the true scores, and in general not attained by one-unit or exactly decorrelating estimators.
  • 增益误差 $\varepsilon_{ij} = \hat P_{ij}/\hat P_{ii}$ 衡量源 $j$ 漏进源 $i$ 的估计中的多少；$\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$。把输出归一化之后，Amari 指标无需把输出与源配对就能概括 $\hat\bP$，并以 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 的速度下降。
  • 对等变估计量，误差的分布与混合矩阵无关，只取决于源和方法。
  • 由估计方程定义的估计量误差为 $1/\sqrt{T}$ 量级；在 ICA 中渐近方差按源的两两配对解耦，而严格去相关使 $\sqrt{T}(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ 渐近服从 $\Ncal(0, 1)$。
  • 单单元 FastICA 的渐近方差为 $V_g$，它只依赖于被估计的源（对偏斜的源，还取决于数据是否做了中心化）；得分函数使它达到最小值 $1/(\mathcal{J} - 1)$。
  • Cramér–Rao 界为 $\mathcal{J}_j/(T(\mathcal{J}_i\mathcal{J}_j - 1))$：两个源都是高斯时为无穷大，源接近高斯时很大；用真实得分函数的最大似然可以达到它，单单元估计量和严格去相关的估计量一般达不到。

（b）记 $\mu'_k = d_k\mu_k$、$\rho'_k = d_k\rho_k$ 为 $g_k$ 对应的常数，于是 $\tau_k = \mu'_k - \rho'_k = |\mu_k - \rho_k|$；再记 $u_{kl} = \hat\E[g_k(s_k)s_l]$，$\Delta_{kl} = \hat\E[s_ks_l]$。与的证明相同，$\hat\E[g_k(y_k)y_l] = u_{kl} + \mu'_ke_{lk} + \rho'_ke_{kl} + o_p(T^{-1/2})$，所以对称条件为 $u_{kl} + \mu'_ke_{lk} + \rho'_ke_{kl} = u_{lk} + \mu'_le_{kl} + \rho'_le_{lk}$。由，$e_{lk} = -\Delta_{kl} - e_{kl}$。代入并整理得

$$(\tau_k + \tau_l)\,e_{kl} = \hat\E\bigl[s_l\,\bigl(g_k(s_k) - \mu'_ks_k\bigr)\bigr] - \hat\E\bigl[s_k\,\bigl(g_l(s_l) - \rho'_ls_l\bigr)\bigr] + o_p(T^{-1/2}).$$

The two averages are uncorrelated, since $T$ times their covariance is $\E[s_k(g_k(s_k) - \mu'_ks_k)]\,\E[s_l(g_l(s_l) - \rho'_ls_l)] = 0 \cdot (\mu'_l - \rho'_l) = 0$. Their variances, times $T$, are $\E[(g_k(s_k) - \mu'_ks_k)^2] = \beta_k - \mu_k^2 = \gamma_k$ and $\E[(g_l(s_l) - \rho'_ls_l)^2] = \beta_l - 2\rho'_l\mu'_l + \rho_l'^2 = \gamma_l + \tau_l^2$. Therefore $T\Var(e_{kl}) \to (\gamma_k + \gamma_l + \tau_l^2)/(\tau_k + \tau_l)^2$, and $\varepsilon_{kl} = e_{kl} + O_p(T^{-1})$. If the data are first centered with the sample mean, $\bs$ is replaced by $\bs - \bar\bs$. To first order this subtracts $\bar s_l\,\E[g_k(s_k)]$ from $u_{kl}$ and $\bar s_k\,\E[g_l(s_l)]$ from $u_{lk}$, so in the two averages $g_k(s_k)$ and $g_l(s_l)$ are replaced by their deviations from their means. The averages remain uncorrelated, $\gamma_k$ becomes $\gamma_k - (\E[g(s_k)])^2$, $\gamma_l + \tau_l^2$ becomes $\gamma_l + \tau_l^2 - (\E[g(s_l)])^2$, and the numerator loses $(\E[g(s_k)])^2 + (\E[g(s_l)])^2$.

这两个平均不相关，因为它们的协方差乘以 $T$ 等于 $\E[s_k(g_k(s_k) - \mu'_ks_k)]\,\E[s_l(g_l(s_l) - \rho'_ls_l)] = 0 \cdot (\mu'_l - \rho'_l) = 0$。它们的方差乘以 $T$ 分别为 $\E[(g_k(s_k) - \mu'_ks_k)^2] = \beta_k - \mu_k^2 = \gamma_k$ 和 $\E[(g_l(s_l) - \rho'_ls_l)^2] = \beta_l - 2\rho'_l\mu'_l + \rho_l'^2 = \gamma_l + \tau_l^2$。因此 $T\Var(e_{kl}) \to (\gamma_k + \gamma_l + \tau_l^2)/(\tau_k + \tau_l)^2$，而 $\varepsilon_{kl} = e_{kl} + O_p(T^{-1})$。若先用样本均值把数据中心化，就要把 $\bs$ 换成 $\bs - \bar\bs$。在一阶近似下，这使 $u_{kl}$ 减去 $\bar s_l\,\E[g_k(s_k)]$，$u_{lk}$ 减去 $\bar s_k\,\E[g_l(s_l)]$，相当于在两个平均中把 $g_k(s_k)$ 和 $g_l(s_l)$ 换成它们与各自均值之差。两个平均仍不相关，$\gamma_k$ 变为 $\gamma_k - (\E[g(s_k)])^2$，$\gamma_l + \tau_l^2$ 变为 $\gamma_l + \tau_l^2 - (\E[g(s_l)])^2$，所以分子减少 $(\E[g(s_k)])^2 + (\E[g(s_l)])^2$。
