An fMRI recording is a changing image. ICA rewrites it as a collection of spatial patterns, each multiplied by a time course. The first challenge is keeping those two roles straight. The second is remembering that a mathematically extracted pattern needs additional evidence before it is called neural signal or noise.

fMRI 记录可以看成不断变化的图像。ICA 将它改写为一组空间模式，每个模式乘上自己的时间过程。第一步是分清这两个角色；第二步是记住，数学上提取出的模式还需要额外证据，才能解释为神经信号或噪声。

Build three images from one map用一张空间图生成三幅图像

  1. Take $T=3$ time points, $V=4$ voxels and one component. Let $\bA=(1,2,-3)^\top$ be the $3\times1$ time course and $\bS=(1,0,-1,0)$ the $1\times4$ spatial map. Values are arbitrary signal units.
  2. Multiplying gives three image rows: $(1,0,-1,0)$, $(2,0,-2,0)$ and $(-3,0,3,0)$. Thus $\bX=\bA\bS$ is $3\times4$. The same map is weighted differently over time; both temporal and spatial means happen to be zero here.
  3. Flip both factors: use $-\bA$ and $-\bS$. Every product entry stays the same because the two minus signs cancel. A displayed map changing color sign therefore need not mean the measured images changed.
  1. 取 $T=3$ 个时间点、$V=4$ 个体素和一个成分。时间过程为 $3\times1$ 的 $\bA=(1,2,-3)^\top$，空间图为 $1\times4$ 的 $\bS=(1,0,-1,0)$，数值使用任意信号单位。
  2. 相乘得到三幅图像的行：$(1,0,-1,0)$、$(2,0,-2,0)$、$(-3,0,3,0)$，所以 $\bX=\bA\bS$ 的尺寸为 $3\times4$。同一空间图在不同时刻获得不同权重；本例恰好同时具有零时间均值和零空间均值。
  3. 同时把两个因子改成 $-\bA$ 和 $-\bS$。两个负号抵消，乘积的每个元素都不变。因此，显示出来的空间图颜色正负反转，不一定表示实测图像发生了变化。

One component makes the multiplication visible but does not test independence between components. Real recordings contain multiple patterns, noise and preprocessing choices. This toy example establishes dimensions and ambiguity, not a diagnosis or a neural interpretation.

BOLD data as a matrix作为矩阵的 BOLD 数据

Functional MRI measures brain activity indirectly. Neural activity changes local blood flow and oxygenation, and the blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) signal follows these changes slowly: the response to a brief stimulus peaks roughly five seconds later and lasts several seconds more. The scanner acquires a volume of the whole brain every repetition time (TR), typically between half a second and three seconds, so a ten-minute session yields roughly 200 to 1,200 volumes, each containing tens of thousands to a few hundred thousand voxels inside the brain. Nothing in these recordings is labelled by function: networks of regions, physiological noise and head motion all add up in every voxel. Separating them is the task that made ICA a standard tool of brain imaging.

功能磁共振（fMRI）间接地测量脑活动。神经活动改变局部的血流与血氧，血氧水平依赖（blood-oxygen-level-dependent, BOLD）信号缓慢地跟随这些变化：对短暂刺激的响应大约在 5 秒后达到峰值，并再持续数秒。扫描仪每隔一个重复时间（TR，通常在半秒到三秒之间）采集一个全脑体积，因此十分钟的扫描大约得到 200 到 1,200 个体积，每个体积包含脑内数万到数十万个体素。这些记录中没有任何按功能划分的标签：脑区网络、生理噪声和头动在每个体素中叠加在一起。把它们分开，正是 ICA 成为脑影像标准工具的原因。

Before ICA the data are preprocessed. Standard steps correct for the different acquisition times of the slices within a volume, realign the volumes to compensate for head motion, register each subject's images to a standard template so that voxels correspond across subjects, smooth spatially with a Gaussian kernel a few millimetres wide, remove slow drifts, and restrict the analysis to voxels inside the brain. The result is arranged as a matrix $\bX \in \R^{T \times V}$ whose row $t$ is the image at time $t$, flattened into a vector of $V$ voxels, and whose column $v$ is the time series of voxel $v$. Spatial ICA treats voxels as samples, so each row of $\bX$ is centered by subtracting its mean over voxels; voxel time series are commonly centered in time as well, and sometimes scaled to percent signal change or to unit variance. Note the orientation. The general model of this book writes observations as columns (); for fMRI we follow the convention of the field and put time in the rows.

做 ICA 之前要先对数据进行预处理。标准步骤包括：校正同一体积内各层采集时刻的差异；对各体积做重新对齐以补偿头动；把每个被试的图像配准到标准模板上，使体素在被试之间一一对应；用宽度为几毫米的高斯核做空间平滑；去除缓慢漂移；并把分析限制在脑内体素上。处理后的数据排成矩阵 $\bX \in \R^{T \times V}$：第 $t$ 行是时刻 $t$ 的图像，展平成 $V$ 个体素构成的向量；第 $v$ 列是体素 $v$ 的时间序列。空间 ICA 把体素当作样本，所以要把 $\bX$ 的每一行减去它在体素上的均值；通常也把各体素的时间序列在时间上中心化，有时还把它们换算成信号变化百分比或缩放到单位方差。请注意矩阵的方向。本书的一般模型把观测写成列（）；对 fMRI，我们沿用该领域的惯例，把时间放在行上。

The spatial ICA model空间 ICA 模型

McKeown et al. (1998) proposed to model fMRI data as a sum of $n$ spatiotemporal components, each the product of a time course and a spatial map:

McKeown 等（1998）提出把 fMRI 数据建模为 $n$ 个时空成分之和，每个成分是一条时间过程与一张空间图的乘积：

$$\bX = \bA\bS = \sum_{i=1}^n \ba_i\,\bs_i^\top, \qquad \bA \in \R^{T\times n}, \quad \bS \in \R^{n\times V},$$

where $\ba_i$, the $i$-th column of $\bA$, is the time course of component $i$, and $\bs_i^\top$, the $i$-th row of $\bS$, is its spatial map (in this chapter $\bs_i \in \R^V$ denotes a whole map). Read column by column, the model says that the time series of voxel $v$ is $\bA$ times the vector of the $n$ map values at $v$. This is the linear model of with the roles assigned as follows: the $T$ time points are the sensors, the $n$ maps are the sources, $\bA$ is the mixing matrix, and the $V$ voxels are the samples. Spatial ICA assumes that the maps are independent: for a voxel drawn at random, the values $S_{1v}, \dots, S_{nv}$ of the $n$ maps are independent random variables. Temporal independence is not required, but the mixing matrix must have full column rank for the noiseless full-source model to be identifiable; perfectly collinear time courses cannot be separated this way.

其中 $\bA$ 的第 $i$ 列 $\ba_i$ 是成分 $i$ 的时间过程，$\bS$ 的第 $i$ 行 $\bs_i^\top$ 是它的空间图（本章中 $\bs_i \in \R^V$ 表示一整张图）。按列来读，这个模型说的是：体素 $v$ 的时间序列等于 $\bA$ 乘以 $n$ 张图在 $v$ 处取值构成的向量。这就是中的线性模型，只是角色分配如下：$T$ 个时间点是传感器，$n$ 张图是源，$\bA$ 是混合矩阵，$V$ 个体素是样本。空间 ICA 假设各张图相互独立：随机抽取一个体素，$n$ 张图在该处的取值 $S_{1v}, \dots, S_{nv}$ 是相互独立的随机变量。不要求时间过程相互独立，但无噪完整源模型的可辨识性要求混合矩阵列满秩；完全共线的时间过程不能通过这种方式分开。

Real data contain noise, $\bX = \bA\bS + \mathbf{N}$, and far more time points than components worth estimating, so estimation starts with PCA reduction (). Let $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top$ be the eigendecomposition of the $T \times T$ covariance across voxels, and keep the $n$ leading eigenvectors $\mathbf{E}_n$ and eigenvalues $\bLambda_n$. The whitened, reduced data and the ICA estimates are

真实数据含有噪声，$\bX = \bA\bS + \mathbf{N}$，而且时间点数远多于值得估计的成分数，所以估计从 PCA 降维开始（）。设 $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top$ 是体素方向上 $T \times T$ 协方差矩阵的特征分解，保留 $n$ 个最大特征值对应的特征向量 $\mathbf{E}_n$ 与特征值 $\bLambda_n$。白化降维后的数据与 ICA 的估计为

$$\bZ = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX, \qquad \hat\bS = \bU\bZ, \qquad \hat\bA = \mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n^{1/2}\bU^\top,$$

where the orthogonal matrix $\bU$ is chosen by an ICA algorithm to make the rows of $\hat\bS$ as independent as possible. The unmixing matrix is $\bW = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$, and $\hat\bA$ is its pseudo-inverse. Two facts are worth noting. First, $\hat\bA\hat\bS = \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX$ is the projection of the data onto the $n$ leading principal components, whatever $\bU$ is: ICA does not change the fit of the rank-$n$ approximation, it only chooses among its factorizations. Second, the estimated maps are uncorrelated with unit variance across voxels, $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bI$, but the time courses need not be orthogonal.

其中正交矩阵 $\bU$ 由 ICA 算法选取，使 $\hat\bS$ 的各行尽可能独立。解混矩阵为 $\bW = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$，$\hat\bA$ 是它的伪逆。有两点值得注意。第一，无论 $\bU$ 取什么，$\hat\bA\hat\bS = \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX$ 都是数据在前 $n$ 个主成分上的投影：ICA 并不改变秩 $n$ 近似的拟合程度，只是在它的各种分解中作出选择。第二，估计出的各张图在体素方向上互不相关且方差为一，即 $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bI$，而时间过程则不必正交。

The following script builds a small synthetic data set with three maps on a $40 \times 40$ grid of voxels. Two components have block-design time courses that overlap in time, the third a slow oscillation. After adding Gaussian noise, it runs spatial ICA with symmetric FastICA ().

下面的脚本构造一个小型合成数据集：$40 \times 40$ 体素网格上的三张空间图，其中两个成分的时间过程是在时间上相互交叠的组块设计，第三个是缓慢的振荡。加入高斯噪声后，用对称 FastICA（）做空间 ICA。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
side, T, n = 40, 150, 3
yy, xx = np.mgrid[0:side, 0:side]
def blob(cy, cx, r):                                  # Gaussian blob on the grid
    return np.exp(-((yy - cy)**2 + (xx - cx)**2) / (2 * r**2)).ravel()
S = np.vstack([blob(12, 10, 3) + blob(12, 30, 3),     # three spatial maps, n x V
               blob(28, 20, 5),
               blob(22, 6, 2) + blob(32, 33, 3)])
t = np.arange(T)
block = ((t // 15) % 2).astype(float)                 # 15 scans on, 15 off
A = np.column_stack([block, np.sin(2 * np.pi * t / 40), np.roll(block, 3)])
X = A @ S + 0.3 * rng.standard_normal((T, side * side))   # T x V data

# Spatial ICA: voxels are the samples. Center each row, PCA-whiten to n dims.
Xc = X - X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
d, E = np.linalg.eigh(Xc @ Xc.T / Xc.shape[1])
d, E = d[-n:], E[:, -n:]
Z = np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T @ Xc                     # n x V, identity covariance

U = np.linalg.qr(rng.standard_normal((n, n)))[0]      # symmetric FastICA, g = tanh
for _ in range(1000):
    U_old = U.copy()
    Y = U @ Z
    U = np.tanh(Y) @ Z.T / Z.shape[1] - np.diag((1 - np.tanh(Y)**2).mean(1)) @ U
    u, _, vt = np.linalg.svd(U)
    U = u @ vt                                        # U <- (U U^T)^(-1/2) U
    if np.max(np.abs(np.abs(np.diag(U @ U_old.T)) - 1)) < 1e-9:
        break
else:
    raise RuntimeError("FastICA did not converge; inspect or restart.")
S_hat = U @ Z                                         # estimated maps
A_hat = E @ np.diag(d ** 0.5) @ U.T                   # estimated time courses

print(np.round(np.corrcoef(A.T)[0, 2], 2))            # true time courses 1 and 3
print(np.round(np.abs(np.corrcoef(S_hat, S)[:n, n:]), 2))
print(np.round(np.abs(np.corrcoef(A_hat.T, A.T)[:n, n:]), 2))

The first line of output is $0.6$, the correlation between the two block-design time courses. The next two print absolute correlations between estimated components (rows) and true components (columns): the maps are recovered with correlations $0.98$, $0.94$ and $0.94$ and the time courses with $0.99$, $0.98$ and $0.99$, in the order $(2, 1, 3)$, an arbitrary permutation as always (). The off-diagonal time-course correlations of up to $0.55$ only reflect the correlation of the true time courses. Correlated time courses do not prevent spatial ICA from separating the components, because temporal independence is not assumed, provided $\bA$ has full column rank. shows the result.

输出的第一行是 $0.6$，即两条组块设计时间过程之间的相关系数。接下来两部分输出估计成分（行）与真实成分（列）之间相关系数的绝对值：空间图的恢复相关系数为 $0.98$、$0.94$、$0.94$，时间过程为 $0.99$、$0.98$、$0.99$，顺序为 $(2, 1, 3)$，同往常一样是一个任意的置换（）。时间过程之间高达 $0.55$ 的非对角相关，只是反映了真实时间过程之间的相关。时间过程相关并不妨碍空间 ICA 分离各成分，因为这个模型不要求时间独立性，但仍要求 $\bA$ 列满秩。结果见。

Spatial versus temporal ICA空间 ICA 与时间 ICA

Follow the sample axis. In spatial ICA, select a voxel and read its values across time: that is one multivariate observation. Independence concerns the component maps across spatial locations. It does not require the time courses to be independent, nor does it make nearby voxels independent statistical replications.

跟着样本轴读。在空间 ICA 中，选一个体素并读出它在各时刻的值，就得到一个多变量观测。独立性讨论的是空间位置上的成分图，不要求时间过程独立，也不会让相邻体素自动成为统计上独立的重复样本。

The same matrix can be factorized with independence imposed on the other factor. Temporal ICA treats the time points as samples and the voxels as sensors. It transposes the model to $\bX^\top = \bS^\top\bA^\top$ and asks the time courses, the columns of $\bA$, to be independent, leaving the maps unconstrained. Both are legitimate models of the same data; they differ in what is assumed to be independent, and they generally give different decompositions. Calhoun, Adalı, Pearlson and Pekar (2001) compared them on fMRI data containing a pair of task-related waveforms and showed that the two can lead to different results, depending on whether the underlying sources are independent in space or in time.

同一个矩阵也可以在另一个因子上施加独立性来分解。时间 ICA 把时间点当作样本、把体素当作传感器：它把模型转置为 $\bX^\top = \bS^\top\bA^\top$，要求时间过程（$\bA$ 的各列）相互独立，而对空间图不加约束。两者都是同一数据的合理模型，区别在于假设哪个因子独立，一般会给出不同的分解。Calhoun、Adalı、Pearlson 与 Pekar（2001）在含有一对任务相关波形的 fMRI 数据上比较了二者，表明根据潜在的源是在空间上独立还是在时间上独立，两种方法可能得出不同的结果。

The singular value decomposition makes the difference precise. Let $\bX$ be centered over voxels and over time, and let $\mathbf{E}_n\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$ be its truncated SVD with $n$ terms, with orthonormal columns in $\mathbf{E}_n \in \R^{T\times n}$ and $\bH_n \in \R^{V\times n}$; the left singular vectors are the eigenvectors $\mathbf{E}_n$ above, and $\bLambda_n = \bSigma_n^2/V$. Substituting into , and repeating the computation with the roles of time and space exchanged (), gives

奇异值分解能把这一区别说清楚。设 $\bX$ 在体素方向和时间方向都已中心化，$\mathbf{E}_n\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$ 是它保留 $n$ 项的截断 SVD，$\mathbf{E}_n \in \R^{T\times n}$ 与 $\bH_n \in \R^{V\times n}$ 的列都是单位正交的；左奇异向量就是上文的特征向量 $\mathbf{E}_n$，且 $\bLambda_n = \bSigma_n^2/V$。代入 ，再把时间与空间的角色互换重复同样的计算（），得到

$$\begin{aligned} \hat\bS &= \sqrt{V}\,\bU\bH_n^\top, & \hat\bA &= \tfrac{1}{\sqrt V}\,\mathbf{E}_n\bSigma_n\bU^\top, \\ \hat\bS &= \tfrac{1}{\sqrt T}\,\bU'\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top, & \hat\bA &= \sqrt{T}\,\mathbf{E}_n\bU'^\top, \end{aligned}$$

The first line describes spatial ICA and the second temporal ICA. Both methods factor the same rank-$n$ truncated SVD and thus have the same reconstruction error, but they distribute its singular values differently. Spatial ICA makes the maps uncorrelated with unit variance and permits correlated time courses; temporal ICA makes the time courses uncorrelated and permits correlated maps. Under a correctly specified identifiable noiseless model, temporal ICA cannot exactly recover correlated time courses, and spatial ICA cannot exactly recover spatially correlated maps. Spatial overlap is not the same as correlation or dependence: two maps can be nonzero at the same voxels and still have independent values under the voxel-sampling model. Conversely, non-overlapping sparse maps can be dependent. Whether a decomposition is appropriate depends on statistical assumptions, not on overlap alone.

第一行对应空间 ICA，第二行对应时间 ICA。两种方法都分解同一个秩为 $n$ 的截断 SVD，所以重建误差相同，但奇异值在两个因子中的分配不同。空间 ICA 使空间图不相关且方差为一，允许时间过程相关；时间 ICA 使时间过程不相关，允许空间图相关。在模型正确、可辨识且无噪的条件下，时间 ICA 不能精确恢复相关的时间过程，空间 ICA 不能精确恢复空间相关的图。空间重叠不等于相关或依赖：两张图可以在相同体素上非零，同时在体素抽样模型下取值独立；反之，不重叠的稀疏图也可能相关或依赖。分解是否合适取决于统计假设，不能仅凭是否重叠判断。

In practice spatial ICA dominates fMRI analysis, for three reasons. The first is sample size: spatial ICA estimates an $n\times n$ rotation from $V$ samples, tens of thousands or more, while temporal ICA has only $T$ samples, a few hundred in a typical session. The second is the shape of the sources: the maps of brain networks are sparse, near zero in most voxels and large in a few, which makes them strongly super-Gaussian, matching the source model built into Infomax with the logistic nonlinearity, the algorithm used by McKeown et al. (1998) (). The third is that the time courses of different networks are often correlated, through a shared task or shared physiology, which violates temporal independence but does not affect spatial ICA. Temporal ICA becomes feasible with the much higher temporal sampling of accelerated acquisitions; Smith et al. (2012) used it to find temporally independent functional modes whose maps overlap, including several that split the default-mode network into distinct subnetworks.

实践中空间 ICA 在 fMRI 分析中占主导地位，原因有三。其一是样本量：空间 ICA 用 $V$ 个样本（数万甚至更多）估计一个 $n\times n$ 的旋转，而时间 ICA 只有 $T$ 个样本，一次典型扫描不过几百个。其二是源的形态：脑网络的空间图是稀疏的，大多数体素接近零，少数体素取值很大，因而是强超高斯的，这正好符合 McKeown 等（1998）所用的 logistic 非线性 Infomax 内置的源模型（）。其三，不同网络的时间过程往往相关，原因可能是共同的任务，也可能是共同的生理过程；这违背了时间独立性，却不影响空间 ICA。加速采集带来更高的时间采样率之后，时间 ICA 变得可行：Smith 等（2012）用它找到了空间图相互重叠的时间独立功能模式，其中有几个把默认模式网络分成了不同的子网络。

Dimension reduction and model order降维与模型阶数

The number $n$ of components must be chosen before the rotation is estimated, and it shapes the result more than the choice of algorithm. PCA reduction keeps the $n$ leading eigenvectors of $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top$ and discards the rest as noise. If $n$ is too small, distinct networks are merged into one component; if it is too large, networks are split across several components and noise is fitted ().

成分个数 $n$ 必须在估计旋转之前选定，它对结果的影响比算法的选择更大。PCA 降维保留 $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top$ 的前 $n$ 个特征向量，其余部分作为噪声舍弃。$n$ 太小，不同的网络会被并入同一个成分；$n$ 太大，网络会被拆到几个成分中，噪声也会被拟合进来（）。

Information criteria such as AIC and MDL estimate $n$ from the eigenvalue spectrum under the assumption of independent samples (). In fMRI the samples are voxels, which are strongly dependent, not least because of spatial smoothing, and the criteria then overestimate the order. Li, Adalı and Calhoun (2007) subsample the voxel grid to obtain approximately independent samples before applying them. Beckmann and Smith (2004) choose the order that maximizes a Laplace approximation to the Bayesian evidence of probabilistic PCA (Minka, 2000), after adjusting the eigenspectrum for the limited number of samples.

AIC、MDL 等信息准则在样本独立的假设下，根据特征值谱估计 $n$（）。在 fMRI 中样本是体素，而体素之间依赖很强（空间平滑更加剧了这一点），这些准则因此会高估阶数。Li、Adalı 与 Calhoun（2007）先对体素网格做子采样，得到近似独立的样本，再应用信息准则。Beckmann 与 Smith（2004）则先针对样本数有限对特征值谱作校正，再选择使概率 PCA 的贝叶斯证据的 Laplace 近似（Minka, 2000）最大的阶数。

Such estimates are a guide rather than an answer. On resting-state data, low orders of a few tens give large-scale networks, and higher orders split many of them into smaller subnetworks (Abou-Elseoud et al., 2010). Neither is wrong: the order sets the spatial scale of the decomposition, and studies choose it for the question at hand. Group analyses also reduce each subject's data before the group-level reduction ().

这类估计只是参考，而不是答案。在静息态数据上，几十的低阶数给出大尺度网络，更高的阶数则把其中许多网络拆分成更小的子网络（Abou-Elseoud et al., 2010）。两者都不算错：阶数决定了分解的空间尺度，研究者应根据所要回答的问题来选择。组分析还会先对每个被试的数据降维，再做组水平的降维（）。

Algorithms in practice实践中的算法

Any ICA algorithm can rotate the whitened data $\bZ$. Three are common in fMRI.

任何 ICA 算法都可以用来旋转白化后的数据 $\bZ$。fMRI 中常用的有三种。

  • Infomax. McKeown et al. (1998) used Infomax (Bell & Sejnowski, 1995) with the logistic nonlinearity, whose implicit source model is super-Gaussian. This suits sparse maps, and Infomax remains a common choice, for example in analyses with the GIFT toolbox. Its fixed nonlinearity is also its weakness: sources that are not super-Gaussian can violate its stability condition (), a point that returns in .
  • FastICA. The fixed-point algorithm (Hyvärinen, 1999) with the $\tanh$ or Gaussian nonlinearity is equally widespread and is used in the example above.
  • Probabilistic ICA. PICA (Beckmann & Smith, 2004), implemented in the MELODIC tool of the FSL software, models the time series of voxel $v$ as $\bA\bs_v + \bmu + \boldsymbol\eta_v$, where $\bs_v$ collects the $n$ source values at $v$, there are fewer sources than time points, and the Gaussian noise $\boldsymbol\eta_v$ may have a different covariance at each voxel. It estimates the signal subspace and the model order with probabilistic PCA, the rotation with a fixed-point algorithm, and converts the maps into statistics for thresholding ().
  • Infomax。McKeown 等（1998）使用的是带 logistic 非线性函数的 Infomax（Bell & Sejnowski, 1995），其隐含的源模型是超高斯的。这与稀疏的空间图相吻合，Infomax 至今仍是常用的选择，例如在使用 GIFT 工具箱的分析中。固定的非线性函数也是它的弱点：非超高斯的源可能违背它的稳定性条件（），还会回到这一点。
  • FastICA。采用 $\tanh$ 或高斯型非线性函数的不动点算法（Hyvärinen, 1999）同样应用广泛，上面的例子用的就是它。
  • 概率 ICA。PICA（Beckmann & Smith, 2004）在 FSL 软件的 MELODIC 工具中实现。它把体素 $v$ 的时间序列建模为 $\bA\bs_v + \bmu + \boldsymbol\eta_v$，其中 $\bs_v$ 汇集了 $n$ 个源在 $v$ 处的取值，源的个数少于时间点数，高斯噪声 $\boldsymbol\eta_v$ 的协方差可以随体素而不同。它用概率 PCA 估计信号子空间与模型阶数，用不动点算法估计旋转，并把空间图转换为可用于阈值化的统计量（）。

Whatever the algorithm, the estimate depends on the random initialization, because ICA contrasts have local optima and their finite-sample landscape is noisy. ICASSO (Himberg, Hyvärinen & Esposito, 2004) runs the algorithm many times, with different initializations or on bootstrap resamples, clusters the estimated components, and reports the most central estimate of each cluster together with a stability index (); the GIFT toolbox includes it. A high index means that an estimate is reproducible under these perturbations, not that it is correct.

无论用哪种算法，估计结果都依赖于随机初始化，因为 ICA 对比函数存在局部最优，而且有限样本下的目标函数地形带有噪声。ICASSO（Himberg, Hyvärinen & Esposito, 2004）用不同的初始值或在自助重抽样的数据上多次运行算法，对估计出的成分做聚类，报告每一类中最居中的估计以及一个稳定性指标（）；GIFT 工具箱包含了这一方法。指标高说明估计在这些扰动下可以复现，并不说明它是正确的。

Reading the components解读成分

Each component $\ba_i\bs_i^\top$ is determined only up to a factor that can move between time course and map, since $\ba_i\bs_i^\top = (c\,\ba_i)(\bs_i/c)^\top$ for every $c \neq 0$ (). Two conventions make maps comparable. Each map is scaled to z-scores over voxels, dividing it by its standard deviation (the maps of spatial ICA already have mean zero), and the time course is multiplied by the same standard deviation so that the product is unchanged. Map and time course are then flipped together, if necessary, so that the map has positive skewness: its heavy tail of strongly involved voxels points upward. PICA instead divides each map by the voxelwise standard deviation of the residual noise, aiming at a statistic with the same null distribution at every voxel.

每个成分 $\ba_i\bs_i^\top$ 只确定到一个可以在时间过程与空间图之间转移的因子，因为对任意 $c \neq 0$ 都有 $\ba_i\bs_i^\top = (c\,\ba_i)(\bs_i/c)^\top$（）。有两条约定使空间图可以相互比较。把每张图除以它在体素上的标准差，换算为 z 分数（空间 ICA 的图本来就是零均值的），同时把时间过程乘以同一个标准差，使乘积保持不变。然后在必要时把图和时间过程一起变号，使图的偏度为正，即强烈参与的体素构成的重尾朝上。PICA 则把每张图除以残差噪声在各体素上的标准差，目的是得到在每个体素上零假设分布都相同的统计量。

Thresholds such as $|z| > 2$ or $3$ are descriptive conventions rather than tests: a z-scored ICA map is not a test statistic with a known null distribution, and neighboring voxels are strongly dependent. PICA fits a mixture model to the histogram of each statistic map, with one class for background noise and further classes for activation, and labels a voxel active when its posterior probability of belonging to the activation classes exceeds a chosen level such as 0.5 (Beckmann & Smith, 2004). Components are usually ordered by the variance they explain or by spatial correlation with reference templates.

$|z| > 2$ 或 $3$ 之类的阈值只是描述性的约定，而不是检验：z 分数化的 ICA 图并不是零假设分布已知的检验统计量，而且相邻体素之间依赖很强。PICA 对每张统计量图的直方图拟合一个混合模型，其中一类对应背景噪声，其余各类对应激活；当某体素属于激活类的后验概率超过选定的水平（例如 0.5）时，把它标记为激活（Beckmann & Smith, 2004）。成分通常按其解释的方差排序，或按与参考模板的空间相关排序。

Applied to resting-state data, spatial ICA finds a reproducible set of large-scale networks. Beckmann et al. (2005) reported networks resembling known functional systems such as visual and sensorimotor cortex; Damoiseaux et al. (2006) found ten networks consistent across subjects and sessions, covering motor, visual, auditory, executive and memory-related regions and the default-mode network; and Smith et al. (2009) showed that the major networks found by ICA of resting-state data from 36 subjects correspond closely to networks extracted by ICA from thousands of task activation maps in the BrainMap database.

用于静息态数据时，空间 ICA 能找到一组可复现的大尺度网络。Beckmann 等（2005）报告的网络与视觉皮层、感觉运动皮层等已知功能系统相似；Damoiseaux 等（2006）发现了十个在被试之间和扫描之间都一致的网络，涵盖运动、视觉、听觉、执行与记忆相关脑区以及默认模式网络；Smith 等（2009）则表明，对 36 名被试的静息态数据做 ICA 得到的主要网络，与对 BrainMap 数据库中数千张任务激活图做 ICA 得到的网络高度对应。

Not every component is a network. ICA also isolates structured noise, and this is one of its most practical uses: components caused by head motion, often a rim at the edge of the brain; by cerebrospinal fluid in the ventricles; by white matter; by cardiac and respiratory pulsation near large vessels; and by scanner artifacts, often with time courses dominated by high frequencies. Classifying components by eye is laborious, and two automatic classifiers are widely used. ICA-FIX (Salimi-Khorshidi et al., 2014; Griffanti et al., 2014) computes many spatial and temporal features of each component and feeds them to a hierarchical fusion of classifiers trained on hand-labelled data. ICA-AROMA (Pruim et al., 2015) targets head motion with four features: the maximum robust correlation of the time course with time series derived from the realignment parameters, the high-frequency content of the time course, and the fractions of the map that lie at the edge of the brain and in cerebrospinal fluid. It was designed to avoid classifier retraining, but its applicability and denoising quality still need checking for a new acquisition.

并不是每个成分都是网络。ICA 还能把结构化的噪声单独分离出来，这是它最实用的功能之一：头动造成的成分（常表现为脑边缘的一圈）、脑室中脑脊液造成的成分、白质成分、大血管附近心跳与呼吸搏动造成的成分，以及扫描仪伪迹，它们的时间过程常以高频为主。靠肉眼对成分分类很费力，常用的自动分类器有两种。ICA-FIX（Salimi-Khorshidi et al., 2014; Griffanti et al., 2014）计算每个成分的大量空间与时间特征，送入一个在人工标注数据上训练的分层融合分类器。ICA-AROMA（Pruim et al., 2015）针对头动，只用四个特征：时间过程与由重新对齐参数导出的时间序列之间的最大稳健相关，时间过程的高频成分比例，以及空间图位于脑边缘和位于脑脊液中的比例。它的设计不要求针对新数据重新训练分类器，但新的采集方案仍须检查适用性与去噪质量。

Once noise components are labelled, they are removed from the data. Split the estimated time courses into signal and noise columns, $\hat\bA = [\hat\bA_{S},\ \hat\bA_{N}]$, and let $\hat\bB = \hat\bA^+\bX$ be the coefficients of the full regression of the data on all time courses, with corresponding blocks $\hat\bB_S$ and $\hat\bB_N$. The two usual cleanups are

噪声成分一旦标注出来，就要从数据中去除。把估计的时间过程分为信号列和噪声列，$\hat\bA = [\hat\bA_{S},\ \hat\bA_{N}]$，并令 $\hat\bB = \hat\bA^+\bX$ 为数据对全部时间过程做完整回归的系数，相应地分块为 $\hat\bB_S$ 与 $\hat\bB_N$。常用的两种清理方式是

$$\tilde\bX = \bX - \hat\bA_{N}\hat\bB_{N}, \qquad \check\bX = \bX - \hat\bA_{N}\hat\bA_{N}^+\bX.$$

The first, soft or non-aggressive, cleanup removes only what the full regression attributes to the noise components. The second, aggressive, cleanup removes everything the noise time courses can explain, including the part of the signal components that happens to be correlated with them (). FIX performs the soft cleanup by default, with the aggressive one as an option, and ICA-AROMA offers both.

第一种是温和（非激进）清理，只去除完整回归归于噪声成分的部分。第二种是激进清理，去除噪声时间过程所能解释的一切，包括信号成分中恰好与噪声时间过程相关的那一部分（）。FIX 默认采用温和清理，激进清理作为选项；ICA-AROMA 两种都提供。

Is spatial independence the right assumption?空间独立是正确的假设吗？

Brain networks overlap, share nodes and interact, so the maps of real networks are unlikely to be exactly independent. Even the synthetic maps of are not: their blobs barely overlap, so where one map is large the others are near zero, and their values at a random voxel are dependent. Spatial ICA nevertheless recovered them. What, then, does ICA select for in fMRI?

脑网络相互重叠、共享节点并且相互作用，所以真实网络的空间图不太可能严格独立。就连中的合成图也不独立：它们的斑块几乎不重叠，一张图取值大的地方其他图都接近零，所以它们在随机体素处的取值并不独立。可空间 ICA 仍然把它们恢复了出来。那么，ICA 在 fMRI 中究竟偏好什么？

Daubechies et al. (2009) argued that Infomax and FastICA succeed on fMRI because they favour sparse components rather than independent ones. In simulations with box-shaped sources they found cases in which independent sources were not separated while dependent but sparse ones were, and they suggested that methods aimed directly at sparsity would suit fMRI better. Calhoun et al. (2013) replied that the failures came from a mismatch between the algorithms' fixed source models, super-Gaussian for Infomax with the logistic nonlinearity and high-kurtosis for the FastICA nonlinearity used, and simulated sources with near-zero kurtosis and bimodal distributions. Infomax with a sub-Gaussian nonlinearity separated those sources well to moderately well, and entropy bound minimization, an algorithm with a flexible density model, separated them in all cases. They concluded that the algorithms do what they are designed to do, find maximally independent components, and that sparsity is not the main driver of their success.

Daubechies 等（2009）认为，Infomax 与 FastICA 在 fMRI 上之所以成功，是因为它们偏好稀疏的成分，而不是独立的成分。在以方块为源的仿真中，他们发现有的情形下独立的源没有被分开，而不独立但稀疏的源反而被分开了；他们据此建议，直接以稀疏性为目标的方法更适合 fMRI。Calhoun 等（2013）回应说，那些失败源于失配：算法内置的源模型是固定的（logistic 非线性的 Infomax 对应超高斯，所用 FastICA 非线性对应高峭度），而仿真中的源峭度接近零、分布呈双峰。改用亚高斯非线性的 Infomax，就能较好或尚可地分离这些源；而采用具有灵活密度模型的熵界最小化算法，在所有情形下都能把它们分开。他们的结论是：这些算法做的正是设计它们去做的事，即寻找最大程度独立的成分，稀疏性并不是它们成功的主要原因。

The theory of explains the mechanism: a contrast with a fixed nonlinearity has a stable separating point only for sources that satisfy its stability condition (). In fMRI the useful sources, sparse maps, are super-Gaussian, which is why fixed super-Gaussian nonlinearities work so well and why sparsity and independence are hard to tell apart in practice. Three lessons follow. Treat spatial independence as a working model rather than a literal truth. Prefer algorithms, or nonlinearities, matched to the sources one expects. And check reliability () and agreement with independent knowledge before interpreting a component.

的理论解释了其中的机制：采用固定非线性函数的对比函数，只对满足其稳定性条件的源才有稳定的分离点（）。在 fMRI 中，有用的源（稀疏的空间图）是超高斯的，这正是固定的超高斯非线性函数效果很好的原因，也是实践中很难把稀疏性与独立性区分开的原因。由此得到三条经验：把空间独立当作一个工作模型，而不是字面上的事实；优先选择与预期的源相匹配的算法或非线性函数；在解读某个成分之前，先检查其可靠性（）以及与独立先验知识是否吻合。

Centering, nuisance regression and rank are different decisions中心化、混杂回归与矩阵秩是不同的问题

Keep an explicit record of what was removed before fitting. Subtracting each voxel's temporal mean removes its baseline; subtracting each volume's spatial mean centers the samples for the spatial ICA convention used here. If $\bg=\bX\mathbf1_V/V$, spatial centering gives $\bX_c=\bX-\bg\mathbf1_V^\top$. This is not global-signal regression, which projects each voxel time course off $\bg$ and gives $(\bI-\bg\bg^+)\bX$: its voxelwise regression coefficients need not be one. Store centering terms if the original signal is to be reconstructed. Simultaneous temporal and spatial centering leaves rank at most $\min(T-1,V-1)$; nuisance projection can reduce it further.

拟合前须明确记录去掉了什么。减去每个体素的时间均值去除其基线；减去每幅图像的空间均值，则按本章空间 ICA 的约定对样本中心化。若 $\bg=\bX\mathbf1_V/V$，空间中心化得到 $\bX_c=\bX-\bg\mathbf1_V^\top$。这不等于全局信号回归；后者把每个体素的时间过程投影到 $\bg$ 的正交补上，得到 $(\bI-\bg\bg^+)\bX$，各体素的回归系数不必为一。若要重建原始信号，应保存被减去的均值项。同时做时间与空间中心化后，秩至多为 $\min(T-1,V-1)$；混杂投影还可能进一步降秩。

Choose preprocessing for the acquisition and the estimand: slice-timing correction, smoothing and temporal filtering are not universal mandatory steps in one fixed order. Apply compatible filtering to data and nuisance regressors, and retain the effective degrees of freedom. Realignment does not undo every motion-induced intensity change. Ordinary PCA is optimal for squared reconstruction error under its assumptions; it does not prove that discarded directions are pure noise. In PICA, the simple probabilistic-PCA reduction relies on an isotropic or appropriately standardized noise model; allowing an arbitrary unknown covariance at every voxel is not solved by the same eigenvalue formula (Beckmann & Smith, 2004).

预处理应由采集方案与目标量决定：层间时间校正、平滑和时间滤波并非在固定顺序下普遍必需。数据与混杂回归量应采用相容的滤波，并保留有效自由度的信息。刚体运动校正不能消除所有运动引起的强度变化。普通 PCA 在相应假设下最小化平方重建误差，却不能证明被舍弃方向全是噪声。PICA 中简单的概率 PCA 降维依赖各向同性或适当标准化后的噪声模型；允许每个体素有任意未知协方差，并不能继续用同一个特征值公式解决（Beckmann & Smith, 2004）。

A reproducible interpretation and denoising record可复现的成分解读与去噪记录

For each decomposition record the brain mask and voxel grid, preprocessing, retained rank and model order, algorithm/nonlinearity, seed and convergence status, and the map/time-course scaling convention. Label components using spatial maps, spectra and time courses together. A positive map value is a relative loading with an arbitrary component sign, not direct evidence of excitatory neural activity; ICA is not a causal or neuronal cell-type decomposition. Skewness-based sign fixing is unstable near zero skewness, so an explicit reference-based convention may be preferable for across-subject comparisons.

每次分解应记录脑掩模与体素网格、预处理、保留秩与模型阶数、算法及非线性、随机种子与收敛状态，以及空间图和时间过程的缩放约定。成分分类应综合空间图、频谱与时间过程。图上正值是带任意成分符号的相对载荷，并非神经兴奋活动的直接证据；ICA 也不是因果或神经细胞类型分解。偏度接近零时，按偏度固定符号不稳定，跨被试比较可采用明确的参考图约定。

After cleanup, inspect both the removed signal and the retained residual. Compare motion associations, temporal spectra, retained variance and reproducibility before and after; a cleaner-looking map alone cannot establish successful denoising. Soft cleanup is the full-model allocation defined above, not a guarantee that no neural signal is removed. Keep the selected component labels and aggressive/non-aggressive setting with the analysis so that downstream FNC comparisons can reproduce exactly what was done.

清理后应同时检查被移除的信号与保留的残差。比较清理前后的运动关联、时间频谱、保留方差与可复现性；仅仅图像看起来更干净，不能证明去噪成功。软清理是上文定义的完整模型归属分配，并不保证绝不移除神经信号。应保存选中的噪声成分标签及激进／非激进设置，使后续 FNC 比较能精确复现处理过程。

Key points

本章要点

  • fMRI data form a $T \times V$ matrix. Spatial ICA writes $\bX = \bA\bS$ with time courses in the columns of $\bA$ and independent maps in the rows of $\bS$; the voxels are the samples.
  • After PCA reduction, spatial and temporal ICA rotate the same truncated SVD. Spatial ICA makes the maps uncorrelated and allows correlated time courses; temporal ICA does the reverse. Spatial ICA dominates because $V \gg T$ and network maps are sparse.
  • The model order sets the spatial scale of the decomposition: low orders merge networks, high orders split them. Dependence between voxels biases information criteria upward.
  • Components are z-scored and sign-corrected by skewness; thresholds on z-scored maps are descriptive, while PICA thresholds statistic maps with a mixture model.
  • ICA separates resting-state networks and structured noise. ICA-FIX and ICA-AROMA classify noise components; soft cleanup removes only what the full regression attributes to them.
  • Whether ICA selects for independence or for sparsity was debated; with density models matched to the sources, ICA behaves as its theory predicts.
  • fMRI 数据构成一个 $T \times V$ 矩阵。空间 ICA 把它写成 $\bX = \bA\bS$，时间过程在 $\bA$ 的列中，相互独立的空间图在 $\bS$ 的行中；体素是样本。
  • 经 PCA 降维后，空间 ICA 与时间 ICA 旋转的是同一个截断 SVD。空间 ICA 使空间图互不相关而允许时间过程相关，时间 ICA 则相反。由于 $V \gg T$ 且网络的空间图稀疏，空间 ICA 占主导地位。
  • 模型阶数决定了分解的空间尺度：阶数低会合并网络，阶数高会拆分网络。体素之间的相关会使信息准则偏向高估。
  • 成分要换算为 z 分数并按偏度校正符号；对 z 分数图设阈值只是描述性的，而 PICA 用混合模型对统计量图设阈值。
  • ICA 能分离静息态网络与结构化噪声。ICA-FIX 与 ICA-AROMA 用于识别噪声成分；温和清理只去除完整回归归于噪声成分的部分。
  • ICA 偏好的究竟是独立性还是稀疏性曾有争论；采用与源相匹配的密度模型时，ICA 的表现与理论预期一致。

A resting-state session has $T = 300$ volumes of $V = 60{,}000$ brain voxels and is decomposed into $n = 30$ components. (a) Give the sizes of $\bX$, $\bA$, $\bS$, $\bZ$ and $\bW$ in . (b) In spatial ICA, what are the sensors, the sources and the samples? How many samples are available to estimate the $n(n-1)/2$ parameters of the rotation $\bU$? (c) Answer (b) for temporal ICA.

一次静息态扫描有 $T = 300$ 个体积，每个体积有 $V = 60{,}000$ 个脑内体素，要分解为 $n = 30$ 个成分。(a) 写出 中 $\bX$、$\bA$、$\bS$、$\bZ$ 与 $\bW$ 的尺寸。(b) 在空间 ICA 中，传感器、源和样本分别是什么？估计旋转 $\bU$ 的 $n(n-1)/2$ 个参数时有多少样本可用？(c) 对时间 ICA 回答 (b)。

(a) $\bX$ is $300 \times 60{,}000$, $\bA$ is $300 \times 30$, $\bS$ and $\bZ$ are $30 \times 60{,}000$, and $\bW = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ is $30 \times 300$. (b) The sensors are the 300 time points, the sources are the 30 maps, and the samples are the 60,000 voxels. The rotation has $30 \cdot 29/2 = 435$ free parameters, so there are about 138 samples of a 30-dimensional vector per parameter. (c) In temporal ICA the sensors are the 60,000 voxels (reduced to 30 dimensions by PCA), the sources are the 30 time courses, and the samples are the 300 time points, about 0.7 samples per parameter. Higher-order statistics such as kurtosis are estimated with variance of order $1/T$ (), so 300 samples of a 30-dimensional vector give a noisy contrast; this is one reason why temporal ICA needs many more time points.

(a) $\bX$ 为 $300 \times 60{,}000$，$\bA$ 为 $300 \times 30$，$\bS$ 与 $\bZ$ 为 $30 \times 60{,}000$，$\bW = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ 为 $30 \times 300$。(b) 传感器是 300 个时间点，源是 30 张空间图，样本是 60,000 个体素。旋转有 $30 \cdot 29/2 = 435$ 个自由参数，所以每个参数大约有 138 个 30 维向量样本。(c) 在时间 ICA 中，传感器是 60,000 个体素（经 PCA 降到 30 维），源是 30 条时间过程，样本是 300 个时间点，每个参数只有约 0.7 个样本。峭度这类高阶统计量的估计方差量级为 $1/T$（），用 300 个 30 维样本估计的对比函数噪声很大；这正是时间 ICA 需要多得多的时间点的原因之一。

An estimated component consists of a time course $\hat\ba$ and a map $\hat\bs$ whose mean over voxels is zero and whose standard deviation is $\sigma$. (a) Show that replacing the pair by $(\sigma\hat\ba,\ \hat\bs/\sigma)$ leaves the component $\hat\ba\hat\bs^\top$ unchanged and turns the map into z-scores. (b) Show that negating the map negates its skewness, and explain why requiring positive skewness fixes the sign. What happens when the skewness is zero?

一个估计成分由时间过程 $\hat\ba$ 与空间图 $\hat\bs$ 组成，后者在体素上的均值为零、标准差为 $\sigma$。(a) 证明把这一对换成 $(\sigma\hat\ba,\ \hat\bs/\sigma)$ 不改变成分 $\hat\ba\hat\bs^\top$，并把空间图变成 z 分数。(b) 证明空间图取负号时偏度也取负号，并说明为什么要求偏度为正就能确定符号。偏度为零时会怎样？

(a) $(\sigma\hat\ba)(\hat\bs/\sigma)^\top = \hat\ba\hat\bs^\top$, and $\hat\bs/\sigma$ has mean zero and standard deviation one over voxels, so its entries are z-scores. (b) The skewness of the map values is $\gamma = m_3/\sigma^3$ with $m_3$ the mean of the cubed (centered) values. Negating the map leaves $\sigma$ unchanged and negates every cube, so $\gamma$ becomes $-\gamma$. If $\gamma \neq 0$, exactly one of $\pm\hat\bs$ has positive skewness; flipping the time course together with the map keeps the component unchanged. If $\gamma = 0$, for instance for a symmetric map, the rule does not decide, and another convention is needed, such as making the largest value positive or the correlation with a reference map positive.

(a) $(\sigma\hat\ba)(\hat\bs/\sigma)^\top = \hat\ba\hat\bs^\top$，而 $\hat\bs/\sigma$ 在体素上均值为零、标准差为一，所以它的元素就是 z 分数。(b) 图中取值的偏度为 $\gamma = m_3/\sigma^3$，其中 $m_3$ 是（中心化后）取值立方的平均。图取负号时 $\sigma$ 不变，每个立方都变号，所以 $\gamma$ 变为 $-\gamma$。若 $\gamma \neq 0$，$\pm\hat\bs$ 中恰有一个偏度为正；把时间过程与图一起变号，成分保持不变。若 $\gamma = 0$（例如图是对称的），这条规则无法作出判断，需要别的约定，例如令绝对值最大的取值为正，或令与参考图的相关为正。

Let $\bX$ be centered over voxels and over time, with truncated SVD $\mathbf{E}_n\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$. (a) Show that the spatial ICA estimates take the form of the first line of . (b) Temporal ICA treats the rows of $\bX$ as $T$ samples of a $V$-dimensional vector, whitens with the $n$ leading eigenvectors of $\frac1T\bX^\top\bX$, rotates by an orthogonal $\bU'$ and takes the time courses as the rows of the rotated data. Derive the second line. (c) Compute $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top$ and $\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA$ for both methods.

设 $\bX$ 在体素方向与时间方向都已中心化，截断 SVD 为 $\mathbf{E}_n\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$。(a) 证明空间 ICA 的估计 具有 第一行的形式。(b) 时间 ICA 把 $\bX$ 的各行看作一个 $V$ 维向量的 $T$ 个样本，用 $\frac1T\bX^\top\bX$ 的前 $n$ 个特征向量白化，再用正交矩阵 $\bU'$ 旋转，并把旋转后数据的各行取为时间过程。推导第二行。(c) 对两种方法分别计算 $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top$ 与 $\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA$。

(a) From $\bX = \mathbf{E}\bSigma\bH^\top$ (full SVD), $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top = \mathbf{E}(\bSigma^2/V)\mathbf{E}^\top$, so $\bLambda_n = \bSigma_n^2/V$ and $\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX = \bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$. Hence $\bZ = \sqrt V\,\bSigma_n^{-1}\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top = \sqrt V\,\bH_n^\top$, $\hat\bS = \bU\bZ = \sqrt V\,\bU\bH_n^\top$ and $\hat\bA = \mathbf{E}_n(\bSigma_n/\sqrt V)\bU^\top$. (b) Now $\frac1T\bX^\top\bX = \bH(\bSigma^2/T)\bH^\top$, so the whitened data are the $n\times T$ matrix

(a) 由完整 SVD $\bX = \mathbf{E}\bSigma\bH^\top$ 得 $\frac1V\bX\bX^\top = \mathbf{E}(\bSigma^2/V)\mathbf{E}^\top$，所以 $\bLambda_n = \bSigma_n^2/V$，且 $\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX = \bSigma_n\bH_n^\top$。于是 $\bZ = \sqrt V\,\bSigma_n^{-1}\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top = \sqrt V\,\bH_n^\top$，$\hat\bS = \bU\bZ = \sqrt V\,\bU\bH_n^\top$，$\hat\bA = \mathbf{E}_n(\bSigma_n/\sqrt V)\bU^\top$。(b) 此时 $\frac1T\bX^\top\bX = \bH(\bSigma^2/T)\bH^\top$，白化后的数据是 $n\times T$ 矩阵

$$\bigl(\bSigma_n^2/T\bigr)^{-1/2}\bH_n^\top\bX^\top = \sqrt T\,\bSigma_n^{-1}\bSigma_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top = \sqrt T\,\mathbf{E}_n^\top.$$

Rotating gives the time courses as rows, $\hat\bA^\top = \sqrt T\,\bU'\mathbf{E}_n^\top$, so $\hat\bA = \sqrt T\,\mathbf{E}_n\bU'^\top$. The maps are the mixing matrix of this model, the pseudo-inverse of the unmixing matrix $\bU'(\bSigma_n/\sqrt T)^{-1}\bH_n^\top$, which is $\bH_n(\bSigma_n/\sqrt T)\bU'^\top = \hat\bS^\top$; thus $\hat\bS = \bU'\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top/\sqrt T$. (c) Spatial ICA: $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bU\bH_n^\top\bH_n\bU^\top = \bI$ and $\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU\bSigma_n^2\bU^\top/(TV)$. Temporal ICA: $\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU'\mathbf{E}_n^\top\mathbf{E}_n\bU'^\top = \bI$ and $\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bU'\bSigma_n^2\bU'^\top/(TV)$. The matrix $\bU\bSigma_n^2\bU^\top$ is diagonal when $\bU$ maps singular directions onto the axes; arbitrary rotations within an equal-singular-value subspace also preserve diagonality; for an ICA rotation it is generally not, so spatial ICA yields correlated time courses and temporal ICA yields correlated maps.

旋转后各行就是时间过程：$\hat\bA^\top = \sqrt T\,\bU'\mathbf{E}_n^\top$，所以 $\hat\bA = \sqrt T\,\mathbf{E}_n\bU'^\top$。空间图是该模型的混合矩阵，即解混矩阵 $\bU'(\bSigma_n/\sqrt T)^{-1}\bH_n^\top$ 的伪逆 $\bH_n(\bSigma_n/\sqrt T)\bU'^\top = \hat\bS^\top$；因此 $\hat\bS = \bU'\bSigma_n\bH_n^\top/\sqrt T$。(c) 空间 ICA：$\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bU\bH_n^\top\bH_n\bU^\top = \bI$，$\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU\bSigma_n^2\bU^\top/(TV)$。时间 ICA：$\frac1T\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU'\mathbf{E}_n^\top\mathbf{E}_n\bU'^\top = \bI$，$\frac1V\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top = \bU'\bSigma_n^2\bU'^\top/(TV)$。当 $\bU$ 把奇异方向转到坐标轴上时，$\bU\bSigma_n^2\bU^\top$ 为对角阵；相同奇异值对应子空间内部的任意旋转也保持其对角性；对 ICA 的旋转而言一般不是，所以空间 ICA 给出相关的时间过程，时间 ICA 给出相关的空间图。

Assume that $\hat\bA = [\hat\bA_S,\ \hat\bA_N]$ has full column rank. Show that the two cleanups of differ by $\tilde\bX - \check\bX = \hat\bA_N\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S$. Interpret this difference, and say when the two cleanups agree.

设 $\hat\bA = [\hat\bA_S,\ \hat\bA_N]$ 列满秩。证明 中两种清理之差为 $\tilde\bX - \check\bX = \hat\bA_N\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S$。解释这个差，并说明两种清理何时一致。

Write $\bX = \hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S + \hat\bA_N\hat\bB_N + \bR$, where $\bR = (\bI - \hat\bA\hat\bA^+)\bX$ is the residual of the full regression. Its columns are orthogonal to the columns of $\hat\bA$, so $\hat\bA_N^\top\bR = \bzero$, and hence $\hat\bA_N^+\bR = \bzero$ because $\hat\bA_N^+ = (\hat\bA_N^\top\hat\bA_N)^{-1}\hat\bA_N^\top$. Since $\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_N = \bI$, we get $\hat\bA_N^+\bX = \hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S + \hat\bB_N$, and therefore

写 $\bX = \hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S + \hat\bA_N\hat\bB_N + \bR$，其中 $\bR = (\bI - \hat\bA\hat\bA^+)\bX$ 是完整回归的残差。它的各列与 $\hat\bA$ 的各列正交，所以 $\hat\bA_N^\top\bR = \bzero$；又因为 $\hat\bA_N^+ = (\hat\bA_N^\top\hat\bA_N)^{-1}\hat\bA_N^\top$，从而 $\hat\bA_N^+\bR = \bzero$。由于 $\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_N = \bI$，得 $\hat\bA_N^+\bX = \hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S + \hat\bB_N$，因此

$$\check\bX = \bX - \hat\bA_N\hat\bB_N - \hat\bA_N\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S = \tilde\bX - \hat\bA_N\hat\bA_N^+\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S.$$

The difference is the projection of the signal part $\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S$ onto the span of the noise time courses: aggressive cleanup also removes that portion of the signal. The cleanups agree when $\hat\bA_N^\top\hat\bA_S = \bzero$, that is, when the noise time courses are orthogonal to the signal time courses. Spatial ICA does not make time courses orthogonal (), so in practice the two differ.

这个差是信号部分 $\hat\bA_S\hat\bB_S$ 在噪声时间过程张成的空间上的投影：激进清理连这部分信号也一并去除了。当 $\hat\bA_N^\top\hat\bA_S = \bzero$，即噪声时间过程与信号时间过程正交时，两种清理一致。空间 ICA 并不使时间过程正交（），所以实践中两者有差别。

Let $\bX$ be centered over voxels and let $\hat\bA$, $\hat\bS$ be the estimates . Show that (a) $\hat\bA = \bX\hat\bS^\top(\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top)^{-1}$, so the time courses are the least-squares regression of the data on the maps; and (b) $\hat\bS = \hat\bA^+\bX$, so the maps are the least-squares regression of the data on the time courses. Conclude that the two regressions of dual regression (), applied to the data from which the maps were estimated, return the ICA solution unchanged.

设 $\bX$ 在体素方向已中心化，$\hat\bA$、$\hat\bS$ 为估计 。证明：(a) $\hat\bA = \bX\hat\bS^\top(\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top)^{-1}$，即时间过程是数据对空间图的最小二乘回归；(b) $\hat\bS = \hat\bA^+\bX$，即空间图是数据对时间过程的最小二乘回归。由此说明：把双回归（）的两步回归用于估计空间图时所用的数据，得到的仍是原来的 ICA 解。

(a) Since $\hat\bS = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX$ and $\tfrac1V\bX\bX^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n$,

(a) 由 $\hat\bS = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bX$ 以及 $\tfrac1V\bX\bX^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n$，得

$$\begin{aligned} \hat\bS\hat\bS^\top &= V\,\bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bigl(\tfrac1V\bX\bX^\top\bigr)\mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bU^\top = V\bI, \\ \bX\hat\bS^\top &= \bX\bX^\top\mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\bU^\top = V\,\mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n^{1/2}\bU^\top = V\hat\bA. \end{aligned}$$

Hence $\bX\hat\bS^\top(\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top)^{-1} = \hat\bA$. (b) Since $\mathbf{E}_n^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \bI$, the matrix $\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU\bLambda_n\bU^\top$ is invertible, and

因此 $\bX\hat\bS^\top(\hat\bS\hat\bS^\top)^{-1} = \hat\bA$。(b) 由于 $\mathbf{E}_n^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \bI$，矩阵 $\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA = \bU\bLambda_n\bU^\top$ 可逆，并且

$$\hat\bA^+ = (\hat\bA^\top\hat\bA)^{-1}\hat\bA^\top = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1}\bU^\top\,\bU\bLambda_n^{1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top = \bU\bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top = \bW,$$

so $\hat\bA^+\bX = \bW\bX = \hat\bS$. Dual regression first regresses the data on given maps to obtain time courses, then regresses the data on those time courses to obtain maps. Started from $\hat\bS$ with the same data $\bX$, the first step returns $\hat\bA$ by (a) and the second returns $\hat\bS$ by (b): the ICA solution is a fixed point of the pair of regressions. Dual regression changes the estimates only when applied to other data, such as one subject's data and maps estimated from a group.

所以 $\hat\bA^+\bX = \bW\bX = \hat\bS$。双回归先把数据对给定的空间图回归得到时间过程，再把数据对这些时间过程回归得到空间图。若从 $\hat\bS$ 出发并使用同一数据 $\bX$，由 (a) 第一步得到 $\hat\bA$，由 (b) 第二步又得到 $\hat\bS$：ICA 解是这对回归的不动点。只有用于其他数据时（例如某个被试的数据配上由组数据估计的空间图），双回归才会改变估计。
