What the covariance can do协方差能做什么

counted parameters. For a square model with unit-variance sources, the covariance $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top$ supplies $n(n+1)/2$ numbers about the $n^2$ entries of $\bA$, and every $\bA\bQ$ with $\bQ$ orthogonal has the same covariance (). This chapter turns the count into a procedure. Whitening uses the covariance to remove everything except a rotation: after whitening, the mixing matrix is orthogonal, and ICA reduces to a search over orthogonal matrices.

数过参数：对源为单位方差的方阵模型，协方差 $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top$ 只提供关于 $\bA$ 的 $n^2$ 个元素的 $n(n+1)/2$ 个数，而且对任意正交矩阵 $\bQ$，$\bA\bQ$ 都有相同的协方差（）。本章把这一计数变成一个具体步骤。白化利用协方差消去除旋转以外的一切：白化之后混合矩阵是正交的，ICA 归结为在正交矩阵中的搜索。

Most algorithms in this book use this reduction, from the angle search of to FastICA () and JADE (); Comon (1994) built his ICA algorithm on it, and Hyvärinen, Karhunen and Oja (2001) give a textbook account. shows its geometry. The reduction also has costs, which the second half of the chapter examines. When there are more sensors than sources, whitening is where the dimension is reduced and where noise enters (). And because whitening is fixed before the rotation is estimated, its sampling error can never be corrected afterwards ().

本书的大多数算法都采用这种约化，从中的角度搜索，到 FastICA（）和 JADE（）；Comon（1994）的 ICA 算法就建立在这种约化之上，Hyvärinen、Karhunen 与 Oja（2001）的教材也有系统的介绍。 展示了它的几何含义。这种约化也有代价，本章后半部分专门讨论。当传感器多于源时，降维和噪声的引入都发生在白化这一步（）。而且白化在估计旋转之前就已固定，它的抽样误差事后再也无法纠正（）。

Centering and covariance中心化与协方差

Let $\bx \in \R^m$ have finite second moments. Centering replaces $\bx$ by $\bx - \E[\bx]$; in the model $\bx = \bA\bs$ this removes the source means, which can be restored at the end as $\bW\E[\bx]$ (). From now on $\bx$ has zero mean and covariance $\bC_{\bx} = \E[\bx\bx^\top]$. Under the model, the source covariance $\bC_{\bs} = \E[\bs\bs^\top]$ is diagonal because the sources are independent, and

设 $\bx \in \R^m$ 的二阶矩有限。中心化就是用 $\bx - \E[\bx]$ 代替 $\bx$；在模型 $\bx = \bA\bs$ 中，这一步去掉了源的均值，而源的均值可以在最后用 $\bW\E[\bx]$ 恢复（）。以下总设 $\bx$ 均值为零，协方差为 $\bC_{\bx} = \E[\bx\bx^\top]$。在模型之下，由于各源独立，源的协方差 $\bC_{\bs} = \E[\bs\bs^\top]$ 是对角的，并且

$$\bC_{\bx} = \bA\,\bC_{\bs}\,\bA^\top,$$

which equals $\bA\bA^\top$ for unit-variance sources. The covariance is symmetric and positive semidefinite, and its rank equals that of $\bA$, which is $n$ under . It is positive definite when $m = n$. When $m > n$ it is singular in the noise-free model, and its null space is the orthogonal complement of the column space of $\bA$.

对单位方差的源，它等于 $\bA\bA^\top$。协方差是对称半正定矩阵，其秩等于 $\bA$ 的秩，在下为 $n$。当 $m = n$ 时它正定。当 $m > n$ 时，在无噪声模型中它是奇异的，其零空间是 $\bA$ 列空间的正交补。

From samples $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$, collected as the columns of $\bX \in \R^{m \times T}$, the sample mean $\bar\bx = \frac1T\sum_t \bx(t)$ and the sample covariance

给定样本 $\bx(1), \dots, \bx(T)$，并把它们排成 $\bX \in \R^{m \times T}$ 的各列，用样本均值 $\bar\bx = \frac1T\sum_t \bx(t)$ 和样本协方差

$$\hat\bC_{\bx} = \frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T \bigl(\bx(t) - \bar\bx\bigr)\bigl(\bx(t) - \bar\bx\bigr)^\top = \frac1T\,\tilde\bX\tilde\bX^\top, \qquad \tilde\bX = \bX - \bar\bx\,\mathbf{1}^\top,$$

replace their population counterparts. Dividing by $T - 1$ instead of $T$ makes $\hat\bC_{\bx}$ unbiased; for ICA the choice is immaterial, since it rescales all whitened outputs by the same constant. When fourth moments are finite, each entry of $\hat\bC_{\bx}$ has a standard error of order $T^{-1/2}$, a fact that returns in . Because the centered samples sum to zero, $\hat\bC_{\bx}$ has rank at most $T - 1$, so it is singular whenever $m \ge T$, and a reduction of dimension is then unavoidable. This is the situation in temporal ICA of fMRI, where the voxels play the role of sensors ().

来代替相应的总体量。分母用 $T - 1$ 代替 $T$ 可使 $\hat\bC_{\bx}$ 无偏；对 ICA 而言这无关紧要，因为它只是把所有白化后的输出乘上同一个常数。当四阶矩有限时，$\hat\bC_{\bx}$ 每个元素的标准误都是 $T^{-1/2}$ 量级，这一点在中还会用到。由于中心化后的样本之和为零，$\hat\bC_{\bx}$ 的秩至多为 $T - 1$，所以当 $m \ge T$ 时它必然奇异，此时降维不可避免。fMRI 的时间 ICA 就属于这种情形，在那里体素扮演传感器的角色（）。

Numerically, the eigenvectors and eigenvalues of $\hat\bC_{\bx}$ are best obtained from the singular value decomposition $\tilde\bX = \mathbf{E}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}\bF^\top$: the eigenvectors are the columns of $\mathbf{E}$ and the eigenvalues are $\gamma_i^2/T$. This avoids forming $\tilde\bX\tilde\bX^\top$, which squares the condition number (Golub & Van Loan, 2013).

在数值计算上，$\hat\bC_{\bx}$ 的特征向量与特征值最好通过奇异值分解 $\tilde\bX = \mathbf{E}\boldsymbol{\Gamma}\bF^\top$ 求得：特征向量是 $\mathbf{E}$ 的各列，特征值为 $\gamma_i^2/T$。这样可以避免显式构造 $\tilde\bX\tilde\bX^\top$，后者会把条件数平方（Golub & Van Loan, 2013）。

Whitening matrices白化矩阵

A random vector is white if its components are uncorrelated and have unit variance. Whitening transforms the data linearly so that they become white.

若一个随机向量的各分量互不相关且方差为一，则称它是白的。白化就是对数据作线性变换，使之变白。

Micro example: divide by standard deviation, not variance微型手算：除以标准差，而不是方差

Assume $x_1,x_2$ have mean zero, variances $4,9$, and covariance zero. Thus $\bC_{\bx}=\diag(4,9)$.

设 $x_1,x_2$ 均值为零、方差为 $4,9$，协方差为零，因此 $\bC_{\bx}=\diag(4,9)$。

  1. The standard deviations are $2$ and $3$. Set $z_1=x_1/2$ and $z_2=x_2/3$.
  2. Variances scale by the square of the multiplier: $\Var(z_1)=4/2^2=1$ and $\Var(z_2)=9/3^2=1$. Their covariance remains zero.
  3. So $\bV=\diag(1/2,1/3)$ gives $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top=\bI$. One observation $(2,6)$ becomes $(1,2)$.
  1. 标准差是 $2$ 和 $3$。令 $z_1=x_1/2$、$z_2=x_2/3$。
  2. 方差按乘数的平方缩放：$\Var(z_1)=4/2^2=1$、$\Var(z_2)=9/3^2=1$，二者的协方差仍为零。
  3. 所以 $\bV=\diag(1/2,1/3)$ 满足 $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top=\bI$。一个观测 $(2,6)$ 被变为 $(1,2)$。

This proves whiteness for the specified covariance. It does not prove independence, and it does not require each observation to have length one. Variance describes a distribution across observations, not the size of one vector.

这证明了对给定协方差的白化，却没有证明独立，也不要求每个观测的长度为一。方差描述许多观测构成的分布，不是单个向量的大小。

Quick check 1. If the covariance is $\diag(4,0)$, can a two-dimensional linear transform make it $\bI_2$?

自测 1。若协方差是 $\diag(4,0)$，能否用二维线性变换把它变成 $\bI_2$？

Check the answer查看答案

No. Its rank is one and a linear transform cannot increase that rank. Retain the varying coordinate and whiten it by dividing by two; the constant coordinate contains no second direction to recover.

不能。它的秩为一，线性变换不能增加这个秩。可保留有变化的坐标并除以二来白化；常数坐标并不包含可恢复的第二个方向。

Quick check 2. If $\bz$ is white and $\bQ$ is orthogonal, is $\bQ\bz$ also white?

自测 2。若 $\bz$ 是白的、$\bQ$ 正交，$\bQ\bz$ 也一定白吗？

Check the answer查看答案

Yes: its covariance is $\bQ\bI\bQ^\top=\bI$. This is precisely why whitening leaves an orientation undecided; it does not show the rotated coordinates are independent.

是。它的协方差是 $\bQ\bI\bQ^\top=\bI$。这恰好解释了为什么白化还留有未决定的方向，却不能说明旋转后的坐标独立。

Let $\bx \in \R^m$ have zero mean and covariance $\bC_{\bx}$. A matrix $\bV \in \R^{k \times m}$ is a whitening matrix for $\bx$ if $\bz = \bV\bx$ satisfies $\E[\bz\bz^\top] = \bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI_k$. The vector $\bz$ is then called white, or sphered.

设 $\bx \in \R^m$ 均值为零，协方差为 $\bC_{\bx}$。若 $\bz = \bV\bx$ 满足 $\E[\bz\bz^\top] = \bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI_k$，则称矩阵 $\bV \in \R^{k \times m}$ 为 $\bx$ 的白化矩阵，称 $\bz$ 是白的，或球化的。

Since $\rank(\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top) \le \rank\bC_{\bx}$, necessarily $k \le \rank\bC_{\bx}$. In the noise-free model $\rank\bC_{\bx} = n$, and whitening to $k = n$ components loses no information, because $\bx$ lies almost surely in the $n$-dimensional column space of $\bC_{\bx}$. Write the eigendecomposition of the covariance as $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top$, with $\mathbf{E}$ orthogonal and $\bLambda = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m)$, $\lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_m \ge 0$ (). Suppose first that $m = n$, so that all eigenvalues are positive. Three whitening matrices are in common use:

由于 $\rank(\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top) \le \rank\bC_{\bx}$，必有 $k \le \rank\bC_{\bx}$。在无噪声模型中 $\rank\bC_{\bx} = n$，而白化到 $k = n$ 个分量不会丢失任何信息，因为 $\bx$ 几乎必然落在 $\bC_{\bx}$ 的 $n$ 维列空间里。把协方差的特征分解写成 $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top$，其中 $\mathbf{E}$ 正交，$\bLambda = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m)$，$\lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_m \ge 0$（）。先设 $m = n$，于是所有特征值都为正。常用的白化矩阵有三种：

Decode the product from right to left. $\mathbf{E}^\top$ expresses each observation along the covariance eigenvectors. Each $\lambda_i$ is the variance on one such axis; $\lambda_i^{-1/2}$ means divide by its standard deviation. Thus PCA whitening first changes coordinates and then rescales them. A zero eigenvalue blocks the second step and signals the need to restrict the subspace.

从右向左拆开这个乘积。$\mathbf{E}^\top$ 把每个观测改写到协方差特征向量的坐标系中。$\lambda_i$ 是该轴上的方差，$\lambda_i^{-1/2}$ 表示除以它的标准差。因此 PCA 白化是先换坐标、再缩放；特征值为零时不能进行第二步，需要先限制到有变化的子空间。

  • PCA whitening, $\bV = \bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$. The data are projected onto the principal axes, and each principal component is divided by its standard deviation.
  • Symmetric (ZCA) whitening, $\bV = \bC_{\bx}^{-1/2} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$: PCA whitening followed by the rotation $\mathbf{E}$ back to the original axes. Bell and Sejnowski (1997) named it ZCA, for zero-phase component analysis. Among all whitening matrices it changes the data least, in the sense that it minimizes $\E\|\bV\bx - \bx\|^2$ (; Kessy, Lewin and Strimmer (2018) discuss this and other optimality properties).
  • Cholesky whitening, $\bV = \mathbf{L}^{-1}$, where $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$ with $\mathbf{L}$ lower triangular with a positive diagonal. Since $\mathbf{L}^{-1}$ is also lower triangular, $z_1$ depends only on $x_1$, $z_2$ on $x_1$ and $x_2$, and so on: Cholesky whitening is the Gram–Schmidt orthonormalization of $x_1, \dots, x_m$ in the inner product $\E[uv]$ ().
  • PCA 白化，$\bV = \bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$。先把数据投影到主轴上，再把每个主成分除以它的标准差。
  • 对称（ZCA）白化，$\bV = \bC_{\bx}^{-1/2} = \mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$：先做 PCA 白化，再用旋转 $\mathbf{E}$ 转回原来的坐标轴。Bell 与 Sejnowski（1997）称之为 ZCA，即零相位成分分析（zero-phase component analysis）。在所有白化矩阵中，它对数据的改变最小，即它使 $\E\|\bV\bx - \bx\|^2$ 最小（；Kessy、Lewin 与 Strimmer（2018）讨论了这一性质及其他最优性）。
  • Cholesky 白化，$\bV = \mathbf{L}^{-1}$，其中 $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top$，$\mathbf{L}$ 是对角元为正的下三角矩阵。由于 $\mathbf{L}^{-1}$ 也是下三角的，$z_1$ 只依赖于 $x_1$，$z_2$ 只依赖于 $x_1$ 与 $x_2$，依此类推：Cholesky 白化就是在内积 $\E[uv]$ 下对 $x_1, \dots, x_m$ 作 Gram–Schmidt 标准正交化（）。

Each of them satisfies $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$; for PCA whitening, for instance, $\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top(\mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top)\mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2} = \bI$. They are far from the only ones, but the set of all whitening matrices has a simple structure.

它们都满足 $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$；以 PCA 白化为例，$\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top(\mathbf{E}\bLambda\mathbf{E}^\top)\mathbf{E}\bLambda^{-1/2} = \bI$。白化矩阵远不止这三种，但所有白化矩阵构成的集合有简单的结构。

Let $\bC_{\bx}$ be positive definite and let $\bV_1 \in \R^{m \times m}$ be a whitening matrix. Then the $m \times m$ whitening matrices are exactly the matrices $\bQ\bV_1$ with $\bQ$ orthogonal. In particular, any two $m \times m$ whitening matrices satisfy $\bV_2 = \bQ\bV_1$ with $\bQ = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}$ orthogonal, and the corresponding white vectors satisfy $\bz_2 = \bQ\bz_1$.

设 $\bC_{\bx}$ 正定，$\bV_1 \in \R^{m \times m}$ 是一个白化矩阵。则全体 $m \times m$ 白化矩阵恰好是形如 $\bQ\bV_1$ 的矩阵，其中 $\bQ$ 正交。特别地，任意两个 $m \times m$ 白化矩阵满足 $\bV_2 = \bQ\bV_1$，其中 $\bQ = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}$ 是正交矩阵；相应的白向量满足 $\bz_2 = \bQ\bz_1$。

$(\bQ\bV_1)\bC_{\bx}(\bQ\bV_1)^\top = \bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$, so every $\bQ\bV_1$ whitens. Conversely, $\bV_1\bC_{\bx}\bV_1^\top = \bI$ shows that the square matrix $\bV_1$ is invertible and that $\bC_{\bx} = \bV_1^{-1}\bV_1^{-\top}$. If $\bV_2$ also whitens, then $\bQ = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}$ satisfies $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}\bV_1^{-\top}\bV_2^\top = \bV_2\bC_{\bx}\bV_2^\top = \bI$.

$(\bQ\bV_1)\bC_{\bx}(\bQ\bV_1)^\top = \bQ\bQ^\top = \bI$，所以每个 $\bQ\bV_1$ 都是白化矩阵。反之，由 $\bV_1\bC_{\bx}\bV_1^\top = \bI$ 可知方阵 $\bV_1$ 可逆，且 $\bC_{\bx} = \bV_1^{-1}\bV_1^{-\top}$。若 $\bV_2$ 也是白化矩阵，则 $\bQ = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}$ 满足 $\bQ\bQ^\top = \bV_2\bV_1^{-1}\bV_1^{-\top}\bV_2^\top = \bV_2\bC_{\bx}\bV_2^\top = \bI$。

Let $\bC_{\bx} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. 设 $\bC_{\bx} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$。它的特征值为 3 与 1，对应的特征向量为 $(1, 1)^\top/\sqrt2$ 与 $(-1, 1)^\top/\sqrt2$，所以 $\mathbf{E} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ 是旋转 45° 的矩阵。Cholesky 因子为 $\mathbf{L} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \sqrt2 & 0 \\ 1/\sqrt2 & \sqrt{3/2} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，三个白化矩阵分别是

$$\bV_{\mathrm{pca}} = \begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt6 & 1/\sqrt6 \\ -1/\sqrt2 & 1/\sqrt2 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \bV_{\mathrm{chol}} = \begin{pmatrix} 1/\sqrt2 & 0 \\ -1/\sqrt6 & \sqrt{2/3} \end{pmatrix},$$

As predicts, they differ by rotations: $\bV_{\mathrm{zca}} = \mathbf{E}\,\bV_{\mathrm{pca}}$, the rotation by 45°, and $\bV_{\mathrm{chol}} = \bQ\,\bV_{\mathrm{pca}}$ with $\bQ = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} \sqrt3/2 & -1/2 \\ 1/2 & \sqrt3/2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, the rotation by 30°. The whitened model白化后的模型

Now apply a whitening matrix to the ICA model. If $\bV \in \R^{n \times m}$ whitens $\bx = \bA\bs$, then

现在把白化矩阵作用到 ICA 模型上。若 $\bV \in \R^{n \times m}$ 是 $\bx = \bA\bs$ 的白化矩阵，则

$$\bz = \bV\bx = \tilde\bA\,\bs, \qquad \tilde\bA = \bV\bA \in \R^{n \times n}.$$

The whitened data follow an ICA model with the same sources and a square mixing matrix $\tilde\bA$, which turns out to be orthogonal.

白化后的数据服从一个源不变、混合矩阵为方阵 $\tilde\bA$ 的 ICA 模型，而 $\tilde\bA$ 恰好是正交的。

Let $\bx = \bA\bs$ with $\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ of full column rank and $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI$, and let $\bV \in \R^{n \times m}$ be a whitening matrix for $\bx$. Then $\tilde\bA = \bV\bA$ is orthogonal. If instead $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bD^2$ with $\bD$ diagonal and positive, then $\tilde\bA\bD$ is orthogonal.

设 $\bx = \bA\bs$，$\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ 列满秩，$\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI$，并设 $\bV \in \R^{n \times m}$ 是 $\bx$ 的白化矩阵。则 $\tilde\bA = \bV\bA$ 是正交矩阵。若改设 $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bD^2$，其中 $\bD$ 是对角元为正的对角矩阵，则 $\tilde\bA\bD$ 是正交矩阵。

$\bI = \E[\bz\bz^\top] = \bV\bA\,\E[\bs\bs^\top]\,\bA^\top\bV^\top = \tilde\bA\tilde\bA^\top$, and a square matrix with $\tilde\bA\tilde\bA^\top = \bI$ is orthogonal. With $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bD^2$, the same computation gives $(\tilde\bA\bD)(\tilde\bA\bD)^\top = \bI$.

$\bI = \E[\bz\bz^\top] = \bV\bA\,\E[\bs\bs^\top]\,\bA^\top\bV^\top = \tilde\bA\tilde\bA^\top$，而满足 $\tilde\bA\tilde\bA^\top = \bI$ 的方阵是正交矩阵。若 $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bD^2$，同样的计算给出 $(\tilde\bA\bD)(\tilde\bA\bD)^\top = \bI$。

Three consequences follow. First, all white versions of the data differ by rotations, also when $m > n$: if $\bV_1$ and $\bV_2$ both whiten, then $\bz_k = \tilde\bA_k\bs$ with $\tilde\bA_k$ orthogonal, so $\bz_2 = \tilde\bA_2\tilde\bA_1^\top\bz_1$. Second, ICA on whitened data is a search over orthogonal matrices. (As elsewhere in the book, we loosely call such matrices rotations; strictly, those with determinant $-1$ also contain a reflection.) Why second-order statistics cannot do more为什么二阶统计量做不到更多

After whitening, the covariance is used up. For every orthogonal $\bU$, the output $\by = \bU\bz$ is again white, $\E[\by\by^\top] = \bU\bU^\top = \bI$, so no criterion that depends on the data only through their covariance can prefer one rotation to another. To find $\bU$, ICA must use statistics of order higher than two or, if the sources are time series, their temporal structure.

白化之后，协方差已经用尽。对任意正交矩阵 $\bU$，输出 $\by = \bU\bz$ 仍然是白的，$\E[\by\by^\top] = \bU\bU^\top = \bI$；因此，任何只通过协方差依赖于数据的准则，都无法在不同旋转之间作出取舍。要找到 $\bU$，ICA 必须利用二阶以上的统计量；若源是时间序列，也可以利用它们的时间结构。

For Gaussian sources nothing else is available. If $\bs \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$, then $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$ and $\by = \bU\bz \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$ for every orthogonal $\bU$ (). The distribution of the outputs does not depend on $\bU$ at all, so every rotation is an equally good answer, and higher-order statistics cannot help, since all cumulants of order three and higher vanish ().

对高斯源，除协方差之外别无可用的信息。若 $\bs \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$，则 $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$，并且对任意正交矩阵 $\bU$ 都有 $\by = \bU\bz \sim \Ncal(\bzero, \bI)$（）。输出的分布与 $\bU$ 完全无关，每个旋转都是同样好的答案；高阶统计量也帮不上忙，因为三阶及以上的累积量全为零（）。

For non-Gaussian sources the higher-order cumulants are not rotation invariant. By applied to $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$, the kurtosis of the projection onto a unit vector $\bw$ is

对非高斯源，高阶累积量在旋转下不再不变。把 用于 $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$，可得 $\bz$ 在单位向量 $\bw$ 上投影的峭度：

$$\kurt(\bw^\top\bz) = \sum_{p=1}^n \kappa_p\,(\bw^\top\tilde\ba_p)^4,$$

where $\tilde\ba_p$ is the $p$-th column of $\tilde\bA$. If some $\kappa_p \ne 0$, this function is not constant on the unit sphere: with $\bq = \tilde\bA^\top\bw$ and $\be_p$ the $p$-th coordinate vector, its values at $\bq = \be_p$ and at $\bq = (\be_p + \be_r)/\sqrt2$ are $\kappa_p$ and $(\kappa_p + \kappa_r)/4$, and these can all be equal only if every $\kappa_p$ is zero. Fourth-order statistics therefore distinguish directions, and proves that the local maxima of $|\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ are exactly the directions $\pm\tilde\ba_p$ of the sources with nonzero kurtosis ().

其中 $\tilde\ba_p$ 是 $\tilde\bA$ 的第 $p$ 列。只要某个 $\kappa_p \ne 0$，这个函数在单位球面上就不是常数：记 $\bq = \tilde\bA^\top\bw$，$\be_p$ 为第 $p$ 个坐标向量，它在 $\bq = \be_p$ 与 $\bq = (\be_p + \be_r)/\sqrt2$ 处的值分别为 $\kappa_p$ 与 $(\kappa_p + \kappa_r)/4$，而这些值全部相等的唯一可能是所有 $\kappa_p$ 都为零。所以四阶统计量能够区分方向；将证明，$|\kurt(\bw^\top\bz)|$ 的局部极大点恰好是峭度非零的那些源的方向 $\pm\tilde\ba_p$（）。

Between the two extremes, shows that a single Gaussian source does no harm, whereas with $k \ge 2$ Gaussian sources any rotation within the subspace they span leaves the distribution of $\bz$ unchanged, so only that subspace can be identified (). The special role of the Gaussian is no accident: if a random vector with $n \ge 2$ independent components has a distribution that is invariant under all rotations, its components are Gaussian with a common variance ().

介于两种极端之间的情形，证明：单个高斯源无碍大局；而当有 $k \ge 2$ 个高斯源时，在它们张成的子空间内作任意旋转都不改变 $\bz$ 的分布，所以只能辨识出这个子空间（）。高斯分布的这种特殊地位并非偶然：若一个具有 $n \ge 2$ 个独立分量的随机向量，其分布在一切旋转下不变，则它的各分量都是方差相同的高斯变量（）。

Second-order statistics can do more when the samples are not i.i.d. If the sources are zero-mean stationary time series with cross-covariances zero at every lag (as holds for independent source processes), the lagged covariances of the whitened data are

当样本不再独立同分布时，二阶统计量可以做得更多。若各源是零均值平稳时间序列，且不同源在每个时滞上的互协方差都为零（相互独立的源过程满足此条件），则白化数据的时滞协方差为

$$\bC_{\bz}(\tau) = \E\bigl[\bz(t)\,\bz(t - \tau)^\top\bigr] = \tilde\bA\,\diag\bigl(\rho_1(\tau), \dots, \rho_n(\tau)\bigr)\,\tilde\bA^\top,$$

where $\rho_i(\tau) = \E[s_i(t)\,s_i(t - \tau)]$ is the autocorrelation of source $i$. These matrices are not invariant under rotation. They determine $\tilde\bA$ up to a signed permutation as soon as, for every pair of sources, the two autocorrelation functions differ at some lag, even if all sources are Gaussian; this is the basis of AMUSE and SOBI (, ). Under the i.i.d. model of , however, the covariance is all that second-order statistics provide.

其中 $\rho_i(\tau) = \E[s_i(t)\,s_i(t - \tau)]$ 是源 $i$ 的自相关函数。这些矩阵在旋转下不是不变的。只要对每一对源，它们的自相关函数在某个时滞处不同，这些矩阵就能在带符号置换的意义下确定 $\tilde\bA$，即使所有源都是高斯的也是如此；这正是 AMUSE 与 SOBI 的基础（，）。但在的独立同分布模型下，二阶统计量所能提供的只有协方差。

PCA dimension reductionPCA 降维

When there are more sensors than sources, $m > n$, whitening also reduces the dimension. In the noise-free model, $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top$ has rank $n$, and its column space, the signal subspace, is the column space of $\bA$. Let $\lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n > 0$ be its nonzero eigenvalues, $\mathbf{E}_n \in \R^{m \times n}$ the corresponding orthonormal eigenvectors and $\bLambda_n = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$, so that $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top$. Then $\bV = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top \in \R^{n \times m}$ satisfies $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$, and by $\bV\bA$ is an $n \times n$ orthogonal matrix. The reduction discards the $m - n$ directions in which the data have no variance.

当传感器多于源（$m > n$）时，白化同时完成降维。在无噪声模型中，$\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top$ 的秩为 $n$，它的列空间即信号子空间，就是 $\bA$ 的列空间。设 $\lambda_1 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_n > 0$ 是它的非零特征值，$\mathbf{E}_n \in \R^{m \times n}$ 是相应的标准正交特征向量，$\bLambda_n = \diag(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_n)$，于是 $\bC_{\bx} = \mathbf{E}_n\bLambda_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top$。则 $\bV = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top \in \R^{n \times m}$ 满足 $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$，并且由，$\bV\bA$ 是 $n \times n$ 正交矩阵。降维丢掉的是数据方差为零的那 $m - n$ 个方向。

Real data contain noise, and the eigenvalues beyond the $n$-th are then no longer zero. The simplest model in which the analysis stays exact adds isotropic noise.

真实数据含有噪声，第 $n$ 个以后的特征值不再为零。能让分析保持精确的最简单模型，是加上各向同性的噪声。

Let $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$, where $\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ has full column rank, $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI$, and the noise $\bn$ is independent of $\bs$ with $\E[\bn] = \bzero$ and $\E[\bn\bn^\top] = \sigma^2\bI_m$. Let $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n > 0$ be the nonzero eigenvalues of $\bA\bA^\top$, and let $\mathbf{E}_n$ and $\bLambda_n$ hold the leading $n$ eigenvectors and eigenvalues of $\bC_{\bx}$. Then:

设 $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$，其中 $\bA \in \R^{m \times n}$ 列满秩，$\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI$，噪声 $\bn$ 与 $\bs$ 独立，$\E[\bn] = \bzero$，$\E[\bn\bn^\top] = \sigma^2\bI_m$。设 $d_1 \ge \dots \ge d_n > 0$ 是 $\bA\bA^\top$ 的非零特征值，$\mathbf{E}_n$ 与 $\bLambda_n$ 分别是 $\bC_{\bx}$ 的前 $n$ 个特征向量与特征值。则：

  1. the eigenvalues of $\bC_{\bx}$ are $\lambda_i = d_i + \sigma^2$ for $i \le n$ and $\lambda_i = \sigma^2$ for $i > n$;
  2. the columns of $\mathbf{E}_n$ span the column space of $\bA$;
  3. $\bV = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ whitens $\bx$, but $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bI - \sigma^2\bLambda_n^{-1}$, so $\bV\bA$ is not orthogonal when $\sigma^2 > 0$;
  4. with $\bV = (\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$, the matrix $\bV\bA$ is orthogonal, and $\bV\bx = \bV\bA\bs + \bV\bn$, where the noise term has covariance $\sigma^2(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}$.
  1. $\bC_{\bx}$ 的特征值为：$i \le n$ 时 $\lambda_i = d_i + \sigma^2$，$i > n$ 时 $\lambda_i = \sigma^2$；
  2. $\mathbf{E}_n$ 的各列张成 $\bA$ 的列空间；
  3. $\bV = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ 能白化 $\bx$，但 $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bI - \sigma^2\bLambda_n^{-1}$，所以当 $\sigma^2 > 0$ 时 $\bV\bA$ 不是正交矩阵；
  4. 取 $\bV = (\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}_n^\top$，则 $\bV\bA$ 正交，且 $\bV\bx = \bV\bA\bs + \bV\bn$，其中噪声项的协方差为 $\sigma^2(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1}$。

Write $\bA\bA^\top = \mathbf{E}_n\bD\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ with $\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_n)$, where the columns of $\mathbf{E}_n$ are orthonormal and span the column space of $\bA$. Then $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI = \mathbf{E}_n(\bD + \sigma^2\bI)\mathbf{E}_n^\top + \sigma^2(\bI - \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top)$, where $\bI - \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ is the orthogonal projector onto the complement of the column space. This displays the eigenvalues and eigenvectors, proving 1 and 2; since $d_n > 0$, the leading $n$ eigenvalues are separated from $\sigma^2$, so the leading eigenspace is uniquely determined. Next, $\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bA\bA^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \bD = \bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI$, so for $\bV = \bM\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ with $\bM$ diagonal, $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bM(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bM$ and $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bM\bLambda_n\bM$. The choice $\bM = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}$ gives 3. The choice $\bM = (\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}$ gives 4, with noise covariance $\sigma^2\bV\bV^\top = \sigma^2\bM^2$.

记 $\bA\bA^\top = \mathbf{E}_n\bD\mathbf{E}_n^\top$，$\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_n)$，其中 $\mathbf{E}_n$ 的各列标准正交并张成 $\bA$ 的列空间。则 $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top + \sigma^2\bI = \mathbf{E}_n(\bD + \sigma^2\bI)\mathbf{E}_n^\top + \sigma^2(\bI - \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top)$，其中 $\bI - \mathbf{E}_n\mathbf{E}_n^\top$ 是到列空间正交补上的正交投影。这个式子直接给出了特征值与特征向量，从而证明了 1 与 2；由于 $d_n > 0$，前 $n$ 个特征值与 $\sigma^2$ 分得开，所以前 $n$ 维特征子空间是唯一确定的。其次，$\mathbf{E}_n^\top\bA\bA^\top\mathbf{E}_n = \bD = \bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI$，所以对 $\bV = \bM\mathbf{E}_n^\top$（$\bM$ 为对角矩阵），有 $(\bV\bA)(\bV\bA)^\top = \bM(\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)\bM$，$\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bM\bLambda_n\bM$。取 $\bM = \bLambda_n^{-1/2}$ 得 3；取 $\bM = (\bLambda_n - \sigma^2\bI)^{-1/2}$ 得 4，此时噪声协方差为 $\sigma^2\bV\bV^\top = \sigma^2\bM^2$。

PCA thus recovers the signal subspace exactly, even with noise, and discards the noise in the $m - n$ complementary directions; this is the main denoising effect of the reduction. The noise inside the signal subspace remains. Ordinary whitening then produces a whitened mixing matrix that is not quite orthogonal, so even the best rotation leaves a bias. The bias-corrected whitening of item 4 needs an estimate of $\sigma^2$, such as the average of the $m - n$ smallest eigenvalues, which is the maximum-likelihood estimate in probabilistic PCA (Tipping & Bishop, 1999). 可见即使有噪声，PCA 也能准确地恢复信号子空间，并丢掉其余 $m - n$ 个方向上的噪声，这是降维最主要的去噪作用。信号子空间之内的噪声则保留下来。于是普通白化给出的白化混合矩阵并不严格正交，即使找到最好的旋转也会留下偏差。第 4 条中的偏差校正白化需要估计 $\sigma^2$，例如取最小的 $m - n$ 个特征值的平均，这正是概率 PCA 中的最大似然估计（Tipping & Bishop, 1999）。含噪 ICA 将在中讨论。

In practice $n$ is unknown and must be read off the eigenvalues of the sample covariance, where the noise eigenvalues are no longer equal. Even pure white noise spreads them out: if $m/T \to \gamma \in (0, 1]$, the eigenvalues of the sample covariance of white noise with variance $\sigma^2$ fill the interval $[\sigma^2(1 - \sqrt\gamma)^2,\ \sigma^2(1 + \sqrt\gamma)^2]$ (Marčenko & Pastur, 1967). With $m = 250$ sensors and $T = 1000$ samples, for example, the noise eigenvalues range from about $0.25\,\sigma^2$ to $2.25\,\sigma^2$, and a weak source can hide among them. discusses how to choose $n$. In spatial ICA of fMRI, the role of the $m$ sensors is played by the time points, and PCA reduces them to far fewer components before ICA is run ().

实践中 $n$ 未知，只能从样本协方差的特征值中读出，而样本协方差的噪声特征值不再相等。即使是纯白噪声，也会使它们散开：若 $m/T \to \gamma \in (0, 1]$，方差为 $\sigma^2$ 的白噪声的样本协方差的特征值铺满区间 $[\sigma^2(1 - \sqrt\gamma)^2,\ \sigma^2(1 + \sqrt\gamma)^2]$（Marčenko & Pastur, 1967）。例如有 $m = 250$ 个传感器、$T = 1000$ 个样本时，噪声特征值大约从 $0.25\,\sigma^2$ 分布到 $2.25\,\sigma^2$，较弱的源可能淹没其中。讨论如何选择 $n$。在 fMRI 的空间 ICA 中，扮演 $m$ 个传感器角色的是时间点，运行 ICA 之前要先用 PCA 把它们降到少得多的成分（）。

Whitening with finite samples有限样本下的白化

With finite $T$, whitening uses the sample covariance. The whitened sample $\hat\bz(t) = \hat\bV\bx(t)$ is then exactly white, $\frac1T\sum_t \hat\bz(t)\hat\bz(t)^\top = \bI$ after centering, but the whitened mixing matrix $\hat\bV\bA$ is orthogonal only up to an error of order $T^{-1/2}$ (for sources with finite fourth moments). An orthogonal method estimates an orthogonal $\bU$, so its outputs $\by(t) = \bU\hat\bz(t)$ are exactly uncorrelated in the sample. The true sources are not: their sample covariance $\hat\bC_{\bs}$ differs from $\bI$ by sampling fluctuations. The constraint imposes a property that the sources do not have, and the resulting error passes into the separation, where no choice of $\bU$ can remove it. The following proposition makes this precise; the notation $O_P$ is recalled in .

当 $T$ 有限时，白化用的是样本协方差。这时白化后的样本 $\hat\bz(t) = \hat\bV\bx(t)$（中心化之后）严格满足 $\frac1T\sum_t \hat\bz(t)\hat\bz(t)^\top = \bI$，但白化后的混合矩阵 $\hat\bV\bA$ 只在 $T^{-1/2}$ 量级的误差内是正交的（对四阶矩有限的源而言）。正交方法估计的是正交矩阵 $\bU$，所以它的输出 $\by(t) = \bU\hat\bz(t)$ 在样本中严格不相关。真实的源却并非如此：它们的样本协方差 $\hat\bC_{\bs}$ 由于抽样波动而偏离 $\bI$。这个约束强加了源本身并不具备的性质，由此产生的误差进入分离结果，任何 $\bU$ 都无法消除。下面的命题把这一点说清楚；记号 $O_P$ 的含义见。

Let $\bx(t) = \bA\bs(t)$, $t = 1, \dots, T$, with $\bA \in \R^{n \times n}$ invertible and i.i.d. source vectors whose components are independent with zero mean and unit variance. Let $\hat\bW = \bU\hat\bV$, where $\hat\bV$ whitens the sample covariance of the $\bx(t)$ exactly and $\bU$ is orthogonal, possibly depending on the data. Then the global matrix $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$ satisfies $\hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top = \bI$, where $\hat\bC_{\bs}$ is the sample covariance of the sources. Suppose that, after the order and signs of the outputs have been fixed, $\hat\bP = \bI + \bepsilon$ with $\bepsilon = O_P(T^{-1/2})$. Then for every pair $i \ne j$, $\sqrt T(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ converges in distribution to $\Ncal(0, 1)$. Consequently, if $(\sqrt T\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt T\varepsilon_{ji})$ converges in distribution to a limit with variances $v_{ij}$ and $v_{ji}$, then $v_{ij} + v_{ji} \ge \tfrac12$.

设 $\bx(t) = \bA\bs(t)$，$t = 1, \dots, T$，其中 $\bA \in \R^{n \times n}$ 可逆，源向量独立同分布，其各分量相互独立、均值为零、方差为一。设 $\hat\bW = \bU\hat\bV$，其中 $\hat\bV$ 恰好白化 $\bx(t)$ 的样本协方差，$\bU$ 是正交矩阵（可以依赖于数据）。则全局矩阵 $\hat\bP = \hat\bW\bA$ 满足 $\hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top = \bI$，其中 $\hat\bC_{\bs}$ 是源的样本协方差。设固定输出的顺序与符号之后，$\hat\bP = \bI + \bepsilon$，且 $\bepsilon = O_P(T^{-1/2})$。则对每一对 $i \ne j$，$\sqrt T(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji})$ 依分布收敛到 $\Ncal(0, 1)$。因此，若 $(\sqrt T\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt T\varepsilon_{ji})$ 依分布收敛到某个方差分别为 $v_{ij}$ 与 $v_{ji}$ 的极限，则 $v_{ij} + v_{ji} \ge \tfrac12$。

Centering commutes with $\bA$, so the sample covariance of the observations is $\hat\bC_{\bx} = \bA\hat\bC_{\bs}\bA^\top$, and $\hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top = \bU\hat\bV\hat\bC_{\bx}\hat\bV^\top\bU^\top = \bU\bU^\top = \bI$. Write $\hat\bC_{\bs} = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}$. For $i \ne j$, $\Delta_{ij} = \frac1T\sum_t s_i(t)s_j(t) - \bar s_i\bar s_j$. The first term is an average of i.i.d. variables with mean 0 and variance $\E[s_i^2]\,\E[s_j^2] = 1$, so $\sqrt T$ times it converges to $\Ncal(0, 1)$ by the central limit theorem, and the second term is $O_P(1/T)$. By the law of large numbers $\boldsymbol{\Delta} \to \bzero$ in probability. Expanding $(\bI + \bepsilon)(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)^\top = \bI$ gives $\bepsilon + \bepsilon^\top + \boldsymbol{\Delta} = -(\bepsilon\boldsymbol{\Delta} + \boldsymbol{\Delta}\bepsilon^\top + \bepsilon\bepsilon^\top + \bepsilon\boldsymbol{\Delta}\bepsilon^\top)$, and the right-hand side is $o_P(T^{-1/2})$, since each term is a product of a factor of order $T^{-1/2}$ and a factor tending to zero. Hence $\sqrt T(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji}) = -\sqrt T\,\Delta_{ij} + o_P(1)$, which converges to $\Ncal(0, 1)$. Finally, if $(\sqrt T\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt T\varepsilon_{ji})$ converges to $(X, Y)$, then $X + Y \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$, and $1 = \Var(X + Y) \le 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$ because $\Var(X + Y) + \Var(X - Y) = 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$.

中心化与左乘 $\bA$ 可交换，所以观测的样本协方差为 $\hat\bC_{\bx} = \bA\hat\bC_{\bs}\bA^\top$，于是 $\hat\bP\hat\bC_{\bs}\hat\bP^\top = \bU\hat\bV\hat\bC_{\bx}\hat\bV^\top\bU^\top = \bU\bU^\top = \bI$。记 $\hat\bC_{\bs} = \bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta}$。对 $i \ne j$，$\Delta_{ij} = \frac1T\sum_t s_i(t)s_j(t) - \bar s_i\bar s_j$。第一项是均值为 0、方差为 $\E[s_i^2]\,\E[s_j^2] = 1$ 的独立同分布变量的平均，由中心极限定理，它乘以 $\sqrt T$ 后收敛到 $\Ncal(0, 1)$；第二项是 $O_P(1/T)$。由大数定律，$\boldsymbol{\Delta}$ 依概率收敛到 $\bzero$。展开 $(\bI + \bepsilon)(\bI + \boldsymbol{\Delta})(\bI + \bepsilon)^\top = \bI$ 得 $\bepsilon + \bepsilon^\top + \boldsymbol{\Delta} = -(\bepsilon\boldsymbol{\Delta} + \boldsymbol{\Delta}\bepsilon^\top + \bepsilon\bepsilon^\top + \bepsilon\boldsymbol{\Delta}\bepsilon^\top)$，右端为 $o_P(T^{-1/2})$，因为其中每一项都是一个 $T^{-1/2}$ 量级的因子与一个趋于零的因子之积。因此 $\sqrt T(\varepsilon_{ij} + \varepsilon_{ji}) = -\sqrt T\,\Delta_{ij} + o_P(1)$，它收敛到 $\Ncal(0, 1)$。最后，若 $(\sqrt T\varepsilon_{ij}, \sqrt T\varepsilon_{ji})$ 收敛到 $(X, Y)$，则 $X + Y \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$；又因为 $\Var(X + Y) + \Var(X - Y) = 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$，所以 $1 = \Var(X + Y) \le 2\Var(X) + 2\Var(Y)$。

In the terms of , where the interference-to-signal ratio is $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$ (), every orthogonal method has $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$ to first order, whatever its contrast function and whatever the source distributions, and a method that treats the two entries alike has $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} \ge 1/(4T)$. (Here $\bepsilon = \hat\bP - \bI$ keeps its diagonal; the gain error of , $\hat P_{ij}/\hat P_{ii}$, differs from $\varepsilon_{ij}$ by $O_P(1/T)$, so the two definitions give the same limiting distribution. Passing from this distributional expansion to the stated mean-square ISR approximation additionally requires convergence of second moments, for example uniform integrability of the scaled squared errors.) Cardoso (1994) pointed out this limit of orthogonal source separation (see also Cardoso, 1998). It is attained by an oracle that knows $\bA$: the rotation that brings $\bU\hat\bV\bA$ closest to $\bI$ gives $\hat\bP = \hat\bC_{\bs}^{-1/2}$, which for sources with finite fourth moments equals $\bI - \tfrac12\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O_P(1/T)$, so that $\varepsilon_{ij} \approx \varepsilon_{ji} \approx -\tfrac12\Delta_{ij}$ (). The simulation below whitens a uniform and a Laplacian source with the sample covariance and applies this oracle rotation.

用的语言来说，干扰信号比为 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} = \E[\varepsilon_{ij}^2]$（），那么在一阶近似下，任何正交方法都满足 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$，与它采用的对比函数无关，也与源的分布无关；若方法对两个元素一视同仁，则 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} \ge 1/(4T)$。（这里 $\bepsilon = \hat\bP - \bI$ 保留了对角元；中的增益误差 $\hat P_{ij}/\hat P_{ii}$ 与 $\varepsilon_{ij}$ 只差 $O_P(1/T)$，所以两种定义有相同的极限分布。要进一步把这一依分布展开换成所述均方 ISR 近似，还需要二阶矩收敛，例如缩放后平方误差的一致可积性。）Cardoso（1994）指出了正交源分离的这一极限（另见 Cardoso, 1998）。知道 $\bA$ 的“神谕”可以达到这个下限：使 $\bU\hat\bV\bA$ 最接近 $\bI$ 的旋转给出 $\hat\bP = \hat\bC_{\bs}^{-1/2}$；当源的四阶矩有限时，它等于 $\bI - \tfrac12\boldsymbol{\Delta} + O_P(1/T)$，于是 $\varepsilon_{ij} \approx \varepsilon_{ji} \approx -\tfrac12\Delta_{ij}$（）。下面的模拟用样本协方差白化一个均匀源和一个拉普拉斯源，然后施加这个神谕旋转。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(1)
A = np.array([[1.0, 0.6],
              [0.4, 1.0]])
T, runs = 1000, 10000
err = np.zeros(runs)
for r in range(runs):
    S = np.vstack([rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), T),  # unit variance
                   rng.laplace(0.0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), T)])
    X = A @ S
    X = X - X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
    d, E = np.linalg.eigh(X @ X.T / T)
    V = np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T      # PCA whitening, sample covariance
    M = V @ A                         # whitened mixing, nearly orthogonal
    Um, _, Vmt = np.linalg.svd(M)
    U = (Um @ Vmt).T                  # oracle: U @ M closest to I
    P = U @ M                         # best orthogonal global matrix
    err[r] = P[0, 1]**2 + P[1, 0]**2
mean, se = T * err.mean(), T * err.std() / np.sqrt(runs)
print(f"T * E[eps12^2 + eps21^2] = {mean:.3f} +/- {se:.3f}")

It prints $T\,\E[\varepsilon_{12}^2 + \varepsilon_{21}^2] = 0.512 \pm 0.007$, in agreement with the predicted $\tfrac12$ up to Monte Carlo error and terms of order $1/T$. An algorithm that must estimate the rotation from the data cannot do better, and in general it does worse: it attains the floor only when its estimate of the rotation has no first-order error of its own. Symmetric FastICA with $g(u) = u^3$ applied to binary sources is such a case, because $s^3 = s$ reduces its estimating equations, to first order, to $\varepsilon_{ij} = \varepsilon_{ji}$; for other sources it is less accurate (). Methods that are not constrained to be orthogonal, such as maximum likelihood with the natural gradient (), have no such floor, and for sources with large Fisher information the Cramér–Rao bound () lies far below it. For nearly Gaussian sources, in contrast, the error of estimating the rotation dominates and the floor matters little.

程序输出 $T\,\E[\varepsilon_{12}^2 + \varepsilon_{21}^2] = 0.512 \pm 0.007$，在蒙特卡罗误差与 $1/T$ 量级的项之内与预言值 $\tfrac12$ 一致。必须从数据中估计旋转的算法不可能做得更好，一般还会更差：只有当它对旋转的估计本身没有一阶误差时，才能达到这个下限。对二值源使用 $g(u) = u^3$ 的对称 FastICA 就是这样的例子：由于 $s^3 = s$，它的估计方程在一阶近似下化为 $\varepsilon_{ij} = \varepsilon_{ji}$；对其他源，它的精度要差一些（）。不受正交约束的方法，例如用自然梯度求最大似然（），没有这个下限；对 Fisher 信息很大的源，Cramér–Rao 界（）远低于它。反之，对接近高斯的源，估计旋转本身的误差占主导，这个下限就无关紧要了。

Conditioning. In the noise-free model and exact arithmetic, the statistical error of an orthogonally equivariant method does not depend on the conditioning of $\bA$: whatever $\bA$ is, the whitened data are $\hat\bV\bA\bs = \bQ\hat\bC_{\bs}^{-1/2}\bs$ for some orthogonal $\bQ$ (). This requires the equivariance assumption in that exercise; an arbitrary orthogonal optimizer need not have it. Floating-point errors and noise change the conclusion. In the setting of with $m = n$, ordinary whitening leaves noise with covariance $\sigma^2\bLambda^{-1}$ in the whitened data, so the share of noise along the $i$-th principal direction is $\sigma^2/\lambda_i$. If $\bA$ is ill-conditioned, for example because two of its columns are nearly collinear, some $\lambda_i$ is small, and the corresponding whitened coordinate is dominated by noise. A large ratio $\lambda_1/\lambda_n$ is therefore a warning sign, and a reason to reconsider the number of components ().

条件数。在无噪声模型与精确算术下，正交等变方法的统计误差不依赖于 $\bA$ 的条件数：无论 $\bA$ 是什么，白化后的数据都是 $\hat\bV\bA\bs = \bQ\hat\bC_{\bs}^{-1/2}\bs$，其中 $\bQ$ 为某个正交矩阵（）。这里需要该习题中的等变假设，任意一个正交优化器未必满足。浮点误差和噪声都会改变结论。在的设定下取 $m = n$，普通白化后数据中残留的噪声协方差为 $\sigma^2\bLambda^{-1}$，所以第 $i$ 个主方向上噪声所占的比例为 $\sigma^2/\lambda_i$。若 $\bA$ 病态，例如它有两列几乎共线，则某个 $\lambda_i$ 很小，相应的白化坐标几乎全是噪声。因此 $\lambda_1/\lambda_n$ 很大是一个警示信号，提醒我们重新考虑成分的个数（）。

A rank-aware SVD implementation显式检查秩的 SVD 实现

For centered data $\tilde\bX=\bE\bD\bF^\top$, retain $r$ nonzero singular values and use the divisor $T$ consistently. The whitening and reconstruction identities are

对中心化数据 $\tilde\bX=\bE\bD\bF^\top$，保留 $r$ 个非零奇异值，并统一采用分母 $T$。白化与重构恒等式为

$$\bV=\sqrt T\,\bD_r^{-1}\bE_r^\top,\qquad \bZ=\bV\tilde\bX=\sqrt T\,\bF_r^\top,\qquad \frac{\bZ\bZ^\top}{T}=\bI_r,\qquad \bV^+\bZ=\bE_r\bE_r^\top\tilde\bX.$$

The rank tolerance below detects numerical zeros; it is not a scientific model-order selector. Choosing $r$ smaller than the available rank discards data and must be justified separately (). The returned mean and whitening matrix should be reused when transforming new observations.

下列秩阈值用于识别数值上的零奇异值，并不是科学意义上的模型阶数选择准则。若选择的 $r$ 小于可用秩，就会丢弃数据，必须另行说明理由（）。变换新观测时，应复用函数返回的均值和白化矩阵。

import numpy as np

def whiten_svd(X, rank=None):
    X = np.asarray(X, dtype=float)           # sensors by observations
    if X.ndim != 2 or X.shape[0] < 1 or X.shape[1] < 2 or not np.isfinite(X).all():
        raise ValueError("Expected finite 2-D data with at least two observations")
    mean = X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
    Xc = X - mean
    E, d, _ = np.linalg.svd(Xc, full_matrices=False)
    tol = np.finfo(float).eps * max(Xc.shape) * d[0]
    available = int(np.count_nonzero(d > tol))
    r = available if rank is None else rank
    if not isinstance(r, (int, np.integer)) or not 1 <= r <= available:
        raise ValueError("Requested rank exceeds the numerical signal rank")
    V = (np.sqrt(X.shape[1]) / d[:r, None]) * E[:, :r].T
    Z = V @ Xc
    return mean, V, Z

rng = np.random.default_rng(23)
S = rng.standard_normal((2, 2000))
A = np.array([[1., .4], [.2, 1.], [1.2, 1.4]])
X = A @ S
mean, V, Z = whiten_svd(X)
assert Z.shape == (2, X.shape[1])
assert np.allclose(Z @ Z.T / X.shape[1], np.eye(2))
assert np.allclose(np.linalg.pinv(V) @ Z + mean, X)
print("rank-aware whitening and reconstruction checks passed")

Regularization changes the target. Replacing a small eigenvalue $\lambda_i$ by $\lambda_i+\alpha$ can improve numerical stability, but $(\bC+\alpha\bI)^{-1/2}$ gives transformed covariance eigenvalues $\lambda_i/(\lambda_i+\alpha)$, not one. Such shrinkage is useful in some problems, but it is not exact whitening and the orthogonal-mixing proofs cannot be applied unchanged. Never silently divide by zero, clip a negative covariance eigenvalue into a large inverse weight, or treat a dropped direction as a recovered source.

正则化会改变目标。用 $\lambda_i+\alpha$ 替代很小的特征值 $\lambda_i$ 可以提高数值稳定性，但 $(\bC+\alpha\bI)^{-1/2}$ 使变换后协方差的特征值成为 $\lambda_i/(\lambda_i+\alpha)$，而不是一。此类收缩在某些问题中有用，却不是精确白化，不能原样套用正交混合的证明。不要静默地除以零，把负的协方差特征值裁成极小正数再赋予巨大逆权重，或把丢弃的方向当作已经恢复的源。

Key points

本章要点

  • A whitening matrix satisfies $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$. PCA ($\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$), ZCA ($\bC_{\bx}^{-1/2}$) and Cholesky ($\mathbf{L}^{-1}$) whitening differ only by orthogonal factors, and all white versions of the data differ by rotations.
  • For unit-variance sources the whitened mixing matrix $\tilde\bA = \bV\bA$ is orthogonal. ICA reduces to finding a rotation $\bU$, with $\bW = \bU\bV$; the rotation has $n(n-1)/2$ parameters, and the remaining ambiguity is a signed permutation.
  • White data stay white under every rotation, and Gaussian data are rotation invariant in distribution. For i.i.d. samples second-order statistics cannot determine $\bU$; higher-order statistics can, when the sources are non-Gaussian.
  • When $m > n$, PCA recovers the signal subspace even with isotropic noise; ordinary whitening then leaves $\bV\bA$ slightly non-orthogonal, which bias-corrected whitening repairs.
  • With finite samples, orthogonal methods inherit the error of the sample covariance: $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$ to first order, whatever the contrast.
  • 白化矩阵满足 $\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top = \bI$。PCA 白化（$\bLambda^{-1/2}\mathbf{E}^\top$）、ZCA 白化（$\bC_{\bx}^{-1/2}$）与 Cholesky 白化（$\mathbf{L}^{-1}$）之间只差正交因子，数据的所有白化版本之间只差旋转。
  • 对单位方差的源，白化后的混合矩阵 $\tilde\bA = \bV\bA$ 是正交的。ICA 归结为寻找旋转 $\bU$，且 $\bW = \bU\bV$；旋转有 $n(n-1)/2$ 个参数，剩下的不确定性是带符号的置换。
  • 白数据经任何旋转后仍然是白的，而高斯数据的分布在旋转下不变。对独立同分布样本，二阶统计量无法确定 $\bU$；当源非高斯时，高阶统计量可以。
  • 当 $m > n$ 时，即使有各向同性噪声，PCA 也能恢复信号子空间；此时普通白化使 $\bV\bA$ 略偏离正交，偏差校正白化可以修正这一点。
  • 在有限样本下，正交方法继承了样本协方差的误差：在一阶近似下 $\mathrm{ISR}_{ij} + \mathrm{ISR}_{ji} \ge 1/(2T)$，与对比函数无关。

Let $\bC_{\bx} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & \rho \\ \rho & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ with $|\rho| < 1$. Find the Cholesky whitening matrix and show that $z_1 = x_1$ and $z_2 = (x_2 - \rho x_1)/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2}$. Interpret $z_2$ in terms of linear regression.

设 $\bC_{\bx} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & \rho \\ \rho & 1 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，$|\rho| < 1$。求 Cholesky 白化矩阵，并证明 $z_1 = x_1$，$z_2 = (x_2 - \rho x_1)/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2}$。用线性回归的语言解释 $z_2$。

The matrix $\mathbf{L} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ \rho & \sqrt{1 - \rho^2} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ is lower triangular with a positive diagonal and $\mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & \rho \\ \rho & \rho^2 + 1 - \rho^2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr) = \bC_{\bx}$. Its inverse is $\mathbf{L}^{-1} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -\rho/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2} & 1/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$, which gives the stated $z_1$ and $z_2$. The best linear predictor of $x_2$ from $x_1$ is $(\E[x_1x_2]/\E[x_1^2])\,x_1 = \rho x_1$, with residual variance $1 - \rho^2$, so $z_2$ is the standardized regression residual. This is the Gram–Schmidt step: remove from $x_2$ its component along $z_1$, then normalize. With more variables, $z_k$ is the standardized residual of $x_k$ regressed on $x_1, \dots, x_{k-1}$.

矩阵 $\mathbf{L} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ \rho & \sqrt{1 - \rho^2} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$ 是对角元为正的下三角矩阵，且 $\mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & \rho \\ \rho & \rho^2 + 1 - \rho^2 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr) = \bC_{\bx}$。它的逆为 $\mathbf{L}^{-1} = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -\rho/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2} & 1/\sqrt{1 - \rho^2} \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$，由此即得所述的 $z_1$ 与 $z_2$。用 $x_1$ 对 $x_2$ 作最佳线性预测，结果是 $(\E[x_1x_2]/\E[x_1^2])\,x_1 = \rho x_1$，残差方差为 $1 - \rho^2$，所以 $z_2$ 是标准化的回归残差。这正是 Gram–Schmidt 的一步：从 $x_2$ 中去掉它沿 $z_1$ 的分量，再归一化。变量更多时，$z_k$ 是 $x_k$ 对 $x_1, \dots, x_{k-1}$ 回归后的标准化残差。

对 $n = 10$ 个单位方差源的方阵模型，协方差确定了多少个数？旋转 $\bU$ 有多少个参数？证明形如 $\bPi\bD$（$\bPi$ 为置换矩阵，$\bD$ 为对角矩阵）的正交矩阵必有 $\bD = \diag(\pm 1)$，并数一数能分离给定白化混合的正交矩阵 $\bU$ 有多少个，其中又有多少个是行列式为 $+1$ 的严格意义上的旋转。

The covariance is symmetric, so it determines $n(n+1)/2 = 55$ numbers; the rotation has $n(n-1)/2 = 45$ parameters, and $55 + 45 = 100 = n^2$. If $\bPi\bD$ is orthogonal, then $\bI = (\bPi\bD)^\top(\bPi\bD) = \bD\bPi^\top\bPi\bD = \bD^2$, so every $d_i^2 = 1$. The separating orthogonal matrices are $\bU = \bPi\bD\tilde\bA^\top$, with $\bPi$ one of $n!$ permutations and $\bD$ one of $2^n$ sign patterns. Distinct pairs $(\bPi, \bD)$ give distinct matrices $\bPi\bD$, since the position of the nonzero entry in each column determines $\bPi$ and its sign determines $\bD$. There are therefore $2^n n! = 2^{10} \cdot 10! = 3\,715\,891\,200$ separating orthogonal matrices for $n = 10$, all equally good answers. Exactly half of them, $2^{n-1}n! = 1\,857\,945\,600$, have determinant $+1$, because changing one sign in $\bD$ changes the sign of $\det\bU = \det(\bPi)\det(\bD)\det(\tilde\bA^\top)$.

Let $\bC_{\bx}$ be positive definite ($m = n$). Show that among all $m \times m$ whitening matrices, $\bV = \bC_{\bx}^{-1/2}$ is the unique minimizer of $\E\|\bV\bx - \bx\|^2$. Deduce that for unit-variance sources, ZCA whitening gives the whitened mixing matrix $(\bA\bA^\top)^{-1/2}\bA$.

By , $\bV = \bQ\bC_{\bx}^{-1/2}$ with $\bQ$ orthogonal. Then $\E\|\bV\bx - \bx\|^2 = \tr(\bV\bC_{\bx}\bV^\top) - 2\tr(\bV\bC_{\bx}) + \tr(\bC_{\bx}) = n + \tr(\bC_{\bx}) - 2\tr(\bQ\bC_{\bx}^{1/2})$, so we must maximize $\tr(\bQ\bC_{\bx}^{1/2})$. With $\bC_{\bx}^{1/2} = \sum_i \lambda_i^{1/2}\be_i\be_i^\top$, where the $\be_i$ are the orthonormal eigenvectors (the columns of $\mathbf{E}$), we get $\tr(\bQ\bC_{\bx}^{1/2}) = \sum_i \lambda_i^{1/2}\,\be_i^\top\bQ\be_i \le \sum_i \lambda_i^{1/2}$, because $|\be_i^\top\bQ\be_i| \le \|\be_i\|\,\|\bQ\be_i\| = 1$. Equality requires $\be_i^\top\bQ\be_i = 1$, which for unit vectors means $\bQ\be_i = \be_i$, for every $i$; hence $\bQ = \bI$. For $\bx = \bA\bs$ with $\E[\bs\bs^\top] = \bI$, $\bC_{\bx} = \bA\bA^\top$ and $\tilde\bA = \bC_{\bx}^{-1/2}\bA = (\bA\bA^\top)^{-1/2}\bA$, the orthogonal polar factor of $\bA$ ().

Let $\bs = (s_1, s_2)^\top$ have independent components, and suppose that $\bQ\bs$ has the same distribution as $\bs$ for every rotation $\bQ$ of the plane. Prove that $s_1$ and $s_2$ are both $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$ for a common $\sigma^2 \ge 0$, where $\sigma^2 = 0$ means $s_1 = s_2 = 0$. Extend the result to $n > 2$ components. (Hint: show that $\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega)$ depends only on $\|\bomega\|$ and use .)

设 $\bs = (s_1, s_2)^\top$ 的分量独立，并且对平面上的每个旋转 $\bQ$，$\bQ\bs$ 都与 $\bs$ 同分布。证明 $s_1$ 与 $s_2$ 都服从 $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$，其中 $\sigma^2 \ge 0$ 为公共的方差（$\sigma^2 = 0$ 表示 $s_1 = s_2 = 0$）。再把结论推广到 $n > 2$ 个分量。（提示：证明 $\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega)$ 只依赖于 $\|\bomega\|$，再利用。）

By the rule for linear maps, $\varphi_{\bQ\bs}(\bomega) = \varphi_{\bs}(\bQ^\top\bomega)$, so invariance gives $\varphi_{\bs}(\bQ^\top\bomega) = \varphi_{\bs}(\bomega)$ for all rotations, and $\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega) = g(\|\bomega\|)$ for some function $g$ on $[0, \infty)$. The rotation by $\pi$ maps $\bs$ to $-\bs$, so $\varphi_{\bs}$ is real. By independence $\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega) = \varphi_1(\omega_1)\varphi_2(\omega_2)$; setting $\omega_2 = 0$ gives $\varphi_1(\omega_1) = g(|\omega_1|)$, and likewise $\varphi_2(\omega_2) = g(|\omega_2|)$. Hence $g\bigl(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\bigr) = g(|a|)\,g(|b|)$ for all real $a$ and $b$. Let $h(t) = g(\sqrt t)$ for $t \ge 0$. Then $h(t + u) = h(t)h(u)$, $h$ is continuous and real, and $h(0) = 1$. Since $h(t) = h(t/2)^2 \ge 0$, and $h(t_0) = 0$ would force $h(t_0/2^k) = 0$ for all $k$, contradicting continuity at 0, $h$ is positive. So $\log h$ is a continuous solution of Cauchy's equation $f(t + u) = f(t) + f(u)$ on $[0, \infty)$, hence linear: $h(t) = e^{ct}$. Because $|h| \le 1$, $c \le 0$; write $c = -\sigma^2/2$. Then $\varphi_1(\omega) = \varphi_2(\omega) = e^{-\sigma^2\omega^2/2}$, the characteristic function of $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$, and uniqueness gives the claim. For $n > 2$, the rotations acting on two coordinates $i$ and $j$ and fixing the others are orthogonal matrices, and they rotate the pair $(s_i, s_j)$ in its plane. Applying the two-dimensional result to every pair shows that all components are $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$ with one common $\sigma^2$.

由线性变换的规则，$\varphi_{\bQ\bs}(\bomega) = \varphi_{\bs}(\bQ^\top\bomega)$，所以由不变性，对一切旋转有 $\varphi_{\bs}(\bQ^\top\bomega) = \varphi_{\bs}(\bomega)$，从而 $\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega) = g(\|\bomega\|)$，$g$ 是 $[0, \infty)$ 上的某个函数。旋转 $\pi$ 把 $\bs$ 变成 $-\bs$，所以 $\varphi_{\bs}$ 取实值。由独立性，$\varphi_{\bs}(\bomega) = \varphi_1(\omega_1)\varphi_2(\omega_2)$；令 $\omega_2 = 0$ 得 $\varphi_1(\omega_1) = g(|\omega_1|)$，同理 $\varphi_2(\omega_2) = g(|\omega_2|)$。因此对一切实数 $a$、$b$ 有 $g\bigl(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\bigr) = g(|a|)\,g(|b|)$。令 $h(t) = g(\sqrt t)$，$t \ge 0$，则 $h(t + u) = h(t)h(u)$，$h$ 连续且取实值，$h(0) = 1$。由于 $h(t) = h(t/2)^2 \ge 0$，而若 $h(t_0) = 0$，则对所有 $k$ 有 $h(t_0/2^k) = 0$，与 $h$ 在 0 处连续矛盾，所以 $h$ 处处为正。于是 $\log h$ 是 $[0, \infty)$ 上 Cauchy 方程 $f(t + u) = f(t) + f(u)$ 的连续解，因而是线性的：$h(t) = e^{ct}$。由 $|h| \le 1$ 得 $c \le 0$，记 $c = -\sigma^2/2$。于是 $\varphi_1(\omega) = \varphi_2(\omega) = e^{-\sigma^2\omega^2/2}$，正是 $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$ 的特征函数，由唯一性即得结论。对 $n > 2$，只作用于第 $i$、$j$ 两个坐标而保持其余坐标不动的旋转是正交矩阵，它们在 $(s_i, s_j)$ 所在平面内旋转这一对分量。对每一对分量应用二维的结论，即知所有分量都服从同一个 $\Ncal(0, \sigma^2)$。
