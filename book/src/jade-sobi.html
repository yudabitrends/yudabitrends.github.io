Small calculation: a second lag resolves a tie. Take independent stationary unit-variance sources with autocorrelations $(\rho_1(1),\rho_1(2))=(0.5,0.25)$ and $(\rho_2(1),\rho_2(2))=(0.5,0)$. These occur for an AR(1) source with coefficient $0.5$ and a normalized two-term moving average, respectively. A lag is the number of time steps separating the two observations being compared.

手算：第二个时滞怎样打破平局？取两个相互独立、平稳、单位方差的源，自相关分别为 $(\rho_1(1),\rho_1(2))=(0.5,0.25)$ 与 $(\rho_2(1),\rho_2(2))=(0.5,0)$。它们分别可由系数为 $0.5$ 的 AR(1) 源和归一化的两项滑动平均产生。时滞就是被比较的两个观测相隔多少个时间步。

  1. Rotate the sources by $45^\circ$: $\bz=\bB\bs$ with $\bB=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. This keeps the zero-lag covariance equal to identity.
  2. At lag 1, $\bC_z(1)=\bB(0.5\bI)\bB^\top=0.5\bI$. Every direction is an eigenvector: this matrix cannot reveal the rotation.
  3. At lag 2, $\bC_z(2)=\bB\diag(0.25,0)\bB^\top=\tfrac18\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$. Its eigenvectors are $(1,1)^\top/\sqrt2$ and $(-1,1)^\top/\sqrt2$, with eigenvalues $0.25$ and 0.
  4. Those directions recover the two source axes up to signs and order. This uses exact noiseless covariances; sample estimates need enough data and appropriate temporal regularity. A second matrix helps because its values distinguish the sources, not merely because it is additional.
  1. 把源旋转 $45^\circ$：$\bz=\bB\bs$，其中 $\bB=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&-1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$。这会保持零时滞协方差为单位阵。
  2. 在时滞 1，$\bC_z(1)=\bB(0.5\bI)\bB^\top=0.5\bI$。每个方向都是特征向量，这个矩阵无法透露旋转角度。
  3. 在时滞 2，$\bC_z(2)=\bB\diag(0.25,0)\bB^\top=\tfrac18\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$。其特征向量为 $(1,1)^\top/\sqrt2$ 与 $(-1,1)^\top/\sqrt2$，对应特征值为 $0.25$ 与 0。
  4. 这些方向在符号和顺序意义下恢复两个源轴。这里使用无噪声的精确协方差；样本估计还需要足够数据与适当的时间正则条件。第二个矩阵有用，是因为它的数值能区分源，不只是因为数量多了一个。

Quick check 1. If both sources have identical autocorrelations at every selected lag, does adding those lags identify their axes?

自测 1。若两个源在所有选定时滞上的自相关都相同，加入这些时滞能否确定源轴？

Check the answer查看答案

No. Every selected matrix is still invariant to rotation within that two-source plane. More copies of the same information cannot resolve the ambiguity.

不会。SOBI 利用的是不同的时间特征。普通高斯四阶累积量为零，所以 JADE 无法凭这一特征分离它们。时间结构改变了模型可使用的信息。

Separation by diagonalization通过对角化实现分离

After whitening, the remaining unknown is an orthogonal matrix $\tilde\bA$ (). Suppose we can compute, from the data alone, a symmetric matrix of the form $\bM = \tilde\bA\bLambda\tilde\bA^\top$ with $\bLambda$ diagonal. This is an eigendecomposition: if the diagonal entries of $\bLambda$ are distinct, the eigenvectors of $\bM$ are the columns of $\tilde\bA$, up to sign and order, and $\bU = \tilde\bA^\top$ separates the sources. No iteration, contrast function or step size is needed. The covariance of $\bz$ is useless for this purpose, since it equals $\bI$, but two other families of statistics have exactly this structure: matrices built from fourth-order cumulants, which are informative when the sources are non-Gaussian, and lagged covariance matrices, which are informative when the sources are correlated in time.

白化之后，剩下的未知量是一个正交矩阵 $\tilde\bA$（）。假设能单凭数据算出一个形如 $\bM = \tilde\bA\bLambda\tilde\bA^\top$ 的对称矩阵，其中 $\bLambda$ 是对角阵。这就是一个特征分解：若 $\bLambda$ 的对角元互不相同，$\bM$ 的特征向量就是 $\tilde\bA$ 的各列（相差符号和顺序），于是 $\bU = \tilde\bA^\top$ 就能分离各源。这里不需要迭代，不需要对比函数，也不需要步长。$\bz$ 的协方差对此毫无用处，因为它等于 $\bI$；但另有两族统计量恰好具有这种结构：由四阶累积量构造的矩阵（当源为非高斯时，它们携带信息），以及时滞协方差矩阵（当源在时间上相关时，它们携带信息）。

A single such matrix is fragile: two equal or nearly equal eigenvalues make its eigenvectors undetermined or badly estimated. Using many matrices at once and finding the one rotation that diagonalizes all of them, approximately, removes the fragility. This chapter develops that idea twice: with cumulant matrices it gives FOBI and JADE (Cardoso, 1989; Cardoso & Souloumiac, 1993), with lagged covariances AMUSE and SOBI (Tong et al., 1990; Belouchrani et al., 1997). The common computational tool is the Jacobi method for joint diagonalization (Cardoso & Souloumiac, 1996).

只用一个这样的矩阵是脆弱的：两个特征值相等或接近相等时，特征向量就不确定或估计得很差。同时使用许多矩阵，寻找一个能把它们全部近似对角化的旋转，就消除了这种脆弱性。本章把这一思想发展两次：用累积量矩阵得到 FOBI 和 JADE（Cardoso, 1989; Cardoso & Souloumiac, 1993），用时滞协方差得到 AMUSE 和 SOBI（Tong et al., 1990; Belouchrani et al., 1997）。两者共同的计算工具是联合对角化的 Jacobi 方法（Cardoso & Souloumiac, 1996）。

The cumulant tensor of whitened data白化数据的累积量张量

Tensor without mystery. Here a fourth-order tensor is simply an array with four indices. A cumulant matrix bundles part of that array into a familiar square matrix. JADE looks for common axes across several such bundles; you can understand that goal before following every index contraction.

先把张量看成数组。这里的四阶张量就是带四个下标的数组。累积量矩阵把数组中的一部分组织成熟悉的方阵。JADE 寻找多个这类方阵共有的轴；不必先弄懂每一次指标缩并，才能理解这个目标。

For zero-mean variables the fourth-order cumulant is $\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l) = \E[z_iz_jz_kz_l] - \E[z_iz_j]\E[z_kz_l] - \E[z_iz_k]\E[z_jz_l] - \E[z_iz_l]\E[z_jz_k]$ (); for whitened data the last three terms are $\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl} + \delta_{ik}\delta_{jl} + \delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$. The $n^4$ numbers $\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l)$ form a symmetric tensor, the cumulant tensor of $\bz$. Under the ICA model it has a very simple form.

对零均值变量，四阶累积量为 $\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l) = \E[z_iz_jz_kz_l] - \E[z_iz_j]\E[z_kz_l] - \E[z_iz_k]\E[z_jz_l] - \E[z_iz_l]\E[z_jz_k]$（）；对白化数据，后三项为 $\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl} + \delta_{ik}\delta_{jl} + \delta_{il}\delta_{jk}$。这 $n^4$ 个数 $\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l)$ 构成一个对称张量，即 $\bz$ 的累积量张量。在 ICA 模型下它的形式非常简单。

$$\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l) = \sum_{p=1}^n \kappa_p\,\tilde a_{ip}\,\tilde a_{jp}\,\tilde a_{kp}\,\tilde a_{lp}.$$

To see this, write $z_i = \sum_p \tilde a_{ip}s_p$ and expand by multilinearity: $\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l) = \sum_{p,q,r,u}\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jq}\tilde a_{kr}\tilde a_{lu}\cum(s_p, s_q, s_r, s_u)$. A cross-cumulant of independent variables vanishes unless all four arguments are the same variable (), so only $p = q = r = u$ survives, with $\cum(s_p, s_p, s_p, s_p) = \kappa_p$. In tensor language, the cumulant tensor is $\sum_p\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p$ with orthonormal vectors $\tilde\ba_p$: it is diagonal in the source basis, and ICA of whitened data amounts to an orthogonal decomposition of a symmetric fourth-order tensor (; the non-orthogonal case returns in ). Only $\binom{n+3}{4}$ of the entries are distinct, for example 715 for $n = 10$.

为看出这一点，写 $z_i = \sum_p \tilde a_{ip}s_p$，并利用多重线性展开：$\cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l) = \sum_{p,q,r,u}\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jq}\tilde a_{kr}\tilde a_{lu}\cum(s_p, s_q, s_r, s_u)$。独立变量的交叉累积量为零，除非四个自变量是同一个变量（），所以只剩下 $p = q = r = u$ 的项，且 $\cum(s_p, s_p, s_p, s_p) = \kappa_p$。用张量的语言说，累积量张量等于 $\sum_p\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p \otimes \tilde\ba_p$，其中各 $\tilde\ba_p$ 标准正交：它在源基下是对角的，而白化数据的 ICA 就相当于对一个对称四阶张量作正交分解（；非正交情形将在中再次出现）。其中只有 $\binom{n+3}{4}$ 个元素互不相同，例如 $n = 10$ 时为 715 个。

Contracting the tensor with a matrix produces matrices of the eigendecomposition form. For an $n \times n$ matrix $\bM$ define the cumulant matrix $\bQ_{\bz}(\bM)$ by

把这个张量与一个矩阵作缩并，就得到具有特征分解形式的矩阵。对 $n \times n$ 矩阵 $\bM$，定义累积量矩阵 $\bQ_{\bz}(\bM)$ 为

$$\bigl[\bQ_{\bz}(\bM)\bigr]_{ij} = \sum_{k,l=1}^n \cum(z_i, z_j, z_k, z_l)\,m_{kl}.$$

Let $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$ be whitened data from the ICA model, with $\tilde\bA$ orthogonal and sources of kurtosis $\kappa_p$. For every $n \times n$ matrix $\bM$:

设 $\bz = \tilde\bA\bs$ 是来自 ICA 模型的白化数据，$\tilde\bA$ 为正交矩阵，各源的峭度为 $\kappa_p$。则对每个 $n \times n$ 矩阵 $\bM$：

(a) $\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \tilde\bA\,\diag\bigl(\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p\bigr)\,\tilde\bA^\top$.

（a）$\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \tilde\bA\,\diag\bigl(\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p\bigr)\,\tilde\bA^\top$。

(b) $\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \E\bigl[(\bz^\top\bM\bz)\,\bz\bz^\top\bigr] - \tr(\bM)\,\bI - \bM - \bM^\top$.

（b）$\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \E\bigl[(\bz^\top\bM\bz)\,\bz\bz^\top\bigr] - \tr(\bM)\,\bI - \bM - \bM^\top$。

(c) On the space of symmetric matrices with the inner product $\langle\bB, \bC\rangle = \tr(\bB^\top\bC)$, the linear map $\bM \mapsto \bQ_{\bz}(\bM)$ is self-adjoint, with eigenmatrices $\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$ and eigenvalues $\kappa_p$, $p = 1, \dots, n$; all its other eigenvalues are zero.

（c）在对称矩阵空间上取内积 $\langle\bB, \bC\rangle = \tr(\bB^\top\bC)$，线性映射 $\bM \mapsto \bQ_{\bz}(\bM)$ 是自伴的，其特征矩阵为 $\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$，对应特征值为 $\kappa_p$（$p = 1, \dots, n$）；其余特征值全为零。

(a) Substituting , $[\bQ_{\bz}(\bM)]_{ij} = \sum_p\kappa_p\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jp}\sum_{kl}\tilde a_{kp}m_{kl}\tilde a_{lp} = \sum_p\kappa_p\,(\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p)\,\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jp}$. (b) Contract the definition of the cumulant with $m_{kl}$: the moment term gives $\E[z_iz_j\,\bz^\top\bM\bz]$ and the three products of Kronecker deltas give $\delta_{ij}\tr\bM$, $m_{ij}$ and $m_{ji}$. (c) By (a), $\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \sum_p\kappa_p\langle\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top, \bM\rangle\,\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$. The matrices $\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$ are orthonormal, because $\langle\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top, \tilde\ba_q\tilde\ba_q^\top\rangle = (\tilde\ba_p^\top\tilde\ba_q)^2 = \delta_{pq}$, so this is a spectral decomposition, and the map vanishes on their orthogonal complement.

（a）代入 ，得 $[\bQ_{\bz}(\bM)]_{ij} = \sum_p\kappa_p\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jp}\sum_{kl}\tilde a_{kp}m_{kl}\tilde a_{lp} = \sum_p\kappa_p\,(\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p)\,\tilde a_{ip}\tilde a_{jp}$。（b）把累积量的定义与 $m_{kl}$ 缩并：矩项给出 $\E[z_iz_j\,\bz^\top\bM\bz]$，三个 Kronecker δ 乘积依次给出 $\delta_{ij}\tr\bM$、$m_{ij}$ 和 $m_{ji}$。（c）由（a），$\bQ_{\bz}(\bM) = \sum_p\kappa_p\langle\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top, \bM\rangle\,\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$。由于 $\langle\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top, \tilde\ba_q\tilde\ba_q^\top\rangle = (\tilde\ba_p^\top\tilde\ba_q)^2 = \delta_{pq}$，这些矩阵 $\tilde\ba_p\tilde\ba_p^\top$ 标准正交，所以这就是一个谱分解，而该映射在它们的正交补上为零。

Formula (b) is how cumulant matrices are estimated: replace $\E$ by a sample average over whitened data. We checked (a) and (b) to machine precision on discrete sources for which every expectation can be computed exactly, including a skewed one; symmetry of the sources is not needed.

公式（b）就是估计累积量矩阵的方法：在白化数据上把 $\E$ 换成样本平均。我们在可以精确计算所有期望的离散源（包括一个有偏度的源）上验证了（a）和（b），误差在机器精度以内；源的对称性不是必需的。

FOBI. The simplest choice is $\bM = \bI$. Since $\tilde\ba_p^\top\tilde\ba_p = 1$, part (a) gives $\bQ_{\bz}(\bI) = \tilde\bA\,\diag(\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_n)\,\tilde\bA^\top$, and part (b) gives $\bQ_{\bz}(\bI) = \E[\|\bz\|^2\bz\bz^\top] - (n + 2)\bI$. The eigenvectors of the weighted covariance $\E[\|\bz\|^2\bz\bz^\top]$ therefore separate the sources whenever their kurtoses are distinct. This is FOBI, for fourth-order blind identification (Cardoso, 1989): one weighted covariance and one eigendecomposition. Its weakness is the distinctness requirement: two Laplace sources, or two sources of the same type in general, cannot be separated by FOBI ().

FOBI。最简单的选择是 $\bM = \bI$。由于 $\tilde\ba_p^\top\tilde\ba_p = 1$，（a）给出 $\bQ_{\bz}(\bI) = \tilde\bA\,\diag(\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_n)\,\tilde\bA^\top$，（b）给出 $\bQ_{\bz}(\bI) = \E[\|\bz\|^2\bz\bz^\top] - (n + 2)\bI$。因此，只要各源的峭度互不相同，加权协方差 $\E[\|\bz\|^2\bz\bz^\top]$ 的特征向量就能分离各源。这就是 FOBI（fourth-order blind identification，四阶盲辨识；Cardoso, 1989）：一个加权协方差加一次特征分解。它的弱点在于要求峭度互不相同：两个拉普拉斯源，或者一般地两个同类型的源，都无法用 FOBI 分离（）。

Joint approximate diagonalization联合近似对角化

What a Jacobi sweep does. Hold every direction fixed except two, rotate that pair, and choose the angle that reduces their combined off-diagonal entries. Repeat across pairs. A small final score means the matrices are nearly diagonal together; unique source recovery additionally needs distinct joint signatures.

一次 Jacobi 扫描在做什么？先固定其他方向，只旋转其中一对，选择能减少它们在各矩阵中非对角项总量的角度，再逐对重复。最终分数很小，只说明这些矩阵近似共同对角；唯一恢复源还要求不同的联合特征。

Let $\bQ_1, \dots, \bQ_K$ be symmetric matrices that, in the model, all have the form $\bQ_r = \tilde\bA\bLambda_r\tilde\bA^\top$ with diagonal $\bLambda_r = \diag(\lambda_{r1}, \dots, \lambda_{rn})$. With estimated matrices no single rotation makes all of them exactly diagonal, so we minimize the off-diagonal energy

设 $\bQ_1, \dots, \bQ_K$ 是对称矩阵，在模型中它们都形如 $\bQ_r = \tilde\bA\bLambda_r\tilde\bA^\top$，其中 $\bLambda_r = \diag(\lambda_{r1}, \dots, \lambda_{rn})$ 为对角阵。对估计得到的矩阵，没有一个旋转能把它们全部精确对角化，于是我们最小化非对角能量

$$\mathcal{C}(\bU) = \sum_{r=1}^K \bigl\|\offdiag\bigl(\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top\bigr)\bigr\|_F^2 \qquad \text{over orthogonal } \bU,$$

where $\offdiag(\bB)$ is $\bB$ with its diagonal set to zero. Since $\|\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top\|_F = \|\bQ_r\|_F$, minimizing $\mathcal{C}$ is the same as maximizing the diagonal energy $\sum_r\|\diag(\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top)\|^2$. In the model the minimum is zero, and the next lemma says when it is attained only at the right rotation.

其中 $\offdiag(\bB)$ 表示把 $\bB$ 的对角线置零后的矩阵。由于 $\|\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top\|_F = \|\bQ_r\|_F$，最小化 $\mathcal{C}$ 等价于最大化对角能量 $\sum_r\|\diag(\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top)\|^2$。在模型中最小值为零；下面的引理说明它何时只在正确的旋转处取到。

Let $\bQ_r = \tilde\bA\bLambda_r\tilde\bA^\top$, $r = 1, \dots, K$, with $\tilde\bA$ orthogonal and $\bLambda_r$ diagonal. Suppose that for every pair $p \ne q$ there is an $r$ with $\lambda_{rp} \ne \lambda_{rq}$. Then every orthogonal $\bU$ for which all $\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top$ are diagonal satisfies $\bU\tilde\bA = $ signed permutation matrix. Conversely, if $\lambda_{rp} = \lambda_{rq}$ for all $r$ and some pair $p \ne q$, then a continuum of orthogonal matrices, which are not signed permutations of $\tilde\bA^\top$, diagonalize all $\bQ_r$.

设 $\bQ_r = \tilde\bA\bLambda_r\tilde\bA^\top$，$r = 1, \dots, K$，其中 $\tilde\bA$ 为正交矩阵，$\bLambda_r$ 为对角阵。若对每一对 $p \ne q$ 都存在某个 $r$ 使 $\lambda_{rp} \ne \lambda_{rq}$，则任何使所有 $\bU\bQ_r\bU^\top$ 都成为对角阵的正交矩阵 $\bU$ 都满足：$\bU\tilde\bA$ 是带符号的置换矩阵。反之，若对某一对 $p \ne q$ 和所有 $r$ 都有 $\lambda_{rp} = \lambda_{rq}$，则存在一族连续的、并非 $\tilde\bA^\top$ 的带符号置换的正交矩阵，能对角化所有 $\bQ_r$。

Let $\bB = \bU\tilde\bA$, which is orthogonal, and $\bD_r = \bU\bQ_r\bU^\top = \bB\bLambda_r\bB^\top$, diagonal by assumption. Then $\bB\bLambda_r = \bD_r\bB$, that is, $b_{ij}\lambda_{rj} = d_{r,i}\,b_{ij}$ for all $i, j, r$. If a row $i$ of $\bB$ had two nonzero entries $b_{ij}$ and $b_{il}$, then $\lambda_{rj} = d_{r,i} = \lambda_{rl}$ for all $r$, contradicting the assumption. So each row has at most one nonzero entry; as $\bB$ is invertible it has exactly one, which is $\pm1$ because the rows have unit norm, and the nonzero entries lie in different columns. For the converse, let $\mathbf{G}$ be any rotation acting only on coordinates $p$ and $q$. The $(p, q)$ block of $\bLambda_r$ is a multiple of the $2 \times 2$ identity, so $\mathbf{G}\bLambda_r\mathbf{G}^\top = \bLambda_r$, and $\bU = \mathbf{G}^\top\tilde\bA^\top$ diagonalizes every $\bQ_r$.

令 $\bB = \bU\tilde\bA$（正交矩阵），$\bD_r = \bU\bQ_r\bU^\top = \bB\bLambda_r\bB^\top$，按假设为对角阵。于是 $\bB\bLambda_r = \bD_r\bB$，即对所有 $i, j, r$ 有 $b_{ij}\lambda_{rj} = d_{r,i}\,b_{ij}$。若 $\bB$ 的某一行 $i$ 有两个非零元 $b_{ij}$ 和 $b_{il}$，则对所有 $r$ 有 $\lambda_{rj} = d_{r,i} = \lambda_{rl}$，与假设矛盾。所以每一行至多有一个非零元；由于 $\bB$ 可逆，恰好有一个，又因各行范数为一，它等于 $\pm1$，并且这些非零元位于不同的列。反过来，设 $\mathbf{G}$ 是只作用在坐标 $p$、$q$ 上的任意旋转。$\bLambda_r$ 的 $(p, q)$ 块是 $2 \times 2$ 单位阵的倍数，所以 $\mathbf{G}\bLambda_r\mathbf{G}^\top = \bLambda_r$，从而 $\bU = \mathbf{G}^\top\tilde\bA^\top$ 能对角化每个 $\bQ_r$。

A sweep visits every pair $p < q$ once; for each pair it applies $\bQ_r \leftarrow \bR^\top\bQ_r\bR$ to all matrices and $\bU \leftarrow \bR^\top\bU$, where $\bR = \bR_{pq}(\theta)$ is the identity except for $R_{pp} = R_{qq} = \cos\theta$, $R_{pq} = -\sin\theta$ and $R_{qp} = \sin\theta$. import numpy as np

def whiten(X):
    """Center the rows of X; return Z = V X (identity sample covariance) and V."""
    X = np.asarray(X, dtype=float)
    if X.ndim != 2 or not np.isfinite(X).all() or X.shape[0] < 1 or X.shape[1] <= X.shape[0]:
        raise ValueError("Need finite channels-by-samples data with more samples than channels")
    X = X - X.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)
    d, E = np.linalg.eigh(X @ X.T / X.shape[1])
    if d[-1] <= 0 or d[0] <= 1e-12 * d[-1]:
        raise ValueError("Rank-deficient data: reduce to a signal subspace first")
    V = E @ np.diag(d ** -0.5) @ E.T
    return V @ X, V

def joint_diagonalize(Qs, tol=1e-12, max_sweeps=100):
    """Jacobi joint diagonalization of symmetric matrices Qs (shape K x n x n).
    Returns an orthogonal U that makes every U Q U^T as diagonal as possible."""
    Qs = np.array(Qs, dtype=float, copy=True)
    if Qs.ndim != 3 or Qs.shape[0] == 0 or Qs.shape[1] == 0 or Qs.shape[1] != Qs.shape[2]:
        raise ValueError("Qs must be a nonempty stack of square matrices")
    if not np.isfinite(Qs).all() or not np.allclose(Qs, Qs.transpose(0, 2, 1)):
        raise ValueError("Joint diagonalization requires finite symmetric matrices")
    n = Qs.shape[1]
    U = np.eye(n)
    for sweep in range(max_sweeps):
        rotated = False
        for p in range(n - 1):
            for q in range(p + 1, n):
                h1 = Qs[:, p, p] - Qs[:, q, q]
                h2 = 2 * Qs[:, p, q]
                # closed-form Jacobi angle for the pair (p, q)
                theta = 0.25 * np.arctan2(2 * h1 @ h2, h1 @ h1 - h2 @ h2)
                if abs(theta) > tol:
                    rotated = True
                    c, s = np.cos(theta), np.sin(theta)
                    R = np.eye(n)
                    R[p, p] = R[q, q] = c
                    R[p, q], R[q, p] = -s, s
                    Qs = R.T @ Qs @ R      # rotate all matrices in the (p, q) plane
                    U = R.T @ U
        if not rotated:
            return U
    raise RuntimeError("Joint diagonalization did not converge within max_sweeps")

def jade(X):
    Z, V = whiten(X)
    n, T = Z.shape
    I = np.eye(n)
    M4 = np.einsum('it,jt,kt,lt->ijkl', Z, Z, Z, Z, optimize=True) / T
    C = (M4 - np.einsum('ij,kl->ijkl', I, I) - np.einsum('ik,jl->ijkl', I, I)
            - np.einsum('il,jk->ijkl', I, I))    # cumulant tensor of z
    Qs = C.reshape(n, n, n * n).transpose(2, 0, 1)    # the n^2 matrices Q(e_k e_l^T)
    return joint_diagonalize(Qs) @ V

rng = np.random.default_rng(1)
T = 10000
S = np.vstack([rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), T),    # kappa = -1.2

A = rng.standard_normal((3, 3))
P = jade(A @ S) @ A

The i.i.d. assumption of discards the order of the samples. Many signals, from EEG rhythms to fMRI time courses, are correlated in time, and this structure can replace non-Gaussianity. Assume that the sources are zero-mean, wide-sense stationary, and mutually uncorrelated at every lag: $\E[s_i(t)s_j(t + \tau)] = 0$ for $i \ne j$ and all $\tau$. Write $\rho_i(\tau) = \E[s_i(t)s_i(t + \tau)]$ for the autocovariance of source $i$, with $\rho_i(0) = 1$. The lagged covariance matrices of the data are then

中的独立同分布假设丢弃了样本的顺序。许多信号，从 EEG 节律到 fMRI 时间过程，在时间上都是相关的，这种结构可以取代非高斯性。假设各源均值为零、宽平稳，并且在任何时滞下互不相关：对 $i \ne j$ 和所有 $\tau$ 有 $\E[s_i(t)s_j(t + \tau)] = 0$。记 $\rho_i(\tau) = \E[s_i(t)s_i(t + \tau)]$ 为源 $i$ 的自协方差，$\rho_i(0) = 1$。于是数据的时滞协方差矩阵为

$$\bC_{\bx}(\tau) = \E\bigl[\bx(t)\,\bx(t + \tau)^\top\bigr] = \bA\,\diag\bigl(\rho_1(\tau), \dots, \rho_n(\tau)\bigr)\,\bA^\top.$$

They are symmetric in the model, but their estimates $\hat\bC_{\bx}(\tau) = \frac{1}{T - \tau}\sum_t\bx(t)\bx(t + \tau)^\top$ are not, so one uses $\bar\bC(\tau) = \tfrac12(\hat\bC(\tau) + \hat\bC(\tau)^\top)$. Whitening with $\bC_{\bx}(0) = \bA\bA^\top$ gives $\bar\bC_{\bz}(\tau) = \tilde\bA\,\diag(\rho_i(\tau))\,\tilde\bA^\top$ for every lag: again the joint diagonalization structure. Only second-order statistics are involved, so neither non-Gaussianity nor full independence is required.

在模型中这些矩阵是对称的，但其估计 $\hat\bC_{\bx}(\tau) = \frac{1}{T - \tau}\sum_t\bx(t)\bx(t + \tau)^\top$ 并不对称，所以使用 $\bar\bC(\tau) = \tfrac12(\hat\bC(\tau) + \hat\bC(\tau)^\top)$。用 $\bC_{\bx}(0) = \bA\bA^\top$ 白化后，对每个时滞都有 $\bar\bC_{\bz}(\tau) = \tilde\bA\,\diag(\rho_i(\tau))\,\tilde\bA^\top$：又是联合对角化的结构。这里只涉及二阶统计量，所以既不需要非高斯性，也不需要完全独立。

AMUSE. The AMUSE algorithm of Tong et al. (1990) uses a single lag: whiten, then take the eigenvectors of $\bar\bC_{\bz}(\tau)$. Two consequences stand out. Gaussian sources, any number of them, are separable as soon as their autocorrelation functions differ, which is the case whenever their normalized power spectra differ, since the spectrum and the autocovariance determine each other. And i.i.d. sources are never separable by SOBI, since $\rho_i(\tau) = 0$ for all $\tau \ne 0$ and every $i$. Noise affects the two families in parallel ways. Fourth-order cumulants of Gaussian noise vanish, so the cumulant matrices of $\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$ with Gaussian $\bn$ independent of $\bs$ are those of $\bA\bs$; population lagged covariances at $\tau \ne 0$ are unaffected by temporally uncorrelated noise that is also uncorrelated with the signal at every relevant lag. These population invariances do not remove finite-sample noise variance. In both cases the whitening step, which uses $\bC_{\bx}(0) = \bA\bA^\top + \bC_{\bn}$, is biased by the noise; with more sensors than sources the bias can be reduced as described in . The methods can also be combined, for example by jointly diagonalizing cumulant matrices and lagged covariances together, when the sources are both non-Gaussian and temporally structured.

噪声对这两类方法的影响方式相似。高斯噪声的四阶累积量为零，所以当 $\bn$ 为高斯且与 $\bs$ 独立时，$\bx = \bA\bs + \bn$ 的累积量矩阵与 $\bA\bs$ 的相同；在时间上不相关、且在所有相关时滞处均与信号不相关的噪声，不影响 $\tau \ne 0$ 处的总体时滞协方差。这些总体不变性并不消除有限样本中的噪声方差。两种情形下白化步骤都会受噪声影响而产生偏差，因为它用到 $\bC_{\bx}(0) = \bA\bA^\top + \bC_{\bn}$；当传感器多于源时，可以按所述减小这一偏差。当源既是非高斯的又有时间结构时，两类方法还可以结合起来，例如把累积量矩阵与时滞协方差放在一起作联合对角化。

From identifiability to reliable diagonalization从可辨识性到可靠的对角化

Measure the separation between joint signatures. The uniqueness theorem requires the vectors $\boldsymbol{\lambda}_i=(\lambda_{1i},\ldots,\lambda_{Ki})$ to be distinct. Distinct but nearly equal vectors still give a poorly conditioned problem. At an exactly diagonal solution, a small rotation by $\theta$ in the $(i,j)$ plane creates off-diagonal energy

量化联合特征之间的距离。唯一性定理要求向量 $\boldsymbol{\lambda}_i=(\lambda_{1i},\ldots,\lambda_{Ki})$ 互不相同，但不同却极接近的向量仍会使问题病态。在精确对角解附近，$(i,j)$ 平面内一个小角度 $\theta$ 产生的非对角能量为

$$\mathcal C(\theta)-\mathcal C(0)=\tfrac12\sin^2(2\theta)\sum_{r=1}^K(\lambda_{ri}-\lambda_{rj})^2=2\theta^2\|\boldsymbol{\lambda}_i-\boldsymbol{\lambda}_j\|^2+O(\theta^4).$$

Thus signature gaps control local curvature: when the gap is small, small estimation errors can cause a large rotation. For SOBI inspect estimated autocorrelation signatures across the chosen lags, not just the final diagonality score. Adding lags that distinguish a difficult pair helps; adding many long lags with small correlations and few available products can instead increase estimation noise. Choosing lags on held-out or simulated data with comparable temporal structure is more informative than maximizing the number of matrices.

因此联合特征间距控制局部曲率：间距很小时，微小估计误差就可能造成大的旋转。对 SOBI，应查看所选滞后上的估计自相关特征，而不只看最终的对角化分数。加入能区分困难源对的滞后有帮助；加入大量相关性小、可用乘积样本少的长滞后，反而可能增加估计噪声。用留出数据或时间结构相近的模拟数据选择滞后，比单纯追求矩阵数量更有意义。

Keep population and sample claims separate. Wide-sense stationarity specifies the covariance model but does not alone guarantee that time averages estimate ensemble covariances. Consistency also needs an appropriate ergodicity or law-of-large-numbers condition, and uncertainty estimates need stronger dependence and moment control. The displayed sample lag matrices are only approximately jointly diagonalizable; symmetrizing an estimate does not remove its sampling error. Noise invariance is not noise immunity. Independent Gaussian noise has zero fourth cumulants, and temporally white noise independent of the signal has zero nonzero-lag covariance, but noise still affects whitening and the variance of estimated matrices. Naive orthogonal joint diagonalization after noisy covariance whitening need not recover the correct signal rotation. Use a justified noise model and, when available, corrected signal covariance as in ; multiple sensors alone do not remove arbitrary colored noise.

对噪声的不变性不等于不受噪声影响。独立高斯噪声的四阶累积量为零，与信号独立的时间白噪声在非零滞后处的协方差为零，但噪声仍影响白化和估计矩阵的方差。在含噪协方差白化之后直接做正交联合对角化，未必能恢复正确的信号旋转。应使用有依据的噪声模型，并在可行时采用中的信号协方差校正；传感器多本身不能消除任意有色噪声。

Key points

本章要点

  • After whitening, any statistic of the form $\tilde\bA\bLambda\tilde\bA^\top$ with distinct diagonal entries reveals $\tilde\bA$ through its eigenvectors; jointly diagonalizing many such matrices removes the need for distinct eigenvalues in any single one.
  • The fourth-order cumulant tensor of whitened data is $\sum_p\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p^{\otimes 4}$, and every cumulant matrix is $\tilde\bA\,\diag(\kappa_p\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p)\,\tilde\bA^\top$. FOBI uses $\bM = \bI$ and needs distinct kurtoses; JADE uses all cumulant matrices and needs at most one zero kurtosis.
  • Jacobi sweeps minimize the off-diagonal energy one plane at a time with the closed-form angle $\theta^\star = \tfrac14\operatorname{atan2}(2g_{12}, g_{11} - g_{22})$; the criterion never increases, and a few sweeps suffice.
  • Lagged covariances satisfy $\bC_{\bx}(\tau) = \bA\,\diag(\rho_i(\tau))\,\bA^\top$. SOBI separates the sources if and only if every pair is distinguished by some lag in the set, so Gaussian sources with different spectra are separable and i.i.d. sources are not.
  • JADE costs grow as $n^4$; SOBI is cheap. Cumulant methods and second-order methods rely on different structure and complement each other.
  • 白化之后，任何形如 $\tilde\bA\bLambda\tilde\bA^\top$ 且对角元互不相同的统计量，都能通过其特征向量揭示 $\tilde\bA$；对许多这样的矩阵作联合对角化，就不再要求其中任何一个矩阵的特征值互不相同。
  • 白化数据的四阶累积量张量为 $\sum_p\kappa_p\,\tilde\ba_p^{\otimes 4}$，每个累积量矩阵都等于 $\tilde\bA\,\diag(\kappa_p\tilde\ba_p^\top\bM\tilde\ba_p)\,\tilde\bA^\top$。FOBI 取 $\bM = \bI$，要求峭度互不相同；JADE 使用全部累积量矩阵，只要求至多一个峭度为零。
  • Jacobi 扫描逐个平面地最小化非对角能量，所用闭式角为 $\theta^\star = \tfrac14\operatorname{atan2}(2g_{12}, g_{11} - g_{22})$；准则从不增加，几次扫描就够了。
  • 时滞协方差满足 $\bC_{\bx}(\tau) = \bA\,\diag(\rho_i(\tau))\,\bA^\top$。SOBI 能分离各源，当且仅当每一对源都能被所选集合中的某个时滞区分；因此谱不同的高斯源可以分离，而独立同分布的源不能。
  • JADE 的计算量按 $n^4$ 增长；SOBI 则很便宜。累积量方法与二阶方法依赖不同的结构，相互补充。

(a) For $\tau \ge 1$, multiply $s(t + \tau) = a\,s(t + \tau - 1) + e(t + \tau)$ by $s(t)$ and take expectations; $e(t + \tau)$ is independent of $s(t)$ and has zero mean, so $\rho(\tau) = a\,\rho(\tau - 1)$, and with $\rho(0) = 1$ this gives $\rho(\tau) = a^\tau$.
