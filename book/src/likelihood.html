From heuristics to principles从启发式到基本原理

found the sources by maximizing a measure of non-Gaussianity, justified by the central-limit heuristic, and found that kurtosis, the simplest such measure, is fragile. This chapter starts instead from first principles. There are two natural ones. The statistical principle writes down the likelihood of the ICA model and maximizes it; this requires a model for the source densities. The information-theoretic principle asks for outputs that are as independent as possible and measures dependence by mutual information; this requires no model at all. The two lead to the same place: the likelihood equals minus the mutual information of the outputs, minus a term that measures how badly the source model fits, up to a constant. For whitened data, mutual information in turn equals a constant minus the sum of the negentropies of the outputs, which turns the non-Gaussianity heuristic into an exact statement.

依据中心极限启发式，通过最大化非高斯性度量来寻找源，并发现最简单的度量峭度很脆弱。本章改从基本原理出发。自然的原理有两个。统计原理写出 ICA 模型的似然并使之最大化，这需要源密度的模型。信息论原理要求输出尽可能独立，并用互信息度量相依性，这完全不需要模型。两条路通向同一个地方：似然在相差一个常数的意义下，等于输出互信息的相反数，再减去一个度量源模型拟合有多差的项。对白化数据，互信息又等于一个常数减去各输出负熵之和，于是非高斯性启发式变成了一个精确的命题。

Along the way we meet the score function, the Infomax principle of Bell & Sejnowski (1995), which turns out to be maximum likelihood in disguise, and a key robustness property: the separating solution solves the likelihood equations for almost any source model, and only its stability depends on the model. The chapter ends with practical approximations of negentropy that keep the robustness kurtosis lacks.

沿途我们会遇到得分函数，遇到 Bell & Sejnowski（1995）的 Infomax 原理（它其实是改头换面的最大似然），以及一个关键的稳健性质：对几乎任何源模型，分离解都满足似然方程，只有它的稳定性依赖于模型。本章最后给出负熵的实用近似，它们具备峭度所缺乏的稳健性。

$$\mathcal{L}(\bW) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \E\bigl[\log p_i(y_i)\bigr] + \log|\det\bW|, \qquad \by = \bW\bx,$$

Mutual information and the likelihood互信息与似然

Reading the identity. Mutual information uses the outputs’ actual marginal densities. Likelihood inserts the densities we choose. If those choices are wrong, an extra mismatch penalty remains; maximizing one objective need not minimize the other. Keep that distinction in view while following the algebra.

怎样读下面的恒等式。互信息使用输出真实的边缘密度；似然代入的是我们选定的密度。选择错误时，会多出模型失配的惩罚项，因此最大化一个目标未必最小化另一个。跟随代数推导时，请始终保留这个区别。

The information-theoretic route measures the dependence of the outputs by their mutual information, $I(\by) = \sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by)$ (). It is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when the outputs are independent. Since $\by = \bW\bx$, the joint entropy satisfies $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$ (), so

信息论的路线用互信息 $I(\by) = \sum_i H(y_i) - H(\by)$ 来度量输出之间的相依性（）。它非负，且恰在输出独立时为零。由于 $\by = \bW\bx$，联合熵满足 $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$（），所以

$$I(\by) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} H(y_i) - H(\bx) - \log|\det\bW| .$$

Only the marginal entropies and $\log|\det\bW|$ depend on $\bW$; the joint entropy of the data is a constant. Rescaling an output changes $H(y_i)$ and $\log|\det\bW|$ by the same amount, so $I(\by)$ is invariant under $\bW \mapsto \bPi\bD\bW$, as a measure of independence should be. By , if at most one source is Gaussian, the minimizers of $I(\by)$ over invertible $\bW$, where $I(\by) = 0$, are exactly the separating matrices: mutual information is a contrast function in the sense of Comon (1994). The likelihood is tied to it by an exact identity.

只有各边缘熵和 $\log|\det\bW|$ 依赖于 $\bW$，数据的联合熵是常数。对某个输出做尺度变换，$H(y_i)$ 与 $\log|\det\bW|$ 改变同样的量，所以 $I(\by)$ 在 $\bW \mapsto \bPi\bD\bW$ 下不变，这正是独立性度量应有的性质。由，若至多一个源是高斯的，则 $I(\by)$ 在可逆矩阵 $\bW$ 上的最小点（在那里 $I(\by) = 0$）恰好是分离矩阵：互信息是 Comon（1994）意义下的对比函数。似然与它之间有一个精确的恒等式。

按定义，$D(p_{\by} \| \prod_i p_i) = \E[\log p_{\by}(\by)] - \sum_i \E[\log p_i(y_i)] = -H(\by) - \sum_i \E[\log p_i(y_i)]$。代入 $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$，得 $-D(p_{\by}\|\prod_i p_i) = H(\bx) + \mathcal{L}(\bW)$，这就是第一个等式。对第二个等式，插入 $\prod_i p_{y_i}$：

这里第二个期望只涉及 $y_i$，因而是边缘密度之间的散度。

The non-Gaussianity principle of is not a heuristic after all: it is an exact reformulation of independence, with negentropy in the role of kurtosis, and the sources are the rotation that concentrates all the available negentropy $\sum_i J(s_i)$ in the marginals. Without whitening, the same substitution gives the identity $I(\by) = J(\bx) - \sum_i J(y_i) + \frac12\log\bigl(\prod_i \Var(y_i)/\det\bC_{\by}\bigr)$ for any invertible $\bW$ (Comon, 1994). The last term is nonnegative by Hadamard's inequality and vanishes exactly for uncorrelated outputs, so restricting the search to whitened data and rotations loses nothing.

Infomax is maximum likelihoodInfomax 即最大似然

Bell & Sejnowski (1995) approached separation from the side of neural networks. A layer of units computes $\by = \bW\bx$ and passes each output through an increasing squashing function $g_i : \R \to (0, 1)$, giving $u_i = g_i(y_i)$. Their Infomax principle maximizes the information that the outputs $\bu$ carry about the inputs $\bx$; for a deterministic invertible map of continuous variables, however, $I(\bx;\bu)$ is generally infinite and is not a finite optimization objective. With independent additive output noise $\boldsymbol{\eta}$, $I(\bx;\bu+\boldsymbol{\eta}) = H(\bu+\boldsymbol{\eta})-H(\boldsymbol{\eta})$; in a suitable small-noise limit this motivates maximizing the noiseless output entropy $H(\bu)$. The entropy-to-likelihood identity below is exact for the noiseless transform. With the logistic function $g(y) = 1/(1 + e^{-y})$, the gradient of $H(\bu)$ is $\bW^{-\top} + \E[(\mathbf{1} - 2\bu)\bx^\top]$, and the resulting algorithm separated mixtures of audio signals; it became one of the first widely used ICA methods.

Bell & Sejnowski（1995）从神经网络的角度研究分离问题。一层神经元计算 $\by = \bW\bx$，再把每个输出送入一个递增的压缩函数 $g_i : \R \to (0, 1)$，得到 $u_i = g_i(y_i)$。他们的 Infomax 原理最大化输出 $\bu$ 所携带的关于输入 $\bx$ 的信息；但对于连续变量的确定性可逆映射，$I(\bx;\bu)$ 通常为无穷大，不能直接作为有限的优化目标。加入独立的输出噪声 $\boldsymbol{\eta}$ 后，$I(\bx;\bu+\boldsymbol{\eta}) = H(\bu+\boldsymbol{\eta})-H(\boldsymbol{\eta})$；在适当的小噪声极限下，这为最大化无噪声输出熵 $H(\bu)$ 提供了动机。下面的输出熵与似然恒等式对无噪声变换精确成立。取 logistic 函数 $g(y) = 1/(1 + e^{-y})$ 时，$H(\bu)$ 的梯度为 $\bW^{-\top} + \E[(\mathbf{1} - 2\bu)\bx^\top]$；由此得到的算法成功分离了音频信号的混合，成为最早被广泛使用的 ICA 方法之一。

$$H(\bu) = H(\bx) + \mathcal{L}(\bW) = -D\Bigl(p_{\by} \,\Big\|\, \prod_i p_i\Bigr) \le 0 .$$

Maximizing the output entropy is therefore maximum likelihood estimation with the model densities $g_i'$. The bound $0$ is attained exactly when the outputs are independent with densities $p_i$, that is, when $\bu$ is uniform on the unit cube.

The map $\by \mapsto \bu$ is smooth and invertible with Jacobian $\diag(g_1'(y_1), \dots, g_n'(y_n))$, and under such a map the entropy changes by the expected log-Jacobian: $H(\bu) = H(\by) + \sum_i \E[\log g_i'(y_i)]$. With $H(\by) = H(\bx) + \log|\det\bW|$ and $g_i' = p_i$ this is $H(\bx) + \mathcal{L}(\bW)$, and gives the divergence form. A divergence vanishes only when the two densities coincide, here when $\by$ has density $\prod_i p_i$; by the probability integral transform this happens exactly when the $u_i = g_i(y_i)$ are independent and uniform on $(0, 1)$.

When the source model is wrong源模型设错时

In practice the source densities are unknown, and maximum likelihood is run with a fixed model such as the logistic density. This is less dangerous than it sounds, because of a simple property of the estimating equations.

实践中源密度是未知的，最大似然只能用一个固定的模型（例如 logistic 密度）来运行。这并不像听起来那么危险，原因在于估计方程的一个简单性质。

Let the sources be independent with zero mean, let $\varphi_1, \dots, \varphi_n$ be arbitrary model scores, and suppose that for each $i$ the scale equation

设各源独立且均值为零，$\varphi_1, \dots, \varphi_n$ 是任意的模型得分函数，并设对每个 $i$，尺度方程

$$\E\bigl[\varphi_i(d_i s_i)\, d_i s_i\bigr] = 1$$

has a solution $d_i \neq 0$. Then $\bW = \bD\bA^{-1}$ with $\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_n)$ satisfies the estimating equations $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\, y_j] = \delta_{ij}$, so it is a stationary point of $\mathcal{L}$. The same holds for every ordering of the sources, with each model paired with the source it extracts.

有解 $d_i \neq 0$。则 $\bW = \bD\bA^{-1}$（$\bD = \diag(d_1, \dots, d_n)$）满足估计方程 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)\, y_j] = \delta_{ij}$，因而是 $\mathcal{L}$ 的驻点。对源的任意排列也是如此，只需把每个模型与它所提取的源配对。

Here $y_i = d_i s_i$. For $i \neq j$, independence and $\E[s_j] = 0$ give $\E[\varphi_i(d_i s_i)\, d_j s_j] = \E[\varphi_i(d_i s_i)]\, d_j \E[s_j] = 0$. For $i = j$ the equation is the scale equation.

此时 $y_i = d_i s_i$。对 $i \neq j$，由独立性与 $\E[s_j] = 0$ 得 $\E[\varphi_i(d_i s_i)\, d_j s_j] = \E[\varphi_i(d_i s_i)]\, d_j \E[s_j] = 0$。对 $i = j$，方程就是尺度方程。

So separation is a stationary point of the likelihood whatever the model, as long as the scale equations can be solved; for a nondegenerate source with finite first absolute moment when $\varphi = \tanh$, or finite nonzero fourth moment when $\varphi(u) = u^3$, they can be solved, because $\E[\varphi(ds)\,ds]$ grows continuously from $0$ to $\infty$ as $d$ increases. First-pass bridge. Exact entropy needs an entire density; an approximation replaces it with a few measurable averages. This saves estimation effort but discards information. Negentropy, mutual information and the likelihood all involve densities, which are hard to estimate and awkward to optimize. Practical contrasts replace the negentropy of a standardized output by a function of a few expectations. Throughout this section $\E[y] = 0$ and $\E[y^2] = 1$, as holds automatically for $y = \bw^\top\bz$ with $\|\bw\| = 1$, and $\nu \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$ with density $\phi$. For such $y$, negentropy is a divergence from the Gaussian: $\log\phi$ is quadratic, so $\E[\log\phi(y)] = \E[\log\phi(\nu)] = -H(\nu)$ and $J(y) = H(\nu) - H(y) = D(p_y \,\|\, \phi)$.

负熵、互信息与似然都涉及密度，而密度既难以估计，也不便于优化。实用的对比函数用少数几个期望的函数来代替标准化输出的负熵。本节始终假定 $\E[y] = 0$、$\E[y^2] = 1$（对 $\|\bw\| = 1$ 的 $y = \bw^\top\bz$ 这自动成立），并记 $\nu \sim \Ncal(0, 1)$，其密度为 $\phi$。对这样的 $y$，负熵就是相对于高斯的散度：$\log\phi$ 是二次函数，所以 $\E[\log\phi(y)] = \E[\log\phi(\nu)] = -H(\nu)$，从而 $J(y) = H(\nu) - H(y) = D(p_y \,\|\, \phi)$。

None of the approximations is accurate in absolute terms far from the Gaussian, which is not what they were designed for. The cumulant approximation overweights heavy tails (it gives $0.75$ for the $t_5$ law, sixteen times the true value) and returns zero for the zero-kurtosis mixture. The approximations with $G$ are of the right size for the three super-Gaussian laws (within about $20\%$ with $-e^{-u^2/2}$, within a factor of $1.7$ with $\log\cosh$), react to the zero-kurtosis mixture, and underestimate strongly sub-Gaussian and skewed laws; an even $G$ depends only on the symmetrized density $[p(u)+p(-u)]/2$, so it cannot distinguish a density from its reflection or detect a purely antisymmetric perturbation that leaves this symmetrized density unchanged. It can still detect many skewed laws, including the exponential example in the table. Odd functions add information about asymmetry; they are useful rather than mandatory for every skewed source. What matters for ICA, however, is how a contrast varies across the projections of one data set, and there the approximations follow the exact negentropy closely, as shows.

在远离高斯的地方，这些近似的绝对数值都不准确，这本来也不是它们的设计目标。累积量近似过分看重重尾（对 $t_5$ 分布给出 $0.75$，是真值的十六倍），对峭度为零的混合则给出零。基于 $G$ 的近似对三个超高斯分布给出了量级正确的值（用 $-e^{-u^2/2}$ 时误差约在 $20\%$ 以内，用 $\log\cosh$ 时在 $1.7$ 倍以内），能对峭度为零的混合作出反应，但低估了强亚高斯分布与偏斜分布；偶函数 $G$ 只依赖于对称化密度 $[p(u)+p(-u)]/2$，因此不能区分一个密度与其镜像，也不能发现保持对称化密度不变的纯反对称扰动。但它仍能检测许多偏斜分布，表中的指数分布就是一例。奇函数提供额外的不对称性信息，并非对每个偏斜源都不可缺少。不过，对 ICA 真正重要的是对比函数在同一组数据的不同投影之间如何变化，而在这一点上，这些近似紧紧跟随精确负熵，如所示。

Why these approximations are more robust为什么这些近似更稳健

The advantages over kurtosis come from the slow growth of $G$. First, variance: the sample average $\hat\E[G(y)]$ has variance $\Var(G(y))/T$, which is finite as soon as $\E[G(y)^2] < \infty$. Since $0 \le \log\cosh u \le |u|$, a finite variance of $y$ suffices for $G = \log\cosh$, and $-e^{-u^2/2}$ is bounded, so it needs no moment at all; the sample kurtosis needs $m_8 < \infty$ (). Second, influence: one observation $x_0$ moves $\hat\E[G(y)]$ by roughly $G(x_0)/T$, which is at most about $|x_0|/T$ for $\log\cosh$ and at most $1/T$ for $-e^{-u^2/2}$, against $x_0^4/T$ for the fourth moment. Third, sensitivity: they respond to departures from Gaussianity that leave the fourth moment unchanged. The following script compares the sample kurtosis with the $\log\cosh$ statistic on Gaussian data and on the zero-kurtosis source.

相对于峭度的优势来自 $G$ 的缓慢增长。其一是方差：样本平均 $\hat\E[G(y)]$ 的方差为 $\Var(G(y))/T$，只要 $\E[G(y)^2] < \infty$ 就有限。由于 $0 \le \log\cosh u \le |u|$，对 $G = \log\cosh$ 只需 $y$ 的方差有限；$-e^{-u^2/2}$ 有界，完全不需要矩条件；而样本峭度需要 $m_8 < \infty$（）。其二是影响：单个观测 $x_0$ 使 $\hat\E[G(y)]$ 变化约 $G(x_0)/T$，对 $\log\cosh$ 至多约为 $|x_0|/T$，对 $-e^{-u^2/2}$ 至多为 $1/T$，而对四阶矩则是 $x_0^4/T$。其三是敏感性：它们能察觉不改变四阶矩的那些对高斯的偏离。下面的脚本在高斯数据和峭度为零的源上，比较样本峭度与 $\log\cosh$ 统计量。

import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
T, runs = 10000, 300
EG_nu = 0.374567                          # E log cosh(nu), nu ~ N(0, 1)

def logcosh(u):
    return np.abs(u) + np.log1p(np.exp(-2 * np.abs(u))) - np.log(2)

def zero_kurtosis_source(size):
    """Uniform with probability 5/7, Laplacian with probability 2/7."""
    pick = rng.random(size) < 5 / 7
    return np.where(pick, rng.uniform(-np.sqrt(3), np.sqrt(3), size),
                    rng.laplace(0.0, 1 / np.sqrt(2), size))

sources = [("Gaussian", rng.standard_normal),
           ("zero-kurtosis", zero_kurtosis_source)]
for name, draw in sources:
    k, g = [], []
    for _ in range(runs):
        y = draw(T)
        y = (y - y.mean()) / y.std()              # standardize
        k.append(np.mean(y**4) - 3)               # sample kurtosis
        g.append(np.mean(logcosh(y)) - EG_nu)     # E G(y) - E G(nu)
    print(f"{name:14s} kurtosis {np.mean(k):+.3f} +/- {np.std(k):.3f}   "
          f"G-statistic {np.mean(g):+.4f} +/- {np.std(g):.4f}")

Over 300 samples of size $T = 10000$, the sample kurtosis is $+0.003 \pm 0.049$ for Gaussian data and $+0.017 \pm 0.230$ for the zero-kurtosis source: kurtosis cannot tell them apart, and its spread is almost five times larger for the non-Gaussian law. The $\log\cosh$ statistic $\E G(y) - \E G(\nu)$ is $-0.0001 \pm 0.0008$ for the Gaussian and $+0.0087 \pm 0.0015$ for the zero-kurtosis source, a separation of about six standard deviations. (The logarithm is written in a form that does not overflow for large $|u|$.)

对 300 个样本量为 $T = 10000$ 的样本，高斯数据的样本峭度为 $+0.003 \pm 0.049$，峭度为零的源为 $+0.017 \pm 0.230$：峭度区分不了二者，而且对这个非高斯分布，它的离散程度大了将近五倍。$\log\cosh$ 统计量 $\E G(y) - \E G(\nu)$ 对高斯数据为 $-0.0001 \pm 0.0008$，对峭度为零的源为 $+0.0087 \pm 0.0015$，两者相差约六个标准差。（代码中对数的写法保证 $|u|$ 很大时也不会溢出。）

Using likelihood without overinterpreting it如何使用似然而不作过度解读

Keep the statistical model explicit. A likelihood value is meaningful only with its normalized marginal densities, the change-of-variables term, and the data units. Omitting a density normalizer is harmless only when it is independent of every fitted parameter; this matters when fitting scale or shape parameters. Comparing unmixing matrices by $\sum_i\hat\E\log p_i(y_i)$ alone omits $\log|\det\bW|$ and rewards spurious scale changes. A singular $\bW$ has no square change-of-variables likelihood.

明确统计模型。似然值必须连同归一化的边缘密度、变量变换项和数据单位一起解释。只有归一化常数与所有待拟合参数无关时才能忽略它，拟合尺度或形状参数时尤其需要注意。只用 $\sum_i\hat\E\log p_i(y_i)$ 比较解混矩阵会漏掉 $\log|\det\bW|$，从而错误奖励尺度变化。$\bW$ 奇异时，方阵变量变换的似然不成立。

Stationarity does not certify independence. Monitor $\|\bI-\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top]\|_F$, the objective, convergence across starts and residual dependence on held-out data. A small residual can occur at an unwanted stationary point; low correlation checks only second-order structure. For a fixed standard-Gaussian source model and whitened data, every orthogonal rotation has the same likelihood although most rotations still mix non-Gaussian sources. The identity explains the cancellation between dependence and marginal mismatch.

驻点不证明独立性。同时监测 $\|\bI-\hat\E[\bphi(\by)\by^\top]\|_F$、目标函数、不同初值的收敛结果以及留出数据上的残余依赖。很小的残差也可能对应不需要的驻点；很小的相关系数只检查二阶结构。若源模型固定为标准高斯分布，数据已白化，则所有正交旋转都有相同的似然，但大多数旋转仍然混合着非高斯源。解释了依赖项与边缘失配项如何相互抵消。

Separate fitting from assessment. Estimate the mean, whitening transform, unmixing matrix and density parameters on training observations, then apply the fixed transformations to held-out observations. Higher held-out likelihood supports predictive fit of that model, not the physical meaning of its components. For dependent observations, use time-block or subject-level splits appropriate to the sampling design. An optimized wrong model can still prefer dependent outputs.

区分拟合与评价。用训练观测估计均值、白化变换、解混矩阵和密度参数，再将固定变换用于留出观测。较高的留出似然支持模型的预测拟合，并不证明成分具有物理意义。观测之间有依赖时，应根据抽样设计按时间块或被试划分数据。即使一个错误模型被完全优化，它仍可能偏好有依赖的输出。

Distinguish a local expansion from a density. A truncated Gram–Charlier expression or $\phi(1+\eta\tilde G)$ need not stay nonnegative in the tails. The normalization and moment calculations describe a formal local expansion; they do not make the truncated expression a valid probability density for arbitrary coefficients. Likewise, a slowly growing $G$ limits the influence on its sample average at a fixed projection, but empirical centering and covariance whitening can still be strongly affected by outliers.

区分局部展开与概率密度。截断的 Gram–Charlier 表达式或 $\phi(1+\eta\tilde G)$ 在尾部未必非负。归一化与矩的计算描述的是形式局部展开，并不保证任意系数下的截断式都是有效概率密度。同样，增长较慢的 $G$ 可以限制固定投影下单点对样本平均的影响，但经验中心化与协方差白化仍可能受到离群值的强烈影响。

A score derivative needs regularity. For Laplace sources $\psi(s)=\sqrt2\sign(s)$ and $\mathcal J=2$, but the ordinary derivative of $\psi$ is zero away from the origin. Inserting that derivative into $\E[\psi']=\mathcal J$ would incorrectly give zero: the jump contributes a boundary term, or a delta mass in the distributional derivative. Use nonsmooth estimating-equation theory or a smooth score approximation; do not implement Laplace FastICA by taking sign and setting its derivative to zero.

得分函数的导数需要正则条件。对拉普拉斯源，$\psi(s)=\sqrt2\sign(s)$，$\mathcal J=2$，但 $\psi$ 的普通导数在原点以外都为零。直接将该导数代入 $\E[\psi']=\mathcal J$ 会错误地得到零：跳跃带来了边界项，也可用分布导数中的 delta 质量表示。应采用非光滑估计方程理论或光滑得分近似，不能简单用 sign 并把其导数设为零来实现拉普拉斯 FastICA。

Key points

本章要点

  • The change of variables gives the likelihood $\log L(\bW) = \sum_t\sum_i \log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx(t)) + T\log|\det\bW|$; its stationary points solve the estimating equations $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_j] = \delta_{ij}$, with $\varphi_i = -p_i'/p_i$ the model scores.
  • Exactly, $\mathcal{L}(\bW) = -H(\bx) - I(\by) - \sum_i D(p_{y_i}\|p_i)$: maximum likelihood minimizes the dependence of the outputs plus the mismatch of their marginals with the model.
  • For whitened data and rotations, $I(\by) = J(\bz) - \sum_i J(y_i)$: minimizing dependence is maximizing the non-Gaussianity of the outputs.
  • Infomax with squashing functions $g_i$ is maximum likelihood with model densities $g_i'$; the logistic choice is a super-Gaussian model.
  • When the scale equations have solutions and the expectations exist, separation solves the estimating equations after rescaling; only its stability depends on the model, through the conditions $\gamma_i > 0$ and $\gamma_i\gamma_j > 1$ for every pair. The true score is always stable when at most one source is Gaussian, while a fixed sub- or super-Gaussian model can fail, which is why models are switched per output.
  • Negentropy can be approximated by $\frac1{12}\kappa_3^2 + \frac1{48}\kappa_4^2$, which inherits the weaknesses of kurtosis, or by $k_G[\E G(y) - \E G(\nu)]^2$ with $\log\cosh$ or $-e^{-u^2/2}$, which needs weaker moment conditions and sees beyond the fourth moment; the robustness of the full procedure also depends on centering and whitening.
  • 变量替换给出似然 $\log L(\bW) = \sum_t\sum_i \log p_i(\bw_i^\top\bx(t)) + T\log|\det\bW|$；其驻点满足估计方程 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_j] = \delta_{ij}$，其中 $\varphi_i = -p_i'/p_i$ 是模型得分函数。
  • 精确地有 $\mathcal{L}(\bW) = -H(\bx) - I(\by) - \sum_i D(p_{y_i}\|p_i)$：最大似然最小化的是输出的相依性加上其边缘分布与模型的失配。
  • 对白化数据与旋转，$I(\by) = J(\bz) - \sum_i J(y_i)$：最小化相依性就是最大化输出的非高斯性。
  • 以 $g_i$ 为压缩函数的 Infomax 就是以 $g_i'$ 为模型密度的最大似然；logistic 函数对应超高斯模型。
  • 尺度方程有解且有关期望存在时，经过尺度调整后分离解满足估计方程；只有它的稳定性依赖于模型，由条件 $\gamma_i > 0$ 以及对每一对源的 $\gamma_i\gamma_j > 1$ 决定。至多一个源是高斯时，真实得分函数总是稳定的；固定的亚高斯或超高斯模型则可能失败，这就是要为每个输出切换模型的原因。
  • 负熵可以用 $\frac1{12}\kappa_3^2 + \frac1{48}\kappa_4^2$ 近似，它继承了峭度的弱点；也可以用 $k_G[\E G(y) - \E G(\nu)]^2$（取 $\log\cosh$ 或 $-e^{-u^2/2}$）近似，后者只需较弱的矩条件，并能看到四阶矩以外的信息；整个过程的稳健性还取决于中心化与白化。

(a) $\log p(u) = -\frac12\log 2 - \sqrt2|u|$, so $\psi(u) = \sqrt2\,\sign(u)$ for $u \neq 0$. The mean absolute value of a Laplace variable equals its scale, here $1/\sqrt2$, so $\E[\psi(s)s] = \sqrt2\,\E|s| = 1$, and $\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2] = 2$, which exceeds the Gaussian value $1$. For the hyperbolic secant density, $\log p(u) = -\log\pi - \log\cosh u$, whose derivative is $-\tanh u$, so $\psi(u) = \tanh u$. Both scores saturate, as befits super-Gaussian densities.

（a）$\log p(u) = -\frac12\log 2 - \sqrt2|u|$，所以 $u \neq 0$ 时 $\psi(u) = \sqrt2\,\sign(u)$。拉普拉斯变量的平均绝对值等于其尺度参数，这里为 $1/\sqrt2$，故 $\E[\psi(s)s] = \sqrt2\,\E|s| = 1$，且 $\mathcal{J} = \E[\psi(s)^2] = 2$，大于高斯分布的取值 $1$。（b）对 logistic 密度，$\log p(u) = -u - 2\log(1 + e^{-u})$，其导数为 $-1 + 2e^{-u}/(1 + e^{-u}) = -(1 - e^{-u})/(1 + e^{-u}) = -\tanh(u/2)$，因此 $\psi(u) = \tanh(u/2)$。对双曲正割密度，$\log p(u) = -\log\pi - \log\cosh u$，其导数为 $-\tanh u$，所以 $\psi(u) = \tanh u$。两个得分函数都是饱和的，这与超高斯密度相符。

Show that $I(\bD\by) = I(\by)$ for every invertible diagonal $\bD$, whereas $\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW)$ depends on $\bD$. Show that the condition $\partial\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW)/\partial d_i = 0$ at $\bD = \bI$ is the diagonal estimating equation $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_i] = 1$.

证明对任意可逆对角矩阵 $\bD$ 都有 $I(\bD\by) = I(\by)$，而 $\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW)$ 依赖于 $\bD$。证明在 $\bD = \bI$ 处 $\partial\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW)/\partial d_i = 0$ 恰好就是对角估计方程 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_i] = 1$。

$H(d_i y_i) = H(y_i) + \log|d_i|$ and $H(\bD\by) = H(\by) + \sum_i\log|d_i|$, so the logarithms cancel in $I(\bD\by) = \sum_i H(d_iy_i) - H(\bD\by)$. On the other hand $\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW) = \sum_i \E[\log p_i(d_i y_i)] + \log|\det\bW| + \sum_i \log|d_i|$, which changes with $\bD$ unless the $p_i$ have a special form. Its derivative with respect to $d_i$ is $-\E[\varphi_i(d_iy_i)\,y_i] + 1/d_i$, which at $d_i = 1$ vanishes exactly when $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_i] = 1$. The diagonal estimating equations therefore only fix the scales of the outputs, the one thing that mutual information ignores; the off-diagonal equations carry the information about independence.

$H(d_i y_i) = H(y_i) + \log|d_i|$，$H(\bD\by) = H(\by) + \sum_i\log|d_i|$，所以在 $I(\bD\by) = \sum_i H(d_iy_i) - H(\bD\by)$ 中这些对数项相互抵消。另一方面，$\mathcal{L}(\bD\bW) = \sum_i \E[\log p_i(d_i y_i)] + \log|\det\bW| + \sum_i \log|d_i|$，除非 $p_i$ 具有特殊形式，否则它随 $\bD$ 变化。它对 $d_i$ 的导数为 $-\E[\varphi_i(d_iy_i)\,y_i] + 1/d_i$，在 $d_i = 1$ 处为零当且仅当 $\E[\varphi_i(y_i)y_i] = 1$。因此对角估计方程只确定输出的尺度，而这恰恰是互信息所不关心的；关于独立性的信息由非对角方程承载。

(a) For $\varphi(u) = u^3$ and a unit-variance source with kurtosis $\kappa$, show that $\gamma = 3/(3 + \kappa)$. Let $y$ have zero mean, unit variance, and third and fourth cumulants $\kappa_3, \kappa_4$, and suppose its density is $p = \phi(1 + \varepsilon)$ with $\varepsilon = \frac{\kappa_3}{6}H_3 + \frac{\kappa_4}{24}H_4$, a small correction. (a) Check that $p$ has the stated moments up to order four, using $\int \phi\, H_j H_k = k!\,\delta_{jk}$. (b) Derive from $J(y) = D(p\|\phi)$ by expanding $\log(1 + \varepsilon)$ to second order.

设 $y$ 均值为零、方差为一，三阶与四阶累积量为 $\kappa_3, \kappa_4$，并设其密度为 $p = \phi(1 + \varepsilon)$，其中 $\varepsilon = \frac{\kappa_3}{6}H_3 + \frac{\kappa_4}{24}H_4$ 是小的修正项。（a）利用 $\int \phi\, H_j H_k = k!\,\delta_{jk}$，验证 $p$ 直到四阶的矩与所述相符。（b）由 $J(y) = D(p\|\phi)$ 出发，把 $\log(1 + \varepsilon)$ 展开到二阶，推导 。

(a) The monomials $1, u, u^2$ are combinations of $H_0, H_1, H_2$, which are orthogonal to $H_3$ and $H_4$, so $p$ integrates to one and has the mean $0$ and variance $1$ of $\phi$. Since $u^4 = H_4 + 6H_2 + 3H_0$, $\int u^4 p = 3 + \frac{\kappa_4}{24}\cdot 4! = 3 + \kappa_4$, so the fourth cumulant is $\kappa_4$. (b) $D(p\|\phi) = \int \phi(1+\varepsilon)\log(1+\varepsilon)$. This is the same result as with $G = u^3$ and $G = u^4$: the normalized Hermite polynomials $H_3/\sqrt{6}$ and $H_4/\sqrt{24}$ are exactly the orthonormalized versions of $u^3$ and $u^4$, and their expectations under $y$ are $\kappa_3/\sqrt6$ and $\kappa_4/\sqrt{24}$.

这与在 中取 $G = u^3$ 与 $G = u^4$ 的结果相同：归一化的 Hermite 多项式 $H_3/\sqrt{6}$ 与 $H_4/\sqrt{24}$ 恰好是 $u^3$ 与 $u^4$ 经正交归一化后的形式，它们在 $y$ 下的期望分别为 $\kappa_3/\sqrt6$ 与 $\kappa_4/\sqrt{24}$。

The entropy power inequality states that for independent random variables $X$ and $Y$ with densities, finite variances and finite differential entropies, $e^{2H(X+Y)} \ge e^{2H(X)} + e^{2H(Y)}$, with equality only if $X$ and $Y$ are Gaussian (Cover & Thomas, 2006). Let $\bs$ have independent unit-variance components with densities and finite differential entropies, and let $y = \bq^\top\bs$ with $\|\bq\| = 1$. Show that $J(y) \le \max_i J(s_i)$, and that if some source is non-Gaussian, equality holds only for $\bq = \pm\be_i$ with $J(s_i) = \max_j J(s_j)$.

熵幂不等式断言：对具有密度、有限方差和有限微分熵的独立随机变量 $X$ 与 $Y$，$e^{2H(X+Y)} \ge e^{2H(X)} + e^{2H(Y)}$，等号仅当 $X$ 与 $Y$ 都是高斯时成立（Cover & Thomas, 2006）。设 $\bs$ 的各分量独立、方差为一、有密度且微分熵有限，令 $y = \bq^\top\bs$，$\|\bq\| = 1$。证明 $J(y) \le \max_i J(s_i)$；并证明若有某个源非高斯，则等号仅在 $\bq = \pm\be_i$ 且 $J(s_i) = \max_j J(s_j)$ 时成立。

By induction the inequality extends to finite sums of independent variables, $e^{2H(\sum_i X_i)} \ge \sum_i e^{2H(X_i)}$, with equality only if all $X_i$ are Gaussian. Apply it to $X_i = q_i s_i$ for the indices with $q_i \neq 0$. Since $H(q_i s_i) = H(s_i) + \log|q_i|$, we have $e^{2H(X_i)} = q_i^2 e^{2H(s_i)}$. The sources $s_i$ and the final projection $y$ have unit variance (the scaled summands $X_i$ generally do not), so $H(\cdot) = h - J(\cdot)$ with $h = \frac12\log(2\pi e)$, and the inequality becomes $e^{-2J(y)} \ge \sum_i q_i^2 e^{-2J(s_i)}$. Since the weights $q_i^2$ sum to one, the right side is at least $e^{-2\max_i J(s_i)}$, hence $J(y) \le \max_i J(s_i)$. If two or more $q_i$ are nonzero, equality would require equality in the entropy power inequality, hence Gaussian $s_i$ for those indices, and then $J(y) = 0$; so if $\max_j J(s_j) > 0$, equality forces $\bq = \pm\be_i$, and then $J(y) = J(s_i)$ must be the maximum. In whitened coordinates, the global maximum of $J(\bw^\top\bz)$ on the sphere is therefore attained exactly at the sources of largest negentropy. The argument says nothing about other local maxima; unlike , it does not rule them out.

由归纳法，该不等式可推广到有限个独立变量之和：$e^{2H(\sum_i X_i)} \ge \sum_i e^{2H(X_i)}$，等号仅当所有 $X_i$ 都是高斯时成立。对满足 $q_i \neq 0$ 的下标取 $X_i = q_i s_i$。由 $H(q_i s_i) = H(s_i) + \log|q_i|$ 得 $e^{2H(X_i)} = q_i^2 e^{2H(s_i)}$。各源 $s_i$ 与最终投影 $y$ 的方差为一（缩放后的加项 $X_i$ 通常不是），所以 $H(\cdot) = h - J(\cdot)$，其中 $h = \frac12\log(2\pi e)$，不等式变为 $e^{-2J(y)} \ge \sum_i q_i^2 e^{-2J(s_i)}$。由于权重 $q_i^2$ 之和为一，右边至少为 $e^{-2\max_i J(s_i)}$，因此 $J(y) \le \max_i J(s_i)$。若有两个或更多 $q_i$ 非零，取等号就要求熵幂不等式取等号，从而这些下标对应的 $s_i$ 都是高斯的，此时 $J(y) = 0$；所以当 $\max_j J(s_j) > 0$ 时，等号迫使 $\bq = \pm\be_i$，而此时 $J(y) = J(s_i)$ 必须是最大值。换到白化坐标下，$J(\bw^\top\bz)$ 在球面上的全局最大值恰好在负熵最大的源处取得。这一论证对其他局部极大点只字未提；与不同，它并不排除它们的存在。
