About this book关于本书

In the classical noiseless linear ICA model, independent, nondegenerate sources mixed by a fixed full-column-rank matrix are identifiable up to scaling and permutation when at most one source is Gaussian. These assumptions describe a model, not a guarantee that any algorithm will recover every finite dataset. This book develops the corresponding identifiability theory, contrasts, algorithms and statistical limits, then explains which conclusions change with noise, temporal structure, more sources than sensors, nonlinear mixing, and brain-imaging data.

在经典的无噪线性 ICA 模型中，若各源独立且非退化、混合矩阵固定且满列秩、至多一个源服从高斯分布，则模型在尺度与置换意义下可辨识。这些假设描述的是一个模型，并不保证任意算法都能从有限数据中准确恢复源。本书从可辨识性理论、对比函数、算法和统计极限出发，再解释加入噪声、时间结构、过完备混合、非线性混合或脑影像数据后，哪些结论仍然成立，哪些需要改变。

This book develops the subject from first principles to research level. Every chapter states its results precisely, proves the central ones or points to where the proofs can be found, and checks the theory against small numerical experiments that readers can run themselves. Each chapter closes with exercises, from routine checks to derivations of published results, with worked solutions.

本书从基本原理一直讲到研究前沿。每一章都精确陈述结论，对核心结果给出证明或指出证明的出处，并用读者可以自己运行的小型数值实验来检验理论。每章末尾配有习题，从常规练习到已发表结果的推导，都附有完整解答。

How to read it如何阅读

The prerequisites are linear algebra (eigendecompositions, orthogonal matrices) and probability at the level of an undergraduate course. The chapters on statistical theory also use basic asymptotic statistics; Appendix A collects what is needed. Two paths through the book work well:

预备知识是线性代数（特征分解、正交矩阵）和本科程度的概率论。统计理论各章还会用到基本的渐近统计，所需内容汇集在附录 A。推荐两条阅读路线：

  • Practice first. Chapters 1, 3, 5 and 8, then Chapter 11 on noise and reliability, followed by Part VI on fMRI.
  • Theory first. Parts I and II in order, then Chapters 7, 10, 12 and 13.
  • 应用优先。第 1、3、5、8 章，再读第 11 章的噪声与可靠性，最后进入第六部分（fMRI）。
  • 理论优先。按顺序读第一、二部分，再读第 7、10、12、13 章。

Learning checkpoints学习检查点

  • Before running an algorithm: write down the data orientation, source assumptions, retained dimension and whitening convention. Distinguish population assumptions from sample diagnostics.
  • Before interpreting results: distinguish convergence, independence, stability and scientific validity. A small reconstruction error or orthogonal output does not establish successful source separation.
  • Before comparing methods: use one-to-one component matching and the same preprocessing, sample budget and scale convention. Repeat initializations and report failures as well as successful runs.
  • Before reporting uncertainty: check finite moments, dependence across observations and estimated preprocessing. Appendix A explains numerical rank, long-run covariance and the second-order behavior of squared errors.
  • 运行算法前：写清数据朝向、源假设、保留维数与白化归一化约定，区分总体假设与样本诊断。
  • 解释结果前：区分收敛、独立性、稳定性与科学有效性。重建误差很小或输出正交，都不足以证明源分离成功。
  • 比较方法前：使用一一成分匹配，统一预处理、样本预算与尺度约定；重复随机初始化，同时报告失败与成功运行。
  • 报告不确定性前：检查矩是否有限、观测是否相关、预处理是否由数据估计。附录 A 补充了数值秩、长期协方差以及平方误差的二阶行为。

Contents目录

Part I · Foundations第一部分 · 基础

  1. Chapter 1第 1 章The ICA ProblemICA 问题

    The cocktail-party problem, the linear mixing model, what can and cannot be recovered, and how ICA differs from PCA and factor analysis.

    鸡尾酒会问题、线性混合模型、哪些量可以恢复哪些不能，以及 ICA 与 PCA、因子分析的区别。

  2. Chapter 2第 2 章Independence, Cumulants and Entropy独立性、累积量与熵

    The probability toolkit of ICA: independence versus uncorrelatedness, characteristic functions, moments and cumulants, differential entropy, mutual information and negentropy.

    ICA 的概率工具：独立与不相关、特征函数、矩与累积量、微分熵、互信息与负熵。

  3. Chapter 3第 3 章Whitening and the Geometry of ICA白化与 ICA 的几何

    Second-order statistics determine the mixing matrix up to a rotation. Whitening, PCA reduction, and why a rotation is exactly what higher-order statistics must find.

    二阶统计量只能把混合矩阵确定到一个旋转。白化、PCA 降维，以及为什么高阶统计量要找的恰好是这个旋转。

Part II · Principles第二部分 · 原理

  1. Chapter 4第 4 章Identifiability可辨识性

    The Darmois–Skitovich theorem and Comon's theorem: with at most one Gaussian source, the mixing matrix is determined up to scaling and permutation.

    Darmois–Skitovich 定理与 Comon 定理：至多一个高斯源时，混合矩阵在尺度与置换意义下唯一。

  2. Chapter 5第 5 章Non-Gaussianity and Kurtosis非高斯性与峭度

    Why mixing makes signals more Gaussian, kurtosis as a contrast, the landscape of kurtosis on the sphere, and the price of its sensitivity to outliers.

    为什么混合会让信号更接近高斯、峭度作为对比函数、峭度在球面上的地形，以及它对离群值敏感的代价。

  3. Chapter 6第 6 章Negentropy, Mutual Information and Likelihood负熵、互信息与似然

    Information-theoretic contrasts and maximum likelihood: their equivalences, the score function, Infomax, robust negentropy approximations, and what happens when the source model is wrong.

    信息论对比函数与最大似然：它们的等价关系、得分函数、Infomax、稳健的负熵近似，以及源模型设错时会发生什么。

Part III · Algorithms第三部分 · 算法

  1. Chapter 7第 7 章Natural Gradient, Infomax and Stability自然梯度、Infomax 与稳定性

    The relative (natural) gradient, equivariance, local stability of the separating solutions, and the Infomax and extended Infomax algorithms.

    相对（自然）梯度、等变性、分离解的局部稳定性，以及 Infomax 与扩展 Infomax 算法。

  2. Chapter 8第 8 章FastICAFastICA 算法

    The fixed-point algorithm as an approximate Newton method: one-unit, deflation and symmetric versions, convergence rates, and the choice of nonlinearity.

    作为近似牛顿法的不动点算法：单单元、逐次提取与对称版本，收敛速度，以及非线性函数的选择。

  3. Chapter 9第 9 章Tensor and Second-Order Methods: JADE and SOBI张量方法与二阶方法：JADE 与 SOBI

    Fourth-order cumulant tensors and joint diagonalization (JADE), Jacobi rotations, and separation from temporal structure alone (AMUSE, SOBI).

    四阶累积量张量与联合对角化（JADE）、Jacobi 旋转，以及仅利用时间结构的分离（AMUSE、SOBI）。

Part IV · Statistical Theory第四部分 · 统计理论

  1. Chapter 10第 10 章Asymptotic Performance and the Cramér–Rao Bound渐近性能与 Cramér–Rao 界

    The gain matrix and interference-to-signal ratio, asymptotic variance of contrast-based estimators, the optimal nonlinearity, and the Cramér–Rao bound for ICA.

    增益矩阵与干扰信号比、基于对比函数的估计量的渐近方差、最优非线性函数，以及 ICA 的 Cramér–Rao 界。

  2. Chapter 11第 11 章Noise, Model Order and Reliability噪声、模型阶数与可靠性

    Noisy ICA, PCA reduction and model-order selection, split and unstable components, and how to assess the reliability of an ICA decomposition.

    含噪 ICA、PCA 降维与模型阶数选择、分裂与不稳定的成分，以及如何评估一次 ICA 分解的可靠性。

Part V · Beyond the Square Linear Model第五部分 · 超越方阵线性模型

  1. Chapter 12第 12 章Overcomplete ICA and Sparse Component Analysis过完备 ICA 与稀疏成分分析

    More sources than sensors: what is still identifiable, why sources cannot simply be inverted, tensor rank, and sparsity-based recovery.

    源多于传感器时：哪些仍可辨识、为什么源不能直接求逆、张量秩，以及基于稀疏性的恢复。

  2. Chapter 13第 13 章Nonlinear ICA非线性 ICA

    Why general nonlinear ICA is not identifiable, and how temporal structure or auxiliary variables restore identifiability (TCL, PCL, iVAE).

    为什么一般的非线性 ICA 不可辨识，以及时间结构或辅助变量如何恢复可辨识性（TCL、PCL、iVAE）。

Part VI · ICA for Brain Imaging第六部分 · 脑影像中的 ICA

  1. Chapter 14第 14 章Spatial ICA of fMRIfMRI 的空间 ICA

    Spatial versus temporal ICA, the fMRI data model, preprocessing and dimension reduction, and how to read components as networks or artifacts.

    空间 ICA 与时间 ICA、fMRI 数据模型、预处理与降维，以及如何把成分解读为网络或伪迹。

  2. Chapter 15第 15 章Group ICA, Dual Regression and IVA组 ICA、双回归与独立向量分析

    Multi-subject ICA: temporal concatenation, back-reconstruction, dual regression, spatially constrained ICA, independent vector analysis, network connectivity and multimodal fusion.

    多被试 ICA：时间拼接、反重建、双回归、空间约束 ICA、独立向量分析、网络连接以及多模态融合。
